This is the record of every rules change since the 2 Oct 2026 book, newest first. Each entry's date is the day the change went to the table. When a later change replaces an earlier one, the earlier entry says so. The rules chapters always show the game as it is now, so if you only want to play, read those and skip this.

## 27 Oct 2026: Armour's quality, and a box for every blow {#c-2026-10-27-armour}

**Armour, shields and helmets have a quality now: Common, Fine or Masterwork.** That's how many boxes the piece has, 1, 2 or 3, and every blow it takes for you ticks one. It's still a line on your Defend till its last box is ticked, and then it breaks. So the more you wear, the more blows you can take. Before, a piece broke the first time it took a blow for you. Quality doesn't add a step: a Masterwork shield's one line, the same as a Common one. See [Armour](#s-armour), [Quality](#s-armour-quality) and [Consequences](#s-consequences).

**A helmet's armour like any other:** a line on your Defend, one of your equipment, and a quality with its boxes. It's in the [item list](#the-item-list) now. The item list's fine sword is a sword, so it doesn't read as a quality.

**A hit that's two Hurts is two boxes**, on one piece or two, or one box and a Hurt.

**You start with Common, and one piece can be Fine.** Bram's plate is Fine, with two boxes, and his shield and Vesk's leathers are Common, with one each. See [What you carry](#s-carrying) and [the crew](#s-pregens).

**A slot in downtime mends one piece, every box on it**, broken or not, usually for a purse of gold, the same as mending something broken. A Workshop does it without a slot. See [Recover](#recover).

**What it costs:** Common armour's a purse of gold, Fine's several, and Masterwork's a small fortune, if you can find a smith who makes it. Before, real armour was several purses. See [What you can afford](#what-you-can-afford) and [Upgrading gear](#upgrading-gear).

## 27 Oct 2026: A good Aspiration, and a good Goal {#c-2026-10-27}

**When your Aspiration resolves, you get what you were after**, as well as the new archetype and its Title. Before, that was only in Vesk's example. See [Aspiration](#s-aspiration).

**What makes a good Aspiration is in the book now.** Past d12 you get what you wrote, so a good one passes four tests: it's one thing, you'd know the day it's done, it's about someone or something in the world, and the story goes on after it, with no *never* or *can't*. Make it something you could have in an arc or two (Vesk's first one took her seven sessions). The crew's are the examples, each next to a version that's too big or too vague. Before, the book only said it's one thing you want. See [A good Aspiration](#s-good-aspiration).

**The Party Goal works the same way**, at the size of one arc, and it should be something the whole crew wants. *Own the Low Market* is one arc of *rule the city*. See [A good Goal](#s-good-goal).

**The GM says when one's too big or too vague**, and you work it out together before it goes on the sheet, usually by keeping its first piece. The rest makes a good next Aspiration, or it's where the campaign's heading. Before, the book didn't say who decides.

## 26 Oct 2026: Confidence that swings, threads that turn, one action a faction {#c-2026-10-26}

Mo's answers after reading the 24 Oct change.

**Confidence swings with every roll now.** It still starts each scene on d6. It goes up a size for every telegraph your Defend or Confidence stops, and for every action where one of your dice wins. It goes down a size every time your wound die steps up (not when your armour or kit takes the blow), for every action where all your dice miss, and when fear, lies or pressure get through, unless 1 Resolve holds your ground. At d2 you're shaken, and you can't act at what the scene's about without 1 Resolve, so your action goes on pulling yourself together, back up to d4. That replaces Confidence going up only when you stopped all of it or took a threat down, and "bury it" is gone from what fear costs you. On d2, fear that gets through is a Hurt or 1 Resolve, and anything else leaves you on d2. See [Confidence](#s-confidence) and [When fear gets through](#s-fear-gets-through).

**Defend doubles your Defend, and nothing else.** Defending counts every line on your Defend twice, and your Confidence rolls as it is. It used to double your Confidence too. See [Defend](#s-defend).

**Nobody can intercept fear.** Intercept takes blows, so fear, lies and pressure stay with whoever they're aimed at, and a Bard's song is what shields the crew from them. Any creature can intercept blows, a summon included. See [Intercept](#s-intercept).

**A vulnerability can take another tag.** You choose where each extra tick goes: the same tag again, or another tag the hit could reach, so one vulnerability can strip two plain tags. It used to stay on the tag your die went for. See [Vulnerabilities](#s-vulnerable).

**A multiplier's as big as the creature needs,** placed where it makes sense: a dragon's *Hide ×8*, a dark paladin's *Plate Armour ×5* and *Shield ×3*. That replaces "usually the Challenge on the tag that matters most". See [Multipliers](#s-tough) and [Running the Game](#s-tough-gm).

**Threads are bond threads or hostile threads.** A bond thread's something good, and a hostile thread's something gone bad, like a debt or a grudge. Either can be strained, and with no thread you're neutral. A hostile thread bites like a Catch. You start with two bond threads with the crew, from your answers in Connect, and writing a hostile thread lets you write a third. That replaces three threads and a fourth for writing one as a debt, and debt's gone as a kind of thread. See [Bonds and threads](#s-bonds) and [Connect](#s-connect).

**One attuned thing to start.** It used to be two. The crew have one each now, and their other things are ordinary items, still on the same set actions. See [Attunement](#s-attunement).

**One action a faction, the crew included.** The crew takes one action a downtime, like any faction, instead of two party slots. A player can put a personal slot into it, and two slots on one move still count everything twice. Nothing buys a second action, so a spare can't buy the crew an extra party slot any more. See [Downtime](#party-slots) and [The Crew and the Factions](#s-faction-crew).
-   **A spare resource is an extra resource now:** a resource that isn't paying for an asset. See [Resources and Assets](#s-wealth).
-   **A new mini faction sits out the downtime it's made,** then acts on its own: at first it mostly does what its parent wants, then it goes its own way. See [Mini Factions](#s-mini-factions).
-   **Factions' threads are bond and hostile threads too.** A bond thread's a step, a hostile one's a vulnerability. See [Bonds](#s-faction-bonds).
-   **There's a list of assets to borrow from,** for a faction or the crew. The people who turn up when a faction calls add Power, and the rest do one thing when the story fits. See [Appendix B](#faction-strength).
-   ***Friends at Court* and *Influential* add a step** to the crew's rolls they fit. They used to give the crew a free move on a faction every downtime. See [Crew Assets](#s-crew-tags).

## 24 Oct 2026: Defend, wounds, vulnerabilities and factions {#c-2026-10-24}

These came out of the 23 Oct playtests.

**Defend's a set action, and the Armour die's gone.** Defend, Fight, Move, Sneak, Study and Talk are on every sheet now. Plain armour and a shield are lines on your Defend, a step each, and a piece of armour can break instead of you taking a Hurt. (From 27 Oct a piece has a quality, and it takes a blow a box at a time.) See [Armour](#s-armour) and [When It Lands](#s-defending).
-   **Each die of defence stops one telegraph.** When you let it ride, you roll your Defend once and your Confidence once, and each die stops one telegraph it beats or ties. Everything else gets through. That replaces one defence roll standing against everything aimed at you.
-   **Defending doubles it.** When you Defend, every line on your Defend counts twice, and so does your Confidence, and you can split each of them once. Defend doesn't pull an enemy's attack onto you any more. See [Defend](#s-defend). (Since 26 Oct, it doesn't double your Confidence.)
-   **A threat aims one die at each of you**, unless a tag like *Flurry* or *Two Knives* says it can do more. See [Telegraphs](#s-telegraphs).
-   *Poisoned*'s spend makes your Defend a d2 till the round ends, and *Warded* and *Covered* are a step on your Defend. They used to work on your Armour.
-   "Step your Armour down" is gone from the costs. See [Consequences](#s-consequences).
-   The Armour, Take the hit and Hurt Catches in downtime Boons are gone, and so are the Armour and Hurt Catches Burdens. The Defend Burden is "you can't Defend as your action" now, and the new Intercept Burden is "you can't intercept". A Boon you'd built in goes back for its two permissions, and an attuned thing whose Burden is gone takes another. See [Boons and Burdens](#s-boons).

**Intercept replaces taking the hit.** For 1 Aura and no action, you take the dice aimed at a friend within your stride, and your own defence handles them. A [Guardian](#a-guardian) intercepts for free, for every friend within his stride. See [Intercept](#s-intercept).

**A permission goes on two set actions at most**, Defend included, and the same permission never goes on one set action twice. Anywhere else it's 1 Aura to bring in, and Titles, items and traits still go wherever they fit. **Single-word permissions are back**, like *Deceptive*, because the cap keeps them in check, so a permission doesn't need a doing word and how, what or where any more. Magic's still written narrow. In a combo, the same permission from two of you counts for each. See [How permissions stack](#how-permissions-stack) and [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions).

**Vulnerabilities are new.** Every vulnerability that fits a hit adds a tick: one more Hurt on you, or one more win off a threat's tag. The same one never counts twice, and you've still got to win. (Since 26 Oct, you choose which tag each tick goes on.) *Prone* and *Exposed* are vulnerabilities now. See [Vulnerabilities](#s-vulnerable).

**Tough's gone, and tags carry multipliers instead.** *Armoured ×4* takes four wins to strip. It's usually the threat's Challenge on the tag that matters most to it, and less on the rest, and it only ever counts wins, never dice. (Since 26 Oct, it's as big as the creature needs.) Every roll's win or lose now, because nothing takes off the number it rolled. The bestiary and the frames have theirs. See [Multipliers](#s-tough) and [Running the Game](#s-tough-gm).

**Your wound die is your Hurt now**, and the Hurt track's bars and boxes are gone. It starts at d2, every Hurt puts it up a size, and a Hurt at d12 Blazes. See [Wounds, Blaze and Scars](#s-blaze).
-   At the end of every scene you rest: roll the Confidence you finished on against your wound die, and a win brings it down a size. Downtime clears it, with no slot. That replaces the loose boxes clearing, and downtime's Recover is only for mending what's broken now.
-   Heals and quick heals bring it down a size. See [Healing](#s-healing).
-   After a Blaze, the wound becomes a Scar, and a Scar's a Catch. Each one starts your wound die a size bigger, and with five, your next Blaze is your last. That replaces a Scar crossing out a box.
-   Nothing writes a Catch on your Hurt mid-scene any more, so your Confidence doesn't drop for it.
-   *Unyielding*, the Hurt Boon and [playing alone](#s-solo-gm) mean the first Hurt each scene doesn't move your wound die, instead of an extra bar.
-   *Tend the Crew* works on the rest now: everyone in the crew who was in the scene rolls two Confidence dice for it, and the best counts. It used to heal Catches in downtime.

**Every archetype has a Resolve spend**, something big for 1 Resolve. See [1 Resolve on your mechanic](#resolve-on-your-mechanic). And:
-   The [Bard](#a-bard)'s *Hold them* is a Defend against fear for everyone who can hear, and the song can split as often as you like. It replaces one roll that stopped all of it.
-   The [Oracle](#a-oracle) rolls its dice as foresight and swaps those numbers into anyone's dice, friend or foe. It replaces taking steps off telegraphs.
-   The [Hunter](#a-hunter)'s Mark jumps to another creature when its target falls.
-   The [Guardian](#a-guardian) intercepts for free, instead of pulling every attack onto himself when he Defends.

**Factions are rebuilt.** See [Factions](#factions).
-   Four to twelve core factions, depending on the campaign, and each can make up to three [mini factions](#s-mini-factions).
-   A faction's Power is 1 step, plus 1 for each asset that adds Power. Its resources and assets are permissions: each asset needs a resource paying for it every downtime, and a spare resource hires another faction's action. Wealth and faction tags are gone.
-   A faction's action is one of [six outcomes](#s-faction-outcomes) (Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire and Establish) or work on its agenda, and it's still telegraphed at the start of downtime. The crew's party slots get the same six, plus setting back an agenda and working for a faction.
-   Every faction has [three bonds](#s-faction-bonds), Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty, with threads in them. A good thread's a step and a bad one's a vulnerability. (Since 26 Oct they're called bond threads and hostile threads.)
-   Standing's gone. How a faction sees the crew is its threads toward them.
-   The crew's on the same card, its crew tags are [crew assets](#s-crew-tags) now, and party resources are [party holdings](#s-party-resources). Crew assets don't add a step to rolls they fit any more: each one does the one thing it says, and the ones that are people who turn up when the crew calls, like *Muscle*, add Power. *Healers* steps everyone's wound die down a size between two scenes, once a session. A spare resource can still buy the crew an extra party slot, the way Wealth could. (Since 26 Oct it can't, the crew takes one action a downtime, and a spare's called an extra resource.)

**Bonds can be with factions, places and causes**, as well as people. Borrowing through a thread with a faction brings in one of its assets, for 1 Aura, and a thread with a place or a cause strains for Resolve like any other. See [Bonds and threads](#s-bonds).

## 17 Oct 2026: A two-page character sheet, and the player reference on its own {#c-2026-10-17}

**The character sheet is one A4 sheet now**, printed back to back. The front's for play and the back's who you are. It was four pages, with the player reference on the backs. The long reminders, like what Aura and Resolve buy, moved to the reference. You can still type into it on screen, or print it blank and use a pencil. See [The character sheet](#s-sheet-character).

**The player reference is a PDF of its own**: the [quick reference](#appendix-quick) in four parts, on four pages. Both are on the [Sheets page](/sheets/).

## 16 Oct 2026: Resolve, Confidence that swings, threads and the Bard {#c-2026-10-16}

These all came out of the playtests.

**Resolve replaces Sparks.** Resolve is your character pushing themselves, and it pays for the big stuff. You have as much as you have Titles, so 2 to start. See [Resolve](#s-resolve).
-   Spend 1 to take an extra action this round, reroll a roll you just failed (all your dice or just one), go beyond what a permission normally does, hold your ground when fear gets through, or go at a threat when you're shaken.
-   Get 1 back at the end of every scene and all of it after downtime, and 1 each when your Catch bites in a big way, when the GM rewards great roleplay, and when you strain a thread.
-   Sparks are gone, and so are making an opportunity and adding a permission for the scene.

**GM trouble is gone.** The GM doesn't offer you trouble for a Spark any more, and nobody turns trouble down. Letting your Catches bite is up to you. See [Catches and Trouble](#s-trouble) and, for the GM, [Catches and Resolve at the Table](#s-catches-gm).

**Aura keeps the small stuff:** bringing something in, keeping a Catch quiet or turning it your way, winning a tie when you're acting, what 1 Aura gets your archetype, a quick heal, and borrowing a permission through a thread. **Going beyond costs 1 Resolve now**, not 1 Aura. See [Aura](#s-aura) and [Going Beyond](#s-going-beyond).

**Confidence swings up and down in a scene.** See [Confidence](#s-confidence).
-   Every scene starts on d6, a size up in clothes that fit. Titles and bonds don't make it bigger any more, and it doesn't come back in downtime, because it goes back to d6 at the end of every scene.
-   Fear is a telegraph, and that's your fear check. Stop all of it and you go up a size. Take a threat down and you go up a size too.
-   When fear, lies or pressure get through, you choose: let it shake you and drop a size, bury it and take a Hurt, or hold your ground for 1 Resolve. The GM doesn't name that cost any more. See [When fear gets through](#s-fear-gets-through).
-   Write a Catch on your Hurt track and you drop a size as well. (Gone from 24 Oct, when the wound die replaced the Hurt track.)
-   At d12 you're fired up: cash it in for an extra action or to clear a Hurt box, and you're back on d6. (From 24 Oct it steps your wound die down a size instead of clearing a box.) At d2 you're shaken, and going at a threat costs 1 Resolve each time. Take a breath (your action) to come up a size, as far as d6.
-   Confidence never goes past d12 any more. Past d12 is for Armour only. (The Armour die went on 24 Oct.)

**The heal die is now the wound die.** Same rule, new name. See [Healing](#s-healing).

**Connections are now threads.** See [Bonds and threads](#s-bonds).
-   Borrow a permission through a thread for 1 Aura, the same as before.
-   Strain a thread, by leaning on them in the story, and take 1 Resolve. Mend it by making it right with them. Strain it again before it's mended and it snaps.
-   A thread with someone in the crew goes on both your sheets.
-   A bond with three threads doesn't make your Confidence bigger any more.
-   There's a guide to [playing the strain](#s-playing-the-strain) when someone strains a thread with your character.

**A new archetype, the [Bard](#a-bard),** for nerve: lift the crew's Confidence, or answer all the fear coming at them with one song. (From 24 Oct the song's a Defend for everyone who can hear, and each die stops one telegraph.)

**Boons and Burdens, three rows:** the Spark row is now Resolve (+1 or −1 to your maximum), Confidence starts you a size up or down every scene, and the Going beyond Boon lets you go beyond once a scene without spending Resolve. See [Boons and Burdens](#s-boons).

## 14 Oct 2026: Archetypes {#c-2026-10-14}

**Every Title is an archetype now.** An archetype gives you permissions the way a Title did, and it has a mechanic of its own, something only it can do. Your Title is the name you give it. You start with two, and you can take the same one twice. The mechanic takes the place of the trait each Title used to bring. There's a new chapter, [The Archetypes](#s-how-archetypes).
-   The sixteen: Bloodsworn, Elementalist, Face, Guardian, Healer, Hunter, Illusionist, Leader, Maker, Oracle, Scholar, Shadow, Shifter, Summoner, Teleporter and Warrior. (The Bard came on 16 Oct.)
-   On most of them, 1 Aura adds +2 to one die on the mechanic, after you roll. Take the archetype twice and it's +3.
-   Every Title idea now says which archetypes it suits. See [Title Ideas](#s-title-ideas).
-   Keep the traits you've got. Where traits go now is still being worked out.

**Some things everyone used to do now belong to one archetype:**
-   **Leading** is the [Leader](#a-leader)'s. Anyone can still put their action into a friend's roll.
-   **Areas** are the [Elementalist](#a-elementalist)'s, and they don't cost Aura any more. Sweep weapons still go at everyone in reach, free.
-   **Heal rolls** are the [Healer](#a-healer)'s. A quick heal is still for everyone.
-   **Catching souls and summoning** are the [Summoner](#a-summoner)'s. The summoning Boon's gone: take Summoner twice.
-   **Pulling more than one enemy's attack** when you Defend is the [Guardian](#a-guardian)'s. The Boon that pulled from two is gone. Anyone can still Defend and pull one. (On 24 Oct pulling became intercepting: 1 Aura for anyone, free for a Guardian.)

**Changes for everyone:**
-   Wards and blessings reach one person, and the second die of a split can reach another friend you can touch. Healing a group is gone. See [Wards and blessings](#s-wards).
-   Study finds a hidden tag or a straight answer. *Something that helps* is gone, and a [Scholar](#a-scholar) can introduce a truth instead. See [Studying Something](#s-study).
-   Coming out of hiding gives you an extra action, whoever you are. See [Extra actions](#s-extra-actions).

## 13 Oct 2026: Items, attunement, taking the hit, and the name Zi'Dara {#c-2026-10-13}

**The game is called Zi'Dara.** This rulebook became the one master copy of the rules, and the website, the printed book and the character tools are all built from it.

**Items.** See [Items](#s-items) and [What you carry](#s-carrying).
-   Plain items are just what they are. A sword counts as a line when it fits. Each piece of plain armour makes your Armour a size bigger. (From 24 Oct it's a line on your Defend.)
-   Resources are items now. You carry 3 equipment, 3 utility items and a keepsake, and your bag holds 10 more.
-   Rarity (common, rare, legendary) is gone.

**Attunement.** You've got two attunement slots on top of what you carry. An attuned thing has permissions of its own and grows in three stages, Awakened, Tempered and Perfected, each needing a material. It can't be broken, and you can hand it to a friend. See [Attunement](#s-attunement).

**Titles don't have Catches any more.** Catches come from attunement and from Hurt. (From 24 Oct, from attunement and Scars.) You can still bring real trouble on yourself with no Catch written down, for 1 Aura. Banes only come on attunement now, as a Burden.

**Taking the hit.** Anyone can step in front of an attack aimed at someone within their stride, free, with no defence roll. It has its own Boon and Burden, and it replaced the *Step in* Boon. (Intercept replaced it on 24 Oct: see [Intercept](#s-intercept).)

**Summoning.** Souls of fallen creatures are caught into a summoning attunement, and you summon against their dice. This replaced the 12 Oct summoning. (On 14 Oct it became the Summoner's.) See [Summoning](#s-summoning).

**Permissions need two parts:** the doing word, and how, what or where. *Deceive* is half a permission, and *deceive through actions* is a whole one. (Single-word permissions came back on 24 Oct, with each permission on two set actions at most.) See [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions).

**Splitting past a d12** was made clearer: dice past six steps are their own, and going past 6 steps gives you a split on top of your action's. See [Splitting](#s-splitting).

## 12 Oct 2026: Defend, Hurt bars, healing, Boons and Banes {#c-2026-10-12}

**Defend replaces Guard**, and you roll your defence once a round. Anything being attacked rolls its defence once, and that roll stands against everything that round. Defend takes your action: roll your Armour once for every die coming at you and choose which roll blocks which, and pull one enemy's whole attack onto yourself. (On 24 Oct each defence die started stopping one telegraph, Defending started doubling your Defend, and pulling became Intercept.) See [When It Lands](#s-defending) and [Defend](#s-defend).

**Hurt is three bars of three.** Fill a bar and write a Catch in it. At the end of every scene, the loose boxes clear. From your first Blaze you can choose for your character to die, and otherwise you take a Scar, which is for the story only. (On 24 Oct the wound die replaced the bars, and a Scar became a Catch.) See [Wounds, Blaze and Scars](#s-blaze).

**Healing happens in a scene.** A heal rolls against the patient's heal die, and a quick heal is 1 Aura for one box. Catches on your Hurt track take a downtime slot each. (On 14 Oct heal rolls became the Healer's and healing a group went. On 16 Oct the heal die became the wound die. On 24 Oct heals started stepping the wound die down, and downtime started clearing it for free.) See [Healing](#s-healing).

**Catches bite as often as you like**, 1 Aura each time, as long as it's real trouble. You got a Spark for each Title, all back every downtime. (Sparks became Resolve on 16 Oct.) The GM can give you 1 Aura when a great setup doesn't land.

**Permissions:** how to write them (a doing word and what it's done to, and the size test), and swapping them in downtime. See [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions).

**Boons and Banes:** permanent upgrades that cost two permission slots, and downsides that pay for them, from one menu. (On 13 Oct Banes moved to attunement as Burdens.) See [Boons and Burdens](#s-boons).

## 9 and 10 Oct 2026: Titles, set actions and one action a round {#c-2026-10-09}

-   **Titles hold your permissions.** A new character has two Titles with three permissions each, up to six on a Title. The Title itself counts 1 on any roll it fits. See [Archetypes and Titles](#s-title).
-   **Set actions:** Fight, Move, Sneak, Study and Talk, and your own, with everything that fits counted in advance. (Defend joined them on 24 Oct.) See [Set Actions](#s-set-actions).
-   **One action a round.** There's no Help action: to help, put your action into the same thing as a combo. See [Your Action](#s-actions) and [Working Together](#s-teamwork).
-   **Bring something in** for 1 Aura: anything that isn't on your set action. That replaced stretching a permission.
-   **Going beyond** what a permission normally does cost 1 Aura, for a sword or a spell. (1 Resolve from 16 Oct.) It replaced big magic.
-   **Telegraphs** come to about 5 steps a round for each player. The threat budget starts at 10 a character with two Titles. See [Telegraphs a Round](#s-budget) and [The Threat Budget](#s-threat-budget).
-   **Title ideas,** fourteen roles and fifteen schools of magic, replaced the old archetypes and quick-build kits. See [Title Ideas](#s-title-ideas).

## 7 and 8 Oct 2026: Permissions replace abilities {#c-2026-10-07}

-   Abilities, tiers and specializations are gone. Everything your character can do is a permission, written on a Title. See [Permissions](#s-permissions).
-   A school of magic is a Title like any other, and magic is written as narrow as anything else (*throw fire*). See [Schools of Magic](#s-schools).
-   Area dice started costing Aura. (On 14 Oct areas became the Elementalist's, free.)

## 2 Oct 2026: The book {#c-2026-10-02}

The 2 Oct 2026 rulebook is where this record starts. Everything in it that isn't changed above still stands.

## Still open {#c-still-open}

These are being worked out at the table. Play them as the chapters have them for now.

-   **The Summoner:** it's being reworked, because it played weak. Play it as the chapter has it for now.
-   **Traits:** where they go, now that each archetype's mechanic has taken the place of a Title's trait. Keep yours, and a new character picks two.
-   **The Teleporter** needs a better name.
-   **Taking the Teleporter or the Bloodsworn twice:** neither has a +2 to grow, so what you get is still to come.
-   **The Shifter:** where you find your animal tags in the first place.
-   **Permissions like teleport, take an animal's form or cast illusions:** whether they still work without the archetype.
-   **Attunement:** how many slots you can have past two, and whether six permissions is the top.
-   **Keepsakes:** what a keepsake does.
-   **Movement:** whether it comes from permissions.
-   **Mending a thread in downtime:** whether it takes a slot.
