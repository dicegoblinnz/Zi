Guardian, Healer and Shadow are archetypes, and there are fourteen more. An **archetype** is the kind of character you are at the table, and it's its own pick, apart from your Titles. Each one's got a **mechanic**, something only that archetype can do, a **Trait** that's always true of you, and **abilities** you pay for in [Aura](#s-aura). Ash is an Elementalist, so one of her dice can be a burst of fire that catches every guard in the doorway, and she's the only one in the crew who can do that.

Start with how they work, then find yours in [Picking Your Archetypes](#s-picking-archetypes). After that, every archetype has a section of its own, in alphabetical order: what it does, its Trait, its abilities, and an example.

## How Archetypes Work {#s-how-archetypes}

-   **An archetype's 5 points** when you make your character, and it comes with its mechanic, its Trait and one ability. You can take as many as you can pay for, and they don't have to be different: Vesk's a Shadow twice over. They're separate from your Titles: Bram's a Guardian and a Leader, and his Titles are *The wall that held the harbour gate* and *A sergeant who lost his stripes*.
-   **Your mechanic** is the thing only your archetype can do, and it's free. When it has you roll, you roll the usual way, one Root at full and any you bring in (see [Steps and Dice](#s-building-die)). Bram's orders come off his Soul, the same as anything else he says.
-   **Your Trait** is something that's always true of you because of your archetype, and every archetype's got one, in its own section. Most cost nothing and never switch off. A few cost Aura or come once a scene, and those say so. Take an archetype twice and it's still the one Trait. Bram's a Guardian, so every blow he intercepts for a friend gives him 1 Aura, whether it gets through or not.
-   **Your abilities** are in your archetype's section, about ten to each. Your archetype comes with one, your pick off its list, Push included. More are a point each when you make your character, or a downtime slot later on, and an archetype holds six at most. Each one costs Aura when you use it, and how that works is just below.
-   **One action a round**, the same as everyone. Your archetypes don't give you more of them. More dice is how you do more (see [Splitting](#s-splitting)).
-   **A new archetype** can come when your [Aspiration](#s-aspiration) comes good, instead of a Title or an attuned thing, with the ability it comes with.

### Abilities {#s-abilities}

:::rule
**Each ability says its Aura**, and you pay it when you use it. Most are 1 Aura, and a few bigger ones are 2. **They stack.** Pay again and you get it again: another +2, another target, another round, whatever the ability says you get more of.

**You can't pay Aura you haven't got.** While you're acting, what's past your maximum is yours to spend like the rest. A [vessel](#s-items)'s charge can pay for your own abilities instead, a charge for each Aura.
:::

An ability's never steps on a roll. It does what it says, and that's all. It can work with what you've got, though: an item's tier, your Resources, a vessel's charge or your armour's soak. With the Warrior's *Parry*, your weapon's tier goes on your roll when blows land, which a weapon never does on its own, and with the Shadow's *I had it planned*, you say a Resource without ticking one. The Maker's *Tune it up* puts an item a tier higher for the scene, and *Made it earlier* hands you one at tier 2, and it's the item's tier that goes on the roll, never the ability. Wren's roll to mark a creature is her Body as usual, and paying for Mark the pack sends that roll at two more.

**Push** is the one most archetypes have on their list: +2 to one die on your mechanic, once you've rolled and you know you need it. A die the +2 takes to its top face, or past it, doesn't pay Aura. **Take the same archetype twice** and its Push is +3, three times it's +4, and so on. You only need Push the once: Vesk's first Shadow came with Push and her second with Vanish, and her Push is +3.

:::example[Bram stays on the wall]
The brute's club comes at Bram, a d10 to knock him off the wall. It's at his Body, so when it lands he rolls his Body at full, 9 steps, and his Trusty shield's a guard, so its tier's 2 more. That's 11, and he keeps it as a d12 and a d10. The club comes up 9, and Bram rolls 7 and 3. Nothing's stopped it, and he isn't going off that wall, so he pays 1 Aura for his Guardian's Push and makes the 7 a 9. That's a tie, and ties go to him, so he's still standing on the wall, with all his points. If he'd taken Guardian twice, it'd be a 10.
:::

### What only one archetype does {#only-one-archetype}

A few things in the game belong to one archetype, and if you're not that archetype, you can't do them:

-   **Leading** is the [Leader](#a-leader)'s: handing your steps out to friends. Anyone can put their action into a friend's roll as a [combo](#s-teamwork).
-   **Areas** are the [Elementalist](#a-elementalist)'s. A *Sweep* weapon goes at everyone in your reach whoever's swinging it, and threats have areas of their own (see [Area](#s-area)).
-   **Heal rolls** are the [Healer](#a-healer)'s. Anyone can [quick heal](#s-healing) for 1 Aura.
-   **Catching souls and summoning** are the [Summoner](#a-summoner)'s.
-   **Intercepting for free** is the [Guardian](#a-guardian)'s. Anyone can [intercept](#s-intercept) for 1 Aura.
-   **Flipping dice** is the [Gambler](#a-gambler)'s.

## Bard {#a-bard}

The Bard's for nerve. You're the one keeping the crew's heads straight, and Defending everyone who can hear you against fear, lies and pressure.

**Lift them.** Spend your action, and every friend who can hear you gets a point back on their Mind, as far as it can hold. It isn't a heal, so it adds no Toll.

**Hold them.** When fear, lies or pressure come at you or friends who can hear you, sing, as your action. It's a [Defend](#s-defend) against fear, lies and pressure for all of you: your song's a Root at full, usually your Soul, split however you like. When the telegraphs land, each of your dice stops one it meets or beats, aimed at any of you, since ties go to you. Anything it doesn't stop still has to get past that person's own Mind when it lands, and that goes for yours too.

:::rule
**Trait:** *All Eyes on Me*. While you're singing, playing or telling a story, pay 1 Aura for each threat you want watching you, and a whole crowd can be one threat. Till you stop, it notices nothing else short of a scream or a blade, so the crew can get on with it behind your back.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your song dice.

**Crescendo (1 Aura):** pay as you Lift them, and everyone who hears you gets 2 points back, not 1. It's once a Lift, so paying again doesn't make it 3.

**Hymn (1 Aura):** pay as you sing, and the song's for the Soul. A Lift puts its points back on everyone's Soul instead of their Mind, and a Hold them stops despair, temptation and curses at your Souls instead of fear, lies and pressure at your Minds. It's one or the other for the whole song, never both, and it's still not a heal.

**Shake it off (1 Aura):** any time, with no action and no roll, one person who can hear you, you included, drops *Frightened*, *Charmed*, *Taunted* or *Shaken*, as long as the GM hasn't spent it yet. Pay again for another person or another condition.

**Old songs (1 Aura):** with no action and no roll, say there's a song about the place, the person or the thing in front of you, and ask the GM one question about it. She answers it truthfully, the way the song tells it. Pay again for another question.

**Odd one out (1 Aura):** while you're singing, playing or telling a story to a room, ask the GM who isn't watching you and what they're doing instead. It's no action, and she tells you straight, or tells you everyone's watching.

**Marching song (1 Aura):** sing as you go, with no action. This round everyone who can hear you can move as far as whoever of you moves furthest, as long as they could go the same way, so nobody keeps up with a friend who flies. Pay again for another round.

**Answer back (1 Aura):** when one of your song dice stops something at anyone's Mind, or at a Soul under a Hymn, that die goes back at whoever sent it, with its number, against that threat's roll for the round (roll it now if it hasn't got one). Its intent's anything a word can do, like stripping a tag of that kind, shaming it or leaving it *Shaken*. Pay again for another die that stopped something.

**Sing them through it (1 Aura):** with no action, one friend who can hear you can bring their Mind into their roll this round with your song as the reason, and no permission needed. They still pay for it the usual way, a point of Mind or 3 Aura. Pay again for another friend.

**A song about them (1 Aura):** sing about a faction where a crowd can hear you, as your action, and roll it against the Challenge of that faction's face. A win's an Influence or an Undermine on that faction, your pick, the way the crew's would be (see [The Six Outcomes](#s-faction-outcomes)), and it doesn't cost the crew its action. Only your first song between one faction turn and the next counts.
:::

:::example[A dragon roars]
A dragon roars at four of you, a d10 at each one's Mind: you, Bram, Wren and Isolde. Say you're the Bard, *The best voice on the coast*, with a Soul of 8. You sing, and split your 8 steps four ways, so you've got a d4 for each roar.

The roar comes up 1 at you, 2 at Wren, 4 at Isolde and 5 at Bram. Your d4s come up 3, 3, 2 and 1. The 1 stops the 1 at you, because the tie's yours, and the 2 stops Wren's 2. You pay 1 Aura to push a 3 to a 5, and that stops Isolde's 4. Your last 3 can't stop Bram's 5, so the roar has to get past his own Mind. That's 3, a d6, and it comes up 5. The tie's his, so it's stopped too. Nobody loses a point, and two of your dice won without a push, so that's 2 Aura for you.
:::

:::example[Up the stair at Lord Vane's party]
You're at Lord Vane's party, and Vesk needs to get up the stair past the guests and the guard at the foot of it. You start on a ballad and pay 2 Aura, 1 for the guests, who are one crowd, and 1 for the guard. Vesk walks up the stair behind them, and nobody watching you sees her go. If she knocks a vase off the landing, though, they'll all turn round.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Bard](#k-bard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Bloodsworn {#a-bloodsworn}

The Bloodsworn gets more dangerous every time they bleed.

**Every point your Body loses adds 3 steps to an action.** If you lose it as you act, because you spent it, the steps go on that roll. If a hit takes it, they go on your next action. Two hits from one die, because it found a [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), add 6.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Paid in Blood*. Every point your Body loses gives you 1 Aura, whether a hit took it or you spent it yourself.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one die on a roll your blood's put steps on.

**Bleed for it (1 Aura):** before you roll, spend a point of your Body for nothing but the blood, and your mechanic puts its 3 steps on this roll. Pay again to spend another point for 3 more.

**Scab over (1 Aura):** pay any time in the round, and your Body gets a point back at the end of it, as far as half what it holds, rounded up. It isn't a heal, so it adds no Toll. Pay again for another.

**Red mist (1 Aura):** pay before the telegraphs land. This round, fear, lies and pressure at your Mind come at your Body instead, and your Body rolls against them and the blows in one roll. What gets through is a hit on your Body, never the condition it carried, so it feeds your mechanic and *Paid in Blood*.

**Not yet (1 Aura):** when your blood's put steps on your next action, hold them instead, till you let go of them this scene. Anything more your blood gives you while you wait joins them, and when you let go it all goes on one roll. You can act in between, and whatever's still held at the end of the scene is gone.

**Make them bleed (1 Aura):** when one of your dice wins on a roll your blood's put steps on, whatever it beat is *Bleeding* at that die's size, on top of whatever the die was for. Pay again for another die.

**Mine to take (1 Aura):** when a hit gets through to the Body of a friend within your stride, take it yourself, after it's landed. It's a point off your Body instead of theirs, and your armour doesn't soak it, so it's 3 steps on your next action and 1 Aura. Pay again for another hit.

**Now I know you (1 Aura):** when a hit takes a point off your Body, the GM tells you one of the hidden tags of whatever hit you, or that it hasn't got any.

**Blood oath (1 Aura):** cut your palm as you swear something, and spend a point of your Body. Everyone who sees it knows you mean it, and nobody can take it for a bluff or a lie, though they can still say no. The point's 3 steps go on your next action, and it pays *Paid in Blood*.

**Scent of blood (1 Aura):** till the scene ends you can smell blood. You know where anything that's bleeding is, a long way off, you can follow a blood trail at a run, and you know if blood's been spilled where you're standing.
:::

:::example[Two hits]
Two blows get through to you this round, and your Body goes from 9 to 7, so your next action's got 6 more steps, and *Paid in Blood* gives you 2 Aura. You weren't acting when they landed, so that Aura stops at your maximum, and anything over it can go to a friend.

Next round you swing at the one who did it. Your Body's still 9 steps, whatever it's lost, so it's 15 with the blood: two d12s and a d6. Pay 1 Aura for Bleed for it as you swing and spend a point, and that's 3 more, 18 in all, three d12s. Losing the point gives you the Aura straight back, and your Body's down to 6.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Warrior](#k-warrior) · [Hexer](#k-warlock)

## Elementalist {#a-elementalist}

The Elementalist hits everyone in an area at once, like fire across a doorway or lightning down a corridor.

Areas are yours. One of your dice can be your **area** instead of an intent. Say its shape, roll it with the rest, and the number's how far it goes:

-   **Burst:** that many squares out from a point.
-   **Cone:** that long the way you face, and as wide at the end.
-   **Line:** twice as far, straight out.

It doesn't cost Aura. Everyone the story puts in it defends against your other die, and you can always make it smaller, which you'll want when your friends are in it. Without a grid, 1 or 2 is everyone close to the target, 3 to 5 is everyone within a few steps, and 6 or more is the whole room. There's more in [Area](#s-area).

:::rule
**Trait:** *Eye of the Storm*. Your own areas never catch you, and your element can't hurt you once it's loose in the scene, like the fire your burst left in the curtains. It still can when someone aims it at you.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to your area die.

**Sculpt (1 Aura):** your area skips one person you choose, and they stay skipped if it Lingers. Pay again for each one more.

**Raise it (1 Aura):** your area raises ground instead of catching anyone, like a wall of ice, a ditch, a bank of fog or a bridge of stone, the size the die rolled, and it stays till the scene ends. It needs no other die with it. Anything that wants through it or over it has to beat it as a Challenge the size of your area die, so a d8 area makes d8 ground.

**Ride the line (1 Aura):** you go with your line, and end up anywhere along it as part of your stride: over water, up a wall or across a gap.

**Linger (1 Aura):** your area stays where it is till the end of next round, and anyone who comes into it before then defends against the number your other die rolled. Pay again for another round.

**Blow them back (1 Aura):** after you've rolled, everyone in your area that your other die beat is thrown to its edge as well as whatever that die was for, all of them the same way, your call. The ground does the rest, like off the wall or into the canal.

**Ask it (1 Aura):** with no roll, where your element's in the scene, like a hearth, the wind, the river or the stone underfoot, ask the GM one thing it's touched or carried: who lit this, what the wind brought, who crossed here. She answers straight. Pay again for another question.

**Change the weather (1 Aura):** with no roll and no action, your element fills the place you're in, like a fog off the water, a gale or a hard frost. It's a tag on the scene till the scene ends, for the crew and the GM both to lean on like any ground, and it never does a hit on its own. *Eye of the Storm* means it never gets in your way.

**Two shapes (1 Aura):** one more of your dice can be an area, with its own shape and its own die going at everyone in it, and Sculpt's paid for each area. Pay again for another.

**Shield of the storm (2 Aura):** when you [Defend](#s-defend), one of your dice can be your area, and another stops every blow at the Body of anyone in it that it meets or beats, not just one, and pays Aura once. It only stops blows your element could, and it's one number, so anything that rolls over it gets through to everyone.
:::

:::example[Five guards]
Ash is in the middle of five guards, and she throws fire: her Soul, 11 steps, her ember stone's tier, 2 more, and its *Fire without a word*, 2 more again, 15. She shapes them into two d12s and a d6, and the d6's her burst, going off around her. It rolls 4, but the guards are five squares out, so she pays 1 Aura and it's a 6. That's the d6's top face, but the +2 got it there, so there's no Aura for it. All five defend against her first d12, her second goes at the door behind them, and the burst never touches her.

The curtains catch, and next round the GM telegraphs the fire spreading to the doorway, a d8 at whoever's in it. Ash walks out through the flames without a mark on her.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Fire](#k-sorcerer) · [Storm](#k-storm) · [Kinesis](#k-kinesis)

## Face {#a-face}

The Face gets into a threat's head, so its own attacks go where you say.

Spend your action and build your dice the usual way, but don't roll them. Put them on a threat as **influence**, and they stay until you use them or the scene ends. At any point before one of its telegraphs lands, roll your influence against it. Each of its dice that your best influence die beats is yours: say what it does instead, as long as it fits the story, like hitting one of its own or the ground. Then the influence is spent.

:::rule
**Trait:** *I Know a Guy*. Once a scene, say you know someone who could turn up here, and one true thing about them. They come into the scene, the GM plays them, and they help you however they can.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your influence dice.

**Turn them (1 Aura):** when your influence takes one of a Mook's or a Named threat's dice, that threat's on the crew's side till the round ends: every die it's telegraphed this round is yours to aim, the ones you didn't beat too. Never a Boss. Pay again for another threat your influence took a die from.

**In passing (1 Aura):** when you act, some of your dice can go on a threat as influence, unrolled, while the rest roll as usual with their own intents. It's still your one action, and dice on a threat that's already got your influence join it.

**Know their price (1 Aura):** when your influence takes one of a threat's dice, the GM tells you what drives it as well, one of its hidden tags.

**Shake them (1 Aura):** when your influence takes one of a threat's dice, your best influence die goes at the threat as well, for any intent you like, against its roll for the round.

**Keep them sweet (1 Aura):** once you've rolled your influence, it stays on the threat instead of being spent, and you can roll it again before one of its later telegraphs this scene. Pay again each time you want it to stay.

**Grease the palm (1 Aura):** tick a Resource that'd tempt them, like a purse, a bottle or a favour owed, and one of a threat's dice this round is yours as if your influence had taken it, with no roll. It has to be someone who can be bought. Turn them and Know their price work off it, but Shake them doesn't, since there's no influence die to send.

**And I know his sister (1 Aura):** use *I Know a Guy* again this scene, for someone else. Pay again for another.

**Word gets round (1 Aura):** when someone believes something you've told them, it spreads. By the next scene everyone they'd talk to has heard it, and believes it as much as they do.

**Friends in high places (2 Aura):** put your influence on a faction's face in a scene, and it stays on that faction till the next [faction turn](#s-faction-turn). When that faction's action is telegraphed, roll your influence against it. Each of its dice your best beats is yours, and you say what it does instead, as long as the story allows.
:::

:::example[The bandit captain]
Dorrie's put her influence on the bandit captain: her Mind, 7 steps, which she's made a d8 and a d6. He telegraphs a d6 at Bram's Body and another at Wren's, and she rolls her influence against them: her best is a 5. They come up 3 at Bram and 6 at Wren. The 3's hers, so that cut lands on his own sergeant. She wants the other one too, so she pays 1 Aura to push her 5 to a 7, and the cut at Wren goes into the wall.
:::

:::example[Dorrie knows a guy]
The crew's papers are wrong and the harbour gate's shut for the night. Dorrie says she knows the clerk on the night shift, Tam, and that he owes her for keeping his brother out of the stocks. Tam comes down from the office with his lamp. He can't open the gate himself, but he knows which guard plays cards with the sergeant, and he'll vouch for her.
:::

## Gambler {#a-gambler}

The Gambler turns a die over after it lands, whoever's it is.

**Once a round, flip a die.** When any die you can see lands, yours, a friend's or a threat's, you can flip it: its new number is its size plus 1, take away what it rolled. A 2 on a d10 becomes a 9, and a 9 becomes a 2. It costs no action. A flipped die never pays Aura, and it doesn't explode.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Bad Luck Pays*. Every die of yours that comes up 1 gives you 1 Aura, as long as it stays a 1.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to a die of yours or a friend's that you've just flipped.

**Another flip (1 Aura):** flip one more die this round. Pay again for another.

**Roll it again (1 Aura):** any one die you can see rolls again, a threat's or a friend's as well as yours, and the new number stands. If it's yours or a friend's, it pays Aura like any die.

**Double or nothing (1 Aura):** flip every die in one roll at once, the good with the bad: everything one person rolled together, or everything one threat's rolled this round. It's on top of your flip for the round.

**Luck of the draw (1 Aura):** flip something in the story that comes down to luck, like a coin, a card, a drawn lot or which door the guard tries first, and it goes the other way.

**Spot the tell (1 Aura):** with no roll, ask the GM if someone's bluffing about what they've got, what they'll do or how sure they are. She tells you straight. Pay again for another person.

**Fold (1 Aura):** once your dice have landed, take back any that answered a telegraph and lost. They don't count, and those telegraphs don't land on you then and there. They land at the end of the round with the rest, and your defence gets its go at them.

**Press your luck (1 Aura):** when a die of yours wins, or a friend's if they let you, roll it again against the number it just beat. Win again and it's a second win at the same target, with any intent you like, but lose and the first win's gone too. A tie's a loss unless you pay for it as usual, the second roll pays no Aura, and you can still flip it. Pay again to press a third time.

**Their bad luck (1 Aura):** when a threat's die comes up 1, you say what it does instead, as long as the story allows, like hitting one of its own or the ground. A die you flipped to a 1 counts.

**Side bet (1 Aura):** before the scene ends, name a faction in it. At the next [faction turn](#s-faction-turn) you can flip one die that faction rolls, or one rolled against it.
:::

:::example[The guard's lucky night]
Bram's Defending against the guard captain's d10, and it comes up 9. You're the Gambler, so you flip it: 11 take away 9 is a 2, and Bram stops it with anything. Later in the round Vesk's d4 for keeping quiet comes up 1, so you pay 1 Aura and flip that too, and it's a 4.

Then your own d6 comes up 1 when you go at the guard. Turning it into a 6 would cost another Aura, so you leave it, and *Bad Luck Pays* gives you 1 Aura instead.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Guardian {#a-guardian}

The Guardian stands in front of the crew and takes what's coming at them.

You [intercept](#s-intercept) **for free**, for every friend within your stride. Any time before the telegraphs land, take any or all of the blows aimed at them, whichever enemy they're from, and they come at your Body instead. Fear and despair are inside their heads, so nobody can step in front of those, and keeping fear off the crew is a [Bard](#a-bard)'s job. When they land, your Body rolls against them the usual way, and since one die stops one, if you've pulled a lot onto yourself, you'll want to [Defend](#s-defend) as well. To get past you, threats have to pull the crew apart.

If you're a Teleporter as well, you intercept for every friend within 10 units (see [Teleporter](#a-teleporter)).

:::rule
**Trait:** *Better Me Than You*. Every blow you intercept for a friend gives you 1 Aura, whether it gets through to you or not.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your defence dice, the ones you roll when blows land or on a Defend.

**Stand firm (1 Aura):** once the dice are down and you can see what's got through, two hits that got through to you this round take 1 point between them, not 2. Pay again for two more.

**Reach them (1 Aura):** intercept for a friend out past your stride, as long as you can see them and could get to them this round. You're beside them when the blows land. Pay again for another friend.

**Carry them (1 Aura):** a friend you can reach comes with you when you move this round, carried or dragged, as far as your stride and any dice you put on moving. It costs them nothing. Pay again for another friend.

**My charge (1 Aura):** name someone who isn't in the crew, like a witness, a prisoner or a child. Till the scene ends, they're a friend for your intercepts and everything else you do as a Guardian. Pay again for another.

**In the way (1 Aura):** intercept a telegraph aimed at a stake, a place or a clock, if you can get in front of it, like the runner going for the bell or the ram at the gate. It comes at your Body as a blow. Stop it and it doesn't happen, and if it gets through you take its hits, but it still doesn't do what it said. Pay again for another.

**Knock it back (1 Aura):** when one of your dice stops a blow you intercepted, whoever threw it is *Staggered* at that die's size. Pay again for another die.

**Your turn (1 Aura):** when one of your dice stops a blow you intercepted, the friend it was meant for gets a die that size, to roll fresh with their next roll as one more die with an intent of its own. It's gone once it's rolled or the scene ends, and they hold one of these from you at a time. Pay again for another die.

**Put it on me (1 Aura):** when a hit gets through to the Body of a friend within your stride, or one of them misses and the GM names what it costs them, it's yours instead. A hit comes off you the usual way, soak first, and a cost has to be one you could take standing there, like the guard grabbing you instead of them. It isn't an intercept, so *Better Me Than You* doesn't pay. Pay again for another hit or cost.

**Keep watch (1 Aura):** in the last scene before downtime, say what you're guarding: one of the crew, or an asset or resource on the party sheet. If a faction's action goes at it in the next faction turn, you Obstruct it with your Body at full (see [The Six Outcomes](#s-faction-outcomes)), and it doesn't cost the crew its action. Pay again to guard another.
:::

:::example[Three goblins]
Three goblins telegraph a d6 each at Vesk, Wren and Isolde, all within Bram's stride, and their chief's got a d8 aimed at Bram himself. He intercepts all three for free, and that's 3 Aura for *Better Me Than You* before anything's rolled. He's Defending, so when they land he's got his Body twice over, his Defend's 9 steps and the landing's 9, with his Trusty shield's 2 on the landing, 11. He makes the landing a d12 and a d10 and his Defend a d12 and a d6. That's four dice for four blows. The goblins land 6, 4 and 1 and the chief 7, and Bram rolls 5 and 2 on his d12s, 4 on his d10 and 3 on his d6. The 4 holds the goblin's 4, because ties go to him, and the 2 holds the 1. That leaves the 6 and the 7, and his 5 and 3 can't stop either, so he pays 1 Aura for Stand firm, and the two of them are 1 point between them, which his armour soaks. Two of his dice won, so that's 2 more Aura. He isn't acting when it comes in, so whatever takes him past his maximum goes to the crew.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Guardian](#k-guardian) · [Warding](#k-wizard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Healer {#a-healer}

The Healer patches people up in the middle of a scene, so they don't have to wait for the end of it.

Only a Healer can make a **heal roll**. It's an action: roll the Root that fits how you're healing, at full and never split, its dice filled to a d12 first, so 8 steps is a d12 and a d4. **Your patient gets back what it rolls**, spread across their Roots however they like, less 5 for each **Toll** they've got already, and never past what a Root can hold, or half that while it's wounded. Then it's 1 more Toll for them. Its top faces give you Aura the way any die's do, but what it heals never does. Nothing heals a [Scar](#s-blaze), and there's more on Toll in [Healing](#s-healing).

A heal reaches one person. Anyone can [quick heal](#s-healing) for 1 Aura, though.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Good Hands*. Your heals ignore a step of Toll: each one loses 5 less for the Toll your patient's got, so a heal from you on someone with 1 Toll is whole.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your dice when you heal, so 2 more points for your patient.

**Talk them through it (1 Aura):** your heal reaches someone who can see and hear you, not just someone you can reach. They do the stitching while you tell them how.

**Clean it out (1 Aura):** your heal also ends a condition a healer could treat on your patient, like *Bleeding*, *Burning*, *Poisoned*, *Blinded* or *Stunned*, with no roll. It goes on a heal roll, never a quick heal. Pay again for another.

**Go round (1 Aura):** split your heal like any other roll, and give each die to a different patient you can reach. The dice aren't filled to a d12 first, since you're shaping them yourself. Each patient gets what their die rolls, less 5 for each Toll they've got, and takes 1 Toll.

**On the move (1 Aura):** your heal can be one die of a roll instead of your whole action, so the rest of your dice can do whatever else you're up to. Make that die as big as you like.

**Up you get (1 Aura):** your patient's *Blessed* with the die that healed them, the biggest if more than one did, on the next thing they've told the table they're about to do. It works even if their Toll ate the whole heal. With Go round, pay for each patient.

**What did this (1 Aura):** when you win a [Study](#s-study) on someone hurt, sick or dead, you get two of its picks instead of one: two straight answers, or an answer and a hidden tag. Pay once you've won.

**Owed (1 Aura):** someone you've healed or patched up, the GM's people included, does one thing for you that they'd do for whoever saved their life. There's no roll, and it's once for each of them.

**Rest easy (1 Aura):** when the scene ends, one Root of yours or of a friend you can reach rolls its rest twice and keeps the better. Pay again for another Root, or another friend.

**Pull them back (1 Aura):** when someone you can reach takes a Scar, they stay in the scene instead of leaving it. It costs no action. That Root's still wounded and on 0, so they'll want to keep their head down. Pay again for another.

**Close the wound (2 Aura):** your heal clears a wound as well, so that Root can heal to its maximum again, starting with this heal. It's still a heal, so it gives Toll and loses for Toll the same as any other.
:::

:::example[Healing Vesk]
Say you're a Healer, *The field surgeon of the Red Company*, and you heal with your Mind, 7 steps, so it's a d12 and a d2. Vesk's Body is down to 3 of the 10 it can hold, and her Mind to 2 of 5, and she's had one heal from you already this scene, so she's got 1 Toll. You roll 6 and 2, 8 in all. Her Toll would take 5 off that, but your *Good Hands* ignore a step of it, so she gets the whole 8. She puts 7 on her Body and 1 on her Mind, and now she's got 2 Toll. The d2 landed on its top face, so that's 1 Aura for you, and what you healed gets you nothing.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Healer](#k-healer) · [Mending](#k-mending) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Hunter {#a-hunter}

The Hunter marks the prey, and everyone who goes at it rolls an extra die.

Put one of your dice on a creature to mark it. Win and it's ***Marked***, a special condition carrying the die you won with. It doesn't give a step and nobody spends it. Instead, anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too, as one more die against it. It fades a size every round like any [condition](#s-conditions).

**The Mark jumps.** When a *Marked* creature falls, the Mark moves to another creature you can see, with the die it had.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Good Hunting*. Every die a friend wins against a creature you've *Marked*, going at it or stopping it, gives you 1 Aura as well as them.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to the Mark die once it's rolled. It's your Aura, whoever's rolling.

**Draw blood (1 Aura):** before you roll, give your marking die a second intent at the same creature. If it wins, the creature's *Marked* and the other intent lands too. If that drops it, the Mark jumps straight on, at that die.

**Mark the pack (1 Aura):** your roll to mark goes at two more creatures you can see, and each one it beats is *Marked* with it. Each Mark fades and jumps on its own. Pay again for two more.

**Watch its hands (1 Aura):** when a *Marked* creature's telegraphs land this round, whoever they land on rolls the Mark die with their defence, as one more die that can stop one. Pay any time before they land.

**Where it's soft (1 Aura):** when the Mark die wins, the GM tells you one of the creature's hidden tags, its weak point first if it's got one. Pay once it's won, and if there's nothing hidden she says so and you keep your Aura. Once a round for each creature.

**One by one (1 Aura):** when the Mark jumps, roll the Mark die there and then at the creature it jumped to, on its own, with an intent you give it, against that creature's roll for the round. It costs no action. Pay again each time it jumps.

**Mark the way (1 Aura):** your die to mark can go at a stake or an event instead of a creature. Win and it's *Marked*, and anyone who works it rolls the Mark die too. It doesn't jump.

**Set the snare (1 Aura):** your marking die goes on a spot instead of a creature, like a doorway or the gap they'll run for, or a name dropped in a conversation. Nothing pushes back, so you don't roll it, and it waits there without fading till the scene ends. The first creature to cross it, or flinch at the name, is *Marked* with that die. Pay again for another spot.

**On its trail (1 Aura):** put your marking die on what something left behind, like its footprints or the knife it dropped, against its Challenge. Win and you know which way it went and how far ahead it is till the next downtime, and it's *Marked* with that die when you find it. A *Marked* creature that gets away works the same with no roll, and it's *Marked* again at the die it had when you catch up.

**Hunt them down (1 Aura):** when a scene ends with a *Marked* creature that answers to a faction, the Mark goes on that faction in the next faction turn, at the die it ended the scene on. The crew's roll against that faction rolls the Mark die too. A creature you're on the trail of counts.
:::

:::example[Marking the Warden]
Wren marks the Warden with an arrow: her Body and *Long shot*, 10 steps, a d12 and a d8. Her d12 wins, so it's *Marked d12*. Bram swings at it and rolls the Mark d12 with his own dice, and two of his dice beat it, so Bram takes 2 Aura and Wren takes 2 for *Good Hunting*. Next round it's *Marked d10*. When the Warden falls, the Mark jumps to the guard captain behind it, and he's *Marked d10*.

Wren's taken Hunter twice, so her Push puts +3 on the Mark die.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Ranger](#k-ranger) · [Investigator](#k-investigator)

## Illusionist {#a-illusionist}

The Illusionist makes threats believe in things that aren't there.

Spend your action and roll your illusion dice against the threats' Challenge. **Meet or beat it** and they believe your illusion until they interact with it or try to get through it: a clone of you for them to hit, a wall to run around, a crate to hide under. To see through it before then, a threat has to beat what you rolled. A tie's yours here, and you don't pay Aura for it.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Made You Look*. When a threat swings at one of your illusions, talks to it or tries to get through it, take 1 Aura.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your illusion dice, which raises what a threat has to beat to see through it.

**Wear it (1 Aura):** your illusion goes on you or a friend you can touch: another face, or a guard's coat. It holds up when someone talks to them close up, and only beating what you rolled sees through it. Pay again for another person.

**Nothing there (1 Aura):** your illusion hides something that's there instead of showing something that isn't, like a friend in a doorway or the blood on the floor. Pay again for another.

**For their eyes (1 Aura):** one of your illusions shows only to the people you choose. Everyone else sees what's really there.

**Solid enough (1 Aura):** one of your illusions passes for the real thing up close, so it moves, answers back and holds up to a touch. A threat that touches it or talks to it still believes it, and only beating what you rolled sees through it. It still does nothing real, so a guard who throws his whole weight at the wall goes through.

**Decoy (1 Aura):** when a blow's about to land on someone your illusion's copied, it lands on the illusion instead, as long as the threat still believes it, and the illusion's gone. The copy has to be standing there already.

**Fell for it (1 Aura):** when a threat swings at one of your illusions, talks to it or tries to get through it, it takes the condition your illusion would leave it with, at the size of your biggest illusion die: *Taunted* by the clone, *Frightened* by the monster or *Blinded* by the flash. Never a vulnerability like *Prone*.

**Takes one to know one (1 Aura):** you know an illusion, a disguise or a forgery when you see one, with no roll, and the GM tells you what it's hiding. If it's none of those, she says so and you keep your Aura.

**That was never me (1 Aura):** the you everyone's been watching was an illusion, and you've been somewhere else in the scene all along, anywhere you could have walked to unseen since the scene began or since you were last touched or hit. Nothing you've done or taken is undone, and a telegraph already aimed at you still follows you.

**Leave it standing (1 Aura):** one of your illusions stays after you've gone, till someone sees through it or the next downtime ends. A faction's action in the next faction turn aimed at what it shows or hides has to beat what you rolled first, or it's wasted on the illusion.

**Make it real (1 Aura):** one illusion's real till the round ends: the wall stops them, the bridge takes your weight, the door locks. It does what the real thing would and nothing more, so a clone can be stood behind and takes one blow before it's gone, but it won't swing for you. Pay again for another round, or another illusion. When the round ends it's an illusion again, so get everyone off the bridge first.
:::

:::example[A wall across the corridor]
Say you throw a wall across the corridor. Your d10 rolls 6 and the guards' d6 rolls 6, which is enough. They run the long way round, and if one stops to look hard at it, he has to beat your 6. If one of them puts his shoulder into it instead, he goes straight through, and that's 1 Aura for *Made You Look*.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Illusion](#k-illusion)

## Leader {#a-leader}

The Leader hands their own steps out to the crew.

Only a Leader leads. An order or a rallying song is an action: a Root at full and any you bring in, the same as any roll, and instead of rolling it you hand its steps out however you like to friends who can hear or see you. They add them to their own rolls this round. A lead can be a combo too, with friends putting their actions in before you hand it out.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Where It's Needed*. Each round, you can move steps around between your own roll and the rolls of friends who can hear or see you, up to 1 step for each of you, counting yourself. Take them off one roll and put them on another before either's rolled. It's free and takes no action, and nobody's roll goes below 1 step.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of a friend's dice, on a roll your steps went into, once it's rolled and you can see it's needed. A roll your steps went into is any roll this round that got steps from your lead or your *Where It's Needed*.

**Size them up (1 Aura):** before anyone acts this round, ask the GM who's in charge here, who's closest to breaking or who's about to run, and she tells you straight. It works on a room full of courtiers the same as a fight. Pay again for another of the three.

**Take charge (1 Aura):** people in the scene who aren't threats, like a crowd or a ship's crew, do what you tell them this round, as long as it's something they'd do for whoever's in charge. There's no roll. They won't fight for you, but what they do changes the ground: the crowd blocks the street, or the crew cuts the anchor. Pay again for another round.

**On me (1 Aura):** shout, and every friend who can hear or see you can move their stride right now, wherever the round's got to, even off their turn. It's a stride, never an action, so it gets them out of a telegraph aimed at a place before it lands, but one aimed at a person still lands on them wherever they go.

**Signal (1 Aura):** this round your lead and your *Where It's Needed* reach one friend who can't hear or see you, as long as you've agreed a signal or sent word. Pay again for another friend.

**Rally them (1 Aura):** when you lead, one friend you hand steps to is *Rallied d6* as well. Pay again for another friend.

**Lead from the front (1 Aura):** keep some of your lead's steps and roll them yourself, as dice with intents of their own, and hand the rest out. Your kept dice are a roll your steps went into, so your *Push* and *Change of plan* work on them.

**Change of plan (1 Aura):** once a roll your steps went into has landed, pick one of its dice that lost, or whose job's already done, and send it at something else that friend could reach, with a new intent and the number it rolled. It goes against that target's roll for the round (rolled now if nobody's gone at it yet) or a telegraph's die, and it pays Aura if it wins there. Pay again for another die.

**Follow it up (1 Aura):** lead after a friend's rolled instead of before. Your steps all go to that one friend, and they roll them straight away as more dice on the same roll, each with its own intent. It's still your one action, and it can't go on an Oracle's foresight.

**Everyone gets it (1 Aura):** every friend who can hear or see you gets all your lead's steps, not a share. With *Lead from the front*, they each get all the steps you didn't keep.

**Orders for the week (1 Aura):** a lead you make in the last scene before downtime can wait for the faction turn, and its steps go on the crew's roll there. You pay for it in the scene.
:::

:::example[Orders from the doorway]
Bram barks orders from the doorway with his Soul, 4 steps, and he gives 2 to Vesk and 2 to Wren. Vesk's slipping past the guard on her Body, 11 steps, so with Bram's 2 she's on 13, two d12s and a d2. She rolls 6, 5 and 1 against the guard's 7, so Bram pays 1 Aura to push her 6 to an 8, and she's past him.
:::

:::example[Where it's needed]
Bram can see Vesk, Wren, Isolde and Dorrie, so with him that's five, and 5 steps he can move this round. Wren's about to put an arrow through the Warden's eye, and Bram's only holding the stair, so he takes 2 off his own Defend and 3 off Isolde's Study and puts all 5 on Wren's shot. Her 10 steps are 15 now, two d12s and a d6, and his Defend's down to 7.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Captain](#k-captain) · [Courtier](#k-courtier) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Maker {#a-maker}

The Maker gives an item something extra for the scene.

Spend your action and roll against the item's size: a d4 for something tiny like a ring, a d6 for something small like a dagger, a d8 for something medium like a shield, a d10 for something large like armour, and a d12 for anything intricate. Each die that beats it gives the item a **tag for the scene**.

A tag works like a permission for whoever's holding the item: it lets them do something the item couldn't before, and it can justify bringing a Root in. A ring that lets you see in the dark is fine, and a ring that gives you haste isn't. Whoever's holding the item can break a tag instead of taking a hit.

**You can spend your Aura on a friend's awakening** when you can touch their attuned thing. Each Aura's 2 more steps on their roll, the same as if they'd spent it themselves (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). Nobody else can.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Maker's Mark*. One thing you carry, or something of a friend's you've worked on, has your mark on it: a tag of your own, like *Fire* on a sword or *Supercharged* on a wand. It works like a permission for whoever's holding it, and they can break it instead of taking a hit. You put it back on in downtime, on that or something else, for free.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your making dice.

**Made it earlier (1 Aura):** say something you could have made, like a grapnel or a copy of the duke's seal, and you or a friend you can reach has it: an item at tier 2 till the scene ends, on top of your six, with no Resource ticked. Say what kind it is, a tool, a weapon or a guard, and never armour, a vessel or anything attuned. Pay again for another.

**On the fly (1 Aura):** your making can be one die of a roll instead of your whole action, so you can fix your hook halfway up the wall and keep climbing with the rest.

**Rig it (1 Aura):** your making can go on something nobody carries, like a door, a cart or a crane, and its tags work for the crew there till the scene ends. The GM says its size, and anyone using it can break a tag on it instead of taking a hit.

**Mend it (1 Aura):** a making die that wins against something broken mends it instead of giving it a tag, and it stays mended. That's a broken item, something broken in the scene like an axle or a bridge rope, or a tag you made that's been broken, your mark included, which goes straight back on.

**Tune it up (1 Aura):** a making die that wins can put the item a tier higher till the scene ends instead of giving it a tag, as far as tier 4. Tiers 5 and 6 only come out of the story. Pay again and another winning die can.

**Know how it's made (1 Aura):** when you win a [Study](#s-study) on something made, like a lock, a ship or a construct, the GM tells you its weak point as well, or that it hasn't got one.

**Built to break (1 Aura):** when a tag you made, or your mark, is broken to turn a hit, it goes off, and whoever landed the blow takes a condition that comes from the tag, at a d6, with no roll. *Fire* leaves them *Burning*, and *catches blades* leaves them *Disarmed*. You can pay it off your turn, wherever you are, 1 Aura for each tag that breaks.

**I built that (1 Aura):** till the scene ends, you can spend your Aura on the awakening of any friend who can hear or see you, without touching their attuned thing.

**Masterwork (1 Aura):** the tags you make this time last till downtime.

**A flaw in it (1 Aura):** when a making die of yours wins on something a faction owns or leans on, like its press, its ship or its gate, pay as the scene ends and it fails them when they need it. At the next faction turn that's a Destroy on that faction, a tick off the asset it's part of, and it doesn't cost the crew its action. It's one tick on a faction a faction turn.
:::

:::example[Vesk's coin]
Vesk hands you the first coin she ever stole, a d4. Your d8 rolls 6 against its 3, so the coin gets a tag for the scene, *turns a blade*, and you hand it back. When a blade gets through to her, she can break that tag instead of losing a point of her Body.

Your own mark's on Bram's shield, *catches blades*. When a sword gets through to him at the end of a round, he breaks your mark instead of losing a point of his Body, and you put it back on in downtime.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Tinker](#k-artificer) · [Artifice](#k-artifice)

## Oracle {#a-oracle}

The Oracle sees how the dice will fall, and decides who gets the good ones.

Spend your action and roll a Root. The numbers you rolled are your **foresight**. Until the round ends, whenever anyone rolls, friend or foe, you can swap one of your foresight numbers in for one of their dice, as long as it fits that die: a 7 can go on a d8 or bigger, but not on a d6. Each number goes once, and a number you swap in doesn't give anyone Aura or explode.

Your foresight's two numbers at most, however many steps you've got, so make them one die or two, and each friend who combos in gives you one more. Steps past six don't make more dice here, so steps a [Leader](#a-leader) hands you make your dice bigger, not more of them. Whatever's left when the round ends is gone.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Seen It Coming*. Each round, once the telegraphs are out and before anyone does anything else, you can roll up to 3 of the threats' dice: their telegraphs, or a threat's one roll for the round. They stay as they landed, face up, so the crew knows where to put its dice before anyone commits.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your foresight numbers, when you roll them. A 12 on your d12 can be a 14 this way, and it still fits a d12.

**Hold it (1 Aura):** your foresight lasts till you use it or the scene ends. You never hold more numbers than one foresight roll gives you, so if you roll it again while you're holding some, keep whichever you like, up to that many.

**Another glimpse (1 Aura):** your foresight's one number more this time. Pay again for another.

**See further (1 Aura):** *Seen It Coming* rolls 2 more dice this round, and they can be any dice the GM's about to roll, in a fight or out of one: a lock's, a wall's or a magistrate's. Each one stands as that thing's roll for the round, or for the next try outside rounds. Pay again for 2 more.

**Look again (1 Aura):** roll one of the dice from *Seen It Coming* again, and keep whichever of the two you like. Pay again for another die.

**I'll take that (1 Aura):** when you swap a foresight number in, the number you took off that die is yours. It's a foresight number now, in place of the one you just spent, and it goes once like any other. Pay again each time you keep one.

**Leave an omen (1 Aura):** instead of swapping a foresight number in, hang it on someone you can see as *Blessed* or *Hexed*, at the die it came off, so a 9 off your d12 is *Blessed d12*. Pay again for another number.

**Echoes (1 Aura):** hold something, or stand somewhere, and see the moment that mattered most in its past. The GM tells you what you see.

**What's coming (1 Aura):** ask the GM what'll happen in this scene if the crew leaves it alone, and she tells you straight.

**Foretold (1 Aura):** win a [Study](#s-study) and introduce a truth instead. It's true from now on, and anyone in the crew can bring a Root in on it this scene, like a permission they've all got.

**Read the year (1 Aura):** as the last scene before downtime ends, roll your foresight with no action and keep it for the faction turn. Its numbers swap into any faction's dice there, the crew's included.
:::

:::example[Seeing the brute's club]
Isolde's foresight comes off her Mind, 9 steps, and one action only ever gives her two numbers, so she makes it a d12 and a d6. They come up 9 and 1. Bram swings at the brute, and one of his dice rolls 2 against its 4, so she swaps her 9 in, and he's through. At the end of the round the brute's club comes up 7 at Wren, and Isolde swaps her 1 in, so Wren's Body stops it whatever it rolls.
:::

:::example[Isolde sees it coming]
The GM telegraphs the brute's club at Bram, a d10, and two guards' swords at Wren and Vesk, a d8 each. Before anyone moves, Isolde rolls all three. The club comes up 2, so Bram leaves it to his Body when it lands, since nearly any die of his stops a 2. The swords come up 7 and 8, so Wren and Vesk answer those instead of leaving them to their Bodies.
:::

:::example[The crack in the circle]
Isolde turns her Study on the Warden: her Mind, 9 steps, a d12 and a d6. Her d12 rolls 8 against its 6, so she's won, and she pays 1 Aura for Foretold. She says the chalk circle it's bound to has a crack in it. Now it does, and anyone in the crew can bring a Root in on it this scene.
:::

## Shadow {#a-shadow}

The Shadow notices things from where nobody's looking, and what they've seen is worth dice later.

When you get into hiding, the dice you rolled to hide go into your **shadow pool**. They're the things you noticed while nobody was looking. Add them to any roll after that, talking and Study included, as their own dice or as steps (a d8 is 4 steps). A die's gone once you use it, and the next time you hide, your pool starts again with the new dice.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Any Shadow Will Do*. Any patch of dark's somewhere you can hide. Everyone else needs something to get behind, like a corner or a pillar, and all you need's a shadow. You still roll to get into hiding.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one die on your roll to hide, or to a pool die when you use it.

**Vanish (1 Aura):** you're hidden again, no roll, even in plain sight. Your pool stays as it is, because you didn't roll. Pay again and a friend beside you vanishes too.

**I had it planned (1 Aura):** say a Resource without ticking one, as long as it's something you could've set up before the scene: a window left on the latch, a guard paid to look the other way, a change of clothes stashed in the laundry. Pay again for another.

**Seen from up there (1 Aura):** spend a die from your pool without rolling it, and ask the GM one thing you'd have seen while you were hidden, like where the key's kept or what the steward did when he thought he was alone. She answers it straight, and the answer can be a hidden tag of someone you were watching.

**Pass it on (1 Aura):** give a die from your pool to a friend who can see or hear you. They add it to a roll the way you would, and it's gone once they've used it. Pay again for another die.

**Light fingers (1 Aura):** when a die of yours wins against someone who hasn't seen you, take one thing they're carrying as well as whatever the die was for: a key, a letter, a purse. Pay again for another thing, or another person.

**Never saw it (1 Aura):** when a die of yours wins against someone who hasn't seen you, they're *Exposed* at that die's size as well as whatever the die was for. Pay again for another die.

**Gone into the dark (1 Aura):** one die that gets you into hiding answers a telegraph aimed at you as well, with the same roll. Pay again for another die.

**Not done yet (1 Aura):** when a pool die you rolled as its own die wins, it goes back in your pool a size smaller instead of being gone, and a d2 that wins is gone. Pay again for another die.

**Wait for it (1 Aura):** while you're hidden, take your action after the telegraphs' dice are rolled and before they land, so you see every number before you choose what your dice do. It's still your one action.

**Kept for later (1 Aura):** a die left in your pool when the last scene before downtime ends keeps for the faction turn, and it goes on the crew's roll there as its own die, as long as what you saw was that faction's business. You pay for it in the scene. Pay again for another die.
:::

:::example[Under the gallery]
The hall's lit up for the party, and the only dark's the shadow under the gallery. That's enough for Vesk. She slips into it on her Body, 11 steps, a d12 and a d10, and gets away with it, so both dice go in her pool. Later the steward's lying to her face, and from under there she saw him slip the letter into his coat. Her Mind's 5, so she takes the d12 from her pool as 6 steps, and that's 11: a d12 and a d10.
:::

## Shifter {#a-shifter}

The Shifter takes on what animals can do, whether that's wings or a breath attack.

You collect **animal tags**, the way a [Summoner](#a-summoner) catches souls. Spend your action, pick one and roll your Shifter dice against its die: most are a d4, swimming's a d6, flying's a d8 and a breath attack's a d12. Beat it and the tag's yours until downtime. It lets you do what the animal does, like a permission, so it can justify bringing a Root in too. Split to go for more than one, and strip one of your animal tags instead of taking a hit if you want to.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Something of the Beast*. At the start of every scene, pick one thing a beast has: a hawk's eyes, a hound's nose, a cat's ears, or being small enough to slip through a gap or big enough to fill a doorway. It's true of you till the scene ends, so you can do what it lets you do. It isn't an animal tag, so it can't take a hit for you.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your Shifter dice.

**Become it (1 Aura):** take any animal tag now with no roll, even a breath attack, as part of whatever you're doing, and even when it isn't your go, like wings as you fall. It's borrowed, so it lasts till the scene ends and you can't strip it. Pay again for another.

**Another beast (1 Aura):** pick a second *Something of the Beast* now, on top of the one you've got, till the scene ends. Pay again for another.

**Nose for it (1 Aura):** your *Something of the Beast* finds one thing nobody else in the crew could. Say which sense you're using, and the GM tells you straight what it finds: where someone's hiding, what's been through here, or what's coming. Pay again for another.

**Kin (1 Aura):** till the scene ends, one kind of animal treats you as one of its own, so a guard dog won't bark at you, a horse lets you up, or a hawk shows you where it's been. A beast that's a threat still fights you if its handler sets it on you, but it won't raise the alarm. Pay again for another kind.

**A little bird (1 Aura):** send a bird or a small beast off with a message or one small thing to someone you could name, or to watch a place. It gets there this scene if it's close, or by the next scene if it's across town, and a watcher comes back and the GM tells you what it saw, the way a beast sees it. Send it to watch a faction's place in the last scene before downtime, and when the GM telegraphs that faction's action, ask her one question about it and she answers it straight.

**Share the shape (1 Aura):** a friend you're touching gets one of your animal tags till the scene ends, and it does for them what it does for you. It's still yours too, and only you can strip it. Pay again for another tag, or another friend.

**Slip it (1 Aura):** strip one of your animal tags to break a condition on you, any time, with no roll. The tag has to fit: an eel's skin slips the grip, and wings get you out from under the net. Pay again for another.

**Tooth and claw (1 Aura):** till the scene ends, one of your animal tags is an item at tier 2 on any roll it fits, on top of your six: claws or jaws as a weapon, a shell or a thick hide as a guard, or gills or a climbing grip as a tool. It's the one item on that roll, and if the tag's stripped, it's gone. Pay again for another tag.

**The pounce (1 Aura):** when an animal tag's moving you, like wings or a cat's spring, one die you put on moving has an intent as well. Its number counts twice, for how far you go and against their defence when you land, so long as it gets you to them. Pay again for another die.

**All beast (1 Aura):** you're the animal, whole, till the scene ends or you change back, which is free whenever you like. Anyone who didn't see you change sees a stray dog or a crow on the gutter, and nobody looks twice unless you do something no animal would. You can't talk or use anything you carry, but your armour still soaks, and your animal tags and Trait work the same.
:::

:::example[Wings]
Say you want wings. Flying's a d8, and your d10 rolls 7 against its 5, so you've got *wings* as an animal tag until downtime. When an axe gets through, you can strip the wings instead of losing a point of your Body.

You started this scene with a hound's nose as well, so you can follow the smugglers through the rain by their smell, which nobody else in the crew can.
:::

## Summoner {#a-summoner}

The Summoner catches the souls of fallen creatures and calls them back out when there's work to do.

Catching souls and summoning are the Summoner's, and nobody else can do them. Here's what you'll need at the table, and the rest is in [Summoning](#s-summoning).

-   **Catching a soul:** when a creature falls, put a die on it and roll against its Challenge. Beat it and you're carrying its soul, which ticks one of your Resources till downtime. Miss and it's gone. In downtime, put it into your summoning attunement.
-   **Your souls live in your summoning attunement**, one to each awakening, on the same stages as any attuned thing (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). A soul's die is its Challenge die when you caught it. A soul's in an awakening's place, but it isn't steps, so it doesn't lock a point the way an awakening does.
-   **Calling one out** spends Aura, as part of an action: a d4 of summons for each Aura, built into one summon or several, and none of them bigger than that soul's own die. It needs a reason in the story.
-   **A soul is what it was.** A caught courtier talks and a hound tracks. Summons act in the round like anyone, fade a size every round, and lose a size to every hit that gets through. Their dice never pay Aura.
-   **A summon intercepts for free.** It can [intercept](#s-intercept) the blows aimed at someone within its stride, but not fear or despair, and it rolls its die to stop one of them.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Pocket Summon*. One of your souls is your pocket summon. Once a scene it comes out the moment you call it, at its own die, with no action and no Aura. Once it's gone, you can call it straight back the same way for the usual 1 Aura for each d4 of it. It still needs a reason in the story.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your dice when you're catching a soul.

**Call it now (1 Aura):** call a summon out the moment you need it, without waiting for your action: in the middle of someone else's go, or as the telegraphs land. You still pay its Aura. It can intercept straight away, but it doesn't act till next round.

**Hold together (1 Aura):** the next time this round one of your summons would lose a size, to the fade or to a hit that gets through, it doesn't. Pay again for another.

**Ask the dead (1 Aura):** ask a soul you hold, in your attunement or one you're carrying, one thing it knew while it was alive, without calling it out. It answers as well as it can, and a hound's answer is a hound's. Pay again for another question.

**Borrow its knack (1 Aura):** one thing a soul you hold could do is yours till the scene ends, like a permission, so it can bring a Root in: the courtier's manners, the hound's nose or the locksmith's hands. Pay again for another.

**Speak through it (1 Aura):** see and hear what one of your summons does, and talk through it, wherever it goes till it's gone. Send the rats under the door and you're in the room.

**Call the horde (1 Aura):** every summon you call out this time brings a d4 one just like it, so call three and six come. It works on your pocket summon too. Pay again and each brings another.

**Catch it whole (1 Aura):** when a die of yours puts a creature down, the same die catches its soul as well, with no die of its own on the catch. A summon's die counts as yours for this. The soul still ticks one of your Resources.

**Stand with them (1 Aura):** one of your summons goes with a friend this round, and instead of acting on its own, its die's one of theirs: it rolls in their throw, they give it its intent, and it counts as theirs for their archetype, so it explodes for a Warrior. It's still a summon's die, so it never pays Aura. Pay again for another summon, or another friend.

**Rise (1 Aura):** a soul you've caught this scene can be called out now, though it isn't in your attunement yet. You pay for it the usual way, none bigger than its own die. It's what it was, so the bandit captain knows his own men and his own door.

**Haunt them (1 Aura):** as a scene ends, leave a soul you hold haunting a faction's face or one of its places, and it's not yours to call or ask till the next downtime's over. When that faction telegraphs its action, the soul's die goes against it as an Obstruct, and it doesn't cost the crew its action (see [The Faction Turn](#s-faction-turn)). Win or lose, the soul comes back and the GM tells you what it saw.
:::

:::example[Sable calls them out]
Sable's got three souls in her summoning attunement: a rat swarm (d4), a hound (d6) and the harbour Warden (d8), and the Warden's her pocket summon. The watch is coming down the alley, so the Warden's out at her side as a d8 before anyone's moved, with no action and no Aura. She's still got her action, so she spends 2 Aura on the rats, as two swarms of d4. Next round the Warden's a d6 and the rats are d2s, and the round after that, the rats are gone. When the Warden goes too, she can have it straight back for 2 Aura.
:::

## Teleporter {#a-teleporter}

The Teleporter jumps from spot to spot, and opens portals for the crew.

Your [stride](#s-movement) can be a teleport of up to 10 units. If you're a [Guardian](#a-guardian) as well, you intercept for every friend within those 10 units, because you jump to each of them.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Not There*. Once a scene, whenever you like, even as something's landing on you, teleport anywhere you can see within 10 units. It takes no action, and whatever was about to land on you misses. It's the one time moving gets you out of a telegraph aimed at you.

**Portal (1 Aura):** open a portal between two spots you can see within 10 units. It takes no action and lasts till the round ends, and anyone who steps in either end comes out the other as part of their move, threats included. A telegraph aimed at someone follows them through, and one aimed at a place stays behind. Pay again for another portal, or another round on one that's open.

**Further (1 Aura):** everything of yours that goes 10 units goes 10 more this round, your teleport and *Not There* included. Pay again for 10 more.

**Fetch (1 Aura):** something you can see within 10 units that nobody's holding or wearing, and that you could carry in your arms, is in your hand, or something in your hand goes there, a willing friend's hand included. Pay again for another thing.

**Take them with you (1 Aura):** when you teleport, someone willing you're touching comes too, and if it's your *Not There*, whatever was about to land on them misses as well. Someone unwilling takes Drop them. Pay again for another.

**Swap (1 Aura):** swap places with a friend or a Mook you can see within 10 units, with no roll. Anyone bigger takes Drop them. Pay again for another swap.

**Keyhole (1 Aura):** open a hole the size of a coin to any spot within 10 units, wall or no wall. Till the round ends you see and hear through it, and what you see through it counts as seen for your teleport, Portal, Fetch and Swap. When nobody's counting rounds, it lasts as long as you keep still and watch. Only sight and sound go through, and someone looking right at the other end can spot it. Pay again for another round, or another keyhole.

**Leave a door (1 Aura):** mark the spot you're standing on. Till the next downtime's over, it counts as within 10 units from anywhere in the same city, for your teleport and your portals. A new mark takes the old one's place, unless you pay again to keep both.

**Through the portal (1 Aura):** this round, your dice and the crew's can go through a portal you've got open, at anything near its other end, and theirs can come back the same way.

**In and out (1 Aura):** this round each of your dice can go at anyone within 10 units, each at someone different if you like, and you finish the round back where you started. You count as right beside each of them as its die lands, so anything that needs a touch works.

**Drop them (1 Aura):** when a die of yours wins against someone you're touching, you send them anywhere within 10 units you can see, as well as whatever the die was for: off the wall, into the cell, or back across the square. Over a drop they fall, and the GM says what that does, but never into anything solid. Pay again for another.
:::

:::example[Out through the portal]
Say you're the Teleporter, and Vesk's cornered on a balcony across the square, 8 units from the alley you're in. Your stride's a teleport, so you're beside her, and it hasn't cost your action. Then you pay 1 Aura and open a portal on the balcony, linked to the alley. It lasts the round, so Vesk steps in and comes out in the alley.

At the end of the round the sergeant's crossbow and a guard's spear both come at you. You teleport onto the fountain in the middle of the square, and both of them miss. That's your *Not There* for the scene.
:::

## Warrior {#a-warrior}

The Warrior's for fighting, with dice that don't stop at their top face.

Your dice **explode** when you fight. A die that lands on its top face gives you 1 Aura as usual, then you roll it again and add the new number on, the only time dice add apart from moving and what abilities add. It keeps going as long as it keeps landing on top, so a d8 that comes up 8, then 5, is a 13.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Second Skin*. Your armour's a guard as well. On a roll avoiding harm, when something lands at a Root or you Defend it, your armour on that Root adds its tier to your roll, the way a shield would, and it still soaks whatever gets through. It's still one item a roll, so it's your armour or your shield.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to any one of your dice in a fight. If that takes it to its top face or past, there's no Aura for it, but it still explodes.

**Not just fights (1 Aura):** your dice explode on this roll even when it isn't a fight: a door to break, a wall to climb, or a man to stare down.

**Parry (1 Aura):** this round, your weapon's tier goes on your roll when things land at your Body, the way a guard's would. It's still one item a roll.

**Opened (1 Aura):** when one of your dice wins in a fight, the target's *Opened* till the round ends. *Opened* is the Warrior's own [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), so it stacks with fire, blades and the rest. Every hit on it ticks once more till the round ends, starting with the one that opened it. Two Warriors can each open the same target, and both count. Pay again to open another target your dice beat.

**Read the fighter (1 Aura):** when one of your dice wins in a fight, the GM tells you one of the target's hidden tags as well. Pay again for another.

**Face me (1 Aura):** when one of your dice wins against a threat, its next telegraph has to come at you, as well as whatever the die was for. Pay again for another threat your dice beat.

**Through them (1 Aura):** when one of your dice puts a mook down, it goes on at another within your reach, with the same number, against their defence. Pay again and it goes on again.

**Follow through (1 Aura):** when one of your dice explodes, say so before you roll the new one, and it's a die of its own, with its own intent, instead of adding on. It pays Aura and explodes like any die. Pay again for another.

**Riposte (1 Aura):** when one of your dice stops a blow aimed at you, whether you're answering it, Defending or it's landing, it goes at whoever swung it as well, with the number it rolled and an intent you give it, against their roll for the round. Pay again for another die.

**Show them how (1 Aura):** a friend beside you rolls dice that explode in this fight, the way yours do, till the round ends, and the Aura from their explosions is theirs. Pay again for another friend.

**Call them out (1 Aura):** challenge someone in front of people whose opinion they care about, with no roll. The GM picks: they face you alone, and while nobody else touches either of you their telegraphs only come at you, or they back down and lose face, and one tag that rests on their name is gone, her pick. It needs a name to lose and people watching who matter to them, so a beast, a zealot or a man with nobody watching just says no.
:::

:::example[The sellsword]
Say you're a Warrior, and your swing's your Body, 8 steps, which you keep as a d12 and a d4. You go at a sellsword whose d10 comes up 9. Your d12 lands on 7, so you pay 1 Aura and it's a 9, a tie, and you pay 1 Aura more to win it. A d12 that lands on 12 would have given you 1 Aura and rolled again, and so would your d4 on a 4.

You're in a Trusty mail shirt, tier 2 armour, and next round you're swinging again, not Defending, when two blades come at your Body. When they land, your Body's 8 steps and your mail's tier is 2 more, 10, and you make it two d10s, one for each blade. Whatever gets through takes its point off the mail's soak before it gets to your Body.
:::
