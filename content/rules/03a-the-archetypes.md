Guardian, Healer and Shadow are archetypes, and there are fourteen more. An **archetype** is the kind of character you are at the table, and it's its own pick, apart from your Titles. Each one's got a **mechanic**, something only that archetype can do, a **Trait** that's always true of you, and **abilities** you pay for out of your [tracks](#s-tracks). Ash is an Elementalist, so one of her dice can be a burst of fire that catches every guard in the doorway, and she's the only one in the crew who can do that.

Start with how they work, then find yours in [Picking Your Archetypes](#s-picking-archetypes). After that, every archetype has a section of its own, in alphabetical order: what it does, its Trait, its abilities, and an example.

## How Archetypes Work {#s-how-archetypes}

-   **An archetype's 5 points** when you make your character, and it comes with its mechanic, its Trait and one ability. You can take as many as you can pay for, and they don't have to be different: Vesk's a Shadow twice over. They're separate from your Titles: Bram's a Guardian and a Leader, and his Titles are *The wall that held the harbour gate* and *A sergeant who lost his stripes*.
-   **Your mechanic** is the thing only your archetype can do, and it's free. When it has you roll, you build the dice the usual way, from a Root and everything on your sheet that fits (see [Steps and Dice](#s-building-die)). Bram's orders come off his Soul, his second Title, *bark an order that gets obeyed* and Leader, the same as anything else he says.
-   **Your Trait** is something that's always true of you because of your archetype, and every archetype's got one, in its own section. Most cost nothing and never switch off. A few cost Aura or come once a scene, and those say so. Take an archetype twice and it's still the one Trait. Bram's a Guardian, so every blow he intercepts for a friend gives him 1 Aura, whether it gets through or not.
-   **Your abilities** are in your archetype's section. Your archetype comes with one, your pick off its list, Push included. More are a point each when you make your character, or a downtime slot later on, and an archetype holds six at most. Each one costs a size of a track when you use it, and how that works is just below.
-   **It's a step on every roll it fits**, like everything else on your sheet, and an archetype you've taken twice is two steps.
-   **There's no action limit.** Your archetypes don't cap your actions. Extra actions are bought with Aura, 3 for the first in a round, then 4, then 5 and so on (see [Extra actions](#s-extra-actions)).
-   **A new archetype** can come when your [Aspiration](#s-aspiration) climbs past d12, instead of a Title or an attuned thing, with the ability it comes with.

### Abilities {#s-abilities}

:::rule
Every archetype has a **home track**: Stamina, Confidence or Resolve. **Each ability costs 1 from its home track**, so paying 1 Stamina drops your Stamina a size. You can pay **2 from your other two tracks** instead, in any mix.

**They stack.** Pay again and you get it again: another +2, another target, another round, whatever the ability says you get more of.

**Nothing you pay takes a track below d2.** Nobody Blazes on purpose.
:::

An ability's a step on a roll you use it on, like anything else on your sheet that fits. Wren's roll to mark a creature gets 1 more when she pays for Mark the pack to mark three of them at once.

**Push** is the one most archetypes have on their list: +2 to one die on your mechanic, once you've rolled and you know you need it, so it's never a step. A die the +2 takes to its top face, or past it, doesn't pay Aura. **Take the same archetype twice** and its Push is +3, three times it's +4, and so on. You only need Push the once: Vesk's first Shadow came with Push and her second with Vanish, and her Push is +3.

| Home track | Archetypes |
| --- | --- |
| Stamina | Bloodsworn, Guardian, Maker, Shadow, Shifter, Warrior |
| Confidence | Face, Gambler, Hunter, Illusionist, Oracle |
| Resolve | Bard, Elementalist, Healer, Leader, Summoner, Teleporter |

:::example[Bram stays on the wall]
The brute's club comes at Bram, a d10 to knock him off the wall, and Bram's Defending: his Body, both Titles, *hold the line*, *fight in a shield wall*, Guardian, his plate and his shield, 14 steps, two d12s and a d4, and his Stamina's d12 rolls with them when it lands. The club comes up 9, and Bram rolls 7, 6 and 3, and his Stamina 4. Nothing's stopped it, and he isn't going off that wall, so he pays 1 Stamina for his Guardian's Push and makes the 7 a 9. That's a tie, and ties go to him, so he's still standing on the wall, on a d10 of Stamina now. If he'd taken Guardian twice, it'd be a 10.
:::

### What only one archetype does {#only-one-archetype}

A few things in the game belong to one archetype, and if you're not that archetype, you can't do them:

-   **Leading** is the [Leader](#a-leader)'s: handing your steps out to friends. Anyone can put their action into a friend's roll as a [combo](#s-teamwork).
-   **Areas** are the [Elementalist](#a-elementalist)'s. A *Sweep* weapon goes at everyone in your reach whoever's swinging it, and threats have areas of their own (see [Area](#s-area)).
-   **Heal rolls** are the [Healer](#a-healer)'s. Anyone can [quick heal](#s-healing) for 1 Aura.
-   **Catching souls and summoning** are the [Summoner](#a-summoner)'s.
-   **Intercepting for free** is the [Guardian](#a-guardian)'s. Anyone can [intercept](#s-intercept) for 1 Aura.
-   **Flipping dice** is the [Gambler](#a-gambler)'s.

## Bard {#a-bard}

The Bard's for nerve. You're the one keeping the crew's heads straight, and Defending everyone who can hear you against fear, lies and pressure.

**Lift them.** Spend your action, and every friend who can hear you rolls their Confidence the way they would at the end of a scene: a 4 or more puts it up a size, as far as d10.

**Hold them.** When fear, lies or pressure come at you or friends who can hear you, sing, as your action. It's a [Defend](#s-defend) against fear, lies and pressure for all of you: build your song from a Root and everything that fits, usually your Soul, and split it as many times as you like. When the telegraphs land, each of your dice stops one it beats, aimed at any of you, and ties go to you. Anything it doesn't stop still has to get past that person's own Confidence, rolled once the same as letting it ride, and that goes for yours too.

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *All Eyes on Me*. While you're singing, playing or telling a story, pay 1 Aura for each threat you want watching you, and a whole crowd can be one threat. Till you stop, it notices nothing else short of a scream or a blade, so the crew can get on with it behind your back.

**Push (1 Resolve):** +2 to one of your song dice.

**Crescendo (1 Resolve):** this time your Lift needs no roll: everyone who hears you comes up a size, as far as d12. Pay again and it's two sizes.
:::

:::example[A dragon roars]
A dragon roars at four of you, a d10 at each one's Mind: you, Bram, Wren and Isolde. Say you're the Bard, with a Soul of 4. Your Title's *The best voice on the coast*, and with *hold a crowd* and Bard, your song's 7 steps. You split it four ways, so you've got a die for each roar: three d4s and a d2.

The roar comes up 1 at you, 2 at Wren, 4 at Isolde and 5 at Bram. Your d4s come up 3, 3 and 1, and your d2 comes up 1. The d2 stops the 1 at you, because the tie's yours, and a 3 stops Wren's 2. You pay 1 Resolve to push the other 3 to a 5, and that stops Isolde's 4. Your last 1 can't stop Bram's 5, so the roar has to get past his own Confidence, a d12, and it comes up 5. The tie's his, so it's stopped too. Nobody's Confidence drops, and two of your dice won without a push, so that's 2 Aura for you.
:::

:::example[Up the stair at Lord Vane's party]
You're at Lord Vane's party, and Vesk needs to get up the stair past the guests and the guard at the foot of it. You start on a ballad and pay 2 Aura, 1 for the guests, who are one crowd, and 1 for the guard. Vesk walks up the stair behind them, and nobody watching you sees her go. If she knocks a vase off the landing, though, they'll all turn round.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Bard](#k-bard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Bloodsworn {#a-bloodsworn}

The Bloodsworn gets more dangerous every time they bleed.

**Every size your Stamina drops adds 3 steps to an action.** If it drops as you act, because you paid it, the steps go on that roll. If a hit takes it, they go on your next action. A hit that's two sizes, because it found a [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), adds 6.

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Paid in Blood*. Every size your Stamina drops gives you 1 Aura, whether a hit took it or you paid it yourself.

**Bleed for it (1 Stamina):** pay it as you act, for nothing but the blood, and your mechanic puts 3 steps on this roll. Pay again for 3 more.

**Bleed out (1 Stamina):** one roll gets 3 steps for every size your Stamina's below d12, counting the size you've just paid.
:::

:::example[Two hits]
Two blows get through to you this round, and your Stamina goes from d12 to d8, so your next action's got 6 more steps, and *Paid in Blood* gives you 2 Aura. You weren't acting when they landed, so that Aura stops at your maximum, and anything over it can go to a friend.

Next round you swing at the one who did it. It'd be 8 steps on its own, so it's 14 with the blood: two d12s and a d4. Pay 1 Stamina for Bleed for it as you swing, and that's the blood's 3 steps and 1 for the ability, 18 in all, three d12s. Paying it drops your Stamina another size, so *Paid in Blood* gives you 1 more Aura.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Warrior](#k-warrior) · [Hexer](#k-warlock)

## Elementalist {#a-elementalist}

The Elementalist hits everyone in an area at once, like fire across a doorway or lightning down a corridor.

Areas are yours. One of your dice can be your **area** instead of an intent. Say its shape, roll it with the rest, and the number's how far it goes:

-   **Burst:** that many squares out from a point.
-   **Cone:** that long the way you face, and as wide at the end.
-   **Line:** twice as far, straight out.

It takes one of your splits, and it doesn't cost Aura. Everyone the story puts in it defends against your other die, and you can always make it smaller, which you'll want when your friends are in it. Without a grid, 1 or 2 is everyone close to the target, 3 to 5 is everyone within a few steps, and 6 or more is the whole room. There's more in [Area](#s-area).

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *Eye of the Storm*. Your own areas never catch you, and your element can't hurt you once it's loose in the scene, like the fire your burst left in the curtains. It still can when someone aims it at you.

**Push (1 Resolve):** +2 to your area die.

**Sculpt (1 Resolve):** your area skips one person you choose. Pay again for each one more.
:::

:::example[Five guards]
Ash is in the middle of five guards, and she throws fire: her Soul, her first Title, *throw fire*, Elementalist, her ember stone and *Fire without a word*, 13 steps. She shapes them into a d12, a d10 and a d4, and the d4's her burst, going off around her. It rolls 2, but the guards are three squares out, so she pays 1 Resolve and it's a 4. That's the d4's top face, but the +2 got it there, so there's no Aura for it. All five defend against her d12, her d10 goes at the door behind them, and the burst never touches her.

The curtains catch, and next round the GM telegraphs the fire spreading to the doorway, a d8 at whoever's in it. Ash walks out through the flames without a mark on her.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Fire](#k-sorcerer) · [Storm](#k-storm) · [Kinesis](#k-kinesis)

## Face {#a-face}

The Face gets into a threat's head, so its own attacks go where you say.

Spend your action and build your dice the usual way, but don't roll them. Put them on a threat as **influence**, and they stay until you use them or the scene ends. At any point before one of its telegraphs lands, roll your influence against it. Each of its dice that your best influence die beats is yours: say what it does instead, as long as it fits the story, like hitting one of its own or the ground. Then the influence is spent.

:::rule
**Home track:** Confidence.

**Trait:** *I Know a Guy*. Once a scene, say you know someone who could turn up here, and one true thing about them. They come into the scene, the GM plays them, and they help you however they can.

**Push (1 Confidence):** +2 to one of your influence dice.

**Turn them (1 Confidence):** when your influence takes one of a Mook's or a Named threat's dice, the threat fights for the crew till the round ends. Pay again for another threat your influence took a die from.
:::

:::example[The bandit captain]
Dorrie's put her influence on the bandit captain: her Mind, both Titles, *make a lie sound like old news*, *keep my story straight under questions* and Face, 11 steps, a d12 and a d10. He telegraphs a d6 at Bram's Body and another at Wren's, and she rolls her influence against them: her best is a 5. They come up 3 at Bram and 6 at Wren. The 3's hers, so that cut lands on his own sergeant. She wants the other one too, so she pays 1 Confidence to push her 5 to a 7, and the cut at Wren goes into the wall.
:::

:::example[Dorrie knows a guy]
The crew's papers are wrong and the harbour gate's shut for the night. Dorrie says she knows the clerk on the night shift, Tam, and that he owes her for keeping his brother out of the stocks. Tam comes down from the office with his lamp. He can't open the gate himself, but he knows which guard plays cards with the sergeant, and he'll vouch for her.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Face](#k-face) · [Courtier](#k-courtier) · [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Enchantment](#k-enchantment)

## Gambler {#a-gambler}

The Gambler turns a die over after it lands, whoever's it is.

**Once a round, flip a die.** When any die you can see lands, yours, a friend's or a threat's, you can flip it: its new number is its size plus 1, take away what it rolled. A 2 on a d10 becomes a 9, and a 9 becomes a 2. It costs no action. A flipped die never pays Aura, and it doesn't explode.

:::rule
**Home track:** Confidence.

**Trait:** *Bad Luck Pays*. Every die of yours that comes up 1 gives you 1 Aura, as long as it stays a 1.

**Another flip (1 Confidence):** flip one more die this round. Pay again for another.

**Double or nothing (1 Confidence):** flip every die in one roll, all at once.
:::

:::example[The guard's lucky night]
Bram's Defending against the guard captain's d10, and it comes up 9. You're the Gambler, so you flip it: 11 take away 9 is a 2, and Bram stops it with anything. Later in the round Vesk's d4 for keeping quiet comes up 1, so you pay 1 Confidence and flip that too, and it's a 4.

Then your own d6 comes up 1 when you go at the guard. Turning it into a 6 would cost another Confidence, so you leave it, and *Bad Luck Pays* gives you 1 Aura instead.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Guardian {#a-guardian}

The Guardian stands in front of the crew and takes what's coming at them.

You [intercept](#s-intercept) **for free**, for every friend within your stride. Any time before the telegraphs land, take any or all of the blows aimed at them, whichever enemy they're from, and they come at you instead. Fear and despair are inside their heads, so nobody can step in front of those, and keeping fear off the crew is a [Bard](#a-bard)'s job. Then [Defend](#s-defend) or let it ride the usual way, and since one die stops one, if you've pulled a lot onto yourself, you'll want to Defend. To get past you, threats have to pull the crew apart.

If you're a Teleporter as well, you intercept for every friend within 10 units (see [Teleporter](#a-teleporter)).

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Better Me Than You*. Every blow you intercept for a friend gives you 1 Aura, whether it gets through to you or not.

**Push (1 Stamina):** +2 to one of your defence dice, your Defend's or a track's.

**Stand firm (1 Stamina):** the next two hits that get through to you this round do nothing. Pay again for two more.
:::

:::example[Three goblins]
Three goblins telegraph a d6 each at Vesk, Wren and Isolde, all within Bram's stride, and their chief's got a d8 aimed at Bram himself. He intercepts all three for free, and that's 3 Aura for *Better Me Than You* before anything's rolled. Then he Defends: his Body, both Titles, *hold the line*, *fight in a shield wall*, Guardian, his plate and his shield, 14 steps, two d12s and a d4. His Stamina's d12 rolls with them, so that's four dice for four blows. The goblins land 6, 4 and 1 and the chief 7, and Bram rolls 5 and 2 on his d12s and 4 on his d4, and his Stamina 3. The 4 holds the goblin's 4, because ties go to him, and the 2 holds the 1. That leaves the 6 and the 7, and his 5 and 3 can't stop either, so he pays 1 Stamina for Stand firm, and neither of them does a thing. Two of his dice won and the d4 landed on its top face, so that's 3 more Aura, and two hits have cost him one size. He isn't acting when it comes in, so whatever takes him past his maximum goes to the crew.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Guardian](#k-guardian) · [Warding](#k-wizard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Healer {#a-healer}

The Healer patches people up in the middle of a scene, so you don't have to wait for downtime.

Only a Healer can make a heal roll. It's an action, built like any other roll from a Root and everything that fits how you're doing it, and **each of your dice that rolls a 4 or more puts one of your patient's tracks up a size**, whichever they need most. Nothing heals a [Scar](#s-blaze).

A heal reaches one person. Split it to bring them up further, or to reach a second person you can touch. Anyone can quick heal, though: 1 Aura and your action puts one person's track up a size, no roll (see [Healing](#s-healing)).

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *Good Hands*. Every die of yours that puts a track up when you heal gives you 1 Aura, the same as a die that wins.

**Push (1 Resolve):** +2 to one of your dice when you heal.

**Pull them back (1 Resolve):** when someone you can reach Blazes, they stay in the scene. It costs no action. They still get their extraordinary moment and the choice to die, and if they live, they take the Scar and that track's on its smallest size that isn't locked. Pay again for another.
:::

:::example[Healing Vesk]
Say you're a Healer with a Soul of 2, and you're *The field surgeon of the Red Company* with *stitch a wound*. Your Title, the permission and Healer all fit, so that's 5 steps, and Vesk's Stamina is down to a d4. You split it into a d6 and a d4 and roll 5 and 3. The 5 puts her up to a d6, and that's 1 Aura for *Good Hands*. You pay 1 Resolve to push the 3 to a 5, so she's on a d8, but the +2 took that d4 past its top face, so there's no Aura for it.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Healer](#k-healer) · [Mending](#k-mending) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Hunter {#a-hunter}

The Hunter marks the prey, and everyone who goes at it rolls an extra die.

Spend your action and roll against a creature to mark it. Win and it's ***Marked***, a special condition carrying the die you won with. It doesn't give a step and nobody spends it. Instead, anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too, as one more die against it. It fades a size every round like any [condition](#s-conditions).

**The Mark jumps.** When a *Marked* creature falls, the Mark moves to another creature you can see, with the die it had.

:::rule
**Home track:** Confidence.

**Trait:** *Good Hunting*. Every die a friend wins against a creature you've *Marked*, going at it or stopping it, gives you 1 Aura as well as them.

**Push (1 Confidence):** +2 to the Mark die when it's rolled. It's your Confidence, whoever's rolling.

**Mark the pack (1 Confidence):** your roll to mark goes at two more creatures you can see, and each one it beats is *Marked* with it. Pay again for two more.
:::

:::example[Marking the Warden]
Wren marks the Warden with an arrow: her Body, both Titles, Hunter twice, her longbow and *Long shot*, 12 steps, two d12s. One of her d12s wins, so it's *Marked d12*. Bram swings at it and rolls the Mark d12 with his own dice, and two of his dice beat it, so Bram takes 2 Aura and Wren takes 2 for *Good Hunting*. Next round it's *Marked d10*. When the Warden falls, the Mark jumps to the guard captain behind it, and he's *Marked d10*.

Wren's taken Hunter twice, so her Push puts +3 on the Mark die.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Ranger](#k-ranger) · [Investigator](#k-investigator)

## Illusionist {#a-illusionist}

The Illusionist makes threats believe in things that aren't there.

Spend your action and roll your illusion dice against the threats' Challenge. **Meet or beat it** and they believe your illusion until they interact with it or try to get through it: a clone of you for them to hit, a wall to run around, a crate to hide under. To see through it before then, a threat has to beat what you rolled. A tie's yours here, and you don't pay Aura for it.

:::rule
**Home track:** Confidence.

**Trait:** *Made You Look*. When a threat swings at one of your illusions, talks to it or tries to get through it, take 1 Aura.

**Push (1 Confidence):** +2 to one of your illusion dice.

**Make it real (1 Confidence):** one illusion's real till the round ends. The wall stops them, and the clone hits back. Pay again for another round, or another illusion.
:::

:::example[A wall across the corridor]
Say you throw a wall across the corridor. Your d10 rolls 6 and the guards' d6 rolls 6, which is enough. They run the long way round, and if one stops to look hard at it, he has to beat your 6. If one of them puts his shoulder into it instead, he goes straight through, and that's 1 Aura for *Made You Look*.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Illusion](#k-illusion)

## Leader {#a-leader}

The Leader hands their own steps out to the crew.

Only a Leader leads. An order or a rallying song is an action: build it from a Root and everything that fits, then hand its steps out however you like to friends who can hear or see you. They add them to their own rolls this round, and the steps don't have to fit. Steps you're handed don't give you a split. A lead can be a combo too, with friends putting their actions in before you hand it out.

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *Where It's Needed*. Each round, you can move steps around between your own roll and the rolls of friends who can hear or see you, up to 1 step for each of you, counting yourself. Take them off one roll and put them on another before either's rolled. It's free and takes no action, the steps don't have to fit, and nobody's roll goes below 1 step.

**Push (1 Resolve):** +2 to one of a friend's dice, on a roll your steps went into.

**Everyone gets it (1 Resolve):** every friend who can hear or see you gets all your steps, not a share.
:::

:::example[Orders from the doorway]
Bram barks orders from the doorway: his Soul, his second Title, *bark an order that gets obeyed* and Leader, 6 steps, and he gives 3 to Vesk and 3 to Wren. Vesk's slipping past the guard on her Body, her first Title, *move unseen*, Shadow twice and her soft-soled boots, 13 steps, so with Bram's 3 she's on 16, two d12s and a d8. She rolls 6, 5 and 4 against the guard's 7, so Bram pays 1 Resolve to push her 6 to an 8, and she's past him.
:::

:::example[Where it's needed]
Bram can see Vesk, Wren, Isolde and Dorrie, so with him that's five, and 5 steps he can move this round. Wren's about to put an arrow through the Warden's eye, and Bram's only holding the stair, so he takes 2 off his own Defend and 3 off Isolde's Study and puts all 5 on Wren's shot. Her 12 steps are 17 now, two d12s and a d10, and his Defend's down to 12.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Captain](#k-captain) · [Courtier](#k-courtier) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Maker {#a-maker}

The Maker gives an item something extra for the scene.

Spend your action and roll against the item's size: a d4 for something tiny like a ring, a d6 for something small like a dagger, a d8 for something medium like a shield, a d10 for something large like armour, and a d12 for anything intricate. Each die that beats it gives the item a **tag for the scene**.

A tag is a special property, and it follows the same rules as a permission. A ring that gives you an extra step to dodge is fine, and a ring that gives you haste isn't. Whoever's holding the item can break a tag instead of taking a hit.

**You can power a friend's passive awakening** when you can touch their attuned thing. It's your own Aura, and it works the same way theirs would: it comes off what you've got and off your maximum till the scene ends (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). Nobody else can.

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Maker's Mark*. One thing you carry, or something of a friend's you've worked on, has your mark on it: a tag of your own, like *Fire* on a sword or *Supercharged* on a wand. It's a step on any roll it fits, like a permission, and whoever's holding it can break it instead of taking a hit. You put it back on in downtime, on that or something else, for free.

**Push (1 Stamina):** +2 to one of your making dice.

**Masterwork (1 Stamina):** the tags you make this time last till downtime.
:::

:::example[Vesk's coin]
Vesk hands you the first coin she ever stole, a d4. Your d8 rolls 6 against its 3, so the coin gets a tag for the scene, an extra step when she dodges, and you hand it back. When a blade gets through, she can break that tag instead of losing a size of Stamina.

Your own mark's on Bram's shield, *catches blades*, so it's a step on top of the shield whenever he Defends against one. When a sword gets through to him at the end of a round, he breaks your mark instead of losing a size of Stamina, and you put it back on in downtime.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Tinker](#k-artificer) · [Artifice](#k-artifice)

## Oracle {#a-oracle}

The Oracle sees how the dice will fall, and decides who gets the good ones.

Spend your action, build your dice from a Root and everything that fits, and roll them. The numbers you rolled are your **foresight**. Until the round ends, whenever anyone rolls, friend or foe, you can swap one of your foresight numbers in for one of their dice, as long as it fits that die: a 7 can go on a d8 or bigger, but not on a d6. Each number goes once, and a number you swap in doesn't give anyone Aura or explode.

Your foresight only comes from your splits: one action gives you two numbers at most, and each friend who combos in gives you one more. Steps past six don't make more dice here, so steps a [Leader](#a-leader) hands you make your dice bigger, not more of them. Whatever's left when the round ends is gone.

:::rule
**Home track:** Confidence.

**Trait:** *Seen It Coming*. Each round, once the telegraphs are out and before anyone does anything else, you can roll up to 3 of the threats' dice: their telegraphs, or a threat's one roll for the round. They stay as they landed, face up, so the crew knows where to put its dice before anyone commits.

**Push (1 Confidence):** +2 to one of your foresight numbers, when you roll them.

**Hold it (1 Confidence):** your foresight lasts till you use it or the scene ends.

**Foretold (1 Confidence):** win a [Study](#s-study) and introduce a truth instead, a free step for the crew this scene.
:::

:::example[Seeing the brute's club]
Isolde's foresight comes off her Soul, Oracle twice and her deck of the order's cards, 8 steps. One action only ever gives her two numbers, so she makes it a d12 and a d4, and they come up 9 and 1. Bram swings at the brute, and one of his dice rolls 2 against its 4, so she swaps her 9 in, and he's through. At the end of the round the brute's club comes up 7 at Wren, and Isolde swaps her 1 in, so Wren's Stamina stops it whatever it rolls.
:::

:::example[Isolde sees it coming]
The GM telegraphs the brute's club at Bram, a d10, and two guards' swords at Wren and Vesk, a d8 each. Before anyone moves, Isolde rolls all three. The club comes up 2, so Bram lets it ride, since his Stamina stops a 2 with anything but a 1. The swords come up 7 and 8, so Wren and Vesk answer those instead of trusting their tracks.
:::

:::example[The crack in the circle]
Isolde turns her Study on the Warden: her Mind, her second Title and *know the history of anything old*, 9 steps, a d12 and a d6. Her d12 rolls 8 against its 6, so she's won, and she pays 1 Confidence for Foretold. She says the chalk circle it's bound to has a crack in it. Now it does, and anyone in the crew can use that this scene.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Divination](#k-divination) · [Warding](#k-wizard) · [Medium](#k-medium) · [Hexer](#k-warlock) · [Scholar](#k-scholar) · [Investigator](#k-investigator)

## Shadow {#a-shadow}

The Shadow notices things from where nobody's looking, and what they've seen is worth dice later.

When you get into hiding, the dice you rolled to hide go into your **shadow pool**. They're the things you noticed while nobody was looking. Add them to any roll after that, talking and Study included, as their own dice or as steps (a d8 is 4 steps). A die's gone once you use it, and the next time you hide, your pool starts again with the new dice.

Coming out of hiding gives you an [extra action](#s-extra-actions), the same as it does for anyone. It's one extra action, even if you're *Unseen* as well.

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Any Shadow Will Do*. Any patch of dark's somewhere you can hide. Everyone else needs something to get behind, like a corner or a pillar, and all you need's a shadow. You still roll to get into hiding.

**Push (1 Stamina):** +2 to your roll to hide, or to a shadow die when you use it.

**Vanish (1 Stamina):** you're hidden again, no roll, even in plain sight. Nothing goes in your pool, because you didn't roll. Pay again and a friend beside you vanishes too.
:::

:::example[Under the gallery]
The hall's lit up for the party, and the only dark's the shadow under the gallery. That's enough for Vesk. She slips into it on her Body, her first Title, *move unseen*, Shadow twice and her soft-soled boots, 13 steps, two d12s and a d2, and gets away with it, so all three dice go in her pool. Later the steward's lying to her face, and she saw him pocket the letter from under there. Her Mind's 4 and nothing she knows fits, so she takes a d12 from her pool as 6 steps, and that's 10: a d12 and a d8.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Rogue](#k-rogue) · [Face](#k-face) · [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Veil](#k-veil)

## Shifter {#a-shifter}

The Shifter takes on what animals can do, whether that's wings or a breath attack.

You collect **animal tags**, the way a [Summoner](#a-summoner) catches souls. Spend your action, pick one and roll your Shifter dice against its die: most are a d4, swimming's a d6, flying's a d8 and a breath attack's a d12. Beat it and the tag's yours until downtime. It lets you do what the animal does, and it's a step when it fits. Split to go for more than one, and strip one of your animal tags instead of taking a hit if you want to.

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Something of the Beast*. At the start of every scene, pick one thing a beast has: a hawk's eyes, a hound's nose, a cat's ears, or being small enough to slip through a gap or big enough to fill a doorway. It's true of you till the scene ends, so you can do what it lets you do. It isn't an animal tag, so it's not a step and it can't take a hit for you.

**Push (1 Stamina):** +2 to one of your Shifter dice.

**Become it (1 Stamina):** take any animal tag with no roll, even a breath attack. Pay again for another.
:::

:::example[Wings]
Say you want wings. Flying's a d8, and your d10 rolls 7 against its 5, so you've got *wings* as an animal tag until downtime. When an axe gets through, you can strip the wings instead of losing a size of Stamina.

You started this scene with a hound's nose as well, so you can follow the smugglers through the rain by their smell, which nobody else in the crew can.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Wildcraft](#k-druid)

## Summoner {#a-summoner}

The Summoner catches the souls of fallen creatures and calls them back out when there's work to do.

Catching souls and summoning are the Summoner's, and nobody else can do them. Here's what you'll need at the table, and the rest is in [Summoning](#s-summoning).

-   **Catching a soul:** when a creature falls, spend your action and roll against its Challenge. Beat it and its soul goes into one of your utility slots. Miss and it's gone. In downtime, put it into your summoning attunement.
-   **Your souls live in your summoning attunement**, one to each awakening, on the same stages as any attuned thing (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). A soul's die is its Challenge die when you caught it.
-   **Calling one out** spends Aura, as part of an action: a d4 of summons for each Aura, built into one summon or several, and none of them bigger than that soul's own die. It needs a reason in the story.
-   **A soul is what it was.** A caught courtier talks and a hound tracks. Summons act in the round like anyone, fade a size every round, and lose a size to every hit that gets through. Their dice never pay Aura.
-   **A summon intercepts for free.** It can [intercept](#s-intercept) the blows aimed at someone within its stride, but not fear or despair, and it rolls its die to stop one of them.

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *Pocket Summon*. One of your souls is your pocket summon. Once a scene it comes out the moment you call it, at its own die, with no action and no Aura. Once it's gone, you can call it straight back the same way for the usual 1 Aura for each d4 of it. It still needs a reason in the story.

**Push (1 Resolve):** +2 to one of your dice when you're catching a soul.

**Call the horde (1 Resolve):** this time each Aura you spend calls up a d6, not a d4, still no bigger than the soul's own die. Pay again and it's a d8.
:::

:::example[Sable calls them out]
Sable's got three souls in her summoning attunement: a rat swarm (d4), a hound (d6) and the harbour Warden (d8), and the Warden's her pocket summon. The watch is coming down the alley, so the Warden's out at her side as a d8 before anyone's moved, with no action and no Aura. She's still got her action, so she spends 2 Aura on the rats, as two swarms of d4. Next round the Warden's a d6 and the rats are d2s, and the round after that, the rats are gone. When the Warden goes too, she can have it straight back for 2 Aura.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Medium](#k-medium)

## Teleporter {#a-teleporter}

The Teleporter jumps from spot to spot, and opens portals for the crew.

Your [stride](#s-movement) can be a teleport of up to 10 units. If you're a [Guardian](#a-guardian) as well, you intercept for every friend within those 10 units, because you jump to each of them.

:::rule
**Home track:** Resolve.

**Trait:** *Not There*. Once a scene, whenever you like, even as something's landing on you, teleport anywhere you can see within 10 units. It takes no action, and whatever was about to land on you misses. It's the one time moving gets you out of a telegraph aimed at you.

**Portal (1 Resolve):** open a portal and link it to another spot within 10 units. It lasts the round, and anyone who steps in comes out the other end. Pay again for another.

**Swap (1 Resolve):** swap places with anyone you can see, friend or foe. Pay again for another swap.
:::

:::example[Out through the portal]
Say you're the Teleporter, and Vesk's cornered on a balcony across the square, 8 units from the alley you're in. Your stride's a teleport, so you're beside her, and it hasn't cost your action. Then you pay 1 Resolve and open a portal on the balcony, linked to the alley. It lasts the round, so Vesk steps in and comes out in the alley.

At the end of the round the sergeant's crossbow and a guard's spear both come at you. You teleport onto the fountain in the middle of the square, and both of them miss. That's your *Not There* for the scene.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Phase](#k-phase)

## Warrior {#a-warrior}

The Warrior's for fighting, with dice that don't stop at their top face.

Your dice **explode** when you fight. A die that lands on its top face gives you 1 Aura as usual, then you roll it again and add the new number on, the only time dice add apart from moving and what abilities and awakenings add. It keeps going as long as it keeps landing on top, so a d8 that comes up 8, then 5, is a 13.

:::rule
**Home track:** Stamina.

**Trait:** *Second Skin*. Your armour fights with you even when you haven't Defended. When something lands at a Root and Defend wasn't your action, every piece of armour on that Root is 1 step, and those steps make dice of their own that roll beside your track.

**Push (1 Stamina):** +2 to any one of your dice in a fight. If that takes it to its top face or past, there's no Aura for it, but it still explodes.

**Opened (1 Stamina):** when one of your dice wins in a fight, the target's *Opened* till the round ends. *Opened* is the Warrior's own [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), so it stacks with fire, blades and the rest. Every hit on it ticks once more till the round ends, starting with the one that opened it. Two Warriors can each open the same target, and both count. Pay again to open another target your dice beat.
:::

:::example[The sellsword]
Say you're a Warrior, and your swing's 8 steps, a d12 and a d4. You go at a sellsword whose d10 comes up 9. Your d12 lands on 7, so you pay 1 Stamina and it's a 9, a tie, and you pay 1 Aura to win it. A d12 that lands on 12 would have given you 1 Aura and rolled again, and so would your d4 on a 4.

You're in Fine mail and a Common helm, and next round you're swinging again, not Defending, when two blades come at your Body. You let them ride, but your armour still counts: 2 steps, a d4, beside your Stamina, so you've got two dice for the two blades.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Warrior](#k-warrior) · [Sailor](#k-sailor) · [Kinesis](#k-kinesis)
