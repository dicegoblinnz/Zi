Guardian, Healer and Shadow are archetypes, and there are fourteen more. An **archetype** is the kind of character you are at the table, and it's its own pick, apart from your Titles. Each one's got a **mechanic**, something only that archetype can do, a **Trait** that's always true of you, and **abilities** you pay for in [Aura](#s-aura). Ash is an Elementalist, so one of her dice can be a burst of fire that catches every guard in the doorway, and she's the only one in the crew who can do that.

Start with how they work, then find yours in [Picking Your Archetypes](#s-picking-archetypes). After that, every archetype has a section of its own, in alphabetical order: what it does, its Trait, its abilities, and an example.

## How Archetypes Work {#s-how-archetypes}

-   **An archetype's 5 points** when you make your character, and it comes with its mechanic, its Trait and one ability. You can take as many as you can pay for, and they don't have to be different: Vesk's a Shadow twice over. They're separate from your Titles: Bram's a Guardian and a Leader, and his Titles are *The wall that held the harbour gate* and *A sergeant who lost his stripes*.
-   **Your mechanic** is the thing only your archetype can do, and it's free. When it has you roll, you roll the usual way, one Root at full and any you bring in (see [Steps and Dice](#s-building-die)). Bram's orders come off his Soul, the same as anything else he says.
-   **Your Trait** is something that's always true of you because of your archetype, and every archetype's got one, in its own section. Most cost nothing and never switch off. A few cost Aura or come once a scene, and those say so. Take an archetype twice and it's still the one Trait. Bram's a Guardian, so every blow he intercepts for a friend gives him 1 Aura, whether it gets through or not.
-   **Your abilities** are in your archetype's section. Your archetype comes with one, your pick off its list, Push included. More are a point each when you make your character, or a downtime slot later on, and an archetype holds six at most. Each one costs Aura when you use it, and how that works is just below.
-   **One action a round**, the same as everyone. Your archetypes don't give you more of them. More dice is how you do more (see [Splitting](#s-splitting)).
-   **A new archetype** can come when your [Aspiration](#s-aspiration) climbs past d12, instead of a Title or an attuned thing, with the ability it comes with.

### Abilities {#s-abilities}

:::rule
**Each ability costs 1 Aura** when you use it. **They stack.** Pay again and you get it again: another +2, another target, another round, whatever the ability says you get more of.

**You can't pay Aura you haven't got.** While you're acting, what's past your maximum is yours to spend like the rest.
:::

An ability's never steps on a roll. It does what it says, and that's all. Wren's roll to mark a creature is her Body as usual, and paying for Mark the pack sends that roll at two more.

**Push** is the one most archetypes have on their list: +2 to one die on your mechanic, once you've rolled and you know you need it. A die the +2 takes to its top face, or past it, doesn't pay Aura. **Take the same archetype twice** and its Push is +3, three times it's +4, and so on. You only need Push the once: Vesk's first Shadow came with Push and her second with Vanish, and her Push is +3.

:::example[Bram stays on the wall]
The brute's club comes at Bram, a d10 to knock him off the wall. It's at his Body, so when it lands he rolls his Body at full, 9 steps, and keeps it as a d12 and a d6. The club comes up 9, and Bram rolls 7 and 3. Nothing's stopped it, and he isn't going off that wall, so he pays 1 Aura for his Guardian's Push and makes the 7 a 9. That's a tie, and ties go to him, so he's still standing on the wall, with all his points. If he'd taken Guardian twice, it'd be a 10.
:::

### What only one archetype does {#only-one-archetype}

A few things in the game belong to one archetype, and if you're not that archetype, you can't do them:

-   **Leading** is the [Leader](#a-leader)'s: handing your steps out to friends. Anyone can put their action into a friend's roll as a [combo](#s-teamwork).
-   **Areas** are the [Elementalist](#a-elementalist)'s. A *Sweep* weapon goes at everyone in your reach whoever's swinging it, and threats have areas of their own (see [Area](#s-area)).
-   **Heal rolls** are the [Healer](#a-healer)'s. Anyone can [quick heal](#s-healing) for 1 Aura.
-   **Catching souls and summoning** are the [Summoner](#a-summoner)'s.
-   **Intercepting for free** is the [Guardian](#a-guardian)'s. Anyone can [intercept](#s-intercept) for 1 Aura.
-   **Flipping dice** is the [Gambler](#a-gambler)'s.

## Bard {#a-bard}

The Bard's for nerve. You're the one keeping the crew's heads straight, and Defending everyone who can hear you against fear, lies and pressure.

**Lift them.** Spend your action, and every friend who can hear you gets a point back on their Mind, as far as it can hold. It isn't a heal, so it adds no Toll.

**Hold them.** When fear, lies or pressure come at you or friends who can hear you, sing, as your action. It's a [Defend](#s-defend) against fear, lies and pressure for all of you: your song's a Root at full, usually your Soul, split however you like. When the telegraphs land, each of your dice stops one it meets or beats, aimed at any of you, since ties go to you. Anything it doesn't stop still has to get past that person's own Mind when it lands, and that goes for yours too.

:::rule
**Trait:** *All Eyes on Me*. While you're singing, playing or telling a story, pay 1 Aura for each threat you want watching you, and a whole crowd can be one threat. Till you stop, it notices nothing else short of a scream or a blade, so the crew can get on with it behind your back.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your song dice.

**Crescendo (1 Aura):** this time your Lift gives everyone who hears you 2 points back, not 1. Pay again and it's 3.
:::

:::example[A dragon roars]
A dragon roars at four of you, a d10 at each one's Mind: you, Bram, Wren and Isolde. Say you're the Bard, *The best voice on the coast*, with a Soul of 8. You sing, and split your 8 steps four ways, so you've got a d4 for each roar.

The roar comes up 1 at you, 2 at Wren, 4 at Isolde and 5 at Bram. Your d4s come up 3, 3, 2 and 1. The 1 stops the 1 at you, because the tie's yours, and the 2 stops Wren's 2. You pay 1 Aura to push a 3 to a 5, and that stops Isolde's 4. Your last 3 can't stop Bram's 5, so the roar has to get past his own Mind. That's 3, a d6, and it comes up 5. The tie's his, so it's stopped too. Nobody loses a point, and two of your dice won without a push, so that's 2 Aura for you.
:::

:::example[Up the stair at Lord Vane's party]
You're at Lord Vane's party, and Vesk needs to get up the stair past the guests and the guard at the foot of it. You start on a ballad and pay 2 Aura, 1 for the guests, who are one crowd, and 1 for the guard. Vesk walks up the stair behind them, and nobody watching you sees her go. If she knocks a vase off the landing, though, they'll all turn round.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Bard](#k-bard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Bloodsworn {#a-bloodsworn}

The Bloodsworn gets more dangerous every time they bleed.

**Every point your Body loses adds 3 steps to an action.** If you lose it as you act, because you spent it, the steps go on that roll. If a hit takes it, they go on your next action. Two hits from one die, because it found a [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), add 6.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Paid in Blood*. Every point your Body loses gives you 1 Aura, whether a hit took it or you spent it yourself.

**Bleed for it (1 Aura):** spend a point of your Body as you act, for nothing but the blood, and your mechanic puts 3 steps on this roll. Pay again for 3 more.

**Bleed out (1 Aura):** one roll gets 3 steps for every point your Body's down from what it can hold.
:::

:::example[Two hits]
Two blows get through to you this round, and your Body goes from 9 to 7, so your next action's got 6 more steps, and *Paid in Blood* gives you 2 Aura. You weren't acting when they landed, so that Aura stops at your maximum, and anything over it can go to a friend.

Next round you swing at the one who did it. Your Body's still 9 steps, whatever it's lost, so it's 15 with the blood: two d12s and a d6. Pay 1 Aura for Bleed for it as you swing and spend a point, and that's 3 more, 18 in all, three d12s. Losing the point gives you the Aura straight back, and your Body's down to 6.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Warrior](#k-warrior) · [Hexer](#k-warlock)

## Elementalist {#a-elementalist}

The Elementalist hits everyone in an area at once, like fire across a doorway or lightning down a corridor.

Areas are yours. One of your dice can be your **area** instead of an intent. Say its shape, roll it with the rest, and the number's how far it goes:

-   **Burst:** that many squares out from a point.
-   **Cone:** that long the way you face, and as wide at the end.
-   **Line:** twice as far, straight out.

It doesn't cost Aura. Everyone the story puts in it defends against your other die, and you can always make it smaller, which you'll want when your friends are in it. Without a grid, 1 or 2 is everyone close to the target, 3 to 5 is everyone within a few steps, and 6 or more is the whole room. There's more in [Area](#s-area).

:::rule
**Trait:** *Eye of the Storm*. Your own areas never catch you, and your element can't hurt you once it's loose in the scene, like the fire your burst left in the curtains. It still can when someone aims it at you.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to your area die.

**Sculpt (1 Aura):** your area skips one person you choose. Pay again for each one more.
:::

:::example[Five guards]
Ash is in the middle of five guards, and she throws fire: her Soul, 11 steps, and her ember stone's *Fire without a word* is 2 more, 13. She shapes them into a d12, a d10 and a d4, and the d4's her burst, going off around her. It rolls 2, but the guards are three squares out, so she pays 1 Aura and it's a 4. That's the d4's top face, but the +2 got it there, so there's no Aura for it. All five defend against her d12, her d10 goes at the door behind them, and the burst never touches her.

The curtains catch, and next round the GM telegraphs the fire spreading to the doorway, a d8 at whoever's in it. Ash walks out through the flames without a mark on her.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Fire](#k-sorcerer) · [Storm](#k-storm) · [Kinesis](#k-kinesis)

## Face {#a-face}

The Face gets into a threat's head, so its own attacks go where you say.

Spend your action and build your dice the usual way, but don't roll them. Put them on a threat as **influence**, and they stay until you use them or the scene ends. At any point before one of its telegraphs lands, roll your influence against it. Each of its dice that your best influence die beats is yours: say what it does instead, as long as it fits the story, like hitting one of its own or the ground. Then the influence is spent.

:::rule
**Trait:** *I Know a Guy*. Once a scene, say you know someone who could turn up here, and one true thing about them. They come into the scene, the GM plays them, and they help you however they can.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your influence dice.

**Turn them (1 Aura):** when your influence takes one of a Mook's or a Named threat's dice, the threat fights for the crew till the round ends. Pay again for another threat your influence took a die from.
:::

:::example[The bandit captain]
Dorrie's put her influence on the bandit captain: her Mind, 7 steps, which she's made a d8 and a d6. He telegraphs a d6 at Bram's Body and another at Wren's, and she rolls her influence against them: her best is a 5. They come up 3 at Bram and 6 at Wren. The 3's hers, so that cut lands on his own sergeant. She wants the other one too, so she pays 1 Aura to push her 5 to a 7, and the cut at Wren goes into the wall.
:::

:::example[Dorrie knows a guy]
The crew's papers are wrong and the harbour gate's shut for the night. Dorrie says she knows the clerk on the night shift, Tam, and that he owes her for keeping his brother out of the stocks. Tam comes down from the office with his lamp. He can't open the gate himself, but he knows which guard plays cards with the sergeant, and he'll vouch for her.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Face](#k-face) · [Courtier](#k-courtier) · [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Enchantment](#k-enchantment)

## Gambler {#a-gambler}

The Gambler turns a die over after it lands, whoever's it is.

**Once a round, flip a die.** When any die you can see lands, yours, a friend's or a threat's, you can flip it: its new number is its size plus 1, take away what it rolled. A 2 on a d10 becomes a 9, and a 9 becomes a 2. It costs no action. A flipped die never pays Aura, and it doesn't explode.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Bad Luck Pays*. Every die of yours that comes up 1 gives you 1 Aura, as long as it stays a 1.

**Another flip (1 Aura):** flip one more die this round. Pay again for another.

**Double or nothing (1 Aura):** flip every die in one roll, all at once.
:::

:::example[The guard's lucky night]
Bram's Defending against the guard captain's d10, and it comes up 9. You're the Gambler, so you flip it: 11 take away 9 is a 2, and Bram stops it with anything. Later in the round Vesk's d4 for keeping quiet comes up 1, so you pay 1 Aura and flip that too, and it's a 4.

Then your own d6 comes up 1 when you go at the guard. Turning it into a 6 would cost another Aura, so you leave it, and *Bad Luck Pays* gives you 1 Aura instead.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Guardian {#a-guardian}

The Guardian stands in front of the crew and takes what's coming at them.

You [intercept](#s-intercept) **for free**, for every friend within your stride. Any time before the telegraphs land, take any or all of the blows aimed at them, whichever enemy they're from, and they come at your Body instead. Fear and despair are inside their heads, so nobody can step in front of those, and keeping fear off the crew is a [Bard](#a-bard)'s job. When they land, your Body rolls against them the usual way, and since one die stops one, if you've pulled a lot onto yourself, you'll want to [Defend](#s-defend) as well. To get past you, threats have to pull the crew apart.

If you're a Teleporter as well, you intercept for every friend within 10 units (see [Teleporter](#a-teleporter)).

:::rule
**Trait:** *Better Me Than You*. Every blow you intercept for a friend gives you 1 Aura, whether it gets through to you or not.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your defence dice.

**Stand firm (1 Aura):** the next two hits that get through to you this round take 1 point between them, not 2. Pay again for two more.
:::

:::example[Three goblins]
Three goblins telegraph a d6 each at Vesk, Wren and Isolde, all within Bram's stride, and their chief's got a d8 aimed at Bram himself. He intercepts all three for free, and that's 3 Aura for *Better Me Than You* before anything's rolled. He's Defending, so when they land he's got his Body twice over, his Defend's 9 steps and the landing's 9, and he makes each of them a d12 and a d6. That's four dice for four blows. The goblins land 6, 4 and 1 and the chief 7, and Bram rolls 5 and 2 on his d12s and 4 and 3 on his d6s. The 4 holds the goblin's 4, because ties go to him, and the 2 holds the 1. That leaves the 6 and the 7, and his 5 and 3 can't stop either, so he pays 1 Aura for Stand firm, and the two of them take 1 point off his Body between them. Two of his dice won, so that's 2 more Aura. He isn't acting when it comes in, so whatever takes him past his maximum goes to the crew.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Guardian](#k-guardian) · [Warding](#k-wizard) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Healer {#a-healer}

The Healer patches people up in the middle of a scene, so they don't have to wait for the end of it.

Only a Healer can make a **heal roll**. It's an action: roll the Root that fits how you're healing, at full and never split, its dice filled to a d12 first, so 8 steps is a d12 and a d4. **Your patient gets back what it rolls**, spread across their Roots however they like, less 5 for each **Toll** they've got already, and never past what a Root can hold, or half that while it's wounded. Then it's 1 more Toll for them. Its top faces give you Aura the way any die's do, but what it heals never does. Nothing heals a [Scar](#s-blaze), and there's more on Toll in [Healing](#s-healing).

A heal reaches one person. Anyone can [quick heal](#s-healing) for 1 Aura, though.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Good Hands*. Your heals ignore a step of Toll: each one loses 5 less for the Toll your patient's got, so a heal from you on someone with 1 Toll is whole.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your dice when you heal.

**Pull them back (1 Aura):** when someone you can reach takes a Scar, they stay in the scene instead of leaving it. It costs no action. That Root's still wounded and on 0, so they'll want to keep their head down. Pay again for another.
:::

:::example[Healing Vesk]
Say you're a Healer, *The field surgeon of the Red Company*, and you heal with your Mind, 7 steps, so it's a d12 and a d2. Vesk's Body is down to 3 of the 10 it can hold, and her Mind to 2 of 5, and she's had one heal from you already this scene, so she's got 1 Toll. You roll 6 and 2, 8 in all. Her Toll would take 5 off that, but your *Good Hands* ignore a step of it, so she gets the whole 8. She puts 7 on her Body and 1 on her Mind, and now she's got 2 Toll. The d2 landed on its top face, so that's 1 Aura for you, and what you healed gets you nothing.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Healer](#k-healer) · [Mending](#k-mending) · [Faith](#k-faith)

## Hunter {#a-hunter}

The Hunter marks the prey, and everyone who goes at it rolls an extra die.

Spend your action and roll against a creature to mark it. Win and it's ***Marked***, a special condition carrying the die you won with. It doesn't give a step and nobody spends it. Instead, anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too, as one more die against it. It fades a size every round like any [condition](#s-conditions).

**The Mark jumps.** When a *Marked* creature falls, the Mark moves to another creature you can see, with the die it had.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Good Hunting*. Every die a friend wins against a creature you've *Marked*, going at it or stopping it, gives you 1 Aura as well as them.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to the Mark die when it's rolled. It's your Aura, whoever's rolling.

**Mark the pack (1 Aura):** your roll to mark goes at two more creatures you can see, and each one it beats is *Marked* with it. Pay again for two more.
:::

:::example[Marking the Warden]
Wren marks the Warden with an arrow: her Body and *Long shot*, 10 steps, a d12 and a d8. Her d12 wins, so it's *Marked d12*. Bram swings at it and rolls the Mark d12 with his own dice, and two of his dice beat it, so Bram takes 2 Aura and Wren takes 2 for *Good Hunting*. Next round it's *Marked d10*. When the Warden falls, the Mark jumps to the guard captain behind it, and he's *Marked d10*.

Wren's taken Hunter twice, so her Push puts +3 on the Mark die.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Ranger](#k-ranger) · [Investigator](#k-investigator)

## Illusionist {#a-illusionist}

The Illusionist makes threats believe in things that aren't there.

Spend your action and roll your illusion dice against the threats' Challenge. **Meet or beat it** and they believe your illusion until they interact with it or try to get through it: a clone of you for them to hit, a wall to run around, a crate to hide under. To see through it before then, a threat has to beat what you rolled. A tie's yours here, and you don't pay Aura for it.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Made You Look*. When a threat swings at one of your illusions, talks to it or tries to get through it, take 1 Aura.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your illusion dice.

**Make it real (1 Aura):** one illusion's real till the round ends. The wall stops them, and the clone hits back. Pay again for another round, or another illusion.
:::

:::example[A wall across the corridor]
Say you throw a wall across the corridor. Your d10 rolls 6 and the guards' d6 rolls 6, which is enough. They run the long way round, and if one stops to look hard at it, he has to beat your 6. If one of them puts his shoulder into it instead, he goes straight through, and that's 1 Aura for *Made You Look*.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Illusion](#k-illusion)

## Leader {#a-leader}

The Leader hands their own steps out to the crew.

Only a Leader leads. An order or a rallying song is an action: a Root at full and any you bring in, the same as any roll, and instead of rolling it you hand its steps out however you like to friends who can hear or see you. They add them to their own rolls this round. A lead can be a combo too, with friends putting their actions in before you hand it out.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Where It's Needed*. Each round, you can move steps around between your own roll and the rolls of friends who can hear or see you, up to 1 step for each of you, counting yourself. Take them off one roll and put them on another before either's rolled. It's free and takes no action, and nobody's roll goes below 1 step.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of a friend's dice, on a roll your steps went into.

**Everyone gets it (1 Aura):** every friend who can hear or see you gets all your steps, not a share.
:::

:::example[Orders from the doorway]
Bram barks orders from the doorway with his Soul, 4 steps, and he gives 2 to Vesk and 2 to Wren. Vesk's slipping past the guard on her Body, 11 steps, so with Bram's 2 she's on 13, two d12s and a d2. She rolls 6, 5 and 1 against the guard's 7, so Bram pays 1 Aura to push her 6 to an 8, and she's past him.
:::

:::example[Where it's needed]
Bram can see Vesk, Wren, Isolde and Dorrie, so with him that's five, and 5 steps he can move this round. Wren's about to put an arrow through the Warden's eye, and Bram's only holding the stair, so he takes 2 off his own Defend and 3 off Isolde's Study and puts all 5 on Wren's shot. Her 10 steps are 15 now, two d12s and a d6, and his Defend's down to 7.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Captain](#k-captain) · [Courtier](#k-courtier) · [Sailor](#k-sailor)

## Maker {#a-maker}

The Maker gives an item something extra for the scene.

Spend your action and roll against the item's size: a d4 for something tiny like a ring, a d6 for something small like a dagger, a d8 for something medium like a shield, a d10 for something large like armour, and a d12 for anything intricate. Each die that beats it gives the item a **tag for the scene**.

A tag works like a permission for whoever's holding the item: it lets them do something the item couldn't before, and it can justify bringing a Root in. A ring that lets you see in the dark is fine, and a ring that gives you haste isn't. Whoever's holding the item can break a tag instead of taking a hit.

**You can spend your Aura on a friend's awakening** when you can touch their attuned thing. Each Aura's 2 more steps on their roll, the same as if they'd spent it themselves (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). Nobody else can.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Maker's Mark*. One thing you carry, or something of a friend's you've worked on, has your mark on it: a tag of your own, like *Fire* on a sword or *Supercharged* on a wand. It works like a permission for whoever's holding it, and they can break it instead of taking a hit. You put it back on in downtime, on that or something else, for free.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your making dice.

**Masterwork (1 Aura):** the tags you make this time last till downtime.
:::

:::example[Vesk's coin]
Vesk hands you the first coin she ever stole, a d4. Your d8 rolls 6 against its 3, so the coin gets a tag for the scene, *turns a blade*, and you hand it back. When a blade gets through to her, she can break that tag instead of losing a point of her Body.

Your own mark's on Bram's shield, *catches blades*. When a sword gets through to him at the end of a round, he breaks your mark instead of losing a point of his Body, and you put it back on in downtime.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Tinker](#k-artificer) · [Artifice](#k-artifice)

## Oracle {#a-oracle}

The Oracle sees how the dice will fall, and decides who gets the good ones.

Spend your action and roll a Root. The numbers you rolled are your **foresight**. Until the round ends, whenever anyone rolls, friend or foe, you can swap one of your foresight numbers in for one of their dice, as long as it fits that die: a 7 can go on a d8 or bigger, but not on a d6. Each number goes once, and a number you swap in doesn't give anyone Aura or explode.

Your foresight's two numbers at most, however many steps you've got, so make them one die or two, and each friend who combos in gives you one more. Steps past six don't make more dice here, so steps a [Leader](#a-leader) hands you make your dice bigger, not more of them. Whatever's left when the round ends is gone.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Seen It Coming*. Each round, once the telegraphs are out and before anyone does anything else, you can roll up to 3 of the threats' dice: their telegraphs, or a threat's one roll for the round. They stay as they landed, face up, so the crew knows where to put its dice before anyone commits.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your foresight numbers, when you roll them.

**Hold it (1 Aura):** your foresight lasts till you use it or the scene ends.

**Foretold (1 Aura):** win a [Study](#s-study) and introduce a truth instead. It's true from now on, a tag on whatever it's about for the whole crew this scene.
:::

:::example[Seeing the brute's club]
Isolde's foresight comes off her Mind, 9 steps, and one action only ever gives her two numbers, so she makes it a d12 and a d6. They come up 9 and 1. Bram swings at the brute, and one of his dice rolls 2 against its 4, so she swaps her 9 in, and he's through. At the end of the round the brute's club comes up 7 at Wren, and Isolde swaps her 1 in, so Wren's Body stops it whatever it rolls.
:::

:::example[Isolde sees it coming]
The GM telegraphs the brute's club at Bram, a d10, and two guards' swords at Wren and Vesk, a d8 each. Before anyone moves, Isolde rolls all three. The club comes up 2, so Bram leaves it to his Body when it lands, since nearly any die of his stops a 2. The swords come up 7 and 8, so Wren and Vesk answer those instead of leaving them to their Bodies.
:::

:::example[The crack in the circle]
Isolde turns her Study on the Warden: her Mind, 9 steps, a d12 and a d6. Her d12 rolls 8 against its 6, so she's won, and she pays 1 Aura for Foretold. She says the chalk circle it's bound to has a crack in it. Now it does, *Cracked* on the circle for the whole crew this scene.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Divination](#k-divination) · [Warding](#k-wizard) · [Medium](#k-medium) · [Hexer](#k-warlock) · [Scholar](#k-scholar) · [Investigator](#k-investigator)

## Shadow {#a-shadow}

The Shadow notices things from where nobody's looking, and what they've seen is worth dice later.

When you get into hiding, the dice you rolled to hide go into your **shadow pool**. They're the things you noticed while nobody was looking. Add them to any roll after that, talking and Study included, as their own dice or as steps (a d8 is 4 steps). A die's gone once you use it, and the next time you hide, your pool starts again with the new dice.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Any Shadow Will Do*. Any patch of dark's somewhere you can hide. Everyone else needs something to get behind, like a corner or a pillar, and all you need's a shadow. You still roll to get into hiding.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to your roll to hide, or to a shadow die when you use it.

**Vanish (1 Aura):** you're hidden again, no roll, even in plain sight. Nothing goes in your pool, because you didn't roll. Pay again and a friend beside you vanishes too.
:::

:::example[Under the gallery]
The hall's lit up for the party, and the only dark's the shadow under the gallery. That's enough for Vesk. She slips into it on her Body, 11 steps, a d12 and a d10, and gets away with it, so both dice go in her pool. Later the steward's lying to her face, and from under there she saw him slip the letter into his coat. Her Mind's 5, so she takes the d12 from her pool as 6 steps, and that's 11: a d12 and a d10.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Rogue](#k-rogue) · [Face](#k-face) · [Streetwise](#k-streetwise) · [Veil](#k-veil)

## Shifter {#a-shifter}

The Shifter takes on what animals can do, whether that's wings or a breath attack.

You collect **animal tags**, the way a [Summoner](#a-summoner) catches souls. Spend your action, pick one and roll your Shifter dice against its die: most are a d4, swimming's a d6, flying's a d8 and a breath attack's a d12. Beat it and the tag's yours until downtime. It lets you do what the animal does, like a permission, so it can justify bringing a Root in too. Split to go for more than one, and strip one of your animal tags instead of taking a hit if you want to.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Something of the Beast*. At the start of every scene, pick one thing a beast has: a hawk's eyes, a hound's nose, a cat's ears, or being small enough to slip through a gap or big enough to fill a doorway. It's true of you till the scene ends, so you can do what it lets you do. It isn't an animal tag, so it can't take a hit for you.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your Shifter dice.

**Become it (1 Aura):** take any animal tag with no roll, even a breath attack. Pay again for another.
:::

:::example[Wings]
Say you want wings. Flying's a d8, and your d10 rolls 7 against its 5, so you've got *wings* as an animal tag until downtime. When an axe gets through, you can strip the wings instead of losing a point of your Body.

You started this scene with a hound's nose as well, so you can follow the smugglers through the rain by their smell, which nobody else in the crew can.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Wildcraft](#k-druid)

## Summoner {#a-summoner}

The Summoner catches the souls of fallen creatures and calls them back out when there's work to do.

Catching souls and summoning are the Summoner's, and nobody else can do them. Here's what you'll need at the table, and the rest is in [Summoning](#s-summoning).

-   **Catching a soul:** when a creature falls, spend your action and roll against its Challenge. Beat it and its soul goes into one of your utility slots. Miss and it's gone. In downtime, put it into your summoning attunement.
-   **Your souls live in your summoning attunement**, one to each awakening, on the same stages as any attuned thing (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). A soul's die is its Challenge die when you caught it. A soul's in an awakening's place, but it isn't steps, so it doesn't lock a point the way an awakening does.
-   **Calling one out** spends Aura, as part of an action: a d4 of summons for each Aura, built into one summon or several, and none of them bigger than that soul's own die. It needs a reason in the story.
-   **A soul is what it was.** A caught courtier talks and a hound tracks. Summons act in the round like anyone, fade a size every round, and lose a size to every hit that gets through. Their dice never pay Aura.
-   **A summon intercepts for free.** It can [intercept](#s-intercept) the blows aimed at someone within its stride, but not fear or despair, and it rolls its die to stop one of them.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Pocket Summon*. One of your souls is your pocket summon. Once a scene it comes out the moment you call it, at its own die, with no action and no Aura. Once it's gone, you can call it straight back the same way for the usual 1 Aura for each d4 of it. It still needs a reason in the story.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to one of your dice when you're catching a soul.

**Call the horde (1 Aura):** this time each Aura you spend calls up a d6, not a d4, still no bigger than the soul's own die. Pay again and it's a d8.
:::

:::example[Sable calls them out]
Sable's got three souls in her summoning attunement: a rat swarm (d4), a hound (d6) and the harbour Warden (d8), and the Warden's her pocket summon. The watch is coming down the alley, so the Warden's out at her side as a d8 before anyone's moved, with no action and no Aura. She's still got her action, so she spends 2 Aura on the rats, as two swarms of d4. Next round the Warden's a d6 and the rats are d2s, and the round after that, the rats are gone. When the Warden goes too, she can have it straight back for 2 Aura.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Medium](#k-medium)

## Teleporter {#a-teleporter}

The Teleporter jumps from spot to spot, and opens portals for the crew.

Your [stride](#s-movement) can be a teleport of up to 10 units. If you're a [Guardian](#a-guardian) as well, you intercept for every friend within those 10 units, because you jump to each of them.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Not There*. Once a scene, whenever you like, even as something's landing on you, teleport anywhere you can see within 10 units. It takes no action, and whatever was about to land on you misses. It's the one time moving gets you out of a telegraph aimed at you.

**Portal (1 Aura):** open a portal and link it to another spot within 10 units. It lasts the round, and anyone who steps in comes out the other end. Pay again for another.

**Swap (1 Aura):** swap places with anyone you can see, friend or foe. Pay again for another swap.
:::

:::example[Out through the portal]
Say you're the Teleporter, and Vesk's cornered on a balcony across the square, 8 units from the alley you're in. Your stride's a teleport, so you're beside her, and it hasn't cost your action. Then you pay 1 Aura and open a portal on the balcony, linked to the alley. It lasts the round, so Vesk steps in and comes out in the alley.

At the end of the round the sergeant's crossbow and a guard's spear both come at you. You teleport onto the fountain in the middle of the square, and both of them miss. That's your *Not There* for the scene.
:::

**Title ideas:** [Phase](#k-phase)

## Warrior {#a-warrior}

The Warrior's for fighting, with dice that don't stop at their top face.

Your dice **explode** when you fight. A die that lands on its top face gives you 1 Aura as usual, then you roll it again and add the new number on, the only time dice add apart from moving and what abilities add. It keeps going as long as it keeps landing on top, so a d8 that comes up 8, then 5, is a 13.

:::rule
**Trait:** *Second Skin*. Your armour fights with you even when you haven't Defended. When something lands at a Root and Defend wasn't your action, every piece of armour on that Root that isn't broken is 1 step more on your roll.

**Push (1 Aura):** +2 to any one of your dice in a fight. If that takes it to its top face or past, there's no Aura for it, but it still explodes.

**Opened (1 Aura):** when one of your dice wins in a fight, the target's *Opened* till the round ends. *Opened* is the Warrior's own [vulnerability](#s-vulnerable), so it stacks with fire, blades and the rest. Every hit on it ticks once more till the round ends, starting with the one that opened it. Two Warriors can each open the same target, and both count. Pay again to open another target your dice beat.
:::

:::example[The sellsword]
Say you're a Warrior, and your swing's your Body, 8 steps, which you keep as a d12 and a d4. You go at a sellsword whose d10 comes up 9. Your d12 lands on 7, so you pay 1 Aura and it's a 9, a tie, and you pay 1 Aura more to win it. A d12 that lands on 12 would have given you 1 Aura and rolled again, and so would your d4 on a 4.

You're in Fine mail and a Common helm, and next round you're swinging again, not Defending, when two blades come at your Body. When they land, your Body's 8 steps and your armour's 2 more, 10, and you make it two d10s, one for each blade.
:::
