## Quick Build {#s-quick}

:::quote
Ten minutes, and you're playing.
:::

The quick build is the long build with most of the thinking done for you. You pick two [Title ideas](#s-title-ideas) and take what you need from them, and you bring the parts that make the character yours: the Titles, the people and what you want. Each step says where it's explained in full in Building Your Character.

1.  **Pick two Title ideas, and write a Title for each.** Two roles, two schools of magic, or one of each. Take one of the example Titles or write your own, and make it specific: *the best blade in the city* is fine, and *the best blade in the Low Market* is better. See [Titles](#s-title).
2.  **Take a trait and three permissions from each.** That's two traits and six permissions. Change the words to suit your character, or write one that isn't on the list. That's fine as long as every permission's still finished, with a doing word and how, what or where. See [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions).
3.  **Fill in your set actions.** Fight, Move, Sneak, Study and Talk are on every sheet, and if there's something you do all the time, give it a set action of its own. Put your Titles, permissions, items and traits on every set action they fit nearly every time. A set action's steps are 1 for the action and 1 for every line on it. See [Set actions](#s-step-set-actions).
4.  **Take what you carry, and attune two things.** You carry 3 equipment, 3 utility items and a keepsake, and your bag holds 10 more. Your two attuned things start at Awakened with one permission each. If there's trouble you'd like on one, give it a Catch and it gets a second permission. See [What you carry](#s-carrying) and [Attunement](#s-attunement).
5.  **Write three connections**: one each to two of the crew, and one to someone outside it. If one's a debt, you've got room for a fourth. See [Bonds](#s-bonds).
6.  **Write your Aspiration** at d2. It's one thing you want, and it climbs as you chase it. See [Aspiration](#s-aspiration).
7.  **Check the numbers.** You've got 5 Aura, two Sparks, a stride of 3 and three Hurt bars. Armour starts at d2 and goes up a size for each piece of plain armour you wear. Confidence is d6: it's d2 and a size bigger for each Title, and clothes that fit the scene or a trait like Iron Will can take it higher.

:::example[Wren, the quick way]
Wren's built from the Ranger twice, and both her Titles are examples off its list: *the hunter who has never lost a trail* and *an outrider from the border war*. Her first Title has Marksman's Eye and three permissions from the list, *follow any trail*, *move through the wild unseen* and *know a beast's habits*. Her second has Sure-Footed and three permissions that aren't on the list at all: *scout ahead of an army*, *ride hard over rough country* and *read the land for an ambush*.

Her Track is her first Title, *follow any trail* and *know a beast's habits*, so with the action that's 4 steps, a d8. She attunes her longbow with *shoot a longbow*, and gives it the Catch *Wanted* for a second permission, *hit anything I can see*. Both go on her Fight with her first Title and Marksman's Eye, and that's 5 steps, a d10. Her whole sheet's [further down](#s-pregens).
:::

A quick-built character is every bit as real as one built the long way, and everything about it grows the same way. In more of a hurry than that? Take one of the [six ready-made characters](#s-pregens) and play.

## Title Ideas {#s-title-ideas}

Every Title is a role or a school of magic, so here are fourteen roles and fifteen schools to build from, the same ones the Player Companion app offers. Each one has example Titles, permissions, traits that suit it, and ideas for going beyond. Take what fits, change the words so they sound like your character, or use them to get started on something of your own. They're just as good for a Title you earn later, which starts with a trait and one permission.

A Title idea isn't a Title, though. *Warrior* doesn't tell the table what you're the best at, so a Warrior still needs a Title that does, like *the best blade in the city*. Keep each Title to one role or one school. *Archmage* is too wide, so pick the school you're known for.

-   **Permissions** are ready to copy onto your sheet. Every one's finished, with a doing word and how, what or where, so they're also a good guide when you write your own (see [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions)).
-   **Traits** are three that suit the idea. Take one of them, or any trait you like from [the full list](#s-traits).
-   **Going beyond** is what this kind of character does when they push a permission past what it normally does. It costs 1 Aura (see [Going Beyond](#s-going-beyond)).
-   **Trouble** is the kind that follows this sort of character around. Titles don't have Catches, so it isn't part of the Title, and you can bring it into a scene whenever you like. Let it cause real trouble and you take 1 Aura (see [Aura](#s-aura)). Any of it makes a good Catch for something you [attune](#s-attunement), too.

If what you want to play isn't here, it's probably two of these put together, or one of them with a permission of your own.

### Schools of Magic {#s-idea-schools}

A school is the one kind of magic you're known for. You need a magic permission or a magic item to do magic at all, and once you've got one, it does what it says for free, the same as a sword. Raising the dead to fight or carry something as part of your own action is free too. Anything that works on its own, like a summoned beast or a raised servant acting for you, is summoning, and that needs a summoning attunement (see [Summoning](#s-summoning)). There's more on what magic looks like out in the world in [Magic](#s-schools).

## Six Ready-Made Characters {#s-pregens}

:::quote
Pick one and play tonight.
:::

These are the same six you can choose in the Player Companion app. Take one as written, or copy one and change anything you like. They're the crew as they start out. The examples elsewhere in this book sometimes show them later in their careers, with more Titles, more permissions and new tricks.

Each of them is built from two [Title ideas](#s-title-ideas), with a few permissions of their own. Their set actions are already counted. The number's the steps, then the die, then what's on it, and the action itself is the 1 that isn't written down: Vesk's Sneak is her first Title, *move unseen* and Silent Step, plus the action, so it's 4 steps, a d8.

They've each got two attuned things, both at Awakened, and Wren's longbow and Bram's war hammer have each taken a Catch for a second permission. Everyone's got room for more in what they carry, so pick things up in play, or fill the gaps from [What you carry](#s-carrying) before you start.

The last line on each sheet is the trouble that follows them. Bring it into a scene and let it bite, and that's 1 Aura back, the same as a Catch.
