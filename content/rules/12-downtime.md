:::quote
Downtime's the half of the game where the world decides what it's been doing while you were bleeding in a warehouse.
:::

After **every session** there's a downtime: a week, a month, a season, however long the story needs. It's where the world moves, and where you heal and grow.

:::rule
**Progression in Zi'Dara is yours.** Nobody hands out levels, and there's no experience to count. Every downtime, you choose what your character gets: a new permission, a new trait, more from something you've attuned, a roll at your Aspiration, a new thread. What you don't get to choose is how much time you have, and the world's always asking for some of it.
:::

## The Four Pressures {#the-four-pressures}

Every downtime, four pressures land on the party, and the GM names them before anyone spends a slot. The faction pressure comes from the faction turn: the GM telegraphs what every faction's doing, and the one that matters most to the crew is the pressure (see [Factions](#s-faction-turn)).

-   **A personal pressure.** Something on one character's sheet, like a hostile thread or a bond.
-   **A party pressure.** The shared Goal.
-   **A faction pressure.** One faction on the board is moving.
-   **A world pressure.** A world event.

If you ignore a pressure, it doesn't go away. It gets worse, and the GM brings it to the next session. Ignore it twice and it becomes a scene.

## Your Slots {#your-slots}

Every player gets **two personal slots**, or three with *Tireless*. The crew gets **one action**, the same as every faction on the board, and nothing buys it another. Put one of your personal slots into the crew's action and everything counts twice. A personal slot can also chase the Goal, work a project or support an Aspiration on its own, but it can never be a second move on a faction.

Two things happen without costing a slot: when downtime's over, all your [Resolve](#s-resolve) comes back and your [wound die](#s-blaze) goes back to d2, or to your Scars. A trait that fires every downtime, like *Host* or *Cache*, does it by itself.

## What You Can Do With a Slot {#what-you-can-do-with-a-slot}

### Face a pressure {#face-a-pressure}

-   **Deal with it.** Pay the debt, answer the letter, lie low, meet the man. One slot usually settles a pressure, and the GM says if it needs two. If something pushes back, roll for it.

### Recover {#recover}

-   **Mend something you carry that's broken**, usually for a purse of gold as well. Armour and shields are the usual ones, after they've taken a blow for you. Attuned things can't break.

Your wounds don't need a slot. They clear when downtime's over (see [Your Slots](#your-slots)).

### Grow {#grow}

-   **A new permission** on one of your Titles, up to six on it. It has to fit inside the Title, and the story should say where you learned it. Put it on the set actions it fits nearly every time, two at most, and Defend counts as one. See [Permissions](#s-writing-permissions).
-   **A new trait**, either from the list or written in the same shape. Like every trait, it never goes on a set action.
-   **Chase your Aspiration.** Roll against its die, built from whatever you're doing about it. Beat it and it climbs one. See [Aspiration](#s-aspiration).
-   **Deepen a bond.** Write a new thread with someone, a bond thread or a hostile one, whichever the story's given you. If you've already got a bond with them, it goes under that one. If they're in the crew, it goes on both your sheets, so agree on it together.
-   **Get something** the story can give you, whether it's bought, made, won or owed: something to carry, a purse of gold, or the material an attuned thing needs for its next stage.

**Swapping.** In downtime you can also swap any of your permissions for others that fit your Title, as long as it makes sense for your character. Two permissions on one Title can go for a Boon the same way, and a Boon can go back for two (see [Boons and Burdens](#s-boons)). Wren's crew works the city now, so she swaps *ride hard over rough country* for *scout a building before we go in*.

### Attunement {#s-downtime-attunement}

Every new permission on an attuned thing takes a slot, up to what its stage allows. The thing can't go up a stage until you've got its material, found in the story or bought (see [Attunement](#s-attunement)). If you're a Summoner, this is when the souls you've caught go into your summoning attunement, if there's room (see [Summoning](#s-summoning)).

### The crew's action {#party-slots}

The crew's action is its one move a downtime, and it can be any of these. When someone puts a personal slot into it as well, that's two slots on one thing, and everything counts twice.

-   **Chase the Goal.** Whoever does it rolls against the Goal's die: 1 step for the action, plus everything of theirs that fits. Beat it and it climbs one. See [The Party Goal](#s-goal).
-   **Work a project.** The same, against the project's die. See [Projects](#s-projects).
-   **Move on a faction.** Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire or Establish, set back its agenda, or work for it. See [Factions](#s-faction-crew).
-   **Rebuild.** Establish an asset or a resource the crew's lost. An asset still needs a resource paying for it. See [How the crew grows](#s-crew-grows).
-   **Advance a world event.** Push something along, or hold it back.
-   **Support an Aspiration.** One more roll at someone's Aspiration, built by whoever's helping.

A personal slot that chases the Goal, works a project or supports an Aspiration on its own rolls the same way, with 1 step for the slot. Something that climbs still only climbs one step a roll, however many slots went into it.

### Rolling in downtime {#s-downtime-rolls}

Most slots just happen. When something pushes back (the fence wants his money, or the Guild's watching the smithy), roll for it like any Challenge.

-   **Ties go to the defender**, every time. A die you're climbing counts as the defender.
-   **There's no Aura in downtime**, so you can't pay to win a tie. You've got two other ways out: spend 1 Resolve to roll again (your dice only), or spend one of the crew's extra resources and take the tie. It's the crew's money, so ask the table first.
-   **Only the crew can do that.** A faction never rerolls, and never buys a tie.

## Mending Threads {#s-downtime-threads}

Downtime's one of the two places to **mend a strained bond thread**. The other's a scene with them, as long as it isn't the fight where you strained it. Make it right with them, whatever suits them: do them a favour, pay them, or just talk it through. Play it out at the table, and the thread's whole again. If it's a thread with someone in the crew, their player says what would make it right (see [Bonds and threads](#s-bonds) and [Playing the strain](#s-playing-the-strain)).

A hostile thread goes the other way round. Doing right by them is what strains it, and doing right by them once more, in downtime or a scene, snaps it, and the grudge is over.

:::example
Vesk's only thread with Bram is a bond thread: the fire he carried her out of, and the thank-you she never said. She strained it on a wall last session, yelling for him to haul her up again, so he's been cold with her since. In downtime she finds him at the bar and finally says it. The thread's mended, and she rewrites it now that it's said.
:::

## A Full Week, Played Out {#a-full-week-played-out}

:::example
**GM:** First up, *personal*. Vesk, your debt to the fence has been called. He wants his money by the end of the week, or he'll talk to the Guild.

**GM:** Second, *party*. The vault score. Vane's hired investigators, and they've been seen in the district.

**GM:** Third, *faction*. The Vane Estate's working *Vane comes home*. It's at d10, and the Estate's throwing its whole d12 at it this week.

**GM:** Fourth, *world*. There's a festival on the harbour this week, so the law's going to be everywhere.
:::

**Triage.**

:::example
**Vesk:** One slot pays the fence what he's asking for this week. The other gives my grappling line its Catch, *Loud*, because the hook rings off stone every time, and *haul someone up after me* with it.

**Dorrie:** One slot goes on the vault score. The other's a new permission, *tell people what they want to hear*. I've been talking my way past too many doormen not to have learned something.

**Isolde:** I'll put *read how a spirit's been bound* on my first Title. I've spent the week with the Warden's empty armour. My other slot goes on my Aspiration, because whoever bound that Warden knew my order's work.

**Bram:** The Warden's spear went through my shield, so one slot mends it. My ribs'll knit on their own, so my other slot goes in with the crew's action. We're going at the Estate, and it's too big for the action on its own.
:::

**Resolution.** Vesk's slot pays the fence what he asked for. That's doing right by him, so her hostile thread with him, *I owe a debt I would rather not discuss*, is strained now, and one more good turn would snap it. *Haul someone up after me* won't fit her Move nearly every time, so she'll bring it in for 1 Aura when it does.

Dorrie's slot is a roll at the Goal: her first Title, *read a room in a glance*, *make a lie sound like old news* and the slot make 4 steps, a d8, against the Goal's d4. It's 6 against 3, so the Goal climbs to d6. Her new permission fits her Talk nearly every time, so her Talk goes from 4 steps to 5, a d10.

Isolde's Aspiration roll is her second Title, *know the history of anything old*, *find what I need in any library* and the slot, a d8 against its d6. It's 5 against 5, and the tie goes to the Aspiration. There's no Aura in downtime, so she spends 1 Resolve to roll again, gets 7, and it climbs to d8.

Bram's slot mends his shield. His wound doesn't need one: he finished last session on a d6, and it's back to d2 when downtime's over.

The Estate's Power is a d12 and the crew's is a d4, so the crew needs its action and a personal slot on the same move to go at it. Bram's other slot makes it two, and two slots count everything twice: the crew's d4 becomes a d8. They Obstruct, so the d8 has to beat the Estate's d12. The crew rolls 5 and the d12 rolls 9, so the action goes ahead: 9 against the agenda's d10, which rolls 4, and *Vane comes home* climbs to d12. And nobody touched the festival, so next session the law will be on every street.
