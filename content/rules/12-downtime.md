:::quote
Downtime's the half of the game where the world decides what it's been doing while you were bleeding in a warehouse.
:::

After **every session** there's a downtime: a week, a month, a season, however long the story needs. It's where the world moves, and where you heal and grow.

:::rule
**Progression in Zi is yours.** Nobody hands out levels, and there's no experience to count. Every downtime, you choose what your character gets: a better ability, a new one, a new trait, a roll at your Aspiration, a new connection. What you don't get to choose is how much time you have, and the world's always asking for some of it.
:::

## The Four Pressures {#the-four-pressures}

Every downtime, four pressures land on the party, and the GM names them before anyone spends a slot. The faction pressure comes from the faction turn (see [Factions](#s-faction-turn)).

-   **A personal pressure.** Something on one character's sheet, like a debt, a bond, or a Catch that's *Wanted*.
-   **A party pressure.** The shared Goal.
-   **A faction pressure.** One faction on the board is moving.
-   **A world pressure.** A world event.

If you ignore a pressure, it sticks around and gets worse, and the GM brings it to the next session. Ignore it twice and it becomes a scene. A pressure never pays a Spark. Trouble that starts in downtime lands next session, and that's when the GM offers one.

## Your Slots {#your-slots}

Every player gets **two personal slots**, and the crew shares **two party slots**. A player can put one of their own slots toward a party action.

## What You Can Do With a Slot {#what-you-can-do-with-a-slot}

### Face a pressure {#face-a-pressure}

-   **Deal with it.** Pay the debt, answer the letter, lie low, meet the man. One slot usually settles a pressure, and the GM says if it needs two. If something pushes back, roll for it.

### Recover {#recover}

-   **Heal a Hurt**, one box per slot.
-   **Recover all your Confidence.** This takes both slots, because you spend the whole downtime finding yourself.
-   **Step your Armour back to full** at a smith, an armoury, or with a patient friend and a needle and thread.
-   **Tend the crew.** If you have a healing ability (*Medic*, *Mending*, *Faith* with *healing prayers*), one slot heals one Hurt on every member of the crew who wants it, yourself included.
-   **Mend a broken item.**

### Grow {#grow}

-   **Raise an ability a tier.** Novice to Skilled takes one slot, and Skilled to Master takes both. Each tier adds a step and a specialization.
-   **Earn a new ability** at Novice, with one specialization. The story should say where you learned it.
-   **Learn a new trait**, either from the list or written in the same shape.
-   **Chase your Aspiration.** Roll against its die, built from whatever you're doing about it. Beat it and it climbs one. See [Step 8](#s-aspiration).
-   **Deepen a bond.** Write a new connection with someone.
-   **Acquire something**, an item or resource the story can give you, whether it's bought, made, won or owed.

Two things happen in downtime without costing a slot: a Scar you took gets written as a permission or a passive, and once-per-downtime traits refresh.

### Party slots {#party-slots}

A party slot is an action for the crew. Two slots on the same thing count everything twice, the same as two actions.

-   **Chase the Goal.** Whoever does it rolls against the Goal's die: 1 step for the slot, plus their permissions that fit. Beat it and it climbs one. See [The Party Goal](#s-goal).
-   **Work a project.** The same, against the project's die. See [Projects](#s-projects).
-   **Move on a faction.** Interfere with it, strike at it, deal with it or work for it. See [Factions](#s-faction-crew).
-   **Rebuild.** Step the crew's Power or Wealth back up one, never past its best.
-   **Advance a world event.** Push something along, or hold it back.
-   **Support an Aspiration.** One more roll at someone's Aspiration, built by whoever's helping.

Something that climbs still only climbs one step a roll, however many slots went into it.

### Rolling in downtime {#s-downtime-rolls}

Most slots just happen. When something pushes back (the fence wants his money, or the Guild's watching the smithy), roll for it like any Challenge.

-   **Ties go to the defender**, every time. A die you're climbing counts as the defender.
-   **There's no Aura in downtime**, so you can't pay to win a tie. You've got two other ways out: spend your Spark to roll again (your dice only), or step the crew's Wealth down one and take the tie. It's the crew's money, so ask the table first.
-   **Only the crew can do that.** A faction never rerolls, and never buys a tie.

## A Full Week, Played Out {#a-full-week-played-out}

:::example
**GM:** First up, *personal*. Vesk, your debt to the fence has been called. He wants his money by the end of the week, or he talks to the guild.

**GM:** Second, *party*. The vault score. Vane's hired investigators, and they've been seen in the district.

**GM:** Third, *faction*. The Vane Estate is on the move. Its agenda's at d10, and it's going to push it this week.

**GM:** Fourth, *world*. There's a festival on the harbour this week, so the law's going to be everywhere. Ignore it twice and it becomes a scene.
:::

**Triage.**

:::example
**Vesk:** I'll put both slots into paying off the fence. It has to be clean.

**Dorrie:** I'll put one slot into the vault score as a party action. With my other one, I'll raise *Influence* to Skilled. I've been talking my way past too many doormen not to have learned something.

**Isolde:** I'll earn a new ability, *Artifice*, at Novice. I spent the week with the Warden's empty armour. And my other slot goes on my Aspiration, because whoever bound that Warden knew my order's work.

**Bram:** Both party slots go against the Estate. Mine heal my Hurt and get my plate back to full.
:::

**Resolution.** Vesk pays off the fence (a d8 roll against his d6, and she beats it). Dorrie's slot on the vault score is a roll at the Goal: *Courtier*, *gossip*, her Title and the slot make four steps, a d8, against the Goal's d4. It's 6 against 3, so the Goal climbs to d6, and her other slot takes *Influence* to Skilled. Isolde learns her new ability, and her other slot is a roll at her Aspiration: *Scholar*, *forbidden texts*, her Title and the slot, a d8 against its d6. It's 5 against 5. There's no Aura in downtime, so she spends her Spark to roll again, gets 7, and her Aspiration climbs to d8.

Bram's own slots heal his Hurt and put his plate back to full. Both party slots go against the Estate. Its Power's a d12 and the crew's is a d4, so it takes both party slots to roll against its Power, and that counts everything twice: the crew's d4 becomes a d8, rolled against the Estate's Power and its agenda at once. The crew rolls 5. The Power rolls 8 and the agenda rolls 6, so they miss both, and nothing happens. The Estate's move goes ahead: its Power rolls 9 against its own agenda's d10, which rolls 4, so *Vane comes home* climbs to d12. And nobody touched the festival, so next session the law will be on every street.
