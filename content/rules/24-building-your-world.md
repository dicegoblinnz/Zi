:::quote
You build everything else with the same tools you used to make your character.
:::

Everything's built the same way: a few plain sentences, something it wants and a secret. Roll on the tables below, or just read down them for ideas. Factions get their own chapter (see [Factions](#factions)).

## Regions {#regions}

Start with three questions:

-   **What holds it together, and what's pulling it apart?**
-   **Who runs it, really?**
-   **What does a stranger notice first?**
