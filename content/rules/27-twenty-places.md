:::quote
Twenty places you can run tonight, with the people and the secrets already in them.
:::

These drop into any campaign and any kind of world, so rename them, move them and change whatever doesn't fit. None of them needs the Steeplands, but they'll all sit happily there.

To use one, read the entry, pick the stake that matters to your crew, and put the scene clock on the table. Then let the secret come out through Study, through asking the right person, and through paying attention to what's out of place. Every secret has at least two ways in, so if the crew misses one, they'll find another.

-   **The place has a Challenge**, in brackets at the end of its first line, the same as a [site](#s-site). It's what you roll against to Study the place, and it's the die the place telegraphs with when the floor gives way or the alarm goes up.
-   **Who's here** gives everyone a Challenge and the tags that matter most, and they're all in the [Tag Reference](#appendix-tags). People telegraph with their own Challenge dice.
-   **Telegraphs** are a starting point, so add your own. They come out of your [round budget](#s-budget) like any others. One aimed at a stake has no defence, and if nobody answers it, it does what it said, which usually means a new tag in the way. One aimed at the scene clock steps it down one if it lands, on top of its usual step at the end of the round.
-   **Every place has something for everyone**: a fight, someone to talk round, somewhere to sneak, something to work out and something for magic. Check your crew's sheets and point those at the right people (see [A moment for each character](#s-spotlight)).
-   **Elsewhere** notes say how six of them would look in a sea, horror, court or science-fiction game.

## The Copper Baths {#s-place-copper-baths}

:::place
A bathhouse where the city's deals get made (Challenge 3).

Steam rolls out when the door opens, smelling of hot stone and cedar oil. Copper pipes tick, and voices stay low, because everyone's doing business in a towel. In the hot room at the back, there's a moneylender waiting to sell to whoever offers most.

**Who's here** *Orla Strand*, who owns the baths (Challenge 4: *Well-Connected*, *Proud*) · *Casimir Dace*, the moneylender (Challenge 4: *Deep Pockets*, *Cowardly*) · four bath-men (Challenge 3: *Heavy*) · *Old Tamsin*, who stokes the furnace (Challenge 2: *Overlooked*)

**The ground** *Steam*: you can't see past arm's length · *The House Rule*: no blades past the door, and whoever brings one in has broken the peace · *The Hot Pool*: shove someone in and they'll come out pink and gasping

**Stakes** *The deal*, Challenge 4: *Someone Must Agree*, *Wanted By Others* (Dace signs with the crew, or with someone else) · *Dace's satchel*, Challenge 3: *Locked*, *Guarded* (it's in the changing-room cupboards)

**Scene clock** d8: Dace's other buyer arrives, and he signs with them.

**Telegraphs** *Orla raps her cane on the tiles: "Not in my house."* (d8, Confidence) · *A bath-man takes whoever's making trouble by the collar and walks them to the door.* (d6, Armour, Grabbed) · *Dace grabs his towel and heads for the changing room.* (d8, the deal)

**Hooks** Someone the crew needs won't meet anywhere but on neutral ground · Dace holds a debt that'll sink a friend, and he's selling it today

**Elsewhere** In a court game it's the queen's bathing house, and the room over the furnace belongs to her spymaster.

**Secret** *Every Word Is Sold*. The grilles in the hot room carry every word up a pipe to a cupboard over the furnace, where Orla's clerk writes it down for her to sell. Nobody's ever made a private deal in here. Tamsin knows, because she's heard the pen scratching over her head for years. Study the hot room and the grilles are newer than the tiles, and warm on the wrong side. Once the crew knows, the pipe works both ways, and a lie or a voice made with magic goes straight into Orla's book.
:::

## The Teeth Light {#s-place-teeth-light}

:::place
A lighthouse on a rock off the reef they call the Teeth (Challenge 4).

A white tower stands on a black rock, reached by a causeway at low tide and a boat the rest of the time. The wind never drops, and the whole place smells of whale oil. There's still wreckage on the rocks from the last ship that didn't make it.

**Who's here** *Silas Crewe*, the keeper (Challenge 4: *Fortified*, *Protects Someone*) · his sons *Abe* and *Lem* (Challenge 3: *Trained*), though Lem *Wants Out* · *Tam Ollery*, who survived the last wreck, and he's hiding in the oil store (Challenge 2: *Scared*)

**The ground** *The Spiral*: ninety-nine iron steps, and whoever holds the top holds the tower · *Wind*: anything loose goes, and arrows won't fly straight · *The Lens*: the light goes wherever it points

**Stakes** *The ship in the bay*, Challenge 4: *Far Away*, *Storm* (get her off the Teeth before she strikes) · *Tam Ollery*, Challenge 3: *Hidden Away*, *Alive and Scared* (find him before the Crewes do)

**Scene clock** d8: the ship strikes the Teeth.

**Telegraphs** *Abe comes up the spiral and reaches for you with his gaff hook.* (d6, Armour, Grabbed) · *Crewe swings the lamp's shutter closed.* (d8, the ship) · *A wave breaks right over the gallery.* (d8, everyone on the gallery, Armour, Staggered)

**Hooks** A ship the crew cares about is due in tonight · Crewe's selling ship's goods in town, and someone wants to know where he gets them

**Elsewhere** In a science-fiction game it's a beacon in an asteroid field, and the keeper's been moving the signal.

**Secret** *The Light's Been Moved*. The Crewes have shifted the lens so the beam leads ships onto the Teeth, and they live off whatever washes up. Tam saw the light swing the night his ship went down. Study the lamp room and there's fresh scraping where the mount's been moved. In the cottage, a captain's sea chest is doing duty as a table. Put the lens back, or make a light of your own, and they'll see the reef in time. Lem's sick of it, and he'll help anyone who promises he won't hang.
:::

## The Pledge House {#s-place-pledge-house}

:::place
A pawnbroker's where half the street has something on the shelves (Challenge 3).

It's a narrow shop, shelved to the ceiling with ticketed pledges, from wedding rings to a soldier's medal. It smells of camphor, and under that, faintly, of something sweet. Behind an iron grille, the clerk tells everyone the master's ill upstairs and won't see anyone.

**Who's here** *Jessamy Cole*, the clerk (Challenge 3: *Slippery*, *Scared*) · the Rudd brothers, the master's nephews, who collect for him (Challenge 3: *Heavy*, *Can Be Bought*) · *Mister Quill*, who's come to collect from the master (Challenge 3, the Bestiary's Debt Collector)

**The ground** *The Grille*: an iron cage round the counter, and you can't reach whoever's behind it, only talk to them · *Ticket Shelves*: pull one down and the whole wall comes with it · *The Back Stair*: narrow and creaking, with the strongroom door underneath

**Stakes** *The pledge*, Challenge 3: *Locked*, *Paperwork* (it's in the strongroom, and the ticket's gone) · *The ledger*, Challenge 3: *Behind the Grille*, *Written in Code* (who owes the house, and how much)

**Scene clock** d8: Quill stops waiting and goes upstairs to see the master.

**Telegraphs** *Jessamy, through the grille: "No ticket, no pledge."* (d6, Confidence) · *The elder Rudd grabs whoever's nearest the stair and puts them through the shelves.* (d6, Armour, Prone) · *Jessamy tips the ledger toward the stove.* (d6, the ledger) · *Quill straightens his coat and puts a foot on the bottom stair.* (d6, the scene clock)

**Hooks** Someone the crew cares about pledged something they need back · Quill's employer wants to know why the master's stopped paying

**Secret** *The Master's Been Dead a Month*. Abel Mossop died in his chair upstairs four weeks ago. Once anyone finds out, the Rudds inherit and sell every pledge, so Jessamy's kept the shop open in his name, giving the street its things back for a copper each. She'll go still the moment anyone nears the stair. Study the shop and the ledger's been in her hand alone for a month, and his signature's a copy. Study the stair, or feel for the dead, and the smell's coming from the room at the top. Once the crew knows, she'll help anyone who keeps it quiet.
:::

## Kestrel Lodge {#s-place-kestrel-lodge}

:::place
A lord's hunting lodge, full of guests the night before the hunt (Challenge 4).

It's a long timber hall with antlers on every beam and a fire you could roast an ox in. Hounds sleep under the tables, it smells of woodsmoke and wet dog, and the guests in hunting green have been drinking since breakfast. Out the back are the kennels and the hawks, and past them, the forest.

**Who's here** *Lord Edric Hawley*, the host (Challenge 5: *Well-Connected*, *Proud*) · *Piers Hawley*, his nephew and heir (Challenge 3: *Charming*) · *Garrow*, the huntsman (Challenge 4: *Knows the Territory*, *Loyal to a Name*) · the hounds (Challenge 2: *Fast*, *Bites*) · a dozen guests (Challenge 3: *Bored*)

**The ground** *The Great Fire*: a hearth big enough to stand in · *Trophies*: boar spears on every wall, so nobody's unarmed for long · *Hounds*: under every table, and they'll wake up angry · *The Forest*: Garrow knows it, and you don't

**Stakes** *Hawley's ear*, Challenge 5: *Someone Must Agree*, *An Audience* (whatever the crew came to ask him for) · *Hawley's letters*, Challenge 4: *Locked*, *Watched* (in his rooms upstairs, with a guest on every landing)

**Scene clock** d10: dawn, when the horns go up and the hunt rides out.

**Telegraphs** *Hawley lifts his glass: "To our guests, who'll be riding with us in the morning."* (d10, Confidence) · *A hound wakes under the table and goes for whoever's moving.* (d4, Armour) · *Garrow steps out of the shadows at the foot of the stairs.* (d8, the letters)

**Hooks** The crew needs something from Hawley, and this is the only place he'll see them · Piers wrote asking the crew to come, and wouldn't say why

**Secret** *The Hunt's for Piers*. Hawley's arranged an accident for his nephew tomorrow: a crossbow waiting in a gully, and a horse that's been badly shod on purpose. With Piers dead, the estate's his for life. Garrow knows, and it's eating him, because he served Piers's father. Study the stables and Piers's horse has a shoe somebody's loosened. Anyone who can talk to beasts can ask a hawk where the crossbow's waiting. Anyone who reads Hawley sees he can't look his nephew in the eye.
:::

## The Gilt Rooms {#s-place-gilt-rooms}

:::place
An auction house where old families sell what they can't afford to keep (Challenge 4).

Rows of gilt chairs face a rostrum under a glass roof, and every painting on the walls has a lot number on it. It smells of beeswax and perfume, and the talk's all quiet laughter from people who knew each other's grandparents. The thing the crew wants is lot 31.

**Who's here** *Lucan Fairweather*, the auctioneer (Challenge 4: *Silver-Tongued*, *Well-Connected*) · *Dame Hester Quell*, the richest bidder in the room (Challenge 5: *Deep Pockets*, *Proud*) · six porters (Challenge 2: *Overlooked*) · a guard on every door (Challenge 3: *Trained*)

**The ground** *An Audience*: every bid's public, and so is every scene · *The Rostrum*: whoever holds the hammer holds the room · *The Lot Room*: crates and porters behind a velvet curtain · *The Glass Roof*: it's on hinges

**Stakes** *Lot 31*, Challenge 4: *Wanted By Others*, *Guarded* (win the bidding, or get it out of the lot room first) · *The consignment book*, Challenge 4: *Locked*, *Watched* (who sold lot 31, and when)

**Scene clock** d8: lot 31 comes up, and the hammer falls.

**Telegraphs** *Fairweather points his hammer at you: "Is that a bid, or a cough?"* (d8, Confidence) · *Dame Hester lifts her paddle without looking round.* (d10, lot 31) · *A guard comes down the aisle and takes your elbow.* (d6, Armour, Grabbed)

**Hooks** Lot 31 was stolen from someone the crew knows · A client wants it bought quietly, and won't go over a certain price

**Secret** *Stolen to Order*. Fairweather takes commissions: a rich client names a thing, he has it stolen, and a month later it's in his catalogue with a clean history. Lot 31 was stolen for Dame Hester, and the bidding's a show. She's bored, because she's already paid, and anyone who reads people sees it. One of the porters hates Fairweather enough to say which lots came in the back door at night. Study the catalogue and you'll find it was printed the week before lot 31 went missing. Magic that sees the past shows the night it was taken. Proof strips *Wanted By Others*, because Hester won't be seen bidding on a scandal.
:::

## The Breakers' Flats {#s-place-breakers-flats}

:::place
A mudflat where old ships are broken up, walkable only at low tide (Challenge 3).

Twenty hulks lie on their sides in grey mud with their ribs showing, and gulls scream on every mast stump. It smells of rot and tar. At low tide it's a mile of mud out to the sea, and folk on shore say lights move inside the hulks at night.

**Who's here** *Orrin Slade*, the foreman (Challenge 3: *Heavy*, *Greedy*) · eight breakers with axes (Challenge 2: *One of Many*) · *Nance*, a mudlark who knows every hulk (Challenge 2: *Slippery*) · whatever carries the lights (Challenge 3: *Out of Sight*)

**The ground** *Sucking Mud*: anyone who goes down is stuck · *Rotten Decks*: heavy things go through, and light feet don't · *The Tide*: it comes in faster than anyone can walk

**Stakes** *The ship's bell*, Challenge 3: *Hidden Away*, *Heavy Load* (proof of which ship a hulk really is) · *Off the flats*, Challenge 3: *Far Away*, *Watched* (a mile of mud, and the breakers are watching)

**Scene clock** d6: the tide's in, and anyone still out on the flats is swimming.

**Telegraphs** *The mud takes your boot, then your leg.* (d6, Armour, Pinned) · *Slade comes along the walkway with an axe on his shoulder.* (d6, Armour) · *The first channel fills behind you.* (d6, the scene clock)

**Hooks** A ship the crew knows was stolen last month · Someone wants the ghosts laid to rest, and they'll pay well · Someone the crew's after is hiding in the hulks

**Elsewhere** In a horror game, let the breakers fake the ghosts in every hulk but one.

**Secret** *The Breakers Make the Ghosts*. Slade's men hang the lights and moan in the hulks themselves to keep people off the flats at night, because that's when they break up stolen ships brought in on the tide. Nance carries the lamps for a penny a night, and she'll talk for more. Study the hulks and one's got new copper under the tar and her name burned off the stern. Ghosts don't leave bootprints, either. A ghost of your own, made with magic or a lamp and a sheet, frightens the breakers worse than anyone.
:::

## Hollin's Foundry {#s-place-foundry}

:::place
An iron foundry on the river that hasn't let its furnace go out in sixty years (Challenge 4).

You can feel the heat from the street. Inside, a furnace three storeys high glows at the seams, and a ladle of white iron hangs from a crane overhead. The bellows roar so loud that nobody bothers shouting, and the air tastes of hot metal.

**Who's here** *Master Odo Hollin*, the founder (Challenge 5: *Hits Like a Wagon*, *Relentless*, *Proud*) · *Gully*, who feeds the furnace (Challenge 2: *Overlooked*, *Scared*) · a dozen founders with iron bars (Challenge 3: *Trained*)

**The ground** *The Ladle*: tip it and everything under it burns · *Sand Pits*: they're soft and deep, and full of moulds · *The Crane*: run along its beam, or swing on its chains · *The Bellows*: nobody hears a shout past arm's length

**Stakes** *Tonight's casting*, Challenge 4: *Guarded*, *Heavy Load* (whatever's being poured, ruin it or take it) · *The order book*, Challenge 3: *Locked*, *Watched* (who paid for the casting, and why)

**Scene clock** d6: Hollin taps the furnace and the iron runs.

**Telegraphs** *Hollin swings his tapping bar at whoever's closest.* (d10, Armour) · *The crane jerks and the ladle slops over the pits.* (d8, everyone by the pits, Armour, Burning) · *A founder knocks the clay plug half out of the tap hole.* (d6, the scene clock)

**Hooks** Someone's ordered something cast here that mustn't exist · Hollin owes a friend of the crew and won't pay · Gully came to the crew frightened, and wouldn't say of what

**Secret** *Something Lives in the Furnace*. Hollin's grandfather bound a fire-spirit into the furnace, and it's why his iron's the best in the city. The binding's cast into the furnace's iron bands, and it's wearing thin. When the furnace is tapped tonight, the spirit comes out with the iron. Gully knows, because it talks to him while he shovels. Study the furnace and it's hotter than coal can make it, with old sigils all over the bands. The chalk wards round its mouth are freshly drawn, and the fire leans toward whoever stands closest. A ward can hold it back, and anyone who can read the bands can mend the binding, or break it.
:::

## The Weeping Field {#s-place-weeping-field}

:::place
An old battlefield where nothing grows and the dead still hold the line (Challenge 5).

It's a long slope of grey grass with a broken wall across the middle and a roofless chapel at the top. The air smells of wet iron, and there aren't any birds. At dusk there's drumming from nowhere, and the mist comes up out of the grass in ranks.

**Who's here** *The Grey Line*, the dead of the battle (Challenge 5: *Not Really Here*, *Relentless*) · *Captain Ambrose Thorne*, who held the wall (Challenge 6: *Bound to a Place*, *Code of Honour*) · *Widow Hale*, who keeps the chapel (Challenge 3: *Knows the Territory*, *Grieving*) · three grave robbers (Challenge 2: *Greedy*, *Cowardly*)

**The ground** *Mist*: ten paces and the world ends · *The Broken Wall*: the line they died holding, and cover for anyone · *Old Iron*: nobody here's unarmed for long · *Sacred Ground*: the dead won't cross the chapel step

**Stakes** *The far side*, Challenge 4: *Far Away*, *Mist* (cross before dark, or spend the night in the chapel) · *The colours*, Challenge 4: *Hidden Away*, *Guarded* (the regiment's banner, buried where it fell)

**Scene clock** d8: full dark, and the whole Grey Line comes over the wall.

**Telegraphs** *A rank of grey shapes levels spears that aren't there.* (d10, everyone in the open, Confidence, Frightened) · *Thorne's voice comes out of the mist: "Hold the wall."* (d12, the far side) · *A robber's spade hits a coffin lid, and the mist turns toward the noise.* (d4, the scene clock)

**Hooks** The crew has to cross, and the road round takes three days · A family wants its banner back

**Secret** *They Don't Know It's Over*. The peace was signed the morning of the battle, and the rider bringing the order never got through, so the dead are still holding the wall. He's under the chapel floor with the treaty in his satchel. Widow Hale knows the story, and doesn't believe a word of it. Study the chapel and the floor's been laid over a grave that isn't in her book. Anyone who can speak to the dead can ask Thorne what he's waiting for, and he'll tell them plainly: the order. Read him the treaty and the Grey Line lays down its arms.
:::

## The Lantern Theatre {#s-place-lantern-theatre}

:::place
A playhouse on the opening night of a play everyone's talking about (Challenge 3).

There's a row of candles for footlights, a painted sky, and ropes and sandbags up in the dark of the fly loft. The pit's packed and loud, the boxes are full of the quality, and it smells of oranges and too many people. Backstage is a maze of painted flats.

**Who's here** *Madam Iris Penhallow*, who owns the theatre (Challenge 4: *Well-Connected*, *Greedy*) · *Lord Aubrey Kell*, alone in the best box (Challenge 5: *Politically Untouchable*, *Proud*) · two of Kell's men (Challenge 3: *Trained*) · *Pip Larkin*, the prompter (Challenge 2: *Overlooked*)

**The ground** *An Audience*: hundreds of people, all looking the same way · *The Fly Loft*: cut a rope and something comes down · *Footlights*: kick them over and it's dark, or on fire

**Stakes** *The third act*, Challenge 4: *Someone Must Agree*, *Watched* (get it played, or get it stopped, depending on who's paying) · *The playwright*, Challenge 4: *Hidden Away*, *Wanted By Others* (nobody knows who wrote it, and Kell's men are looking too)

**Scene clock** d8: the interval ends and the third act starts, ready or not.

**Telegraphs** *Penhallow pushes you out of the wings: "You're on, darling."* (d8, Confidence) · *One of Kell's men comes down the backstage corridor with his knife held low.* (d6, Armour) · *Kell stands up in his box, and the house goes quiet.* (d10, the third act)

**Hooks** Someone's hired the crew to stop the play · The playwright's a friend who's gone missing

**Secret** *The Play Is a Confession*. The third act shows exactly how Kell's brother died, and the only witness wrote it: Pip Larkin, who was the brother's valet and now feeds the actors their lines from the wings. Pip doesn't look down at the book once. Penhallow knows who wrote it, and she's selling the name to whoever pays more. Study the script and the third act's in a different hand, naming a poison any apothecary would know. A medium who looks at Kell's box feels his brother in the empty chair beside him. Play the third act to the end and Kell's *Proud* breaks in front of the whole city.
:::

**Scene clock** d8: the chamber's empty, the bottom gates open, and the barge is gone downriver.

**Telegraphs** *Nell swings her boat hook at whoever's on the gate beam.* (d8, Armour, into the chamber) · *Ware leans on the windlass, and the water drops away under the barge.* (d6, the scene clock) · *The horses spook and drag the towline across the path.* (d6, everyone on the towpath, Armour, Prone)

**Hooks** Something's coming down the canal that mustn't reach the river · A body turned up in the lock last week

Anyone who can move water with magic can empty the chamber further than the paddles go, and then nobody can miss the door.
:::

## The Basket Monastery {#s-place-basket-monastery}

:::place
A monastery on top of a rock pillar, reached only by a basket on a rope (Challenge 5).

The pillar's four hundred feet of bare rock, with the monastery on top like a nest. You ride up in a net basket on a rope, spinning, and at the top a winch creaks. After that it's quiet, just a chapel in terraced gardens and monks who don't say much.

## A Place at the Table {#s-places-played}

:::example[Four in the Pledge House]
The crew's after a brass key that a Guild clerk pledged last week, and they need it tonight. Vesk, Bram, Isolde and Dorrie are as they start in the ready-made characters, so the GM's budget is 40 a round.

She reads them the shop, camphor and sweet smell and all, and then lays the scene out. "There are two stakes. The key's in the strongroom under the back stair, and you haven't got the ticket, so *The pledge* is Challenge 3, with *Locked* and *Paperwork* in the way. If you want Jessamy's ledger too, that's Challenge 3, with *Behind the Grille* and *Written in Code*. The scene clock's a d8. When it runs out, Quill stops waiting and goes upstairs to see the master."

Then she telegraphs. "Jessamy looks at Dorrie through the grille: 'No ticket, no pledge.' That's a d6 at your Confidence. The elder Rudd's going to take Bram by the collar and put him through the shelves, a d6 at your Armour, and if it lands you're on the floor. And Quill straightens his coat and puts a foot on the bottom stair. That's a d6 at the scene clock." That's 9 steps of her 40. It's a talking scene so far, so she keeps it a breather.

**Bram** goes first. He steps onto the bottom stair in front of Quill and fills it. Defender, his shield and his Title all fit, plus the action: 4 steps, a d8, against Quill's d6. Bram rolls 5, and Quill's d6 comes up 5 too. When you're answering, a tie's a loss unless you pay, so Bram pays 1 Aura, and Quill sits back down. Bram's keeping his second action for next round, and he's happy to let the Rudd ride on his Armour.

**Dorrie** fires Cold Reader first, which is free once a scene, and asks what Jessamy's feeling. "She's frightened, and not of you. Every time anyone goes near that stair she stops breathing." Then Dorrie leans on the counter and works on the missing ticket. Influence and her Title fit, and the GM agrees the street's heard of her, so Reputation Precedes Me adds 1. That's 3, plus the action, so 4 steps an action and 8 for both: a d12 and a d4. She puts both on *Paperwork* for advantage and rolls the stake's d6 with them. The d12 comes up 3, but the d4 lands on 4, its top face, and the stake rolls 2. Jessamy writes her a new ticket. Dorrie's Aura is already full, so the top face goes to Bram and pays back his tie.

**Vesk**'s at the strongroom door under the stair, behind Bram's back. Larceny, mechanical locks, her picks and her Title, plus the action, make 5 steps, a d10. She rolls 6, and so does the stake. She pays 1 Aura for the tie and the door swings open. With *Locked* and *Paperwork* both gone, the key's theirs, ticket and all. With her other action she holds the lamp up the stairwell for Isolde. Nothing on her sheet fits, so that's just the action, 1 step of help.

**Isolde**'s been at the foot of the stair since they came in, because that's where the sweet smell's strongest. She Studies the house with Medium and her recently dead specialization, which is 2 steps, plus the action. That's 3 an action and 6 for both, and Vesk's help makes 7: a d12 and a d2. The Pledge House is Challenge 3, so she rolls its d6 too, and puts both her dice on it. The d12 rolls 10, the d2 rolls 1, and the house rolls 4. She asks what's upstairs, and the GM gives her the hidden tag: *The Master's Been Dead a Month*. "He's up there in his chair, and he's been there four weeks. He isn't happy about what's happening to his shop."

End of the round. Jessamy's d6 rolls 2 against Dorrie's Confidence d6, which rolls 5, so it doesn't land. The Rudd's d6 rolls 6 against Bram's Armour d6, which rolls 3, so it does, and the GM names it: he's on the floor. Bram offers his plate instead, because he isn't leaving that stair, and the GM takes the swap. His Armour's a d4 now. Quill was answered, so the scene clock only takes its usual step, down to a d6.

That changes the whole scene. Jessamy's been giving the street its pledges back before the Rudds can sell them, and she's been terrified of exactly this. The crew knows the tag now, so any of them can stretch it into a roll for 1 Aura. Next round Dorrie won't need to beat her at all. She can lean on the grille and ask, quietly, how long he's been up there.
:::
