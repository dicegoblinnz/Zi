There are three sheets: one for each character, one the whole crew shares, and a faction board you can print as often as you need.

## The character sheet {#s-sheet-character}

The character sheet is one A4 sheet, printed back to back. The front's for play and the back's who you are. Type into it on screen and print it, or print it blank and fill it in with a pencil. It's on the [Sheets page](/sheets/), with the player reference. Here's what's on each side.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara character sheet, front: play**

-   Name · Pronouns · Player
-   Aura (now \_\_\_ · max \_\_\_ · full every scene, and 5 unless your sheet changes it)
-   Armour (full \_\_\_ · now \_\_\_ · d2, and a size for each piece of plain armour)
-   Resolve (a star for each Title, and one more or one fewer for the Resolve Boon or Burden: that's your maximum · fill in the ones you've got)
-   Stride (3 to start)
-   Confidence (a row of dice from d2 to d12: ring the one you're on, and move the ring as it swings · d6 at the start of every scene, a size up in clothes that fit it · d12 is fired up, d2 is shaken · back to d6 when the scene ends)
-   Conditions (4 · the die that placed it, with its size to ring)
-   Hurt (three bars of three boxes, and room for two extra bars · a line in each bar for its Catch, and your Confidence drops a size when you write one · a Scarred tick on every box · what a Blaze does)
-   Wound die (d6 → d8 → d10 → d12: ring the one you're on, a size bigger for each Catch on your Hurt track · a heal has to beat it)
-   Aspiration (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: a new archetype, with a Title you name for it, and a new Aspiration at d2)
-   Party Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: the crew's done it, and everyone writes a new thread)
-   Set actions: Fight (melee and ranged) · Move (run, climb, jump and chase) · Sneak (unseen and unheard) · Study (look closely, know things) · Talk (charm, lies and threats), and six more rows for set actions of your own, like Locks or Ward
-   On each row: what's on it · steps · die
-   Steps into dice (1 step is a d2, 6 is a d12, 12 is two d12s, and 24 is four)
-   Traits (3 · two to start · each with its type, Passive · Triggered · Invoked · Per scene · Per downtime · Used, and two lines for what it does)
-   Kit: equipment (3 · what you wear or wield, with a weapon's properties · each piece of plain armour is a size of Armour) · utility (3 · tools and things you use up · cross it off once it's used) · a keepsake (something that matters to your character) · the bag (holds 10 · getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free)
-   Notes (a thread that won't fit on its bond, and whatever your archetype's holding, like a Shadow's pool or a Shifter's animal tags)
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Back: who you are**

-   Six Titles: I and II to start, III to VI earned in play
-   On each: the Title, the name you give your archetype · the archetype · six permission lines (three to start on I and II, one on a Title you earn later)
-   A Boon takes two permission lines on the Title that pays for it
-   Attunement (2 slots to start, on top of what you carry · on each: the thing · Awakened, Tempered or Perfected, to ring · the material that took it there · its Catch · its Burden · six permission lines · a Summoner's summoning attunement writes its souls and their dice on those lines instead)
-   Bonds (6 · four thread lines on each · tick the first box when a thread's strained, and the second when it snaps)
:::

Title cards don't have a Catch line. Your Catches live on your attuned things, on the back, and in your Hurt bars, on the front.

What Aura and Resolve buy and how they come back, your archetype's +2, building a roll and your action aren't on the sheet. They're in the **player reference**, a separate four-page PDF on the [Sheets page](/sheets/). It's the [quick reference](#appendix-quick) in four parts:

1.  **Making and growing a character:** making a character, Boons and Burdens, things that climb, downtime, bonds and threads, Armour and how Confidence swings, and Hurt, Blaze, Scars, healing and the wound die.
2.  **Archetypes, Aura and the round:** the seventeen archetypes, a line each, what Aura and Resolve buy and how they come back, Catches included, the round, your action, answering, letting it ride, the one defence roll, Defend, taking the hit, what it costs, and conditions.
3.  **Rolling:** building a roll, set actions and the steps ladder, the roll, your dice and splits, and combos.
4.  **Threats, moving and gear:** how far, weapon properties, reading a threat, what a win does, Study, stakes and clocks, chases, what you carry, attunement and summoning.

The GM's side of the quick reference stays in the book: factions, the session, the Oracle, and the round and threat budgets.

## The party sheet {#s-sheet-party}

Two A4 sheets, printed double-sided. The fronts are faction board tiles A and B, and the backs are the crew page and the downtime page. Lay the tiles side by side in the middle of the table, any edge to any edge. The whole thing's explained in [The Party Sheet](#party-sheet).

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Party Sheet · the crew page**

-   The crew's name
-   Power (d2 → d12)
-   Wealth (d2 → d12)
-   Reputations (one for every Goal done · a step on any crew roll it fits)
-   Crew tags (a step where it fits · what it does)
-   How it grows · Spending Wealth
-   The Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → past d12: the crew grows)
-   Projects (what it is · what happens when it's done · d2 → d12)
-   Aspirations (every player's, d2 → d12)
-   Party resources (the big things the crew owns together)
-   People the crew relies on
-   Crew tags to pick from (all twelve, with what each one does)
:::

The **downtime page** has the order a downtime runs in, how downtime rolls and ties work, the Standing ladder, the crew's four moves on a faction, the faction turn with its d6, both tables for when a faction comes for the crew, and **What has changed**, a line for every agenda that happened.

## The faction board and the Faction Sheet {#s-sheet-faction}

Each tile has **six seats**, three down each side. A seat holds one faction: its name, Power and Wealth, two agendas (each a track from d2 up to d12), its tags, its face, its secret once the crew finds it, a few lines of notes, and a Standing ladder from *Enemy* to *Ally*. There are no lines printed between seats. Draw your own, from anything to anything, because the crew decides who's tied to who.

The **Faction Sheet** is the same board on one A4 page, for tables that need more seats or a board of their own. Print as many as you like.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Faction seat**

-   Faction
-   Power
-   Wealth
-   Agendas (two at most · each d2 → d12 → past d12: it happens)
-   Tags
-   Face
-   Secret
-   Standing (Enemy · Wary · Neutral · Friendly · Ally)
:::
