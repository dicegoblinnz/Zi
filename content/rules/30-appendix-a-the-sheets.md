There are three sheets: one for each character, one the whole crew shares, and a faction board you can print as often as you need.

## The character sheet {#s-sheet-character}

The character sheet is one A4 sheet, printed back to back. The front's for play and the back's who you are. Type into it on screen and print it, or print it blank and fill it in with a pencil. It's on the [Sheets page](/sheets/), with the player reference. Here's what's on each side.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara character sheet, front: play**

-   Name · Pronouns · Player
-   Aura (now \_\_\_ · max \_\_\_ · less powering \_\_\_ · full every scene, and your maximum's 5 and 2 for each attuned thing · 1 back for every die of yours that wins and every top face)
-   Extra actions (your first's free, then 3 Aura, 4, 5 and on with no cap, and back to 3 next round)
-   Stride (3 to start)
-   Roots and tracks: Body, Mind and Soul side by side, each with a box for what it adds up to and where its points came from, a box for each Title's spread, I to VI, and one for its permissions · under each Root its track, Stamina, Confidence or Resolve, a row of dice from d12 down to d2 to ring the one you're on, then Blaze, for a hit at d2 · d12 to start · a size off for every hit that gets through, and back a size on a 4 or more when a scene ends · a box to cross on d2, d4, d6, d8 and d10, for the sizes your Scars lock, from the bottom up · and two armour lines, with three boxes beside each line
-   Under the tracks: what an ability costs (1 off its home track, or 2 from the other two), 2 for 1, and Blaze
-   A roll (one Root, and 1 step for every thing on your sheet that fits what you're doing, with what explaining one, going beyond and bringing something in cost) and the steps into dice under it (1 step is a d2, 6 is a d12, 12 is two d12s, and 24 is four)
-   Conditions (4 · the die that placed it, with its size to ring · it steps down a size every round)
-   Scars (5 lines, one for each Scar's Catch · each one locks the lowest size its track's got open)
-   Aspiration (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, with the one it comes with, and a new Aspiration at d2)
-   Party Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: the crew's done it, and everyone writes a new thread)
-   Points (spent \_\_\_ of \_\_\_ · banked \_\_\_, for any you haven't spent yet)
-   Kit: equipment (3 · what you wear or wield, with a weapon's properties and each piece of armour's quality and Root · an item's a step on a roll you use it in) · utility (3 · tools and things you use up · cross it off once it's used) · a keepsake (something that matters to your character) · the bag (holds 10 · getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free)
-   Attunement (two cards · each attuned thing puts 2 on your Aura maximum · on each: the thing · its stage, Awakened, Tempered or Perfected, to ring · the material that took it there · its Catch · its Burden · six awakening lines, each with passive or active to ring and two boxes for the Aura powering a passive one, which comes off what you've got and off your maximum till the scene ends · a Summoner's souls go on those lines, with their dice · a third attuned thing goes in Notes)
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Back: who you are**

-   Six Titles, I to VI, each with two lines for the Title · its spread, a box each for Body, Mind and Soul, 3 in all · six permission lines, each with B, M or S to ring for its Root
-   A Boon takes two permission lines on the Title that pays for it, and puts nothing on a Root
-   Archetypes (three cards · the same one twice is fine · on each: the archetype, with its home track to ring beside it, Stamina, Confidence or Resolve · its mechanic · its Trait · six ability lines · an ability's 1 off its home track, or 2 from the other two)
-   Bonds (4 · three thread lines on each · a bond's usually a person, but it can be a faction, a place or a cause · ring each thread bond or hostile · tick the first box when it's strained, and the second when it snaps)
-   Notes (four lines: whatever your archetype's holding, like a Shadow's pool or a Shifter's animal tags, trouble that follows you, and anything that won't fit)
:::

Vesk's front says Body 8, with 3 from her first Title, 1 from her second and 4 from her permissions, then Mind 4, with 2 from her second Title and 2 from her permissions, and Soul 0. Turn it over and her first Title's spread is Body 3, her second's is Body 1 and Mind 2, and every permission has its Root ringed beside it, so you can always see where a number came from.

Title cards don't have a Catch line. Your Catches live on your attuned things and in your Scars, both on the front.

The rest of what you reach for at the table, what Aura buys, the round and your action among them, is in the **player reference**, a separate four-page PDF on the [Sheets page](/sheets/). It's the [quick reference](#appendix-quick) in four parts, a page each, with what you reach for most first:

1.  **The round and rolling:** the round, your action and extra actions, building a roll and the steps ladder, the roll, splits, combos, and moving, how far and weapon properties.
2.  **The tracks, defence and threats:** Stamina, Confidence and Resolve, what they pay, 2 for 1, how they come back, Blaze, Scars and healing, answering, letting it ride, Defend, intercepting, what it costs, conditions, and reading a threat, its multipliers and vulnerabilities, and what a win does.
3.  **Aura, archetypes and threads:** what Aura buys and how it comes back, Catches included, powering an attuned thing and what extra actions cost, the eighteen archetypes, a line each with its sigil and its home track, and bonds and threads.
4.  **Your character, downtime and gear:** making a character on points, Boons and Burdens, things that climb, downtime, Study, stakes and clocks, chases, what you carry, attunement and summoning.

The GM's side of the quick reference stays in the book: factions, the session, the Oracle, and the round and threat budgets.

## The party sheet {#s-sheet-party}

Two A4 sheets, printed double-sided. The fronts are faction board tiles A and B, and the backs are the crew page and the downtime page. Lay the tiles side by side in the middle of the table, any edge to any edge. The whole thing's explained in [The Party Sheet](#party-sheet).

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Party Sheet · the crew page**

-   The crew's name
-   Reputations (one for every Goal done · a step on any crew roll it fits)
-   Power (d4 to start · 1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up)
-   Resources (where the crew's money comes from, one line each · each one pays for an asset, and one that isn't is an extra resource)
-   Assets (what the crew can do, one line each, with what it does · each one needs a resource paying for it)
-   How it grows · What an extra resource can buy
-   The Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → past d12: the crew grows)
-   Projects (what it is · what happens when it's done · d2 → d12)
-   Aspirations (every player's, d2 → d12)
-   Party holdings (the big things the crew owns together)
-   People the crew relies on
-   Crew assets to pick from (all twelve, with what each one does)
:::

The **downtime page** has the order a downtime runs in, how downtime rolls and ties work, the crew's moves on a faction, the faction turn with its d6, both tables for when a faction comes for the crew, and **What has changed**, a line for every agenda that happened.

## The faction board and the Faction Sheet {#s-sheet-faction}

Each tile has **six seats**, three down each side. A seat holds one faction: its name and Power, its resources and assets, its Want, two agendas (each a track from d2 up to d12), its face, its secret once the crew finds it, its threads, and a few lines of notes. There are no lines printed between seats. Draw your own, from anything to anything, because the crew decides who's tied to who.

The **Faction Sheet** is the same board on one A4 page, for tables that need more seats or a board of their own. Print as many as you like.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Faction seat**

-   Faction
-   Power (1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up)
-   Resources (one line each)
-   Assets (one line each, with what it does)
-   Want
-   Agendas (two at most · each d2 → d12 → past d12: it happens)
-   Face
-   Secret
-   Bonds (Public Sentiment · Morale · Loyalty, each with its bond and hostile threads)
-   Threads toward the crew (bond or hostile · mostly bond threads and it's friendly, mostly hostile and it's hostile, none or as many of each and it does business)
:::
