There are three sheets: one for each character, one the whole crew shares, and a faction board you can print as often as you need.

## The character sheet {#s-sheet-character}

The character sheet is one A4 sheet, printed back to back. The front's for play and the back's who you are. Type into it on screen and print it, or print it blank and fill it in with a pencil. It's on the [Sheets page](/sheets/), with the player reference. Here's what's on each side.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara character sheet, front: play**

-   Name · Pronouns · Player
-   Aura (now \_\_\_ · max \_\_\_ · full every scene, and 5 to start · 1 for every die of yours that wins and every top face)
-   Toll (\_\_\_ · 1 for every heal from someone or something else · a heal loses 5 for each · it drops by 1 at the end of each scene, and downtime clears it)
-   Stride (3 to start)
-   Roots: Body, Mind and Soul side by side, each with a box for its number, where its points came from (a box for each Title's spread, I to VI, and one for its permissions) and the dice it rolls · then what's locked (a point for each awakening on it and each Scar), what it holds, and a big box for the points it has now · a box to tick for its wound, **Bloodied** (Body), **Frayed** (Mind) or **Hollow** (Soul) · two lines for its Scars, each one's negative permission, what you can't do any more, with three boxes beside it for the downtime slots that grow it into a Trait, and the Trait written after it once it has · and two armour lines, with three boxes beside each line
-   Under the Roots: a hit's a point off, or 2 off another Root, armour can take it, a hit at 0 wounds the Root and it heals to half, a hit on it wounded at 0 is a Scar and you leave, three slots grow a Scar into a Trait, and the Blaze
-   A roll (one Root at full, bringing another in on a permission for a point of it or 3 Aura, an awakening's 2 steps and 2 more for each Aura, and splitting any way) and the steps into dice under it (1 step is a d2, 6 is a d12, 12 is two d12s, and 24 is four)
-   Conditions (4 · the die that placed it, with its size to ring · it steps down a size every round)
-   Getting it back (when a scene ends, roll each Root below what it holds, unsplit, and get back what you roll · downtime puts everything back · a heal, and the quick heal)
-   Aspiration (five boxes, one ticked for each point in it · a downtime slot's a point, and there's no roll · at 5 it comes good: a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, with the one it comes with, and a new Aspiration with nothing in it)
-   Party Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: the crew's done it, and everyone writes a new thread)
-   Points (spent \_\_\_ of \_\_\_ · banked \_\_\_, for any you haven't spent yet)
-   Kit: equipment (3 · what you wear or wield, with a weapon's properties and each piece of armour's quality and Root) · utility (3 · tools and things you use up · cross it off once it's used) · a keepsake (something that matters to your character) · the bag (holds 10 · getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free)
-   Attunement (two cards · each awakening locks a point of the Root it fits, and it's 2 steps on a roll it fits and 2 more for each Aura · attuned things don't touch your Aura · on each: the thing · its stage, Awakened, Tempered or Perfected, to ring · the material that took it there · its Catch · its Burden · six awakening lines, each with B, M or S to ring for the Root it locks · a Summoner's souls go on those lines, with their dice · a third attuned thing goes in Notes)
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Back: who you are**

-   Six Titles, I to VI, each with two lines for the Title · its spread, a box each for Body, Mind and Soul, 5 in all · six permission lines, each with B, M or S to ring for its Root
-   A Boon takes two permission lines on the Title that pays for it, and puts nothing on a Root
-   Archetypes (three cards · the same one twice is fine · on each: the archetype · its mechanic · its Trait · seven ability lines, each saying what it costs in Aura, so a long one can take two)
-   Bonds (4 · three thread lines on each · a bond's usually a person, but it can be a faction, a place or a cause · ring each thread bond or hostile · tick the first box when it's strained, and the second when it snaps)
-   Notes (four lines: whatever your archetype's holding, like a Shadow's pool or a Shifter's animal tags, trouble that follows you, and anything that won't fit)
:::

Vesk's front says Body 11, with 5 from her first Title, 2 from her second and 4 from her permissions, so she rolls a d12 and a d10. Her hook's awakening locks 1, so her Body holds 10, and that's what she writes in her points when a scene starts. Mind's 5, with 3 from her second Title and 2 from her permissions, and Soul's 0. Turn it over and her first Title's spread is Body 5, her second's is Mind 3 and Body 2, and every permission has its Root ringed beside it, so you can always see where a number came from.

Title cards don't have a Catch line. Your Catches live on your attuned things and in your Scars, both on the front.

The rest of what you reach for at the table, what Aura buys, the round and your action among them, is in the **player reference**, a separate four-page PDF on the [Sheets page](/sheets/). It's the [quick reference](#appendix-quick) in four parts, a page each, with what you reach for most first:

1.  **The round and rolling:** the round, your one action, building a roll and the steps ladder, the roll, intents and splits, combos, and moving.
2.  **Your points, defence and threats:** a Root's points from full to a Scar, drawn, then your points, hits, spending, wounds, Scars, the Blaze, how they come back, healing and Toll, answering, letting it ride, Defend, intercepting, consequences, conditions, and reading a threat, its multipliers and vulnerabilities, and what a win does.
3.  **Aura, archetypes and threads:** what Aura buys and how it comes back, Catches included, and bringing a Root in for 3, the seventeen archetypes, a line each with its sigil and its Trait, and bonds and threads.
4.  **Your character, downtime and gear:** making a character on points, Boons and Burdens, things that climb, downtime, Study, stakes and clocks, what you carry, attunement and summoning.

The GM's side of the quick reference stays in the book: factions, the session, the Oracle, and the round and threat budgets.

## The party sheet {#s-sheet-party}

Two A4 sheets, printed double-sided. The fronts are faction board tiles A and B, and the backs are the crew page and the downtime page. Lay the tiles side by side in the middle of the table, any edge to any edge. The whole thing's explained in [The Party Sheet](#party-sheet).

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Party Sheet · the crew page**

-   The crew's name
-   Reputations (one for every Goal done · a step on any crew roll it fits)
-   Power (d4 to start · 1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up)
-   Resources (where the crew's money comes from, one line each · each one pays for an asset, and one that isn't is an extra resource)
-   Assets (what the crew can do, one line each, with what it does · each one needs a resource paying for it)
-   How it grows · What an extra resource can buy
-   The Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → past d12: the crew grows)
-   Projects (what it is · what happens when it's done · d2 → d12)
-   Aspirations (every player's, d2 → d12)
-   Party holdings (the big things the crew owns together)
-   People the crew relies on
-   Crew assets to pick from (all twelve, with what each one does)
:::

The **downtime page** has the order a downtime runs in, how downtime rolls and ties work, the crew's moves on a faction, the faction turn with its d6, both tables for when a faction comes for the crew, and **What has changed**, a line for every agenda that happened.

## The faction board and the Faction Sheet {#s-sheet-faction}

Each tile has **six seats**, three down each side. A seat holds one faction: its name and Power, its resources and assets, its Want, two agendas (each a die that climbs from d2 to d12), its face, its secret once the crew finds it, its threads, and a few lines of notes. There are no lines printed between seats. Draw your own, from anything to anything, because the crew decides who's tied to who.

The **Faction Sheet** is the same board on one A4 page, for tables that need more seats or a board of their own. Print as many as you like.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Faction seat**

-   Faction
-   Power (1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up)
-   Resources (one line each)
-   Assets (one line each, with what it does)
-   Want
-   Agendas (two at most · each d2 → d12 → past d12: it happens)
-   Face
-   Secret
-   Bonds (Public Sentiment · Morale · Loyalty, each with its bond and hostile threads)
-   Threads toward the crew (bond or hostile · mostly bond threads and it's friendly, mostly hostile and it's hostile, none or as many of each and it does business)
:::
