There are three sheets: one for each character, one the whole crew shares, and a faction board you can print as often as you need.

## The character sheet {#s-sheet-character}

The character sheet is four A4 pages, printed double-sided, and you can fill it in on screen. The fronts are your character: lay them side by side and you've got You, Titles, Set actions and Kit in a row. The backs are the player reference, so the rules you need are a flip of the page away. Here's what's on each one.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara character sheet, page 1: You**

-   Name · Pronouns · Player
-   Aspiration (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: a new Title, and a new Aspiration at d2)
-   Party Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 · past d12: the crew's done it)
-   Aura (now \_\_\_ · max \_\_\_ · full every scene, and 5 unless your sheet changes it)
-   Armour (full \_\_\_ · d2, and a size for each piece of plain armour)
-   Confidence (full \_\_\_ · d2, and a size for each Title, each bond with three live connections, clothes that fit the scene, and gear that says so)
-   Stride (3 to start)
-   Sparks (a box for each Title · they all come back every downtime)
-   Spend 1 Aura (bring something in · go beyond · win a tie when you're acting · make a die your area · a quick heal) · Get it back (every top face · every time you let a Catch or real trouble bite)
-   Hurt (three bars of three boxes, and room for extra bars · a line in each bar for its Catch · a Scarred tick on every box · what a Blaze does)
-   Bonds (6 · four connections each · three live: Confidence a size bigger)
-   Conditions (4 · the die that placed it, with its size to ring)
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Page 2: Titles**

-   Six Title cards: I and II to start, III to VI earned in play
-   On each card: the Title · its trait (Passive · Triggered · Invoked · Per scene · Per downtime · Used) and what it does · six permission lines (three to start on I and II, one on a Title you earn later)
-   A Boon takes two permission lines on the Title that pays for it
-   Along the bottom: what a permission is (a doing word, and how, what or where), and going beyond for 1 Aura
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Page 3: Set actions**

-   Build a roll (the five steps) · Your action (one a round, holding it, extra actions, combos)
-   Fight (melee and ranged) · Move (run, climb, jump and chase) · Sneak (unseen and unheard) · Study (look closely, know things) · Talk (charm, lies and threats)
-   Nine more rows for set actions of your own, like Locks or Ward
-   On each row: what's on it · steps · die
-   Steps into dice (1 step is a d2, 6 is a d12, and 12 is two d12s)
:::

:::sheet-fields
**Page 4: Kit**

-   Equipment (3 · what you wear or wield, with a weapon's properties · each piece of plain armour is a size of Armour)
-   Utility (3 · tools and things you use up · cross it off once it's used)
-   Keepsake (1 · something that matters to your character)
-   Attunement (2 slots to start, on top of what you carry · on each: the thing · Awakened, Tempered or Perfected · the material that took it there · six permission lines · its Catch · its Burden · a summoning attunement writes its souls and their dice on those lines instead)
-   Bag (holds 10 · getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free)
:::

Title cards don't have a Catch line. Your Catches live on your attuned things, on page 4, and in your Hurt bars, on page 1.

The **player reference** on the backs runs to four pages, each one behind the front it goes with:

1.  **Making and growing a character**, behind You: making a character, things that climb, downtime, bonds, Boons and Burdens, Armour and Confidence, and Hurt, Blaze, Scars and healing.
2.  **Aura, the round and when it lands**, behind Titles: what Aura buys and how it comes back, Sparks and trouble, the round, your action, answering, the one defence roll, Defend, taking the hit, what it costs, and conditions.
3.  **Rolling**, behind Set actions: the steps ladder, set actions, building a roll, the roll, your dice and splits, area dice, combos and leading.
4.  **Threats, moving and gear**, behind Kit: how far, reading a threat, what a win does, Study, stakes and clocks, chases, weapon properties, what you carry, attunement and summoning.

## The party sheet {#s-sheet-party}

Two A4 sheets, printed double-sided. The fronts are faction board tiles A and B, and the backs are the crew page and the downtime page. Lay the tiles side by side in the middle of the table, any edge to any edge. The whole thing's explained in [The Party Sheet](#party-sheet).

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Party Sheet · the crew page**

-   The crew's name
-   Power (d2 → d12)
-   Wealth (d2 → d12)
-   Reputations (one for every Goal done · a step on any crew roll it fits)
-   Crew tags (a step where it fits · what it does)
-   How it grows · Spending Wealth
-   The Goal (d2 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → past d12: the crew grows)
-   Projects (what it is · what happens when it's done · d2 → d12)
-   Aspirations (every player's, d2 → d12)
-   Party resources (the big things the crew owns together)
-   People the crew relies on
-   Crew tags to pick from (all twelve, with what each one does)
:::

The **downtime page** has the order a downtime runs in, how downtime rolls and ties work, the Standing ladder, the crew's four moves on a faction, the faction turn with its d6, both tables for when a faction comes for the crew, and **What has changed**, a line for every agenda that happened.

## The faction board and the Faction Sheet {#s-sheet-faction}

Each tile has **six seats**, three down each side. A seat holds one faction: its name, Power and Wealth, two agendas (each a track from d2 up to d12), its tags, its face, its secret once the crew finds it, a few lines of notes, and a Standing ladder from *Enemy* to *Ally*. There are no lines printed between seats. Draw your own, from anything to anything, because the crew decides who's tied to who.

The **Faction Sheet** is the same board on one A4 page, for tables that need more seats or a board of their own. Print as many as you like.

:::sheet-fields
**Zi'Dara: Faction seat**

-   Faction
-   Power
-   Wealth
-   Agendas (two at most · each d2 → d12 → past d12: it happens)
-   Tags
-   Face
-   Secret
-   Standing (Enemy · Wary · Neutral · Friendly · Ally)
:::
