A **lever** is anything that changes a result: a bigger die, a second try, a blow that lands somewhere else. Here's every one of them, and where it comes from. Use it to plan a character, to write a trait or an item, or to answer a question at the table without making something up.

Where a lever comes from a trait, a condition or a resource, treat those as examples and write your own in the same shape.

## Dice {#s-levers-dice}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One more step** | Every permission that fits (an ability gives its tier). Every action. Help from a friend. A condition for you, or on the threat you go at. Stretching a permission (1 Aura), on every action you put in: one that almost fits, the ground, a tag you know, a Catch, a friend's permission. A *Charge*. |
| **A smaller telegraph** | Strip the tag that makes it bigger, like *Commanding*. A glancing blow, when the GM says so. A Challenge never shrinks, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **A bigger die** | More steps. Two actions on the same thing count every permission twice, traits and stretches included. Help and conditions count once. |
| **More dice** | Past six steps, every six is another die. Splitting: one extra die for each action. |
| **An area** | One die for how far: a burst, a cone or a line. For a ward or a blessing, it counts friends instead. A *Sweep* weapon, free, within reach. |
| **Advantage** | Several dice on one intent: your best die counts. |
| **Win a tie** | 1 Aura, when you're acting. Free, when you're defending. *Fortunate* (condition). In downtime, a step of the crew's Wealth. |
| **Roll again** | Your Spark. *Lucky* (trait, once a scene). *Blessed* (condition, if that action fails). |
| **A die counts as its highest face** | *Focused* (once a scene). *Sprinter* (a Move action). |
| **A die becomes a d12** | *Surging* (condition). |
| **A failure becomes a success** | *Silver Tongue* (social, once a scene). |
| **Their win becomes a tie** | *Hexed* (condition). |
| **One of their dice doesn't count** | *Blinded* (condition). |
| **No roll at all** | A permission that plainly covers it, with nothing pushing back. Spending a condition. *Spare lockpick*, *Acid vial*, *Disguised*. |

## Aura and Spark {#s-levers-aura}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Fill up** | Every new scene, to your maximum. |
| **Gain 1** | Every top face. *Killer Instinct*. *Inspired* (condition). *Child's toy* (once a scene). A top face on the Oracle. |
| **Gain 3** | *Battle Focus* (once a scene). |
| **A bigger maximum** | *Deep Well* (+1). Any trait, Scar or legendary item that says so. |
| **A smaller maximum** | A Catch that says so. |
| **Share it** | A top face when you're full goes to anyone in the scene. |
| **Spend it** | Two things only: stretch a permission, or win a tie. 1 Aura each. |
| **Traits** | Free, as often as they say. An Invoked trait costs the Aura it names. |
| **A Spark** | One at the start of every session. One from the GM for a moment that made the story better, if yours is spent. One for taking the GM's trouble. Never more than one. |
| **Spend a Spark** | Roll a failed roll again. Make an opportunity, even from the past. Add a permission for the scene. Turn down the GM's trouble. |

## Actions {#s-levers-actions}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **An extra action** | Save one: three next round. *Second Wind* (once a scene). *Unseen* (condition). |
| **A free action, for one thing** | *Fast* (a Move). *Quick* weapons (drawing). *Ready Hand* (the bag). *Rallied* (an answer). |
| **Take an action away** | *Exposed*, *Frightened* (condition). On a threat, that's one of its telegraphs. |
| **Shut one thing off** | *Stunned* (no answering). *Silenced* (no speaking). *Deafened* (no Help). *Pinned* (no moving). |
| **Choose what they do** | *Taunted* (at you). *Charmed* (one thing you ask). |
| **Stop a telegraph** | Answer it. Take down its source. Take away what it needs: strip the tag, disarm the crossbow. Running doesn't stop one aimed at you, but getting out of an area does. |

## Defence {#s-levers-defence}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Armour one size bigger** | Every Armour permission. *Warded*, *Covered* (condition). *Steady Hands*. |
| **Bigger, for the round** | Guard: build a die, and add its steps to your Armour or Confidence, or a friend's. |
| **Confidence one size bigger** | Every Title. Every bond with three live connections. The right clothes. Confidence gear. *Iron Will*, *Style*. *Heartened* (condition). |
| **Past d12** | A second die, keep the best. The extra die soaks the first blows. |
| **Back up to full** | Armour: a purse of gold at a smith, or a downtime slot. Confidence: both slots, facing a fear, keeping a promise. |
| **Back up a size or more** | *Heartened* (one size). *Cool Under Pressure* (three, once a scene). |
| **Put the blow somewhere else** | The GM names the cost. Offer another that fits and costs about as much: Armour, Confidence, a Hurt, an item, your Aspiration, the party Goal, a clock. |
| **Cancel a Hurt** | *Warded* (condition). *Tenacious* (your last box, once a scene). |
| **Take it for a friend** | Answer for them, standing in the way. *Covered* (condition). |
| **Stay where you are** | *Braced* (condition). |

## Hurt {#s-levers-hurt}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **More boxes** | *Unyielding* (+1). Playing solo (+1). |
| **Heal in a scene** | *Field Dressing* (once a scene). *Healer's Hands* (once a scene, per person). Nothing else. |
| **Heal in downtime** | A slot heals one box. A healer's slot heals one box on everyone. A *Medical kit* heals one without a slot. |
| **Lose a box for good** | A Scar, after a Blaze. The Scar becomes a permission or a passive. |
| **Blaze** | Fill your last box: something extraordinary happens, no roll. |

## Threats {#s-levers-threats}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Strip a tag** | A win aimed at it. Spending *Burning*, *Bleeding* or *Poisoned*. An *Acid vial*. |
| **End it in one win** | An opening: its weak point, helpless, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two tags, Tough or not. |
| **Wear down Tough** | It works like hit points. Every winning die against it takes off the number it rolled. A condition that would strip a tag takes its die's size off instead. |
| **Find a hidden tag** | Study, with an area die for a whole room. *Quick Study*, *Predator's Eye*. *Sighted*, *Spotted* (condition). |
| **Turn a tag against it** | Stretch it into a permission (1 Aura). |
| **One die fewer** | Against a *Layered* threat or stake, every tag stripped. |
| **End it without a fight** | Strip what drives it, or stretch it into a permission of your own. |

## Moving {#s-levers-moving}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Stride** | 3 to start. *Light on my Feet* +2. Each *Heavy* item −1. *Fleet* (condition): your stride again. |
| **Further** | A Move action: roll it, and the number is extra squares. Its dice add. *Fast*, *Sprinter*. |
| **Difficult ground** | Two units a square. *Sure-Footed* ignores it. |
| **Pin, drag, knock down** | *Pinned*, *Grabbed*, *Staggered*, *Prone*. *Grapple* weapons. |

## The Scene and the Story {#s-levers-story}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Make something true** | Your Spark, from your past or your present. *Prepared Mind*, *Contingency*. The Oracle, when all three dice match. |
| **Find something that helps** | Study: if it could be here, it is. *Tactical Mind*. *Perfect Recall*. |
| **Use the place** | Stretch the ground into a permission (1 Aura). *Scout Ahead* adds three ground tags. |
| **Do something big** | A bigger roll against a bigger Challenge: tiers, specializations, Titles, both actions, help. |
| **Hit many things** | An area die. A *Sweep* weapon, free, within reach. |
| **Have the right thing** | Your Spark. *I Have the Thing*. *Never Unarmed*. *Cache*. A purse of gold, mid-scene, from someone selling. |
| **Stop a clock** | Work the stake: its clock doesn't tick that round. Beat an event to slow it. |
| **Speed up a clock** | Beat an event to drive it. *Hunted* (condition). A consequence. |
| **Know something** | A permission that plainly covers knowing it. Study. *Cold Reader*, *Hard to Lie To*. *Truth serum*. The Oracle. |

## Growth {#s-levers-growth}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **A step closer** | Beat the die of your Aspiration, the Goal or a project: an action when it's within reach, or a slot in downtime. One step a roll. |
| **A new Title** | An Aspiration climbs past d12. |
| **A tier** | One slot to Skilled. Both slots to Master. |
| **A new ability, trait, connection or item** | A slot each. |
| **A rare or legendary item** | The story: won, inherited, taken from a boss. |
| **An extra slot** | *Tireless* (once a downtime). |
| **A bigger crew** | The party Goal's done: a new Reputation and two picks (Power, Wealth, a crew tag, a party resource). The crew takes something big, or a faction it struck falls: one pick. A job that says so. Rebuild, back to its best. |

## Writing Your Own {#s-levers-writing}

Everything in Zi is built from the same few shapes, so price new things against them.

| You're writing | It gets |
| --- | --- |
| **A passive trait** | +1 step on one kind of thing, or one number moved a little: stride +2, one more item and resource, one more Hurt box, Aura maximum +1. |
| **A once-a-scene trait** | One lever from this appendix, used once: an extra action, a second roll, something small that was there all along, a failure made good in one narrow field. |
| **An invoked trait** | The same kind of lever, for 1 Aura each time. |
| **A once-a-downtime trait** | Something that changes the next session: a meeting, a hidden fact, an extra slot. |
| **A condition** | One step for or against, and one strong lever when it's spent. |
| **A tag** | A reason it's still standing, a way it comes at you, or what drives it. A tag only changes dice when it says so. |
| **An item** | One permission. Two if rare, three if legendary. Each Catch carries one more: up to one on a common item, two on a rare one, three on a legendary one. |
| **A resource** | One lever, used once, with no roll. A keepsake: a small edge that never runs out. |

:::rule
**The test.** If what you wrote adds a number to a number, like *+2 to hit*, rewrite it as a permission, a step, or a lever from this list. Zi only ever compares dice, except when you move.
:::
