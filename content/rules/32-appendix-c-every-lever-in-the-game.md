A **lever** is anything that changes a result: a bigger die, a second try, a blow that lands somewhere else. Here's every one of them, and where it comes from. Use it to plan a character, to pick a Boon, to write a trait or an item, or to answer a question at the table without making something up.

Where a lever comes from a trait, a condition or an item, treat those as examples and write your own in the same shape.

## Dice {#s-levers-dice}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One more step** | Every line on your set action that fits: a Title, a permission, an item, a trait, or a permission on an attuned thing. Every action in the roll. A condition for you, or on the threat you go at. What you've got for the scene, free wherever it fits: a Spark's permission, or something that helps from Study. A *Charge*. |
| **One more step, for 1 Aura** | Anything that fits but isn't on your set action: one of your own permissions, items or traits, the ground, a threat's tag you know, a Catch, or a friend's permission through a connection you share. 1 Aura each, every time you bring one in. |
| **Steps from friends** | A combo: they put their actions into the same thing, their set actions add to yours, and nobody pays Aura. A lead: a friend hands you steps for this round, and they don't have to fit. |
| **A bigger die** | More steps. Two sides a step: 1 is a d2, and 6 is a d12. |
| **More dice** | Past 6 steps, a second die built the same way: 7 is a d12 and a d2, and 12 is two d12s. Every split. |
| **A split** | One for every action in the roll, and one more when you go past 6 steps. The Splits Boon gives one more, and the Burden takes one away. Share your steps between the dice you've got, too: a d12 and a d8 can be two d10s. |
| **A smaller telegraph** | Strip the tag that makes it bigger, like *Commanding*. A glancing blow, when the GM says so. A Challenge never shrinks, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **An area** | One die for how far, for 1 Aura and a split: a burst, a cone or a line. For a ward, a blessing or a heal, it counts friends instead. A *Sweep* weapon, free, at everyone within reach. |
| **Advantage** | Several dice on one intent: your best die counts. |
| **Win a tie** | Free, when you're defending. 1 Aura, when you're acting. *Fortunate* (condition). The Ties Boon: every tie, attacking too, with no Aura. In downtime, a step of the crew's Wealth. The Ties Burden loses every one, even defending. |
| **Roll again** | A Spark (all your dice, in downtime too). *Lucky* (one die, once a scene). *Blessed* (condition, if that action fails). |
| **+2 on a die** | 1 Aura, on a catching or summoning die. It's +3 with one summoning Boon, +4 with two and +5 with three. |
| **A die counts as its highest face** | *Focused* (once a scene). *Sprinter* (a Move action). |
| **A die becomes a d12** | *Surging* (condition). |
| **A failure becomes a success** | *Silver Tongue* (social, once a scene). |
| **Their win becomes a tie** | *Hexed* (condition). |
| **One of their dice doesn't count** | *Blinded* (condition). |
| **No roll at all** | A permission that plainly covers it, with nothing pushing back. Spending a condition. A quick heal (1 Aura). *Spare lockpick*, *Acid vial*, *Disguised*. |

## Aura and Spark {#s-levers-aura}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Fill up** | Every new scene, to your maximum. |
| **Gain 1** | Every top face, area dice included, but not a summon's. Letting a Catch bite, as often as you like, as long as it brings real trouble. Bringing real trouble on yourself with no Catch written down. The GM, when a great setup doesn't land. *Killer Instinct*. *Inspired* (condition). A top face on the Oracle. The Boons *Beat a threat* and *In your element* (that one even past your maximum). |
| **Gain 3** | *Battle Focus* (once a scene). |
| **A bigger maximum** | *Deep Well* (+1). The Aura Boon (+2). Anything else on your sheet that says so. |
| **A smaller maximum** | The Aura Burden (−2). |
| **Share it** | A top face when you're full goes to anyone in the scene. |
| **Spend it** | 1 Aura each: bring something in, go beyond, win a tie when you're acting, make a die your area, a quick heal, or +2 on a catching or summoning die. With the Take the hit Boon, 1 Aura rolls your Armour when you take the hit. |
| **Keep a Catch quiet** | On your own roll, 1 Aura turns a Catch your way for a moment, or keeps it quiet this time. |
| **Traits** | Free, as often as they say. An Invoked trait costs the Aura it names. |
| **A Spark** | One for each Title, and they all come back every downtime. One for taking the GM's trouble (all yours full? give it to someone who's spent one). A spent one back from the GM for a great moment, never past your number. The Sparks Boon (+1), and the Burden (−1). |
| **Spend a Spark** | Roll a failed roll again. Take an extra action this round. Make an opportunity, even from the past. Add a permission for the scene. Turn down the GM's trouble. In downtime, roll your own dice again. |

## Actions {#s-levers-actions}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **An extra action** | Hold it: nothing this round but your stride, and you've got two next round. A Spark. *Second Wind* (once a scene). *Unseen* (condition). The Extra action Boon (once a scene). |
| **Put them together** | A combo: actions into the same thing at the same time, yours or your friends', added up and rolled once, with no Aura. Your own extra action brings in another set action as a new approach (Fight with Sneak is a sneak attack), or the same one again for +1. Lines on both count once. |
| **Hand out steps** | Lead or inspire: build an order, a rallying song or a blessing from your set action, and give its steps to friends for this round. They don't have to fit. |
| **A free action, for one thing** | *Fast* (a Move action). *Quick* weapons (drawing, even from a bag). *Ready Hand* (the bag, once a round). *Rallied* (an answer). Putting something away. |
| **Someone on your side** | Summon: your action, with a reason in the story. Each of your Summon dice that beats a soul's die brings it out, and it acts in the round like anyone. |
| **Take an action away** | *Exposed*, *Frightened* (condition). On a threat, that's one of its telegraphs. |
| **Lock it in** | The Extra action Burden: you say your action before the GM telegraphs, and you're stuck with it. |
| **Shut one thing off** | *Stunned* (no answering). *Silenced* (no speaking). *Deafened* (no joining a friend's roll, or having one join yours). *Pinned* (no moving). The Defend Burden (no Defending). |
| **Choose what they do** | *Taunted* (at you). *Charmed* (one thing you ask). |
| **Get rid of a condition** | Break it: an action that beats its die, on you or a friend in reach. It fades a size every round anyway. The Conditions Boon: one you name can't touch you. The Conditions Burden: one doesn't fade on you, and stays until someone breaks it. |
| **Stop a telegraph** | Answer it. Take down its source. Take away what it needs: strip the tag, disarm the crossbow. Running doesn't stop one aimed at you, but getting out of an area does. |

## Defence {#s-levers-defence}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One roll for the round** | Anything attacked rolls its defence once a round, and that roll stands against every die that comes at it. Ties go to the defender. |
| **A roll for every die** | Defend (your action, and it uses no steps): roll your Armour once for every die coming at you, then choose which roll blocks which. Confidence works the same against fear, lies and pressure. |
| **Draw the blows** | Defend: everything one enemy aimed at friends within your stride comes at you instead. The Defend Boon pulls from two. A summon can pull the attacks on someone onto itself, as its action, and roll against each. |
| **Take it for a friend** | Take the hit: step in front of an attack aimed at someone within your stride and take it, free, with no defence roll. Anyone can, summons too. The Take the hit Boon: 1 Aura to roll your Armour against it. The Burden: taking the hit costs you 1 Aura. Answer for them, standing in the way. *Covered* (condition). |
| **Armour one size bigger** | Each piece of plain armour you wear. Gear that says so. The Armour Boon. *Warded*, *Covered* (condition). *Steady Hands*. |
| **Confidence one size bigger** | Every Title. Every bond with three live connections. Clothes that fit the scene. Gear or traits that say so: *Iron Will*, *Style*. The Confidence Boon. *Heartened* (condition). |
| **Armour or Confidence down** | A consequence. Spending *Poisoned* (Armour) or *Shaken* (Confidence). The Armour and Confidence Burdens, which can't go on a d2. |
| **Past d12** | A second die, keep the best. The extra die steps down first. |
| **Back up to full** | Armour: a purse of gold at a smith, or a downtime slot. Confidence: both slots, facing your fear, keeping a promise to yourself. |
| **Back up a size or more** | *Heartened* (one size). *Cool Under Pressure* (three, once a scene). |
| **Put the blow somewhere else** | The GM names the cost. Offer another that fits and costs about as much: Armour, Confidence, a Hurt, something you carry, your Aspiration, the party Goal, a clock. Attuned things can't be broken. |
| **Cancel a Hurt** | *Warded* (condition). *Tenacious* (once a scene). |
| **Stay where you are** | *Braced* (condition). |

## Hurt and Healing {#s-levers-hurt}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **More bars** | *Unyielding* (+1 bar). The Hurt Boon (+1 bar, as often as you take it). Playing solo (+1 bar). The Hurt Burden takes one away. |
| **Heal in a scene** | A heal: an action against the patient's heal die, and every die that beats it clears a box, so split it to heal more. A quick heal: 1 Aura, one box, no roll, and it still takes your action. Traits and gear that say so, like *Field Dressing*, *Healer's Hands* and a *Medical kit*. |
| **Heal a group** | An area die that counts friends (1 Aura and a split). Each friend it reaches clears a box if your heal beats their heal die. |
| **An easier heal die** | It's a d6, a size bigger for each Catch on your Hurt track. The Heals on you Boon makes it a size smaller, and its Burden a size bigger. With the Regen Burden, your heal die's rolled twice and the higher counts. |
| **Heal yourself** | The Regen Boon: at the end of each round, roll your heal die, and a top face clears a box. |
| **Clear the loose boxes** | The end of every scene, free: you're back to 3 boxes for each Catch on your Hurt track. |
| **Heal in downtime** | A slot for each Catch on your Hurt track, and none heal free. A trait that says so, like *Tend the Crew*, spends one slot to clear a Catch on everyone in the crew. The Hurt Catches Boon heals one free each downtime, and with the Burden yours only heal with someone else's slot. |
| **Lose a box for good** | A Scar, when you Blaze and don't choose to die. It's for the story, and it doesn't do anything else. |
| **Blaze** | Take a Hurt with every box full: something extraordinary happens, no roll. From your first Blaze, you can choose to die. |

## Threats {#s-levers-threats}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One roll to beat** | The first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls its Challenge, and everyone who goes at it that round has to beat that roll. A *Layered* threat rolls its dice, and its best one stands. |
| **Strip a tag** | A win aimed at it. Spending *Burning*, *Bleeding* or *Poisoned*. An *Acid vial*. |
| **End it in one win** | An opening: its weak point, helpless, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two tags, Tough or not. |
| **Wear down Tough** | It works like hit points. Every winning die against it takes off the number it rolled. A condition that would strip a tag takes its die's size off instead. |
| **Find a hidden tag** | Study, with an area die for a whole room. *Quick Study*, *Predator's Eye*. *Sighted*, *Spotted* (condition). |
| **Turn a tag against it** | Bring it into your roll (1 Aura). |
| **One die fewer** | Against a *Layered* threat or stake, every tag stripped. |
| **End it without a fight** | Strip what drives it, or bring it into your roll. |
| **Cover for each other** | Anyone can take the hit, threats too: one steps in front of a blow meant for another within its stride. |
| **Catch its soul** | When a creature falls: your action, your Summon dice against its Challenge, and +2 on a die for 1 Aura. Beat it and its soul goes into one of your utility slots. Miss and it's gone. |

## Moving {#s-levers-moving}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Stride** | 3 to start, free every round, before, between or after your action. *Light on my Feet* +2. The Stride Boon +2, and the Burden −2. A *Heavy* Catch on an attuned thing −1. *Fleet* (condition): your stride again. |
| **Further** | A Move action: roll whatever makes you fast here, plus the action, and the number is extra squares. Its dice add. *Fast*, *Sprinter*. |
| **Difficult ground** | 2 a square. *Sure-Footed* ignores it. The Getting around Boon: climb, swim or cross it at full stride (one of them each time you take it). The Burden: 3 a square. |
| **Somewhere else** | A teleport permission does it for free. Further, or bringing friends, is going beyond (1 Aura). |
| **Pin, drag, knock down** | *Pinned*, *Grabbed*, *Staggered*, *Prone*. *Grapple* weapons. |

## The Scene and the Story {#s-levers-story}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Make something true** | A Spark, from your past or your present. *Prepared Mind*, *Contingency*. The Oracle, when all three dice match. |
| **Find something that helps** | Study: if it could be here, it is. *Tactical Mind*. *Perfect Recall*. |
| **Use the place** | Bring the ground into your roll (1 Aura). *Guided* (condition) does it for free. *Scout Ahead* adds three ground tags. |
| **Do more than a permission does** | Go beyond, for 1 Aura: phase through a wall, an aimed shot that takes out the tag you choose, a creature's form for the scene. The Going beyond Boon: once a scene, free. |
| **Do something big** | A bigger roll against a bigger Challenge: more lines on your set action, more Titles that fit, friends in a combo, someone leading. |
| **Hit many things** | An area die (1 Aura and a split). A *Sweep* weapon, free, within reach. |
| **Have the right thing** | A Spark. *I Have the Thing*. *Never Unarmed*. *Cache*. A purse of gold, mid-scene, from someone selling. |
| **Carry more** | The Kit Boon (one more item, each time you take it). *Prepared*. The Kit Burden carries one fewer. |
| **Stop a clock** | Work the stake: its clock doesn't tick that round. Beat an event to slow it. |
| **Speed up a clock** | Beat an event to drive it. *Hunted* (condition). A consequence. |
| **Know something** | A permission that plainly covers knowing it. Study. *Cold Reader*, *Hard to Lie To*. *Truth serum*. The Oracle. |

## Growth {#s-levers-growth}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **A step closer** | Beat the die of your Aspiration, the Goal or a project: an action when it's within reach, or a slot in downtime. One step a roll. |
| **A new Title** | An Aspiration climbs past d12. It comes with a trait and one permission, a size of Confidence and one more Spark. |
| **A new permission** | A Grow in downtime, on one of your Titles, up to six on it. It has to fit inside the Title. Put it on every set action it fits nearly every time. |
| **A different permission** | Swap one in downtime for another that fits your Title, as long as it makes sense for your character. |
| **A Boon** | Two permission slots on one of your Titles: built in when you make your character, or two permissions swapped for it in downtime, and back. A Burden on an attuned thing can pay for one instead. |
| **A new trait, connection or item** | A Grow each: a new trait, a new connection, or get something. |
| **More on an attuned thing** | A downtime slot for every new permission, and a material for every new stage: Awakened (1, or 2 with a Catch), Tempered (2, or 4 with a Burden), Perfected (3, or 6 with a Burden and a Catch). |
| **A new soul** | Catch one when a creature falls, and put it into your summoning attunement in downtime. |
| **An extra slot** | *Tireless* (once a downtime). |
| **A bigger crew** | The party Goal's done: a new Reputation and two picks (Power, Wealth, a crew tag, a party resource). The crew takes something big, or a faction it struck falls: one pick. A job that says so. Rebuild, back to its best. |

### Boons and Burdens {#s-levers-boons}

A **Boon** turns one of these levers your way for good, and it costs two permission slots on one of your Titles. A **Burden** turns one against you. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, where it pays for two more permissions on that thing, or for a Boon for you. You can take the same one more than once, and you give each one a name from the story, like *Iron Lungs* for an extra Hurt bar.

The menu covers your Hurt bars, heals on you, regen, Hurt Catches in downtime, your Aura maximum, stride, getting around, an extra action, splits, ties, going beyond, Armour, Confidence, conditions, Defend, taking the hit, summons, Sparks and how much you carry. Two more Boons get you Aura back, *Beat a threat* and *In your element*, and they've got no Burden. Bringing things in, leading, downtime slots and bonds aren't on it, and that's on purpose. The whole menu, every Boon next to its Burden, is in [Boons and Burdens](#s-boons).

## Writing Your Own {#s-levers-writing}

Everything in Zi'Dara is built from the same few shapes, so price new things against them.

| You're writing | It gets |
| --- | --- |
| **A passive trait** | +1 step on one kind of thing, or one number moved a little: stride +2, one more thing carried, one more Hurt bar, Aura maximum +1. |
| **A once-a-scene trait** | One lever from this appendix, used once: an extra action, a second roll, something small that was there all along, a failure made good in one narrow field. |
| **An invoked trait** | The same kind of lever, for 1 Aura each time. |
| **A once-a-downtime trait** | Something that changes the next session: a meeting, a hidden fact, an extra slot. |
| **A condition** | One step for or against, and one strong lever when it's spent. |
| **A tag** | A reason it's still standing, a way it comes at you, or what drives it. A tag only changes dice when it says so. |
| **A plain item** | Just what it is: a line on your roll when it fits, and it does what that thing does. A weapon brings its properties, and each piece of armour is a size of Armour. No Catch. |
| **A utility item you use up** | One lever, used once, with no roll. Cross it off. |
| **A keepsake** | Something that matters to your character. It does what it would plainly do in the story, and it has no rule of its own. |
| **An attuned thing** | Permissions of its own: 1 at Awakened, 2 at Tempered and 3 at Perfected, and as many again for a Catch, then a Burden, then both. Six at most, with one Catch and one Burden. |
| **A Boon or a Burden** | One lever off the menu, with a name from the story. A Burden has to cost something you actually use. |

:::rule
**The test.** If what you wrote adds a number to a number, like *+2 to hit*, rewrite it as a permission, a step, or a lever from this list. Zi'Dara compares dice. The only things that add are a Move action's dice, and the +2 that Aura buys on a catching or summoning die.
:::
