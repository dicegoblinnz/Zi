A **lever** is anything that changes a result: a bigger die, a second try, a hit that lands somewhere else. Here's every one of them, and where it comes from. Use it to plan a character, to pick an archetype or an item, to write an item, or to answer a question at the table without making something up.

Where a lever comes from a condition or an item, treat those as examples and write your own in the same shape. Where it comes from an archetype, it's that archetype's, and nobody else gets it.

## Dice {#s-levers-dice}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **A bigger Root** | 5 from every Title, spread across your Roots however you like, and 1 from every permission on that Root. Nothing else adds to a Root. A bigger Root is bigger dice and more points, both at once. |
| **A whole Root more** | Bring a Root in: name the permission that explains how it gets in on what you're doing, and pay 1 point of it or 3 Aura, and all of it goes on the roll. Both other Roots, if two permissions explain them, each paid on its own. A truth from an Oracle's *Foretold* is a permission everyone in the crew has, this scene. A Maker's tag and a Shifter's animal tag work like permissions too. *Unseen* and *Rallied* (conditions) bring one in for nothing when you spend them. |
| **An item's tier** | When an item fits what you're doing, its tier's that many steps on top of your Root, 1 to 6. One item a roll, the one doing the work, and a Root you bring in brings no item of its own. A weapon or a tool on a roll it fits. A guard, like a shield, only on a roll avoiding harm, once a landing. Armour, packs and vessels never. A broken item adds nothing till it's mended. The Maker's *Tune it up* (1 Aura) lets a making die that wins put one a tier higher till the scene ends, as far as tier 4, and *Made it earlier* (1 Aura) gives you or a friend a tier 2 item till the scene ends, on top of your six. The Shifter's *Tooth and claw* (1 Aura, held) makes an animal tag a tier 2 item the same way, while it's up. |
| **2 steps** | An awakening that fits the roll, and 2 more for each Aura you put in it, or each charge from a vessel. An attuned item that fits adds its tier and its awakening's steps. A Maker can put their own Aura in a friend's. |
| **One step** | A condition for you, or on the threat you go at. Titles, permissions, archetypes and abilities are never counted, and a Resource's never a step. |
| **A whole Root from friends** | A combo: they put their actions into the same thing, each bringing the Root that fits how they're helping, all of it, and nobody pays Aura for the combo. Each of them can bring another Root in with their own permission, and pays for it themselves. |
| **Steps from a Leader** | An order or a rallying song: a Root at full and any brought in, handed out as steps to friends who can hear or see you, for this round. They don't have to fit. *Where It's Needed* moves a step for each of you from one roll to another before anyone rolls. |
| **3 steps a point** | The Bloodsworn's: every point their Body loses is 3 steps on an action, on the roll they spent it on or, from a hit, on their next. *Bleed for it* (1 Aura) spends a point of Body for 3 more. *Not yet* (1 Aura) holds their blood's steps till they let go of them, all on one roll. |
| **Dice from hiding** | The Shadow's pool: the dice you rolled to get into hiding, added to any roll after that, talking and Study included, as their own dice or as steps (a d8 is 4 steps). Each one's gone once you use it. *Pass it on* (1 Aura) gives one to a friend. |
| **A bigger die** | More steps. Two sides a step: 1 is a d2, and 6 is a d12. |
| **More dice** | Past 6 steps, another die built the same way: 7 is a d12 and a d2, and 12 is two d12s. Split your steps any way you like. The Mark die, when you act against something *Marked*. |
| **Split it** | As many dice as you like, any sizes, as long as they add up to your steps, a d12 at most each. A d12 and a d8 can be two d10s, or five d4s. Every die's an intent. Small dice fish for Aura, and they lose more often. |
| **Change a die's number** | The Oracle's foresight: swap a number it rolled into anyone's die before the round ends, friend or foe, as long as the number fits the die. The Gambler's flip: once a round, any die you can see, its size plus 1 take away what it rolled, and *Another flip* (1 Aura) and *Double or nothing* (2 Aura) do more. The Gambler's *Loaded* (3 Aura): a die of yours or a friend's that's just landed reads as its top face, once a roll. A number swapped in, a die flipped or a *Loaded* die gives no Aura and doesn't explode. |
| **A smaller telegraph** | Strip the tag that makes it bigger, like *Commanding*. A glancing blow, when the GM says so. A Challenge never shrinks, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **An area** | The Elementalist's: one of your dice can be how far instead of an intent, as a burst, a cone or a line. It's free, and *Sculpt* leaves someone out of it. A *Sweep* weapon, for anyone, at everyone within reach. |
| **Reach a second friend** | A ward, a blessing or any condition you put on a friend goes on one friend, at the die you built, and the second die of a split can put it on another friend you can touch. A heal reaches one person. |
| **Advantage** | Several dice on one intent: your best die counts. |
| **Win a tie** | Free, when you're defending. 1 Aura, when you're acting. An Illusionist's illusion only has to meet the Challenge. *Fortunate* (condition). In downtime, an extra crew resource. The Ties Burden loses every one, even defending. |
| **Roll again** | A point of Soul, on a roll you just failed: all your dice or just one, and the threat's dice stay where they landed. It works in downtime too. *Blessed* (condition, if that action fails). The Gambler's *Roll it again* (1 Aura): any one die you can see, and the new number stands. |
| **+2 on a die** | A Push, on most archetypes' mechanics, after you roll: 1 Aura. It's +3 if you've taken the archetype twice, and +4 three times. A die it takes to its top face, or past it, doesn't give you Aura. See [Archetypes](#s-levers-archetypes). |
| **Dice that add** | Dice you put on moving. A Push. A Warrior's dice in a fight: a top face gives you 1 Aura, then you roll that die again and add the new number on, for as long as it keeps landing on top. A flat bonus on a heal roll, like Sister Hale's Thimble's 3. |
| **A die becomes a d12** | *Surging* (condition). |
| **Their win becomes a tie** | *Hexed* (condition). |
| **One of their dice doesn't count** | *Blinded* (condition). |
| **No roll at all** | A permission that plainly covers it, with nothing pushing back. Spending a condition. A quick heal (1 Aura). An awakening that says it just does it, like *Up any hull*. Abilities like the Shadow's *Vanish*, the Shifter's *Become it*, the Bard's *Crescendo* and the Leader's *Take charge*, and every signature ability. A Resource that plainly does it, like a *Spare lockpick* or an *Acid vial*. *Disguised*. |

## Aura {#s-levers-aura}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Fill up** | Every new scene, to your maximum. There's none in downtime. |
| **Gain 1** | Every die of yours that wins, your defence included. Every top face, win or lose, area dice and recovery rolls included, but not a die a Push or an awakening took there, a summon's, a number an Oracle swapped in or a die a Gambler flipped. What a heal heals never gives any. Letting a Catch or a hostile thread bite, as often as you like, as long as it brings real trouble. Bringing real trouble on yourself with no Catch. The GM, when a great setup doesn't land. *Inspired* (condition). A top face when you roll [the Oracle](#oracle). The Traits that say so. |
| **Past your maximum** | While you're acting, it can go past it. Once you've finished acting for the round, anything over is gone, so spend it first. |
| **A bigger maximum** | Nothing, bar what says so. It's 5 to start, however many attuned things you've got. |
| **A smaller maximum** | The Aura Burden (−2). A [held](#s-abilities) ability, its Aura for as long as it's up. |
| **Share it** | When it comes in while you're not acting, like off your defence, it stops at your maximum, and you can give the rest to a friend in the scene. |
| **Spend 1** | An ability, each time you use it, or 2 or 3 for a few big ones. A held one's paid once and stays up till the scene ends or you drop it. 2 more steps on an awakening that fits. Borrow one thing through a bond thread. Go beyond what something normally does. Win a tie when you're acting. Intercept for a friend within your stride (one friend for each Aura). A quick heal (with your action). Keep a Catch quiet this time. |
| **Spend 3** | Bring a Root in, when a permission explains it, instead of a point of that Root. |
| **Spend 4 or 5** | A [signature](#s-abilities) ability, an archetype's one big thing: once a scene, with no roll, and paying again gets you nothing more. |
| **Keep what you'd lose** | A [vessel](#s-vessels), up to its tier in charge: the Aura past your maximum when you finish acting, Aura that'd stop at your maximum while you're not acting, and what's left when the scene ends can go in instead. Or a point from any Root, mid-scene, with no action. Charge pays only for your own abilities and your own awakenings' extra steps, a charge an Aura, and never for a signature at 5. It never turns back into Aura, never goes to a friend, and keeps through downtime. |
| **Call a summon** | The Summoner's: a d4 of summons for each Aura, as part of an action, none of them bigger than the soul's own die. *Call the horde* (4 Aura, signature): once a scene, as your action, every soul in your attunement comes out at a d4 for no more Aura. *Call it now* (1 Aura) calls one out the moment you need it, without waiting for your action. |

## Threads {#s-levers-threads}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Two kinds** | A bond thread is something good between you, and a hostile thread is something gone bad. Either can be strained, which means nearly snapped, and with no thread at all you're neutral. |
| **Borrow** | 1 Aura borrows one thing off a friend's sheet for your roll, through a bond thread you share, and it does there what it does for them, so their permission can bring a Root of yours in, which you still pay for. Or one of a faction's assets, through a bond thread with it. It doesn't strain anything. You can't borrow through a strained thread, or a hostile one. |
| **Strain** | Lean on them in the story, like calling in the favour, using their name, dragging them into it or using the secret, and you get a point of Soul back, as far as its maximum. It's free, it doesn't take an action, and the bond thread's strained. A thread with someone in the crew is strained for both of you. |
| **A hostile thread bites** | Like a Catch: when it brings you real trouble, take 1 Aura, or a point of Soul for a big bite. Doing right by them strains it, and doing it again snaps it, and the grudge is over. |
| **Mend** | Make it right with them, by roleplaying it, doing them a favour or paying them, whatever suits them. That happens in downtime or in a scene with them, not in the middle of the fight where you strained it. Then it's whole again. |
| **Snap** | Strain it again before it's mended. You still get the point of Soul back, but cross the thread out. When the last thread goes, so does the bond. |
| **New threads** | Two bond threads with the crew when you make your character, from Connect, and a third if you write a hostile thread too. A Grow. Everyone writes one when the Party Goal's done. |

How a strained thread plays out at the table, from both sides, is in [Playing the strain](#s-playing-the-strain).

## Actions {#s-levers-actions}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One action** | Everyone's got one a round, and nothing gets you another. Every die in it's an intent, so splitting is how you do more: one roll can answer a telegraph, go at a threat, break a condition and get you across the room. |
| **Go first** | The Shifter's *First to move* (2 Aura): your action as the round starts, before the first telegraph, against each threat's roll for the round, rolled now. You can't answer a telegraph that round. |
| **Put them together** | A combo: actions into the same thing at the same time, yours and your friends', rolled once, with every action bringing the Root that fits how they're helping and the one item doing their part, and no Aura for the combo. Whoever rolls takes the Aura. |
| **Hand out steps** | The Leader's: an order or a rallying song from a Root at full, and any brought in, handed to friends who can hear or see you, for this round. They don't have to fit. The Push (1 Aura) is +2 on one of a friend's dice, on a roll your steps went into, and *Everyone gets it* (1 Aura) gives every one of them all your steps. |
| **Free, for one thing** | *Quick* weapons (drawing one). Putting something away. Saying a Resource. Intercepting (1 Aura, or free for a Guardian). Your stride. |
| **Someone on your side** | The Summoner's: call a soul out with Aura, as part of an action, with a reason in the story, and it acts in the round like anyone. The Face's *Turn them* (1 Aura): a Mook or a Named threat your influence took a die from fights for the crew till the round ends. |
| **Take an action away** | *Exposed*, *Frightened* (condition). On a threat, that's one of its telegraphs. |
| **Lock it in** | The Acting Burden: you say your action before the GM telegraphs, and you're stuck with it. |
| **Shut one thing off** | *Stunned* (no answering). *Silenced* (no speaking). *Deafened* (no joining a friend's roll, or having one join yours). *Pinned* (no moving). The Defend Burden (no Defending). The Intercept Burden (no intercepting). |
| **Choose what they do** | *Taunted* (at you). *Charmed* (one thing you ask). The Face's influence: each of a telegraph's dice your best influence die beats does what you say. The Face's *Grease the palm* (1 Aura): tick a Resource that'd tempt someone who can be bought, and one of a threat's dice this round is yours, no roll. The Warrior's *Face me* (1 Aura): a threat one of your dice beats sends its next telegraph at you. |
| **Get rid of a condition** | Break it: a die that beats its die, on you or a friend in reach. It fades a size every round anyway. A Resource that does it, like a *Medical kit* or an *Antidote*. The Shifter's *Slip it* (1 Aura): strip an animal tag to break one on you. The Bard's *Shake it off* (1 Aura): one person who can hear you drops *Frightened*, *Charmed*, *Taunted* or *Shaken*, with no roll. The Healer's *Clean it out* (1 Aura): a heal roll ends one a healer could treat. The Bloodsworn's *Bite down* (1 Aura): a point of your Body breaks one on you, with no roll. The Bard's *Throw it back* (2 Aura): *Frightened*, *Charmed*, *Taunted* or *Shaken* going on you or a friend goes on the threat that put it there instead. The Conditions Burden: one doesn't fade on you, and stays until someone breaks it. |
| **Stop a telegraph** | Answer it, with one of your dice or more. Take down its source. Take away what it needs: strip the tag, disarm the crossbow. The Bard's song: a Defend against fear, lies and pressure for everyone who can hear it, split as you like, with each of its dice stopping one. The Guardian's *In the way* (1 Aura): intercept one aimed at a stake, a place or a clock. Running doesn't stop one aimed at you, but getting out of an area does. |

## Defence {#s-levers-defence}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Your defence** | The Root a telegraph's at, rolled once when the telegraphs land, at full, split however you like: your Body against what's at your Body, your Mind against what's at your Mind and your Soul against what's at your Soul. Every die stops one telegraph it meets or beats, and everything else gets through. It's your whole Root however many points it's got left. Ties go to you. |
| **More dice to stop with** | Defend (your action): a Root that fits how you're defending, at full, rolled on top of the one they land on, and split as you like. Each die stops one it meets or beats, as long as how you're defending fits it. The Bard's song does the same against fear, lies and pressure for everyone who can hear it. |
| **A bigger defence** | Bring a Root in on it, the same as any roll. An awakening that fits how you're defending. *Warded* and *Covered* (a step on your Body), *Heartened* (a step on your Mind). A guard's tier, once a landing, like a shield on your Body. The Guardian's Push (1 Aura): +2 on one of their defence dice. The Warrior's *Second Skin*: their armour's a guard as well, so the armour on that Root adds its tier on a roll avoiding harm, and *Parry* (1 Aura, held) does the same with their weapon while it's up. It's one item a roll, always. The Hunter's *Watch its hands* (1 Aura): the Mark die on the defence of whoever a *Marked* creature's blows land on. |
| **Defence down** | Spending *Poisoned*: your Body rolls as a d2 when the telegraphs land this round. *Shaken*: a step less on your Mind. Too many telegraphs at one Root, because your dice get smaller as you split them to meet them. |
| **Soak** | [Armour](#s-armour) on the Root a hit's at takes it first, a point of soak a point, as many as its tier, every piece on that Root one after the other, so Bram's plate and helmet soak 5 on his Body. A vulnerability's or a deadly threat's extra hits are a point of soak each, and 2 for 1 is soaked by the other Root's armour. It's all back at the end of every scene, and soaking never breaks it. The Armour Burden: each piece soaks 1 less. |
| **Take it for a friend** | Intercept: 1 Aura and no action, and any of the blows at a friend's Body within your stride come at you instead, for your own Body or Defend to handle. One friend for each Aura. Nobody can step in front of what's at a friend's Mind or Soul. The Guardian's, free, for every friend within their stride, and *Reach them* (1 Aura) reaches one past it. A Guardian who's a Teleporter as well reaches every friend within 10 units. A summon intercepts for free, and so do the GM's people. The Bloodsworn's *Mine to take* (1 Aura): a hit that's got through to a friend's Body within your stride comes off yours instead, unsoaked, for the blood's steps. The Guardian's *Not while I stand* (4 Aura, signature): every blow at their Body this round is stopped, their own and the ones they intercepted. Answer for a friend, standing in the way. *Covered* (condition). The Intercept Burden: you can't. |
| **One die at each of you** | A threat aims one die at each person, unless a tag like *Flurry* says it can do more. |
| **Put the hit somewhere else** | The GM names the cost. Offer another that fits and costs about as much: one of your items breaking, crossed out till it's mended, a point off your Aspiration, the Party Goal, a clock. Attuned things can't be broken. 2 for 1 always fits: take it on another Root for 2 points of it, if it's got them. The Guardian's *Put it on me* (1 Aura): the cost of a friend's miss within your stride is yours instead. |
| **Cancel a hit** | *Warded* (spend it and the hit lands on it instead). The Illusionist's *Decoy* (1 Aura): it lands on an illusion that's copied you instead. The Guardian's *Stand firm* (1 Aura): two hits that got through to them this round take 1 point between them. Break a tag a Maker gave the thing you're holding. Strip one of your animal tags, if you're a Shifter. |
| **Stay where you are** | *Braced* (condition). |

## Your Points {#s-levers-confidence}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Where they start** | Each Root's number is its points, its dice and what it can take. You start every session full, less what's locked. |
| **What's locked** | Each awakening locks a point of the Root it fits, for as long as it's attuned. Each Scar locks a point for good. The Toughness Burden locks one, the way a Scar does. Locked points are still dice. |
| **A point off** | Every hit that gets through to that Root, once the armour there's soaked what it can. Bringing that Root in on a roll. A point into a vessel, mid-scene. A point of Soul to roll again. Spending *Shaken* (a hit at your Mind). Refusing what *Charmed* asks (a hit at your Mind). Bleeding for it, if you're a Bloodsworn. |
| **Two from one hit** | A vulnerability that fits it: one more each (see [Threats](#s-levers-threats)). *Prone* and *Exposed* are the usual ones. A deadly threat's telegraph says how many. 2 for 1 on another Root. |
| **Pushing never hurts you** | You can't spend points you haven't got, so spending to 0 only leaves you exposed. Only a hit wounds you. |
| **What's at your Mind and Soul** | Fear, lies and pressure come at your Mind, and despair, temptation, curses and anything trying to take you over come at your Soul, as telegraphs like any other. Answer them, or let them ride on that Root. Nobody can intercept them, and a Bard's song is what holds fear off the crew. A specific intent, like *Frightened* or *Charmed*, happens when it gets through, unless you take it as a hit instead. |
| **Points back, mid-scene** | A Healer's heal. A quick heal for 1 Aura and your action, 1 point. Straining a bond thread, or a big bite from a Catch or a hostile thread (a point of Soul). Spending *Heartened* (a point of Mind). Take a breath: your action, and a point back on your Mind, as far as half its maximum. The Bard's *Lift them* (a point back on the Mind of every friend who can hear you) and *Crescendo* (2 Aura: 2 points, once a Lift). The Bloodsworn's *Scab over* (1 Aura): a point back on their Body at the end of the round, as far as half its maximum, once a round. The Warrior's *Blood up* (1 Aura): a point back on their Body when one of their dice puts a threat down, once a round. |

## Healing and Blaze {#s-levers-hurt}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Rest** | The end of every scene: your armour's soak is all back, and you roll each Root below its maximum, unsplit, and get back what you roll. The Healer's *Rest easy* (1 Aura): one Root of yours or a friend's rolls twice, and keeps the better. The Recovery Burden: 2 less. |
| **Heal in a scene** | The Healer's heal roll: an action, a Root unsplit, and the patient gets back what it rolls, spread across their Roots. It reaches one person, and the Healer's *Go round* (1 Aura) splits it a die a patient. The Push (1 Aura) is +2 on one of your dice when you heal. A quick heal, for anyone: 1 Aura and your action, 1 point on one of someone's Roots, no roll. A *Medical kit*, a Resource, is a quick heal with no Aura. |
| **Toll** | Every heal from someone or something else is 1 Toll, and a heal loses 5 for each Toll the patient's already got, never below 0. It drops by 1 at the end of each scene and clears in downtime. A quick heal and a rest never give it. |
| **Ease the Toll** | The Healer's *Good Hands*, and an attuned thing like the Surgeon's Casebook (*What worked*): a heal ignores a step of Toll. A flat bonus, like Sister Hale's Thimble's 3, goes on before the Toll comes off. |
| **Heal yourself** | The Bloodsworn's *Scab over* (1 Aura): a point back on your Body at the end of the round, as far as half its maximum, once a round, and it isn't a heal, so no Toll. The Warrior's *Blood up* (1 Aura) is the same when a die of yours puts a threat down. |
| **Between scenes** | The *Healers* crew asset: once a session, everyone in the crew rolls one Root unsplit and gets back what it rolls, a second rest that gives no Toll. |
| **Heal in downtime** | Downtime puts everything back: every Root on its maximum, wounds and Toll gone. |
| **As far as it goes** | Nothing goes past a Root's maximum, or past half of it, rounded up, while it's wounded, and nothing heals a Scar. |
| **A wound** | A hit on a Root with no points left: **Bloodied** (Body), **Frayed** (Mind) or **Hollow** (Soul), one each. Your dice don't change. Downtime or a story moment clears it. The Healer's *Close the wound* (5 Aura, signature): their action, with no roll, clears one wound on someone they can reach, and gives no points and no Toll. |
| **Blaze** | While you're wounded, on your turn: take the Scar, declare something extraordinary, and it happens, no roll, then leave the scene. From your first Blaze, you can choose to die. |
| **A Scar** | A hit on a wounded Root with no points left, and you leave the scene. It's a negative permission on that Root, something they can't do that the GM holds them to, and it locks a point of it for good. Three downtime slots grow it into a Trait as well, and the Scar still holds. A Scar that locks a Root's last point is your last scene. The Healer's *Pull them back* (1 Aura) keeps someone in the scene when they take one. The GM can take a Scar away when the story's earned it. |

## Threats {#s-levers-threats}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **One roll to beat** | The first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls its Challenge, and everyone who goes at it that round has to beat that roll. A *Layered* threat rolls its dice, and its best one stands. |
| **Strip a tag** | A win aimed at it, or one tick off its multiplier. Spending *Burning*, *Bleeding* or *Poisoned*. An *Acid vial*, a Resource. A *Piercing* weapon gets past *Armoured*, and a *Breaker* past *Shield*. |
| **End it in one win** | An opening: its weak point, helpless, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two whole tags, multipliers and all. |
| **Tick down a multiplier** | Every win against a tag with a multiplier, like *Armoured ×4*, ticks one off, and the GM says what's left. A condition that would strip a tag ticks one off instead. |
| **Hit harder** | A vulnerability: every one that fits a win that strips a tag or lands a hit adds one more tick, and you choose where it goes, on the same tag or another one the hit could reach. *Prone*, *Exposed* (condition). The Warrior's *Opened* (1 Aura). Hidden ones are hidden tags, so Study finds them. |
| **Hit you harder** | A *Deadly* threat: every die of it that gets through is 2 hits, or more, a point of soak or a point each, and its telegraphs say so. Each costs the GM its steps and half again for each hit past the first. |
| **Find a hidden tag** | Study. *Sighted*, *Spotted* (condition). A few abilities, like the Face's *Know their price* (1 Aura), when your influence takes a threat's die, and the Bloodsworn's *Now I know you* (1 Aura), one of how whatever hit you comes at you. |
| **Mark it** | The Hunter's: one of your dice at a creature. Win and it's *Marked* at the die you won with, and anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too. The Push (1 Aura) is +2 on the Mark die whenever it's rolled, whoever's rolling, and *Mark the pack* (1 Aura) marks two more. It fades a size a round, and when what it's on falls, it jumps to another creature the Hunter can see. |
| **Turn its blows** | The Face's: build your action but don't roll it, and put it on a threat as influence. Before one of its telegraphs lands, roll your influence against it, and every one of its dice your best influence die beats does what you say instead, as long as it fits the story. The Push (1 Aura) is +2 on one influence die. |
| **One die fewer** | Against a *Layered* threat or stake, every tag stripped. |
| **End it without a fight** | Strip what drives it. |
| **Cover for each other** | The GM's people can intercept too, for free: one steps in front of a blow meant for another. |
| **Catch its soul** | The Summoner's: when a creature falls, a die on it against its Challenge, and the Push (1 Aura) is +2 on that die. Beat it and you're carrying its soul, which ticks one of your Resources till downtime. Miss and it's gone. *Catch it whole* (1 Aura): the die that put it down catches it as well. |

## Moving {#s-levers-moving}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Stride** | 3 to start, free every round, before, during or after your action. An item or an awakening that says so adds to it. The Stride Burden −2. A *Heavy* Catch −1, for each thing you've got that's *Heavy*. *Fleet* (condition): your stride again. The Bard's *Marching song* (1 Aura, held): while you keep singing, everyone who hears it moves as far as whoever of you moves furthest. The Leader's *On me* (2 Aura): every friend who can hear or see you moves their stride right now, even off their turn. The Warrior's *Press on* (1 Aura): your stride right now when one of your dice wins in a fight. |
| **Further** | Put some of your dice on moving, from a Root that fits how you're moving, and what they roll is how many more units you go. Dice on moving add. |
| **Difficult ground** | 2 a square. An awakening, an item or an animal tag that says so climbs, swims or crosses it at full stride, like Vesk's *Up the walls* once her grappling line's Tempered. The Guardian's *Hold the line* (1 Aura, held): everywhere within your stride is difficult ground for threats, never for friends. The Getting around Burden: 3 a square. |
| **Somewhere else** | The Teleporter's stride can be a teleport of up to 10 units. *Portal* (1 Aura) opens a portal between two spots you can see within 10 units till the round ends, and anyone who steps in either end comes out the other. *Swap* (1 Aura) swaps places with a friend or a Mook you can see within 10 units, and *Take them with you* (1 Aura) brings someone willing you're touching. The Elementalist's *Ride the line* (1 Aura): you go with your line, anywhere along it. The Teleporter's *Everybody out* (5 Aura, signature): you and every willing friend you can see within 10 units go to one spot, and whatever was about to land on you misses. |
| **Pin, drag, knock down** | *Pinned*, *Grabbed*, *Staggered*, *Prone*. *Grapple* weapons. The Guardian's *Carry them* (1 Aura): a friend comes with you. |

## The Scene and the Story {#s-levers-story}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Make something true** | The Oracle's *Foretold* (1 Aura): win a Study and say something that wasn't obvious to anyone else. It's true, and anyone in the crew can bring a Root in on it this scene. [The Oracle](#oracle), when all three dice match. |
| **Use the place** | Say how. The ground's tags are open for anyone, so they're often what explains bringing a Root in, or what makes something possible at all. *Guided* (condition) finds your way with no roll. |
| **Do more than a permission does** | Go beyond, for 1 Aura: phase through a wall, an aimed shot that takes out the tag you choose, calling up someone who died a hundred years ago. |
| **Do something big** | A bigger roll against a bigger Challenge: a bigger Root, another brought in, an awakening with Aura on it, friends in a combo, a Leader's steps. |
| **Hit many things** | The Elementalist's area, and *Let it loose* (5 Aura, signature): their element's condition at a d8 on every threat in the scene, with no roll. A *Sweep* weapon, within reach. Split your dice. |
| **Make them believe it** | The Illusionist's: roll your illusion dice against the threats' Challenge, and if you meet or beat it, they believe your illusion until they interact with it or try to get through it. To see through it before then, a threat has to beat what you rolled. *Make it real* (1 Aura): it's real till the round ends. *Grand illusion* (5 Aura, signature): one illusion as big as the scene, believed by every threat in it with no roll. |
| **Do what an animal does** | The Shifter's animal tags: your action, rolled against the tag's die (most are a d4, swimming's a d6, flying's a d8 and a breath attack's a d12). Beat it and the tag's yours until downtime. *Become it* (1 Aura) takes one with no roll till the scene ends, though it can't take a hit for you. |
| **Give a thing a tag** | The Maker's: your action against the item's size, from a d4 for something tiny like a ring to a d12 for anything intricate. Each die that beats it gives the item a tag for the scene, and *Masterwork* (1 Aura) keeps them till downtime. *Mend it* (1 Aura): a making die that wins mends something broken instead, an item, something in the scene or a tag you made. |
| **Have the right thing** | Say a [Resource](#s-resources): something ordinary you've got, said the moment you need it, and ticked. It does what the thing does, and it's never steps. You've got 3, and a pack gives half its tier more, rounded up. The Shadow's *I had it planned* (1 Aura) says one without ticking it, and the Maker's *Made it earlier* (1 Aura) makes you the thing instead, an item at tier 2, with no Resource ticked. A purse of gold buys something ordinary from someone selling. |
| **Get them back** | Downtime puts all your Resources back. The GM gives one back mid-session when the story restocks you, like a market or a friend's cellar. |
| **Carry more** | Six items, and your keepsake on top. The Maker's *Made it earlier* makes one more till the scene ends, and the Shifter's *Tooth and claw* one more while it's up. The Maker's *Keep this on you* charm isn't one of the six either. The Kit Burden: five. The Resources Burden: one fewer Resource. |
| **Stop a clock** | Work the stake: its clock doesn't tick that round. Beat an event to slow it. The Oracle's *Not today* (2 Aura): one clock, event or stake doesn't step down this round, with no roll. |
| **Speed up a clock** | Beat an event to drive it. *Hunted* (condition). A consequence. |
| **Know something** | A permission that plainly covers knowing it. Study. A *truth serum*. [The Oracle](#oracle). Abilities that ask the GM, like the Bard's *Old songs* and the Oracle's *What's coming* (1 Aura each). |

## Growth {#s-levers-growth}

| Lever | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **A step closer** | Beat the die of the Goal or a project: an action when it's within reach, or a slot in downtime. One step a roll. |
| **A point in your Aspiration** | A downtime slot, or the crew's action supporting it. No roll. A setback takes one back off. |
| **A new Title, archetype or attuned thing** | An Aspiration gets its 5 points, and you pick one. A new Title's 5 on your Roots, spread however you like, and comes with one permission. A new archetype comes with its mechanic, its Trait and one ability, and taking one you've already got makes its Push bigger. A new attuned thing starts at Awakened with one awakening, which locks a point of the Root it fits. |
| **A new permission** | A Grow in downtime, on one of your Titles, up to six on it. It has to fit inside the Title, and it's 1 on its Root. |
| **A new ability** | A downtime slot, off one of your archetypes' lists, up to six on it. |
| **A different permission** | Swap one in downtime for another that fits your Title, as long as it makes sense for your character. |
| **A better item** | A Grow: a new item at tier 2, or one you've got a tier up, as far as tier 3. Money buys tier 4, and tiers 5 and 6 only come out of the story, with a material. Aura never buys a tier. |
| **A new thread or item** | A Grow each: a new thread, or get something. Everyone writes a new thread when the Party Goal's done. |
| **More on an attuned thing** | A downtime slot for every new awakening, and a material for every new stage: Awakened (1, or 2 with a Catch), Tempered (2, or 4 with a Burden), Perfected (3, or 6 with a Burden and a Catch). |
| **A new soul** | A Summoner catches one when a creature falls, and puts it into their summoning attunement in downtime. |
| **A bigger crew** | The Party Goal's done: a new Reputation and two picks (a new resource, a new asset, a new holding). The crew takes something big, or a faction it's been fighting falls: one pick. A big job that says so pays a resource. Rebuild with the crew's action: an asset or a resource the crew's lost, built back. |

### Burdens {#s-levers-boons}

A **Burden** turns one of these levers against you for good. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, once it's Tempered, where it pays for two more awakenings on that thing, and you give it a name from the story, like *Bad Knee* for the Stride Burden. Nothing turns a lever your way for good in exchange for permissions. What's always true of you comes from your archetypes' Traits.

The list covers toughness, recovery, your Aura maximum, stride, getting around, acting, ties, armour, conditions, Defend, intercepting, how many items you carry and how many Resources you've got. Bringing a Root in, splitting, leading, downtime slots and bonds aren't on it, and that's on purpose. The whole list is in [Burdens](#s-boons).

### Archetypes {#s-levers-archetypes}

Every archetype has a mechanic only it can do, a Trait that's always true of you, and abilities you pay for in Aura, so a lot of the levers above come from them. [How Archetypes Work](#s-how-archetypes) has the whole of it, and each archetype is in [The Archetypes](#archetypes).

-   **Each ability says what it costs**, 1 Aura for most and 2 or 3 for a few big ones, each time you use it. A vessel's charge can pay for your own.
-   **They stack.** Pay again and you get it again: another +2, another target, another round.
-   **Held** abilities change what's true of you for the scene, like the Warrior's *Parry* or the Shifter's *All beast*. You pay once, it stays up till the scene ends or you drop it, and its Aura comes off your maximum while it's up.
-   **Signature** abilities are an archetype's one big thing, at 4 or 5 Aura: once a scene, with no roll, and paying again gets you nothing more. A vessel's charge can't pay for one at 5. Six archetypes have one: the Elementalist, the Guardian, the Healer, the Illusionist, the Summoner and the Teleporter.
-   **The Push** is the one most archetypes have: +2 on one die of the mechanic, once you've rolled and you know you need it. A die it takes to its top face, or past it, doesn't give you Aura. A Warrior's still explodes.
-   **The same archetype twice.** Its Push becomes +3, three times it's +4, and so on. It's still the one Trait.
-   **Every Trait's a lever of its own.** Six get you Aura back: the Bloodsworn's, the Gambler's, the Guardian's, the Hunter's and the Illusionist's, and the Bard's costs it. The Healer's eases the Toll, the Leader's moves steps from one roll to another, the Oracle's shows the crew three of the threats' dice before anyone moves, and the Warrior's puts their armour's tier on their defence. The Summoner pays the usual Aura to call their pocket summon back, and the Face's and the Teleporter's come once a scene.

Two spears come at Bram, a d10 and a d8, and he's Defending: his Body, 9 steps, on top of the 9 it rolls when they land, so he's got a d12 and a d6 twice over, and his shield's 2 more, a d4. The spears come up 8 and 4, and Bram rolls 7 and 3 on his d12s, 2 and 1 on his d6s and 1 on the d4. The 7 can't stop the 8, and nothing else can stop the 4. He pays 1 Aura for his Guardian's Push and makes the 7 a 9, which stops the 8, and the 4 gets through, a hit, which his plate soaks. If he'd taken Guardian twice, the Push would've made it a 10.

## Writing Your Own {#s-levers-writing}

Everything in Zi'Dara is built from the same few shapes, so price new things against them.

| You're writing | It gets |
| --- | --- |
| **A condition** | One step for or against, and one strong lever when it's spent. |
| **A tag** | A reason it's still standing, a way it comes at you, or what drives it. A tag only changes dice when it says so. A big one carries a multiplier, a weakness is a vulnerability, and a frightening one can be *Deadly*. |
| **A Maker's tag** | A special property for the scene, and it follows the same rules as a permission: a ring that lets you see in the dark is fine, and a ring that gives you haste isn't. It can justify bringing a Root in. |
| **An item** | Its kind and its tier, 1 to 6. A weapon or a tool puts its tier on a roll it fits, a guard only on a roll avoiding harm, armour soaks its tier on its Root, a pack gives half its tier more Resources, rounded up, and a vessel holds its tier in charge. A weapon brings its properties. Tiers 1 to 3 are what you can start with, 4 takes money, and 5 and 6 come out of the story. No Catch. |
| **A Resource** | Something ordinary, said when you need it and ticked. It does what the thing does, once if it's used up, with no roll. It's never steps, never soak and never magic. |
| **A keepsake** | Something that matters to your character. It's free, on top of your six items, and never steps. It does what it would plainly do in the story, and it has no rule of its own. |
| **An attuned thing** | 5 points when you make your character, and another whenever an Aspiration gives you one. It's one of your six items, at tier 2 for its 5 points, and it can't break. It has awakenings: 1 at Awakened, 2 at Tempered and 3 at Perfected, and as many again for a Catch, then a Burden, then both. Six at most, with one Catch and one Burden. |
| **An awakening** | Something the thing itself does, written like a permission, with the Root it fits. It locks a point of that Root while it's attuned, and it's 2 steps on any roll it fits, and 2 more for each Aura. A few do something no roll could, and they just do it. A few ease the Toll or add a flat bonus to heal rolls. |
| **A Burden** | One lever off the list turned against you, with a name from the story. It has to cost something you actually use, and it pays for two awakenings on an attuned thing. |

:::rule
**The test.** If what you wrote adds a number to a number, like *+2 to hit*, rewrite it as a permission, a condition, an item's tier, an awakening's steps, or a lever from this list. Zi'Dara compares dice. The only dice that add are dice on moving, a Warrior's dice exploding, a Push, and a flat bonus on a heal roll.
:::
