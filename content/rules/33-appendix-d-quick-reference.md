## Building the roll {#building-the-die}

-   Pick the Root that fits how you're doing it (Body, Mind or Soul), and roll all of it.
-   **Item:** the one doing the work, if it fits, puts its tier on in steps. One a roll. A guard's only on a roll avoiding harm, a weapon never is (bar a Warrior's *Parry*), and armour (bar a Warrior's *Second Skin*), packs and vessels add nothing. Nothing else on your sheet is counted.
-   **Bring a Root in:** name the permission that explains how it gets in on what you're doing, and pay 1 point of that Root or 3 Aura. All of it goes on the roll, and never another item. You can bring both in, each paid on its own.
-   **Awakenings:** one that fits is 2 steps, and each Aura you put in it is 2 more. Going beyond what something normally does is 1 Aura, sword or spell.
-   Two sides a step, up to a d12. Past 6 steps, more dice. Every roll's at least 1 step, a d2. Points spent, lost or locked never shrink the dice.
-   After you roll, your archetype's Push puts +2 on one die of its mechanic, for 1 Aura, if you've got it and you need it. It's never a step.

## Intents {#intents}

-   Every die gets an intent and a target before you roll, and every die's as good as an action: one roll can answer a telegraph, go at a threat and move you, as long as your Root fits how. Several dice on one intent is advantage: your best die counts. Dice never add, except when you move, when a Warrior's dice explode, and when an ability adds to one.
-   **Splits:** as many as you like, any sizes, as long as they add up to your steps. Small dice fish for Aura, and they lose more often.
-   **Area** (the Elementalist's): one die can be how far instead of an intent: a burst (that many squares out from a point), a cone (that long, and as wide at the end) or a line (twice as far). It costs no Aura, and you can always make it smaller. No grid: 1 to 2 is close, 3 to 5 a few steps, 6 or more the whole room. *Sweep* weapons get one die at everyone in reach, free, whoever's holding them.

## The roll {#the-roll}

-   You roll the threat's die too, in another colour. The GM almost never rolls in a round.
-   A threat rolls its defence once a round, the first time anyone goes at it, and every die at it that round has to beat that roll.
-   **Beat it:** you did it. **Tie:** the defender wins (you when it comes at you, them when you act), but if you're acting, answering included, 1 Aura wins it. An Illusionist meets or beats. **Under:** you didn't, and it only costs you when it matters.
-   **Aura:** 1 for every die of yours that wins, and 1 for every top face, defence and recovery included. Never for what a heal heals, and not for a summon's top face, a die something added to after you rolled, a number an Oracle swapped in or a flipped die.
-   **Failed?** A point of Soul rerolls it, all your dice or just one. The threat's dice stay where they landed.

## Your action {#s-qr-action}

-   One a round, and nothing gets you another. Usually it's a roll, with an intent on every die: act, answer a telegraph, move past your stride, break a condition, study. Or it's Defend, a heal if you're a Healer, a lead if you're a Leader, or taking a breath (1 point back on your Mind, as far as half its maximum, rounded up). Your stride, a few words, saying a Resource, and dropping something or putting it away are free. Intercepting costs 1 Aura but no action.
-   **Combo:** two or more actions into the same thing at the same time, yours and your friends'. You roll once, and every action brings the Root that fits how they're helping, all of it, and the one item doing their part. Each of you can bring a Root in with your own permission and pay for it yourself. No Aura for the combo, and whoever rolls takes the Aura.
-   **Lead** (the Leader's): build an order or a rallying song from a Root and hand its steps to friends who can hear or see you, this round. They don't have to fit.
-   Outside a round, it's one roll for each go at something.

## Aura {#aura}

-   Up to 5. You're full every scene, and there's none in downtime. While you're acting it can go past your maximum, and once you've finished acting for the round, anything over is gone. Off your turn it stops at your maximum, and you can give the rest to a friend in the scene.
-   **Vessel:** holds its tier in charge, and keeps it through downtime. Fill it only with Aura you'd lose (over your maximum when you finish acting, or past it off your turn), never what's left when a scene ends, or a point from any Root mid-scene, no action. A point in it is held there, like a lock, till it's spent, and points are spent first. Spend it only on your own abilities and awakenings' extra steps, a charge for each Aura. It never counts towards your maximum or goes to a friend.
-   **Spend 1:** an ability, each time you use it · 2 more steps on an awakening that fits · borrow one thing through a bond thread · go beyond · win a tie when you're acting · intercept for a friend within your stride · keep a Catch quiet.
-   **Get 1 back:** every die of yours that wins · every top face · letting a Catch bite, as often as you like, as long as it's real trouble · bringing real trouble on yourself with no Catch · a great setup that didn't land, if the GM says so.
-   **Spend 3:** bring a Root in, when a permission explains it, instead of paying a point of that Root.

## Your points {#s-qr-tracks}

-   **A Root's number is its points:** its dice, what it can take, and what you spend. Each awakening locks a point of the Root it fits, each Scar locks one for good, and a point in a vessel's held there till it's spent, so what's left is its maximum. Locked points are still dice. A new character starts full.
-   **Hits:** every telegraph die that gets through is a hit, and a hit's 1 point off its Root. A vulnerability's one more hit, and a deadly threat's telegraph says how many, each one a point. **2 for 1:** take a hit on another Root instead, for 2 points of it, if it's got them.
-   **Soak:** armour on the Root soaks first, as many points as its tier, a point of soak a point, 2 for 1 included. It's all back at the end of every scene, and it never breaks from soaking.
-   **Spending:** a point of the Root you bring in, or a point of Soul to roll again. You can't spend what you haven't got, so pushing never hurts you, and spending to 0 only leaves you exposed.
-   **Wounds:** a hit on a Root with no soak and no points left wounds it, **Bloodied** (Body), **Frayed** (Mind) or **Hollow** (Soul), one each. A wounded Root heals only to half its maximum, rounded up, till downtime or a story moment clears it. Damage carries on past 0.
-   **Scar:** a hit on a wounded Root at 0. You leave the scene, and it's something you can't do, held to, that locks a point for good. Three downtime slots grow it into a Trait. One that locks a Root's last point is your last.
-   **Blaze:** while you're wounded, on your turn, take the Scar, declare something extraordinary and it happens, no roll, then leave the scene. From your first Blaze, you can choose to die.
-   **They come back:** at the end of every scene, roll each Root below its maximum, unsplit, and get back what you roll. Downtime puts everything back, wounds and Toll included. A big bite from a Catch or a hostile thread, or straining a bond thread, gets you a point of Soul back.
-   **Heal** (the Healer's): an action, rolled unsplit, and the patient gets back what it rolls, spread across their Roots. It reaches one person. **Toll:** every heal from someone or something else is 1 Toll, and a heal loses 5 for each Toll they've already got, never below 0. It drops by 1 at the end of each scene, and downtime clears it. Nobody else puts points back mid-scene for Aura. The Healer's *Hold still* (1 Aura, once a round, no action) is 1 point back on one Root, no roll and no Toll.

## Study {#study}

Roll against its Challenge. Win, and pick one: a hidden tag · a straight answer. An Oracle can pay 1 Aura for *Foretold* and introduce a truth instead: say something that wasn't obvious to anyone else, and it's true, and anyone in the crew can bring a Root in on it this scene.

## The round {#s-qr-round}

1.  **The GM telegraphs** everything, face up: what, at whom (their Body, Mind or Soul), how hard, and how many hits when it's more than one.
2.  **Players act**, in any order, one action each, with an intent on every die: answer a telegraph, go at a threat, put your action into a friend's, or anything else.
3.  **The round ends.** Unanswered telegraphs land, clocks and events nobody worked step down, and so does every condition.

## Telegraphs and defence {#telegraphs-and-defence}

-   **Body, Mind or Soul:** blows and teeth are at your Body, fear, lies and pressure at your Mind, and despair, temptation and curses at your Soul.
-   **Answer:** one or more of your dice against its dice, from a Root that fits how you stop it, and friends can join in. Win and it's stopped. Lose and it lands at once, with no defence roll. A tie's a loss unless you pay 1 Aura. You can answer for a friend, and if you stepped in the way and lose, it lands on you.
-   **Dodging** is your Body, and a guard that fits puts its tier on any roll avoiding harm, once a landing. A weapon never does.
-   **Let it ride:** whatever you don't answer lands at the end of the round. Roll each Root something's landing on, once, at full, split how you like, and each die stops one telegraph it meets or beats. Everything else is a hit.
-   **Defend:** your action. When they land, roll a Root that fits how you're defending, at full, on top, and your guard's tier goes on once between the two. Each die stops one it meets or beats, as long as how you're defending fits it.
-   **Intercept:** 1 Aura and no action. Take the blows at a friend's Body within your stride, and your Body rolls against them. A Guardian does it free, for every friend within his stride, and a summon takes the first blow each round at the Body of the one it's guarding. Nobody can intercept what's at a Mind or a Soul.
-   **One die at each of you** from each threat, unless a tag like *Flurry* says it can do more.
-   **Running doesn't dodge.** One aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. Only one aimed at a place can be walked out of.
-   At a stake, a place or a clock there's no defence. Unanswered, it happens.

## Consequences: the GM names one {#consequences-the-gm-names-one}

A hit, soak first, then a point off the Root it's at · something breaks, crossed out till it's mended (attuned things can't break) · Aspiration down · Party Goal down · a clock ticks. You can offer another that fits and costs about as much, and 2 for 1 on another Root always fits, if it's got the points. A specific intent happens unless you take it as a hit instead. A one-answer telegraph can't be traded.

## Threats {#s-qr-threats}

-   Challenge 2 (d4) nuisance · 3 (d6) a real fight · 4 (d8) capable · 5 (d10) serious · 6 (d12) the top. It defends and telegraphs with its Challenge die, and that never steps down unless a tag says so. A tag only changes dice when it says so, and a deadly one makes each of its dice that gets through 2 hits or more.
-   Keeps-it-going tags are its health, and a multiplier, like *Armoured ×4*, is how many wins one takes. Mook: none or one. Named: two. Boss: three or more, multipliers on the big ones, and a weak point. *(first)* means nothing under it can be stripped till it's gone. *Layered* (special encounters only): one die for every one left, rolled the first time it's attacked in a round, and the best one stands.
-   A win: strip a tag, or tick one off its multiplier · take it down · a condition · move it · learn something · stop a telegraph. An opening: one win ends a Mook or Named, and a Boss loses two whole tags (only its weak point ends it).
-   **Vulnerabilities:** each one that fits a win that strips a tag or lands a hit adds one more tick, and you choose where it goes: the same tag, or another one the hit could reach, so one vulnerability can strip two plain tags. On you, it's one more hit. The same one never counts twice. *Prone* and *Exposed* are vulnerabilities, and a hidden one's a hidden tag.
-   **Forms:** when a form's last keeps-it-going tag goes, it changes. It takes 3 to 5 tags from its Forms list, and its multipliers come back on whichever of them matter most. Its weak point ends it in any form.
-   **Threat budget:** the crew's Roots added up, so 64 for a starting four (16 each), and 6 more for every new Title. It sizes the round budget and how long a scene runs. Multipliers are built for four characters and stay as written as the crew grows, and a threat's Challenge less 2 is a good start for your own. A crew of four lands three or four wins a round.
-   **Round budget:** three quarters of the threat budget in steps of telegraphs a round, so 48 for a starting four, and one die at each of the crew from each threat. A Breather's about half, and Hard's about half again, with more piled on whoever's hurting from round 2. It's a most, not a must. A telegraph costs its die's steps, once for each target and half again for each hit past the first, and says Body, Mind or Soul.
-   **The fight of the session:** about 45 minutes at Hard, about four rounds, with the first of the crew Bloodied in round 3 or 4.

## Conditions {#conditions}

-   A tag with a die, the die that placed it, for you or against you. Passive: one step, or for a few, like *Prone* and *Exposed*, a vulnerability. Active: spend it, free, for its big effect.
-   A condition you put on a friend, like a ward or a blessing, goes on one friend at the die you built. The second die of a split can put it on another friend you can touch.
-   It steps down a size every round, and it's gone past d2. Break it with a die that beats its die. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. Placed again later, the new one replaces it.
-   On a threat, spending *Burning*, *Bleeding* or *Poisoned* strips a tag with no roll (on a multiplier, it ticks one off), and a lost action is a telegraph gone.
-   *Marked* is the Hunter's: once a roll, a Hunter's die that wins against a creature Marks it with that die, one creature at a time. It gives no step and nobody spends it: anyone who acts against whatever's *Marked* rolls the Mark die too. When what it's on falls, the Mark jumps to another creature the Hunter can see.

## Stakes, clocks, events {#stakes-clocks-events}

-   A stake: Challenge + tags in the way. Strip them all and it's yours.
-   Things that climb (the Goal, projects, agendas) start low, step up on a win, one step a roll, and past d12 they're done.
-   A clock steps down each round nobody works the stake. An event steps down each round nobody touches it. Past d2, it happens.
-   Every scene gets a scene clock: past d2, the scene turns.

## Moving {#moving}

-   Stride 3, free, before, during or after your action. Dice you put on moving add up, and that's how many squares further you go, while the rest of your roll does what you went there to do. A Teleporter's stride can be a teleport of up to 10 units.
-   Difficult ground costs 2 a square. Diagonals count as one. There are no opportunity attacks. Cover is line of sight, and it doesn't add steps.
-   Close 1 · Reach 2 · Near · Far · Beyond. No grid? Close, Reach and Near are within your stride, Far takes at least one die on moving, and Beyond takes more than a round.
-   Weapon properties: Reach · Thrown · Ranged · Long · Grapple · Sweep · Piercing · Breaker · Charge · Concealed · Quick · Two-Handed · Reload.

## Your character {#your-character}

-   **Points:** 35 to start, more for a one-shot or catching up. A Title, an archetype or an attuned thing is 5, and comes with one permission, ability or awakening. Another permission, ability or awakening is 1, an item's 1 at tier 2, and 1 more raises it a tier, to tier 3 at most. Your keepsake, Resources, threads, Connect and Aspiration are free. Recommended: at least one Title, one archetype and one attuned thing.
-   **Titles:** 5 on your Roots, spread however you like, and the permission it comes with is 1 more, so a Title's 6. Six permissions at most.
-   **Permissions:** a doing word and how, what or where, like *climb sheer walls*. Each one's 1 on its Root, and it fits that and its Title. It's what lets you bring a Root in, and it's never counted on a roll. You need a magic permission or a magic item to do magic at all, and magic's written narrow.
-   **Roots:** each one's what your Titles put on it, and 1 for every permission on it. A roll's one Root at full, and the same number's its points.
-   **Archetypes:** apart from your Titles, and you can take the same one twice. Each one's a mechanic, a Trait that's always true of you, and the ability it comes with, and more abilities are bought, six at most. Abilities cost Aura.
-   **Attuned things:** each awakening locks a point of the Root it fits, and it's 2 steps on a roll it fits, 2 more for each Aura you put in it. Awakened holds one, and its Catch brings a second, free.
-   **Traits** only come from archetypes, one each, and from a Scar grown into one. Nothing trades permissions for one. **Burdens** only come on attuned things, and each pays for two awakenings on it (see [Burdens](#s-boons)).
-   **Aspiration:** 5 points to get there, and no roll. Each downtime slot you spend on it is a point, and a setback takes one back off. At 5 it comes good: take a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, with the one it comes with.
-   **To start:** Aura 5, every Root full, three Resources, a stride of 3, and two bond threads with the crew.

## The archetypes {#s-qr-archetypes}

Each ability's 1 Aura, or 2 or 3 for a few big ones, and they stack. A **held** one's paid once and stays up for the scene, its Aura off your maximum while it's up. A **signature**'s 4 or 5 Aura, once a scene, with no roll, and never stacks. Most have a Push: +2 on one die of the mechanic after you roll, +3 if you've taken it twice. Each one's Trait comes after its mechanic, and the whole of each is in [The Archetypes](#archetypes).

-   **Bard:** your action gives every friend who can hear you a point back on their Mind, or your song Defends you all against fear, split as you like. *All Eyes on Me:* 1 Aura a threat, and it watches only you while you perform.
-   **Bloodsworn:** every point your Body loses is 3 steps on your action, and you can spend as many as you like on the roll you're making, for nothing but the blood. Your armour soaks only when you want it to. *Paid in Blood:* 1 Aura for every point a hit takes off your Body, never one you spend.
-   **Elementalist:** one of your dice can be an area, a burst, a cone or a line, for no Aura. *Eye of the Storm:* your areas and your loose element never hurt you.
-   **Face:** any of your dice can go on a threat unrolled, as influence, and roll them before one of its telegraphs lands: each of its dice your best one beats does what you say. *I Know a Guy:* once a scene, someone you know turns up and helps.
-   **Gambler:** once a round, flip any die you can see after it lands: its size plus 1, less what it rolled. *Bad Luck Pays:* 1 Aura for each 1 of yours, on a d4 or bigger, that stays a 1.
-   **Guardian:** you intercept for free, for every friend within your stride. *Better Me Than You:* 1 Aura for each blow you intercept for a friend.
-   **Healer:** only you make heal rolls: a Root, unsplit, and they get back what it rolls, less 5 for each Toll. *Good Hands:* your heals ignore a step of Toll.
-   **Hunter:** once a roll, a die of yours that wins against a creature Marks it with that die, and anyone acting against it rolls your Mark die too, which jumps when it falls. *Good Hunting:* 1 Aura for each die a friend wins against your Mark, a Marked stake or event included.
-   **Illusionist:** any of your dice can be an illusion, rolled once: meet or beat the highest Challenge among whoever it's meant to fool, and they believe your illusion till they interact with it or try to get through. *Made You Look:* 1 Aura when a threat swings at, talks to or walks into one.
-   **Leader:** an order hands its steps to friends who can hear or see you. *Where It's Needed:* each round, move 1 step for each of you from roll to roll.
-   **Maker:** any of your dice can be a making die, and one that beats an item's size gives it a tag for the scene, one made tag an item at a time, and you can spend Aura on a friend's awakening. *Maker's Mark:* one item keeps a tag of yours, a permission or a hit taken.
-   **Oracle:** roll your action as foresight, and swap the numbers into anyone's dice that round, if they fit. *Seen It Coming:* each round, roll 3 of the threats' dice before anyone moves. *Foretold* (1 Aura): a Study you win can be a truth.
-   **Shadow:** the dice you hide with go in your shadow pool, for any roll after. *Any Shadow Will Do:* any patch of dark's enough to hide in.
-   **Shifter:** any of your dice can go for an animal tag against its die, and keep them till downtime. *Something of the Beast:* a beast's sense or size, picked each scene.
-   **Summoner:** only you catch souls and summon them. *Pocket Summon:* one named soul out once a scene, at its own die, with no action and no Aura, holding none.
-   **Teleporter:** your stride can be a 10-unit teleport, and 1 Aura opens a portal. *Not There:* once a scene, teleport, and what's landing on you misses.
-   **Warrior:** your dice explode in a fight. *Second Skin:* your armour's a guard as well, its tier on a roll avoiding harm, and it still soaks.

## Bonds and threads {#s-qr-threads}

-   A bond's usually a person, but it can be a faction, a place or a cause. Each thread is one truth between you, and it's a **bond thread**, something good, or a **hostile thread**, something gone bad. Either can be strained, which means nearly snapped, and with no thread at all you're neutral. Each person's one bond, with all your threads with them under it.
-   **To start:** two bond threads with the crew, from your answers in Connect. Write a hostile thread too and you can write a third bond thread, and three's the most.
-   **Borrow** through a bond thread: 1 Aura borrows one thing off their sheet for your roll (a faction's, one of its assets), and it doesn't strain anything. Not while it's strained.
-   **Strain** a bond thread: lean on them in the story and you get a point of Soul back. It's free and takes no action, and the thread's strained. One with someone in the crew is strained for both of you.
-   **Mend:** make it right with them, in downtime or in a scene with them (not the fight where you strained it), and it's whole again.
-   **Snap:** strain it again before it's mended. You still get the point of Soul back, but cross the thread out. When the last one goes, so does the bond.
-   **A hostile thread** bites like a Catch: when it brings you real trouble, 1 Aura, or a point of Soul for a big bite. Doing right by them strains it, and doing it again snaps it.

## Items and Resources {#s-qr-carry}

-   **Items:** real gear you've got ready, six at most, attuned things included. Your keepsake's on top, and it's story, never steps. Each item's got a tier, 1 to 6, and that's its steps, so tier 6 is a d12's worth. Tier 1 is rough or borrowed, and you start at tier 2, or 3 for a point more. Grow takes one to 3 in downtime, money to 4, and the story with a material to 5 and 6. The tier names are in [Item tiers](#s-item-tiers).
-   **What each kind does:** a weapon or a tool, its tier on a roll it fits · a guard (a shield, a buckler, a parrying dagger), its tier only on a roll avoiding harm · armour, no steps, it soaks · a pack, more Resources, half its tier rounded up · a vessel, it holds charge.
-   **Armour** sits on a Root: Body unless it says (court clothes on Mind, a holy symbol on Soul). It soaks its tier, first, and it's all back every scene. A broken item's crossed out till it's mended, and adds and soaks nothing.
-   **Resources:** three each, said in the moment you need them (*as it happens, I've got rope on me*). Tick one and it's real, and it's yours from then on, though something you use up, like a smoke bomb, is gone once it's used. It's ordinary, it fits who you are, and it's never magic or steps. The GM can say no to one that doesn't fit the scene. All three come back in downtime, or when the story restocks you. A purse of gold's a Resource, about a month's wages.
-   **Attunement:** 5 points for each attuned thing, and attuned things can't break. Each one's an item, one of your six, keeping its tier, with awakenings, and each awakening locks a point of the Root it fits for as long as it's attuned. One that fits a roll is 2 steps, and each Aura you put in it is 2 more. Awakened: 1 awakening, or 2 with a Catch. Tempered: 2, or 4 with a Burden. Perfected: 3, or 6 with a Burden and a Catch. Each stage needs a material, and each new awakening a downtime slot.

## Summoning {#s-qr-summoning}

-   Only a Summoner catches souls and summons them. A summoning attunement holds souls, one to an awakening, on the same ladder. A starting soul's a d8.
-   **Catch one:** any time before the scene ends, put one of your dice on a creature that fell this scene, against its Challenge die. Beat it and you carry its soul, with that die, till it goes into your attunement in downtime. It isn't one of your items, and it ticks one of your Resources till then. Miss and it's gone.
-   **Call one out:** as part of your action, before you roll, with a reason in the story. 1 Aura is a d4 summon, 2 a d8 and 3 a d12, never bigger than the soul's own die, and 1 more on a later action puts one up a size. The Aura's held off your maximum while it's out. A vessel's charge can pay instead, and then it holds nothing.
-   **In your throw:** whenever you act, every summon moves its stride and its die goes in your roll with an intent of its own. It's never split, merged or added to, bar a *Push*. A win pays you 1 Aura, and its top face nothing.
-   **Guarding:** each one guards one person within its stride, and the first blow each round at their Body comes at it, free.
-   **Harm:** it rolls its die, and meeting or beating stops it. Anything through knocks it down a size (d12 or d10 to d8, d8 or d6 to d4, a d4's gone), freeing 1 Aura it held. Summons never fade, and they go back at the end of the scene, when a hit sends them back, or when you send them.

## Downtime {#s-qr-downtime}

-   Four pressures: personal, party, faction, world. Ignore one and it gets worse, and twice makes it a scene. Every faction's action is telegraphed at the start, and rolled after the slots.
-   Two personal slots each, and the crew takes one action, like any faction. A personal slot can go into the crew's action. Every downtime starts with everything back: every Root full, wounds and Toll gone, soak full and your three Resources back. Vessels keep their charge, and the points held in them.
-   **Mend:** an item that's broken. **Grow:** a slot buys what a point does: a new permission on a Title · a new ability off an archetype's list · a new awakening · a new item at tier 2 · an item a tier up, to 3 at most. Or it's a new thread, a point in your Aspiration, or a slot toward growing a Scar into a Trait (three slots).
-   **Mend a thread** you've strained by making it right with them. Downtime's one place for it, and a scene with them is the other.
-   **Swap** a permission for another that fits your Title. **Attune:** a slot for each new awakening, and the material for a new stage. A soul you're carrying goes into your summoning attunement.
-   Ties go to the defender. No Aura: pay a point of Soul to roll your dice again, or an extra crew resource to take the tie.

## Factions {#s-qr-factions}

-   Power (1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up when it calls), resources, assets, Want, Agenda, Face, Secret, and three bonds: Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty.
-   Every asset needs a resource paying for it, and one that isn't paid for stops counting. An extra resource, one that isn't paying for anything, hires another faction's action, buys an asset or goes in a trade. Nothing buys a faction another action.
-   One action a downtime, telegraphed at the start: Destroy · Undermine · Obstruct · Influence · Acquire · Establish, or work its agenda (past d12, it happens). Up to three mini factions each, with two outcomes apiece, and a new one sits out the downtime it's made.
-   The crew's action: the six outcomes, Set back its agenda, or Work for it. Roll the crew's Power, plus Reputations and bond threads that fit, or one character's Root, the one that fits. A personal slot put into it counts everything twice, and a bigger faction's Power takes both.
-   In its bonds, a bond thread's a step, a hostile one's a vulnerability, and a strained one doesn't count. Its threads toward the crew say how it sees them: mostly bond threads is friendly, mostly hostile is hostile, and anything else does business.

## The session {#s-qr-session}

Open: recap, downtime (everything comes back), the party sheet, a check-in. Play: planning, confrontation, aftermath. Close: a check-in, one line in the log, a preview.

## The Oracle {#the-oracle}

Three d20s: Fate, Boon and Bane. Any two match: read Harmony. Otherwise, Boon and Bane against Fate. Likely: roll two Boon and keep the higher. Unlikely: two Boon, keep the lower. The Twist and Boon tables are in [The Oracle](#oracle).
