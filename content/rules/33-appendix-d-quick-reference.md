## Building the die {#building-the-die}

-   Each action = 1 step, + every permission that fits it: an ability gives its tier (Novice 1, Skilled 2, Master 3), and each specialization, item, Title or trait that fits gives 1. Two actions on one thing: every permission counts twice, stretches included. Help and conditions count once.
-   Die = steps × 2, up to a d12. Every six steps is another d12, and the rest start one more die.
-   Nothing fits? One action is a d2, and both actions are a d4.

| Steps | Dice |
| --- | --- |
| 1 step | d2 |
| 2 steps | d4 |
| 3 steps | d6 |
| 4 steps | d8 |
| 5 steps | d10 |
| 6 steps | d12 |
| 7 steps | d12+d2 |
| 8 steps | d12+d4 |
| 12 steps | 2d12 |
| 18 steps | 3d12 |
| 24 steps | 4d12 |

## Intents {#intents}

-   Every die gets an intent and a target before you roll. Several intents at one target: it defends each one.
-   Several dice on one intent: advantage, best die counts. Dice never add (except to move).
-   Split: one extra die per action. Dice past six steps are free, and each brings its own intent.
-   **Area:** one die can be how far, instead of an intent. Burst: its number, all round. Cone: its number, the way you face. Line: twice its number. A ward or a blessing: its number counts friends. You can always make it smaller.

## The roll {#the-roll}

-   You roll the threat's die too, in another colour. The GM almost never rolls.
-   **Beat it:** you did it. **Tie:** the defender wins, but 1 Aura wins it if you're acting. **Under:** you didn't.
-   **Top face:** 1 Aura back, per die. Full? Give it to a friend.
-   A miss only costs something when it matters.

## Aura {#aura}

-   Up to your maximum (5 to start). Full at the start of every scene.
-   **1 Aura:** stretch a permission (one that almost fits, the ground, a tag you know, a Catch, a friend's permission), and it counts on every action · win a tie.
-   Traits are free, as often as they say. Invoked traits cost what they say.

## Spark {#spark}

-   One at most. Back at the start of every session. The GM can hand you one for a great moment, if yours is spent.
-   Spend it: roll a failure again · make an opportunity, even from the past · add a permission for the scene · turn down trouble.
-   **Trouble:** the GM says what goes wrong for you and offers a Spark. Take it and it happens, and the Spark's yours (already got one? Give it to someone who's spent theirs). Spend your Spark to turn it down. About once a session each.

## Study {#study}

Roll against its Challenge. Win, and pick one: a hidden tag · a straight answer · something that could be here, and is: a permission for the crew this scene.

## The round {#the-round}

1.  **The GM telegraphs** everything, face up: what, at whom, how hard.
2.  **Players act**, any order, two actions each. Answer, guard, act, help, move, break a condition, pull from the bag.
3.  **The round ends.** Unanswered telegraphs land, clocks and events tick, and every condition steps down one.

## Telegraphs and defence {#telegraphs-and-defence}

-   **Answer:** roll against it. Win, and it's stopped. Lose, and it lands at once, no defence. A tie's a loss, unless you pay 1 Aura.
-   **Let it ride:** roll Armour (blows) or Confidence (words, fear) against each die. Ties go to you.
-   **Guard:** an action's steps onto your defence, or a friend's, for the round.
-   **Running doesn't dodge.** A telegraph aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. One aimed at a place only hits whoever's in it when it lands.
-   **Past d12:** a second die, keep the best. The extra die steps down first.
-   **Budget:** 10 a character with one Title, 15 with three, 20 with five, and in between for two and four. Past five, the GM's call. Breather: half. Hard: half again. At Even, about one harm telegraph a round for each character. At Hard, about two.

## Consequences: the GM names one {#consequences-the-gm-names-one}

Armour down · Confidence down · a Hurt · break an item · Aspiration down · party Goal down · a clock ticks. You can offer another that fits and costs about as much. A specific intent happens unless you trade it for one of these. A one-answer telegraph can't be traded.

## Threats {#threats}

-   Challenge 2 (d4) nuisance · 3 (d6) a real fight · 4 (d8) capable · 5 (d10) serious · 6 (d12) the top.
-   It defends and telegraphs with its Challenge die. A tag only changes dice when it says so. The Challenge never steps down, unless a tag says so.
-   Keeps-it-going tags are its health. Mook: none or one. Named: two. Boss: three or more, and a weak point. *Layered* (special encounters only): one die for every one left, best counts.
-   A win: strip a tag · take it down · an opening · a condition · move it · learn · stop a telegraph.
-   Opening: one win ends a Mook or Named. A Boss loses two tags, and only its weak point ends it.
-   **Tough** (*Tough 40*, *Natural Armour 40*) works like hit points: every winning die takes off what it rolled, and at 0 it's gone. *(first)* means nothing under it can be stripped till it's gone. Openings and weak points ignore the numbers.
-   **Forms:** when the last keeps-it-going tag of a form goes, it changes. Tough refills, and it takes 3 to 5 tags from its Forms list. Its weak point ends it in any form.

## Conditions {#conditions}

-   A tag with a die: the die that placed it, for you or against you.
-   Passive: one step, for or against. Active: spend it, free, for its big effect.
-   Steps down one size every round. Break it: an action that beats its die. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. Placed again later, the new one replaces it.

## Stakes, clocks, events {#stakes-clocks-events}

-   A stake: Challenge + tags in the way. Strip them all and it's yours.
-   Things that climb (the Goal, Aspirations, projects, agendas) start low, step up on a win, one step a roll, and past d12 they're done.
-   A clock steps down each round nobody works the stake. An event steps down each round nobody touches it. Past d2, it happens.
-   Every scene gets a scene clock: past d2, the scene turns.

## Moving {#moving}

-   Stride 3, free. A Move action rolls extra squares. Close 1 · Reach 2 · Near · Far · Beyond: how far is the story's call.
-   Weapon properties: Reach · Thrown · Ranged · Long · Grapple · Sweep · Piercing · Breaker · Charge · Concealed · Quick · Two-Handed · Reload.

## Your character {#your-character}

-   **Titles:** a step when one fits, and a size of Confidence each.
-   **Aspiration:** starts at d2 and climbs. Roll against it when you chase it: an action when it's within reach, or a slot. Win: up one. One step a roll. Past d12: a new Title.
-   **Armour** comes from items. **Confidence** from Titles, bonds, clothes and gear. Both start at d2.
-   **Hurt:** 3 boxes. Heals in downtime. Last box: Blaze, then a Scar.
-   **Carrying:** 6 items and 6 resources. A bag holds 10: an item 2, a resource 1. Pulling out costs an action.
-   **Bonds:** three live connections on one bond: Confidence +1. Stretch a friend's permission through a connection for 1 Aura.
-   **Money:** a purse of gold is one resource, about a month's wages.

## Chases {#chases}

The Gap (past d2: caught, past d12: away) · The Pursuer (past d2: shaken off) · The Ground (past d2: the world wins). The Gap and the Ground step down each round unless someone changed them.

## Downtime {#downtime}

-   Four pressures: personal, party, faction, world. Every faction picks one move, and it's rolled after the slots.
-   Two slots each, two for the crew. Face a pressure, recover, or grow. A party slot is an action for the crew.
-   Ties go to the defender. No Aura: spend your Spark to roll again, or a step of the crew's Wealth to take the tie.

## Factions {#factions}

-   Power, Wealth, Want, Agenda, Tags, Face, Secret, Standing.
-   Moves: Advance · Attack · Scheme · Spend · Recover · Move on the crew.
-   The crew's party slots: Interfere (its Power and its agenda at once) · Strike · Deal (always open) · Work for it. Roll the crew's Power, plus its tags and Reputations that fit. Two slots count everything twice. A bigger faction's Power takes both slots.
-   Agendas climb: Advance rolls Power against the agenda's die, and past d12, it happens.
-   Wealth, a step at a time: an extra move, another faction's move, a tag bought.

## The session {#the-session}

Open: recap, downtime, the party sheet, a check-in, Sparks back. Play: planning, confrontation, aftermath. Close: a check-in, one line in the log, a preview.

## The Oracle {#the-oracle}

Three d20s: Fate, Boon and Bane. Any two match: read Harmony. Otherwise, Boon and Bane against Fate. Likely: roll two Boon and keep the higher. Unlikely: two Boon, keep the lower. The Twist and Boon tables are in [The Oracle](#oracle).
