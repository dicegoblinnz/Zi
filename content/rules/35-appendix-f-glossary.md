Here are the rule terms in the book, one line each, and each one links to its rule.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **[2 for 1](#s-consequences)** | When a hit gets through, paying 2 from your other two tracks, in any mix, so the one it's at stays where it is. Paying never takes a track below d2. |
| **[Ability](#s-abilities)** | Something an archetype does on top of its mechanic, for 1 off its home track, or 2 from your other two. They stack, so pay again for more of it. |
| **[Action](#s-actions)** | What you spend to do something. One a round. 3 Aura buys another, up to one a round for each archetype you've got, and holding it, coming out of hiding or the Boon can give you more. |
| **[Advantage](#s-intents)** | Several dice on one intent. Your best die counts. |
| **[Agenda](#s-agendas)** | What a faction is doing right now, on a die that climbs. Past d12, it happens. |
| **[Animal tag](#a-shifter)** | A Shifter's: something an animal does, like wings or a breath attack, caught against its die and yours until downtime. It's a step when it fits, and you can strip one instead of taking a hit. |
| **[Answer](#s-answering)** | Spending your action to stop a telegraph before it lands. Friends can put theirs in too. |
| **[Arc](#s-campaigns)** | Four to six sessions around one Party Goal. |
| **[Archetype](#archetypes)** | One of eighteen, picked apart from your Titles. You start with two, and the same one twice is fine. Each has a mechanic only it can do, a home track, and abilities you pay for out of your tracks, and you can take one action a round for each archetype you've got. They're the Bard, Bloodsworn, Elementalist, Face, Gambler, Guardian, Healer, Hunter, Illusionist, Leader, Maker, Oracle, Scholar, Shadow, Shifter, Summoner, Teleporter and Warrior. |
| **[Area die](#s-area)** | The Elementalist's: one die that says how far your roll reaches instead of an intent, a burst, a cone or a line twice as long. It takes a split and costs no Aura. Threats have areas too. |
| **[Armour](#s-armour)** | Plate, leathers, a shield, a helmet, or clothes that fit the scene. Each piece sits on a Root and it's 1 more when you Defend with it, and when a hit gets through to that Root it can take it instead, ticking one of its boxes. |
| **[Aspiration](#s-aspiration)** | What your character wants: one thing, and you'd know the day it's done (see [A good Aspiration](#s-good-aspiration)). Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12 you get what you were after, and a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, your pick. |
| **[Asset](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for what it can do. People who turn up, like *Standing Army*, are +1 Power, and the rest do one thing. It needs a resource paying for it every downtime, or it stops counting. |
| **[Attunement](#s-attunement)** | One attuned thing to start, on top of what you carry. It's an item, it gets you Aura its own way, it's got awakenings you pour Aura into, it grows in three stages, and it can't be broken. |
| **[Aura](#s-aura)** | Your luck and flair, and the one thing you spend. Up to your maximum, 5 to start, full every scene. 1 brings something in, makes a case, goes beyond, wins a tie when you're acting, intercepts for a friend or pays for a quick heal, and 3 buys an extra action. 1 back for every die that wins, every top face, and every time you let a Catch bite. |
| **[Awakened](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's first stage: 1 awakening, and 1 more with a Catch. |
| **[Awakening](#s-attunement)** | Something an attuned thing does when you pour Aura into it. A passive one holds up to 2 Aura and works while it's there, and an active one spends it as you use the thing. |
| **[Bag](#s-carrying)** | Holds 10 things on top of what you carry. Getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free. |
| **[Blaze](#s-blaze)** | Taking a hit with that track at d2. Something extraordinary happens, no roll. Then you can choose to die, or leave the scene and take a Scar. |
| **[Bond](#s-bonds)** | A person who matters to your character, in the crew or not, or a faction, a place or a cause. Each person's one bond, and every thread you've got with them goes under it. Each thread is one truth between you, a bond thread or a hostile one, and you can have as many as the story gives you. A faction has three bonds of its own: [Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty](#s-faction-bonds). |
| **[Bond thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's something good between you, like a rescue, a secret you keep for each other or a shared past. Borrow one of their specialties through it for 1 Aura, or strain it and your Resolve comes up a size. |
| **[Boon](#s-boons)** | A permanent upgrade, like +2 stride, or the first hit each scene not dropping a track. It costs two permission slots on one of your Titles, and you can swap it back in downtime. |
| **[Boss](#s-staying)** | Challenge 6, three or more tags keeping it going, multipliers on the big ones, and a weak point. |
| **[Boxes](#s-armour-quality)** | How many hits a piece of armour can take for you: 1 for Common, 2 for Fine and 3 for Masterwork. It breaks when the last one's ticked. |
| **[Break](#s-conditions)** | Spending an action to beat a condition's die and end it. |
| **[Bring something in](#s-aura)** | 1 Aura: something that fits but isn't your Root, your specialty or an item you're using is a step on your roll. The ground, a tag you know, a Catch turned your way, or a friend's specialty through a bond thread. |
| **[Burden](#s-boons)** | A permanent downside, like −2 stride. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, once it's Tempered, where it pays for two more awakenings, or for a Boon for you. |
| **[Catch](#s-trouble)** | Trouble that's ready to go: on an attuned thing, or a Scar. Let it bite for 1 Aura, as often as you like, as long as it's real trouble, or a size of Resolve for a big bite. |
| **[Challenge](#s-challenges)** | The die something defends with: 2 (d4) to 6 (d12). It never steps down, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **[Climbs](#s-climb)** | How the Goal, an Aspiration, a project and an agenda move: from d2 up, a step on every win, one step a roll. Past d12, it's done. |
| **[Clock](#s-stakes)** | A die on a stake that steps down every round nobody works it. |
| **[Combo](#s-teamwork)** | Two or more actions into the same thing at the same time, yours or your friends'. One Root, every action brings a specialty and a split, and you roll once, with no Aura. The same specialty from two of you counts for each. |
| **[Condition](#s-conditions)** | A tag carrying a die, for you or against you. One step while it lasts, or for a few, a vulnerability. Spend it for its big effect. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. |
| **[Confidence](#s-tracks)** | Your Mind's track, and your defence against fear, lies and pressure. It starts at d12, drops a size for every hit that gets through to your Mind, and pays for the Face's, the Gambler's, the Hunter's, the Illusionist's, the Oracle's and the Scholar's abilities. |
| **[Consequence](#s-consequences)** | What a landed telegraph or a costly miss does. The GM names it, usually a size off a track, and you can offer another that fits and costs about as much, or pay 2 for 1. |
| **[Crew](#s-party-faction)** | The player characters, together. Also a faction, with Power, resources, assets and Reputations. |
| **[Crew action](#downtime)** | The crew's one action a downtime, like any faction's. A player can put a personal slot into it, and two slots on one thing count everything twice. |
| **[Crew asset](#s-crew-tags)** | An asset on the crew's card, like *Informants* or *A Ship*. It does one thing, and it needs a resource paying for it. |
| **[Defence](#s-defending)** | The die rolled against an intent: a threat's Challenge die, or your tracks and your Defend. A threat rolls it once a round, and that roll stands against everyone who goes at it. Each of your defence dice stops one telegraph. |
| **[Defend](#s-defend)** | Your action, for keeping from getting hit: a Root and a specialty that fit how you're defending, with your armour and your shield as items, rolled with your tracks when the telegraphs land. Each die stops one it beats or ties. |
| **[Despair, temptation and curses](#s-tracks)** | Telegraphs at your Soul, and anything trying to take you over. Answer them or let them ride on your Resolve, and nobody can intercept them. |
| **[Downtime](#downtime)** | Between sessions. The pressures land, everyone spends their slots, and then the factions' actions are rolled. Every track starts it a size up, and a slot spent recovering puts 6 sizes back. |
| **[Epilogue](#s-ending)** | At the end of a campaign, a few sentences on where each character is a year later. |
| **[Equipment](#s-carrying)** | What you wear or wield: weapons, armour and shields. You carry 3. A plain one's 1 more on a roll you use it in. |
| **[Event](#s-stakes)** | Something running on its own clock. Past d2, it happens. |
| **[Extra action](#s-extra-actions)** | One on top of your one a round: 3 Aura, up to one for each archetype you've got, or from holding your action, coming out of hiding, or the Boon. Its own roll, or the same one as a combo. |
| **[Extra resource](#s-wealth)** | A resource that isn't paying for an asset. It's spent each downtime, not saved: it hires another faction, pays for a new asset, goes in a trade, or takes the crew a tie. |
| **[Face](#s-faction-board)** | The person a faction sends to deal with the crew, written as a threat. |
| **[Faction](#factions)** | A power bigger than one person: Power, resources, assets, an agenda, three bonds, and threads toward the crew. |
| **[Fear, lies and pressure](#s-tracks)** | Telegraphs at your Mind. Answer them or let them ride on your Confidence, and nobody can intercept them. Each one that gets through drops your Confidence a size, unless your armour there takes it or you pay 2 for 1. |
| **[Flip](#a-gambler)** | The Gambler's, once a round: a die you can see turned over after it lands, its size plus 1 take away what it rolled. A flipped die gives no Aura. |
| **[Foresight](#a-oracle)** | The Oracle's: the numbers it rolled, swapped into anyone's dice before the round ends, as long as each fits the die. |
| **[Forms](#s-unique-threats)** | A threat with two or more forms. When the last tag keeping a form going is stripped, it changes: it takes new tags, and its multipliers come back on the ones that matter most. |
| **[GM move](#s-gm-moves)** | Something the world does when everyone looks at the GM. Usually a telegraph outside a round. |
| **[Goal](#s-goal)** | What the whole crew is after this arc: one thing they could get done in it (see [A good Goal](#s-good-goal)). Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, the crew has done it, it grows, and everyone writes a new thread. |
| **[Going beyond](#s-going-beyond)** | Pushing a permission past what it normally does, like phasing through a wall. 1 Aura, for a sword or a spell. If you have to make a case for why it's normal, it's going beyond. |
| **[Ground](#the-ground)** | Tags on the place itself. Bring one into your roll for 1 Aura. |
| **[Harmony](#oracle)** | An Oracle reading: two of the three dice match. |
| **[Heal](#s-healing)** | The Healer's roll: an action, and each die that rolls a 4 or more puts one of the patient's tracks up a size. It reaches one person, or a second you can touch with a split. |
| **[Hold](#s-actions)** | Doing nothing this round apart from your stride, for two actions next round. |
| **[Home track](#s-abilities)** | The track an archetype's abilities come off: Stamina, Confidence or Resolve. 1 from it, or 2 from your other two. |
| **[Hook](#s-aftermath)** | Something at the end of a session that pulls the crew into the next one. |
| **[Hostile thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's gone bad, like a debt you can't pay or a grudge. It bites like a Catch: 1 Aura when it brings you real trouble, a size of Resolve for a big bite. |
| **[Influence](#a-face)** | A Face's dice, built but not rolled, sitting on a threat. Roll them before one of its telegraphs lands, and every one of its dice your best one beats does what you say. |
| **[Intent](#s-intents)** | What a die is for, and who or what it's aimed at. Say it before you roll. |
| **[Intercept](#s-intercept)** | 1 Aura and no action: take the blows at a friend's Body within your stride, and your own Stamina or Defend handles them. One friend for each Aura. A Guardian or a summon does it free. Nothing at a friend's Mind or Soul can be intercepted. |
| **[Item](#s-items)** | A thing you carry. A plain one's just what it is: 1 more on a roll it fits, and it does what that thing does. |
| **[Its Aura](#s-attunement)** | The one thing an attuned thing does that's worth 1 Aura, written when you attune it, like Bram's hammer whenever he knocks someone off their feet. |
| **[Keeps it going](#s-staying)** | The tags that are a threat's health. Every win strips one, or ticks one off its multiplier. Strip the last and it falls, unless a tag like *Forms* says otherwise. |
| **[Keepsake](#s-carrying)** | Something that matters to your character, carried on top of your equipment and utility. It has no rule of its own. |
| **[Layered](#s-layered)** | A tag for special encounters only: the first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls one Challenge die for every tag it has left keeping it going, and its best die counts. |
| **[Lead](#a-leader)** | The Leader's action: an order or a rallying song, built from a Root and a specialty, with its steps handed out to friends who can hear or see you for this round. They don't have to fit. |
| **[Let it bite](#s-trouble)** | Bringing a Catch, or any real trouble, down on yourself in the story. The GM runs with it, and you take 1 Aura, or your Resolve comes up a size when a Catch bites in a big way, changing the scene or costing you something you wanted. |
| **[Let it ride](#s-defending)** | Leaving a telegraph unanswered, without Defending, so it lands at the end of the round against your tracks, where each die stops one at its Root. |
| **[Lines and veils](#s-lines-veils)** | What the table won't have in the story at all (a line), or won't play out in detail (a veil). |
| **[Log](#s-log)** | One sentence a session, kept with the party sheet. |
| **[Marked](#a-hunter)** | The Hunter's special condition on a creature, at the die they won with. It gives no step and nobody spends it: anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too. It fades a size a round. |
| **[Material](#s-attunement)** | What an attuned thing needs before it reaches its next stage, found in the story or bought. A summoning attunement's material is souls. |
| **[Mend](#s-bonds)** | Making a strained thread right with them, by playing it out, doing them a favour or paying them, in downtime or in a scene with them. Then it's whole again. |
| **[Mini faction](#s-mini-factions)** | A faction another one made, up to three each. It does two of the six outcomes and nothing else, and its parent hands it one resource and one asset to start. It sits out the downtime it's made, then acts on its own. |
| **[Mook](#s-staying)** | Challenge 2 or 3, with none or one tag keeping it going. Falls to one good win. |
| **[Move action](#s-movement)** | An action that rolls extra squares past your stride. Its dice add. |
| **[Multiplier](#s-tough)** | How many wins a tag takes to strip, like *Armoured ×4*. Every win ticks one off. |
| **[Named](#s-staying)** | Challenge 4 or 5, with two tags keeping it going. |
| **[Odds](#s-oracle-odds)** | Before an Oracle roll, say how likely it is. Likely: two Boon dice, keep the higher. Unlikely: keep the lower. |
| **[Opening](#s-win)** | A way to end a Mook or Named threat in one win: its weak point, helplessness, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two whole tags. |
| **[Oracle](#oracle)** | Three d20s that answer questions about the world. |
| **[Outcome](#s-faction-outcomes)** | What a faction's action goes for: Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire or Establish. Each changes one thing on a card. |
| **[Party holdings](#s-party-resources)** | Big things the crew owns together, like a horse and cart or a house. A step on any roll they fit, for 1 Aura. |
| **[Perfected](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's third stage: 3 awakenings, and 3 more with a Burden and a Catch. Six at most. |
| **[Permission](#s-permissions)** | Something your character can do, a doing word with how, what or where, like *climb sheer walls*. It lives on a Title, and it's 1 on a Root and 1 on a specialty that fit it. |
| **[Power](#s-faction-board)** | A faction's muscle, on a die: 1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up when it calls. Nothing else adds Power. The crew has one too. |
| **[Pressure](#downtime)** | One of the four things pushing on the crew each downtime: personal, party, faction, world. |
| **[Project](#s-projects)** | Something the crew sets out to build or bring about. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, what you wrote happens. |
| **[Push](#s-abilities)** | The ability most archetypes have: +2 on one die of the mechanic after you roll, for 1 off its home track, and +3 if you've taken the archetype twice. A die it takes to its top face gives no Aura. |
| **[Quality](#s-armour-quality)** | What a piece of armour, a shield or a helmet is: Common, Fine or Masterwork, with 1, 2 or 3 boxes. |
| **[Quick heal](#s-healing)** | Anyone's: 1 Aura puts one of someone's tracks up a size, no roll. It still takes your action. |
| **[Reputation](#s-party-faction)** | What the city says about the crew, in one line. A step on any crew roll it fits. One for every Goal done. |
| **[Resolve](#s-tracks)** | Your Soul's track, and your defence against despair, temptation, curses and anything trying to take you over. It starts at d12, drops a size for every hit that gets through to your Soul, and pays for rerolls and the Bard's, the Elementalist's, the Healer's, the Leader's, the Summoner's and the Teleporter's abilities. Straining a bond thread or a big bite brings it up a size. |
| **[Resource](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for where its money comes from, like *Silver Mine*. Each one pays for one asset every downtime, and an extra one, paying for nothing, hires another faction, buys something or goes in a trade. |
| **[Rest](#s-healing)** | At the end of every scene: roll each track below d12, and a 4 or more puts it up a size. A d2 waits for downtime. |
| **[Root](#s-roots)** | Body, Mind or Soul. Every roll starts from one, and it's 1 for every Title and permission on it. Each one has a track. |
| **[Round](#s-round)** | The GM telegraphs, everyone takes one action, the round ends. |
| **[Round budget](#s-budget)** | What the GM telegraphs with each round: about 10 steps for each player. A Breather's about half, and Hard's about half again. |
| **[Scar](#s-blaze)** | The hit that made you Blaze, written as a Catch. It locks the smallest open size on that track, and when one would lock the last, that's your last Blaze. |
| **[Scene](#s-aura)** | One place and one problem. Aura refills when a new one starts, and your tracks rest when it ends. |
| **[Scene clock](#s-scene-clock)** | A die on the whole scene. It steps down every round. Past d2, the scene turns. |
| **[Shadow pool](#a-shadow)** | A Shadow's: the dice you rolled to get into hiding, to add to any roll after that, as dice or as steps. Each one's gone once you use it. |
| **[Size](#s-building-die)** | One die size. Stepping down one size is a d8 becoming a d6. |
| **[Slot](#downtime)** | A unit of downtime. You get two, and the crew takes one action, which a slot of yours can go into. |
| **[Snap](#s-bonds)** | What a thread does when you strain it again before it's mended. Your Resolve still comes up, but cross the thread out. When the last one goes, so does the bond. |
| **[Soul, a Summoner's](#s-summoning)** | A fallen creature a Summoner's caught, rolling against its Challenge, and kept in their summoning attunement. It comes out as a summon, no bigger than its own die. |
| **[Specialty](#s-permissions)** | Something you could be known for or teach, like Locks or Shieldwork, named by you. It's 1 for every Title and permission on it, and a roll's one Root and one specialty, plus your items. |
| **[Split](#s-splitting)** | Turning your steps into more, smaller dice. One for every action in the roll, and dice past 6 steps don't use them up. |
| **[Stake](#s-stakes)** | Something the crew wants from a scene, with tags in the way. |
| **[Stamina](#s-tracks)** | Your Body's track, and your defence against blows, blades and teeth. It starts at d12, drops a size for every hit that gets through to your Body, and pays for the Bloodsworn's, the Guardian's, the Maker's, the Shadow's, the Shifter's and the Warrior's abilities. |
| **[Step](#s-building-die)** | The unit of a roll. Each step is one size of die: 1 is a d2, 6 is a d12. |
| **[Strain](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's nearly snapped. Leaning on a bond thread in the story, like calling in the favour or using their name, strains it and brings your Resolve up a size, free. Doing right by someone strains a hostile thread. You can't borrow through a strained thread. |
| **[Stride](#s-movement)** | Free movement every round. 3 squares to start. A Teleporter's can be a teleport of up to 10 units. |
| **[Study](#s-study)** | An intent that finds a hidden tag or a straight answer. A Scholar can introduce a truth instead. |
| **[Summon](#s-summoning)** | A soul a Summoner calls out with Aura, as part of an action: a d4 of summons for each Aura. It acts in the round like anyone, fades a size every round, and loses a size to every hit that gets through. |
| **[Swap](#grow)** | In downtime, trading a permission for another that fits your Title, or two of them for a Boon, and back. |
| **[Tag](#s-challenges)** | Something true about a threat, a stake or a place. It does what it says, and a big one carries a multiplier. A Maker can give an item tags for the scene, and they work like permissions. |
| **[Take a breath](#s-actions)** | Your action: your Confidence comes up a size, as far as d6. |
| **[Telegraph](#s-telegraphs)** | The GM saying, face up, what's coming, at whom, and how hard. One aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. Fear, lies and pressure come as telegraphs too. |
| **[Tempered](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's second stage: 2 awakenings, and 2 more with a Burden. |
| **[Thread](#s-bonds)** | One truth you share with a bond. A bond thread's something good: borrow one of their specialties through it for 1 Aura, or strain it and your Resolve comes up a size. A hostile thread's gone bad, and it bites like a Catch. You start with two bond threads with the crew, and three if you write a hostile one too. A faction's threads go one way, and they're how it sees the crew. |
| **[Threat budget](#s-threat-budget)** | 15 a character as they start, so 60 for four, and more with every Title, archetype or attuned thing an Aspiration gives them. It sizes multipliers and how long a scene runs. |
| **[Tick](#s-stakes)** | A clock or an event stepping down one, a faction's agenda climbing one, one win off a multiplier, or one size off a track. |
| **[Title](#s-title)** | Who you are: one role or one school of magic, real and specific, with up to six permissions. It's 1 on a Root and 1 on each of three specialties that fit it. You start with two, and they're separate from your archetypes. |
| **[Title idea](#s-title-ideas)** | A place to start a Title: twenty-nine roles and schools, each with the archetypes it suits, and permissions and going-beyond ideas to pick from. |
| **[Top face](#s-the-roll)** | A die's highest number. 1 Aura back, unless a Push or an awakening took it there, it's a summon's, an Oracle swapped it in or a Gambler flipped it. A Warrior's explodes. |
| **[Track](#s-tracks)** | Stamina, Confidence or Resolve, one for each Root. Each starts at d12, drops a size for every hit that gets through, and rolls once as your defence when the telegraphs land. A hit at d2 and you Blaze. |
| **[Trouble](#s-trouble)** | Something going wrong for you that the GM can run with. You bring it yourself, by letting a Catch bite or with no Catch at all, and take 1 Aura for it. |
| **[Utility](#s-carrying)** | Tools and things you use up, like rope, a lantern or a smoke bomb. You carry 3, and cross off what you use. |
| **[Vulnerability](#s-vulnerable)** | Something that makes a hit hurt more, like *Prone* or *Vulnerable to fire*. Each one that fits a win that does harm adds a tick, and you choose where it goes, so one can strip two plain tags. The same one never counts twice. |
| **[Ward](#s-wards)** | A condition like *Warded* or *Blessed* that you put on a friend. Nothing pushes back, so the die you built is its die. One friend, or a second you can touch with a split. |
| **[Weak point](#s-hidden)** | The tag that holds a threat together. Break it and it falls. |
| **[Win](#s-the-roll)** | Beating the other die. Ties go to the defender. |
| **[X-card](#s-x-card)** | A card anyone can tap to rewind the scene, no questions asked. |
