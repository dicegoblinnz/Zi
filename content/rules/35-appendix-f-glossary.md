Here are the rule terms in the book, one line each, and each one links to its rule.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **[2 for 1](#s-consequences)** | When a hit gets through, taking it on another Root instead, for 2 points of that Root, as long as it's got them. Armour on that Root soaks them first. |
| **[Ability](#s-abilities)** | Something an archetype does on top of its mechanic, for 1 Aura each time you use it. They stack, so pay again for more of it. An archetype comes with one, and more are a point or a downtime slot each. |
| **[Action](#s-actions)** | What you spend to do something. One a round, and nothing gets you another. Every die in it is an intent, so one roll can do several things. |
| **[Advantage](#s-intents)** | Several dice on one intent. Your best die counts. |
| **[Agenda](#s-agendas)** | What a faction is doing right now, on a die that climbs. Past d12, it happens. |
| **[Animal tag](#a-shifter)** | A Shifter's: something an animal does, like wings or a breath attack, caught against its die and yours until downtime. You can strip one instead of taking a hit. |
| **[Answer](#s-answering)** | Putting one of your dice, or more, on a telegraph to stop it before it lands. Friends can put their actions in too. |
| **[Arc](#s-campaigns)** | Four to six sessions around one Party Goal. |
| **[Archetype](#archetypes)** | One of seventeen, picked apart from your Titles. It's 5 points, and the same one twice is fine. Each has a mechanic only it can do, a Trait that's always true of you, and abilities you pay for in Aura. They're the Bard, Bloodsworn, Elementalist, Face, Gambler, Guardian, Healer, Hunter, Illusionist, Leader, Maker, Oracle, Shadow, Shifter, Summoner, Teleporter and Warrior. |
| **[Area die](#s-area)** | The Elementalist's: one die that says how far your roll reaches instead of an intent, a burst, a cone or a line twice as long. It costs no Aura. Threats have areas too. |
| **[Armour](#s-armour)** | An item that soaks: plate, leathers, a helmet, or clothes that fit the scene. Each piece sits on a Root, Body unless it says otherwise, and soaks as many points as its tier before that Root loses any. It adds no steps, and its soak's all back at the end of every scene. |
| **[Aspiration](#s-aspiration)** | What your character wants: one thing, and you'd know the day it's done (see [A good Aspiration](#s-good-aspiration)). It takes 5 points, a downtime slot each, and there's no roll. At 5 it comes good: you get what you were after, and a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, your pick. |
| **[Asset](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for what it can do. People who turn up, like *Standing Army*, are +1 Power, and the rest do one thing. It needs a resource paying for it every downtime, or it stops counting. |
| **[Attunement](#s-attunement)** | Something personal bound to you, for 5 points. It's an item, one of your six, keeping its tier, it's got awakenings, each locking a point of the Root it fits, it grows in three stages, and it can't be broken. |
| **[Aura](#s-aura)** | Your luck and flair. Up to 5, full every scene. 3 brings a Root in, and 1 puts 2 more steps on an awakening, pays for an ability, borrows through a bond thread, goes beyond, wins a tie when you're acting, intercepts for a friend or pays for a quick heal. 1 back for every die that wins, every top face, and every time you let a Catch bite. A vessel's charge can stand in for it on your own abilities and awakenings. |
| **[Awakened](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's first stage: 1 awakening, and 1 more with a Catch. |
| **[Awakening](#s-attunement)** | Something an attuned thing does. It locks a point of the Root it fits for as long as it's attuned, and it's 2 steps on a roll it fits, 2 more for every Aura you put in it. |
| **[Blaze](#s-blaze)** | Going out on your own terms while you're wounded: on your turn, take the Scar, declare something extraordinary and it happens, no roll, then leave the scene. From your first Blaze, you can choose to die instead. |
| **[Bloodied](#s-blaze)** | Your Body's wound, from a hit on it with no points left. It heals only to half its maximum till downtime or a story moment clears it. |
| **[Bond](#s-bonds)** | A person who matters to your character, in the crew or not, or a faction, a place or a cause. Each person's one bond, and every thread you've got with them goes under it. Each thread is one truth between you, a bond thread or a hostile one, and you can have as many as the story gives you. A faction has three bonds of its own: [Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty](#s-faction-bonds). |
| **[Bond thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's something good between you, like a rescue, a secret you keep for each other or a shared past. Borrow one thing off their sheet through it for 1 Aura, or strain it and you get a point of Soul back. |
| **[Boss](#s-staying)** | Challenge 6, three or more tags keeping it going, multipliers on the big ones, and a weak point. |
| **[Break](#s-conditions)** | Putting a die on a condition and beating its die to end it. |
| **[Bring a Root in](#s-bring)** | Putting a second Root on a roll, all of it, when a permission explains how it gets in on what you're doing. It costs 1 point of that Root, or 3 Aura. |
| **[Broken](#s-consequences)** | An item the GM's named broken as a consequence. Cross it out: it adds no steps and soaks nothing till a downtime slot mends it. Attuned things can't break. |
| **[Burden](#s-boons)** | A permanent downside, like −2 stride. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, once it's Tempered, where it pays for two more awakenings on that thing. |
| **[Catch](#s-trouble)** | Trouble that's ready to go, on an attuned thing. Let it bite for 1 Aura, as often as you like, as long as it's real trouble, or a point of Soul for a big bite. |
| **[Challenge](#s-challenges)** | The die something defends with: 2 (d4) to 6 (d12). It never steps down, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **[Climbs](#s-climb)** | How the Goal, a project and an agenda move: from d2 up, a step on every win, one step a roll. Past d12, it's done. |
| **[Clock](#s-stakes)** | A die on a stake that steps down every round nobody works it. |
| **[Combo](#s-teamwork)** | Two or more actions into the same thing at the same time, yours or your friends'. You roll once between you, every action brings the Root that fits how they're helping, all of it, and the one item doing their part, and there's no Aura for the combo. |
| **[Condition](#s-conditions)** | A tag carrying a die, for you or against you. One step while it lasts, or for a few, a vulnerability. Spend it for its big effect. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. |
| **[Consequence](#s-consequences)** | What a landed telegraph or a costly miss does. The GM names it, usually a hit, a point off a Root, and you can offer another that fits and costs about as much, or take it 2 for 1. |
| **[Crew](#s-party-faction)** | The player characters, together. Also a faction, with Power, resources, assets and Reputations. |
| **[Crew action](#downtime)** | The crew's one action a downtime, like any faction's. A player can put a personal slot into it, and two slots on one thing count everything twice. |
| **[Crew asset](#s-crew-tags)** | An asset on the crew's card, like *Informants* or *A Ship*. It does one thing, and it needs a resource paying for it. |
| **[Deadly](#s-consequences)** | A threat whose dice are 2 hits or more each when they get through. Its telegraphs say so, face up. |
| **[Defence](#s-defending)** | The die rolled against an intent: a threat's Challenge die, or the Root a telegraph's landing on, and your Defend. A threat rolls it once a round, and that roll stands against everyone who goes at it. Each of your defence dice stops one telegraph. |
| **[Defend](#s-defend)** | Your action, for keeping from getting hit: a Root that fits how you're defending, rolled at full on top of your defence when the telegraphs land. Each die stops one it meets or beats. |
| **[Despair, temptation and curses](#s-fear-gets-through)** | Telegraphs at your Soul, and anything trying to take you over. Answer them or let them land on your Soul, and nobody can intercept them. |
| **[Downtime](#downtime)** | Between sessions. The pressures land, everyone spends their slots, and then the factions' actions are rolled. It starts with everything back: every Root on its maximum, every wound cleared, your Toll gone, your soak full and your three Resources back. |
| **[Epilogue](#s-ending)** | At the end of a campaign, a few sentences on where each character is a year later. |
| **[Event](#s-stakes)** | Something running on its own clock. Past d2, it happens. |
| **[Extra resource](#s-wealth)** | A resource that isn't paying for an asset. It's spent each downtime, not saved: it hires another faction, pays for a new asset, goes in a trade, or takes the crew a tie. |
| **[Face](#s-faction-board)** | The person a faction sends to deal with the crew, written as a threat. |
| **[Faction](#factions)** | A power bigger than one person: Power, resources, assets, an agenda, three bonds, and threads toward the crew. |
| **[Fear, lies and pressure](#s-fear-gets-through)** | Telegraphs at your Mind. Answer them or let them land on your Mind, and nobody can intercept them. Each one that gets through takes a point of Mind, unless armour there soaks it or you take it 2 for 1. |
| **[Flip](#a-gambler)** | The Gambler's, once a round: a die you can see turned over after it lands, its size plus 1 take away what it rolled. A flipped die gives no Aura. |
| **[Foresight](#a-oracle)** | The Oracle's: the numbers it rolled, swapped into anyone's dice before the round ends, as long as each fits the die. |
| **[Forms](#s-unique-threats)** | A threat with two or more forms. When the last tag keeping a form going is stripped, it changes: it takes new tags, and its multipliers come back on the ones that matter most. |
| **[Frayed](#s-blaze)** | Your Mind's wound, from a hit on it with no points left. It heals only to half its maximum till downtime or a story moment clears it. |
| **[GM move](#s-gm-moves)** | Something the world does when everyone looks at the GM. Usually a telegraph outside a round. |
| **[Goal](#s-goal)** | What the whole crew is after this arc: one thing they could get done in it (see [A good Goal](#s-good-goal)). Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, the crew has done it, it grows, and everyone writes a new thread. |
| **[Going beyond](#s-going-beyond)** | Pushing a permission past what it normally does, like phasing through a wall. 1 Aura, for a sword or a spell. If you have to make a case for why it's normal, it's going beyond. |
| **[Ground](#the-ground)** | Tags on the place itself, like a chandelier or a crowd. Anyone can use them in the story, and the GM can telegraph from them. |
| **[Guard](#s-items)** | An item like a shield, a buckler or a parrying dagger. Its tier goes on a roll that's avoiding harm, and never on anything else. |
| **[Harmony](#oracle)** | An Oracle reading: two of the three dice match. |
| **[Heal](#s-healing)** | The Healer's roll: an action, rolled unsplit, and one person gets back what it rolls, spread across their Roots, less 5 for each Toll they've got. |
| **[Hit](#s-consequences)** | A telegraph's die that gets through to you: 1 point off the Root it's at, once the armour there has soaked what it can. A vulnerability's one more, and a deadly threat's telegraph says how many. |
| **[Hollow](#s-blaze)** | Your Soul's wound, from a hit on it with no points left. It heals only to half its maximum till downtime or a story moment clears it. |
| **[Hook](#s-aftermath)** | Something at the end of a session that pulls the crew into the next one. |
| **[Hostile thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's gone bad, like a debt you can't pay or a grudge. It bites like a Catch: 1 Aura when it brings you real trouble, a point of Soul for a big bite. |
| **[Influence](#a-face)** | A Face's dice, built but not rolled, sitting on a threat. Roll them before one of its telegraphs lands, and every one of its dice your best one beats does what you say. |
| **[Intent](#s-intents)** | What a die is for, and who or what it's aimed at. Say it before you roll. Every die's as good as an action. |
| **[Intercept](#s-intercept)** | 1 Aura and no action: take the blows at a friend's Body within your stride, and your own Body or Defend rolls against them. One friend for each Aura. A Guardian or a summon does it free. Nothing at a friend's Mind or Soul can be intercepted. |
| **[Item](#s-items)** | Real gear you've got ready: a weapon, a guard, armour, a tool good enough to matter, a pack or a vessel. Six at most, attuned things included. When it's what's doing the work on a roll, it puts its tier on in steps, one item a roll. |
| **[Keeps it going](#s-staying)** | The tags that are a threat's health. Every win strips one, or ticks one off its multiplier. Strip the last and it falls, unless a tag like *Forms* says otherwise. |
| **[Keepsake](#s-items)** | Something that matters to your character, carried on top of your six items. It's story, never steps, and it has no rule of its own. |
| **[Layered](#s-layered)** | A tag for special encounters only: the first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls one Challenge die for every tag it has left keeping it going, and its best die counts. |
| **[Lead](#a-leader)** | The Leader's action: an order or a rallying song, built from a Root, with its steps handed out to friends who can hear or see you for this round. They don't have to fit. |
| **[Let it bite](#s-trouble)** | Bringing a Catch, or any real trouble, down on yourself in the story. The GM runs with it, and you take 1 Aura, or a point of Soul back when a Catch bites in a big way, changing the scene or costing you something you wanted. |
| **[Let it ride](#s-defending)** | Leaving a telegraph unanswered, without Defending, so it lands at the end of the round against the Root it's at, where each die stops one. |
| **[Lines and veils](#s-lines-veils)** | What the table won't have in the story at all (a line), or won't play out in detail (a veil). |
| **[Locked](#s-tracks)** | A point a Root can't hold, one for each awakening on it and each Scar. It still counts on your dice. |
| **[Log](#s-log)** | One sentence a session, kept with the party sheet. |
| **[Marked](#a-hunter)** | The Hunter's special condition on a creature, at the die they won with. It gives no step and nobody spends it: anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too. It fades a size a round. |
| **[Material](#s-attunement)** | What an attuned thing needs before it reaches its next stage, found in the story or bought. A summoning attunement's material is souls. |
| **[Maximum](#s-tracks)** | A Root's number less its locked points. Its points never go past it, and a wounded Root heals only to half of it. |
| **[Mend](#s-bonds)** | Making a strained thread right with them, by playing it out, doing them a favour or paying them, in downtime or in a scene with them. Then it's whole again. |
| **[Mini faction](#s-mini-factions)** | A faction another one made, up to three each. It does two of the six outcomes and nothing else, and its parent hands it one resource and one asset to start. It sits out the downtime it's made, then acts on its own. |
| **[Mook](#s-staying)** | Challenge 2 or 3, with none or one tag keeping it going. Falls to one good win. |
| **[Moving further](#s-movement)** | Putting dice on moving past your stride. They add up, and that's how many more squares you go. |
| **[Multiplier](#s-tough)** | How many wins a tag takes to strip, like *Armoured ×4*. Every win ticks one off. |
| **[Named](#s-staying)** | Challenge 4 or 5, with two tags keeping it going. |
| **[Odds](#s-oracle-odds)** | Before an Oracle roll, say how likely it is. Likely: two Boon dice, keep the higher. Unlikely: keep the lower. |
| **[Opening](#s-win)** | A way to end a Mook or Named threat in one win: its weak point, helplessness, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two whole tags. |
| **[Oracle](#oracle)** | Three d20s that answer questions about the world. |
| **[Outcome](#s-faction-outcomes)** | What a faction's action goes for: Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire or Establish. Each changes one thing on a card. |
| **[Pack](#s-items)** | An item like a quartermaster's pack or a surgeon's roll. It adds no steps, and gives you more Resources, half its tier rounded up. |
| **[Party holdings](#s-party-resources)** | Big things the crew owns together, like a horse and cart or a house. |
| **[Perfected](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's third stage: 3 awakenings, and 3 more with a Burden and a Catch. Six at most. |
| **[Permission](#s-permissions)** | Something your character can do, a doing word with how, what or where, like *climb sheer walls*. It lives on a Title, it's 1 on the Root it fits, and it's what lets you bring a Root in. It's never counted on a roll. |
| **[Points, a Root's](#s-tracks)** | As many as the Root's number, less what's locked. They're what that Root can take before it's wounded, and what you spend to push yourself, and they never shrink your dice. |
| **[Points, to make a character](#building-character)** | What you make your character with: 35 to start. A Title, an archetype or an attuned thing is 5, with the one permission, ability or awakening it comes with, another permission, ability or awakening is 1, an item's 1 at tier 2, and 1 more raises it a tier, to tier 3 at most. |
| **[Power](#s-faction-board)** | A faction's muscle, on a die: 1 step, plus 1 for each lot of people who turn up when it calls. Nothing else adds Power. The crew has one too. |
| **[Pressure](#downtime)** | One of the four things pushing on the crew each downtime: personal, party, faction, world. |
| **[Project](#s-projects)** | Something the crew sets out to build or bring about. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, what you wrote happens. |
| **[Push](#s-abilities)** | The ability most archetypes have: +2 on one die of the mechanic after you roll, for 1 Aura, and +3 if you've taken the archetype twice. A die it takes to its top face gives no Aura. |
| **[Pushing yourself](#s-tracks)** | Spending a Root's points, to bring it in or to roll again. It never hurts you, since you can't spend what you haven't got, and spending to 0 only leaves you exposed. |
| **[Quick heal](#s-healing)** | Anyone's: 1 Aura and your action, and someone you can reach gets 1 point back on one of their Roots, no roll. It isn't a heal roll, so Toll doesn't touch it. |
| **[Reputation](#s-party-faction)** | What the city says about the crew, in one line. A step on any crew roll it fits. One for every Goal done. |
| **[Resource, a faction's](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for where its money comes from, like *Silver Mine*. The crew's resources are these too. Each one pays for one asset every downtime, and an extra one, paying for nothing, hires another faction, buys something or goes in a trade. |
| **[Resources, your](#s-resources)** | Three ordinary things you say you've got in the moment you need them, like *as it happens, I've got rope on me*. Tick one and it's real. It does what the thing does, never steps or soak, and all three come back in downtime. |
| **[Rest](#s-healing)** | At the end of every scene: your soak comes back, and you roll each Root below its maximum, unsplit, and get back what you roll. |
| **[Root](#s-roots)** | Body, Mind or Soul. Every roll's one of them, rolled at full. Each Title puts 5 on your Roots, spread however you like, and each permission 1 on its Root. A Root's number is its dice and its points. |
| **[Round](#s-round)** | The GM telegraphs, everyone takes one action, the round ends. |
| **[Round budget](#s-budget)** | What the GM telegraphs with each round, worked out for each player. A Breather's about half, and Hard's about half again. |
| **[Scar](#s-blaze)** | A hit on a wounded Root with no points left, or the Scar you take when you Blaze. You leave the scene, and it's a negative permission on that Root, something you can't do that the GM holds you to, and it locks a point of it for good. Three downtime slots grow it into a Trait as well. A Scar that locks a Root's last point is your last. |
| **[Scene](#s-aura)** | One place and one problem. Aura refills when a new one starts, and you rest when it ends. |
| **[Scene clock](#s-scene-clock)** | A die on the whole scene. It steps down every round. Past d2, the scene turns. |
| **[Shadow pool](#a-shadow)** | A Shadow's: the dice you rolled to get into hiding, to add to any roll after that, as dice or as steps. Each one's gone once you use it. |
| **[Size](#s-building-die)** | One die size. Stepping down one size is a d8 becoming a d6. |
| **[Slot](#downtime)** | A unit of downtime. You get two, and the crew takes one action, which a slot of yours can go into. |
| **[Snap](#s-bonds)** | What a thread does when you strain it again before it's mended. You still get your point of Soul back, but cross the thread out. When the last one goes, so does the bond. |
| **[Soak](#s-consequences)** | What armour takes before your Root does: as many points as its tier, a point of soak for each point. It's all back at the end of every scene. |
| **[Soul, a Summoner's](#s-summoning)** | A fallen creature a Summoner's caught, rolling against its Challenge, and kept in their summoning attunement. It comes out as a summon, no bigger than its own die. |
| **[Split](#s-splitting)** | Turning your steps into more, smaller dice: as many as you like and any sizes, as long as they add up to your steps. |
| **[Stake](#s-stakes)** | Something the crew wants from a scene, with tags in the way. |
| **[Step](#s-building-die)** | The unit of a roll. Each step is one size of die: 1 is a d2, 6 is a d12. |
| **[Strain](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's nearly snapped. Leaning on a bond thread in the story, like calling in the favour or using their name, strains it and gets you a point of Soul back, free. Doing right by someone strains a hostile thread. You can't borrow through a strained thread. |
| **[Stride](#s-movement)** | Free movement every round. 3 squares to start. A Teleporter's can be a teleport of up to 10 units. |
| **[Study](#s-study)** | An intent that finds a hidden tag or a straight answer. An Oracle can pay for *Foretold* and introduce a truth instead. |
| **[Summon](#s-summoning)** | A soul a Summoner calls out with Aura, as part of an action: a d4 of summons for each Aura. It acts in the round like anyone, fades a size every round, and loses a size to every hit that gets through. |
| **[Swap](#grow)** | In downtime, trading a permission for another that fits your Title. |
| **[Tag](#s-challenges)** | Something true about a threat, a stake or a place. It does what it says, and a big one carries a multiplier. A Maker can give an item tags for the scene, and they work like permissions. |
| **[Take a breath](#s-actions)** | Your action: 1 point back on your Mind, as far as half its maximum, rounded up. |
| **[Telegraph](#s-telegraphs)** | The GM saying, face up, what's coming, at whom, how hard, and how many hits when it's more than one. One aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. Fear, lies and pressure come as telegraphs too. |
| **[Tempered](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's second stage: 2 awakenings, and 2 more with a Burden. |
| **[Thread](#s-bonds)** | One truth you share with a bond. A bond thread's something good: borrow one thing off their sheet through it for 1 Aura, or strain it and you get a point of Soul back. A hostile thread's gone bad, and it bites like a Catch. You start with two bond threads with the crew, and three if you write a hostile one too. A faction's threads go one way, and they're how it sees the crew. |
| **[Threat budget](#s-threat-budget)** | What the GM builds a scene's threats with, growing as the crew does. It sizes multipliers and how long a scene runs. |
| **[Tick](#s-stakes)** | A clock or an event stepping down one, a faction's agenda climbing one, one win off a multiplier, or one hit on you. |
| **[Tier](#s-item-tiers)** | How good an item is, from 1 to 6, and how many steps it adds when it fits. Tier 6 is a d12's worth. Each one's got a name: Plain, Trusty, Named, Storied, Sung and Mythic. |
| **[Title](#s-title)** | Who you are: one role or one school of magic, real and specific, with up to six permissions. It's 5 points and puts 5 on your Roots, spread however you like, and it comes with one permission. It's separate from your archetypes. |
| **[Title idea](#s-title-ideas)** | A place to start a Title: twenty-nine roles and schools, each with the archetypes it suits, and permissions and going-beyond ideas to pick from. |
| **[Toll](#s-healing)** | What healing takes out of you. Every heal from someone or something else is 1 Toll, and a heal loses 5 for each Toll you've already got. It drops by 1 at the end of each scene, and downtime clears it. |
| **[Top face](#s-the-roll)** | A die's highest number. 1 Aura back, unless a Push took it there, it's a summon's, an Oracle swapped it in or a Gambler flipped it. A Warrior's explodes. |
| **[Trait](#s-how-archetypes)** | Something that's always true of you because of your archetype, written in its rule box. Every archetype has one, most cost nothing, and some get you Aura back. Take the same archetype twice and it's still the one Trait. Traits only come from archetypes, and from a Scar grown into one. |
| **[Trouble](#s-trouble)** | Something going wrong for you that the GM can run with. You bring it yourself, by letting a Catch bite or with no Catch at all, and take 1 Aura for it. |
| **[Vessel](#s-vessels)** | An item that holds charge, as much as its tier: a lantern of captured light, a prayer string. Fill it only with Aura you'd lose or a point mid-scene, and spend it only on your own abilities and awakenings, a charge for each Aura. It keeps through downtime. |
| **[Vulnerability](#s-vulnerable)** | Something that makes a hit hurt more, like *Prone* or *Vulnerable to fire*. Each one that fits a win that does harm adds a tick, and you choose where it goes, so one can strip two plain tags. On you, it's one more hit. The same one never counts twice. |
| **[Ward](#s-wards)** | A condition like *Warded* or *Blessed* that you put on a friend. Nothing pushes back, so the die you built is its die. One friend, or a second you can touch with a split. |
| **[Weak point](#s-hidden)** | The tag that holds a threat together. Break it and it falls. |
| **[Win](#s-the-roll)** | Beating the other die. Ties go to the defender. |
| **[Wound](#s-blaze)** | What a hit on a Root with no points left does: Bloodied for your Body, Frayed for your Mind and Hollow for your Soul, one each. A wounded Root heals only to half its maximum, and a hit on it with no points left is a Scar. |
| **[X-card](#s-x-card)** | A card anyone can tap to rewind the scene, no questions asked. |
