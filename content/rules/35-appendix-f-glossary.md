Here are the rule terms in the book, one line each, and each one links to its rule.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **[Action](#s-actions)** | What you spend to do something. One a round. Hold it and you've got two next round, and a Spark or a trait can give you another. |
| **[Advantage](#s-intents)** | Several dice on one intent. Your best die counts. |
| **[Agenda](#s-agendas)** | What a faction is doing right now, on a die that climbs. Past d12, it happens. |
| **[Alarm](#s-alarm)** | A site's scene clock: how much the place knows you're there. Noise steps it down too. Past d2, the place knows. |
| **[Answer](#s-answering)** | Spending your action to stop a telegraph before it lands. Friends can put theirs in too. |
| **[Arc](#s-campaigns)** | Four to six sessions around one party Goal. |
| **[Area die](#s-area)** | One of your dice that says how far your roll reaches: a burst, a cone, or a line twice as long. For a ward, a blessing or a heal, it counts friends. 1 Aura every time, and it takes a split. |
| **[Armour](#s-armour)** | Your defence against blows. Starts at d2, a size bigger for each piece of plain armour you wear. |
| **[Aspiration](#s-aspiration)** | What your character wants. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, a new Title. |
| **[Attunement](#s-attunement)** | Two slots to start, on top of what you carry. An attuned thing has permissions of its own, grows in three stages, and can't be broken. |
| **[Aura](#s-aura)** | Your nerve and luck. Up to your maximum, full every scene. It brings things in and pays for going beyond, ties when you're acting and area dice. 1 back on every top face, and every time you let trouble bite. |
| **[Awakened](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's first stage: 1 permission, and 1 more with a Catch. |
| **[Bag](#s-carrying)** | Holds 10 things on top of what you carry. Getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free. |
| **[Blaze](#s-blaze)** | Taking a Hurt with every box full. Something extraordinary happens, no roll. Then you can choose to die, or leave the scene and take a Scar. |
| **[Bond](#s-bonds)** | A person who matters to your character. |
| **[Boon](#s-boons)** | A permanent upgrade, like an extra Hurt bar or +2 stride. It costs two permission slots on one of your Titles, and you can swap it back in downtime. |
| **[Boss](#s-staying)** | Challenge 6, three or more tags keeping it going, and a weak point. One that should last has Tough as well. |
| **[Break](#s-conditions)** | Spending an action to beat a condition's die and end it. |
| **[Bring something in](#s-aura)** | 1 Aura: something that fits but isn't on your set action counts on your roll. One of your own permissions, items or traits, the ground, a tag you know, a Catch, or a friend's permission through a connection. |
| **[Burden](#s-boons)** | A permanent downside, like −2 stride. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, where it pays for two more permissions, or for a Boon for you. |
| **[Catch](#s-attunement)** | Trouble that's ready to go: on an attuned thing, or written in a Hurt bar when you fill it. Let it bite for 1 Aura, as often as you like, as long as it's real trouble. |
| **[Challenge](#s-challenges)** | The die something defends with: 2 (d4) to 6 (d12). It never steps down, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **[Climbs](#s-climb)** | How the Goal, an Aspiration, a project and an agenda move: from d2 up, a step on every win, one step a roll. Past d12, it's done. |
| **[Clock](#s-stakes)** | A die on a stake that steps down every round nobody works it. |
| **[Combo](#s-teamwork)** | Two or more actions into the same thing at the same time, yours or your friends'. Add the set actions together and roll once, with no Aura. |
| **[Condition](#s-conditions)** | A tag carrying a die, for you or against you. One step while it lasts. Spend it for its big effect. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. |
| **[Confidence](#s-confidence)** | Your defence against fear, lies and pressure. Starts at d2, a size bigger for every Title. |
| **[Connection](#s-bonds)** | One sentence that's always true between you and a bond. |
| **[Consequence](#s-consequences)** | What a landed blow or a costly miss does. The GM names it, and you can offer another that fits and costs about as much. |
| **[Crew](#s-party-faction)** | The player characters, together. Also a faction, with Power, Wealth, tags and Reputations. |
| **[Crew tag](#s-crew-tags)** | Like a trait for the crew: a step on crew rolls where it fits, and most do one thing as well. |
| **[Debt](#s-bonds)** | A connection someone else holds and can call in. It earns you a fourth connection. |
| **[Defence](#s-defending)** | The die rolled against an intent: a threat's Challenge die, or your Armour or Confidence. Anything attacked rolls it once a round, and that roll stands against everything that comes at it. |
| **[Defend](#s-defend)** | Your action: roll your Armour once for every die coming at you and choose which roll blocks which. You can pull one enemy's whole attack on friends within your stride onto yourself. It uses no steps. |
| **[Downtime](#downtime)** | Between sessions. The pressures land, everyone spends their slots, and then the factions' moves are rolled. Your Sparks all come back. |
| **[Epilogue](#s-ending)** | At the end of a campaign, a few sentences on where each character is a year later. |
| **[Equipment](#s-carrying)** | What you wear or wield: weapons, armour and shields. You carry 3. |
| **[Event](#s-stakes)** | Something running on its own clock. Past d2, it happens. |
| **[Extra action](#s-actions)** | One on top of your one a round: from holding your action, a Spark, a trait that says so, or the Boon. Its own roll, or the same one as a combo. |
| **[Face](#s-faction-board)** | The person a faction sends to deal with the crew, written as a threat. |
| **[Faction](#factions)** | A power bigger than one person: Power, Wealth, an agenda, and a Standing with the crew. |
| **[Forms](#s-unique-threats)** | A threat with two or more forms. When the last tag keeping a form going is stripped, it changes: its Tough refills and it takes new tags. |
| **[Frame](#frames)** | A ready-made kind of campaign: a world, a tone, a board of factions and a first job. |
| **[Gap](#chases)** | In a chase, the distance between you and what's after you. Past d2, caught. Past d12, away. |
| **[GM move](#s-gm-moves)** | Something the world does when everyone looks at the GM. Usually a telegraph outside a round. |
| **[Goal](#s-goal)** | What the whole crew is after this arc. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, the crew has done it, and it grows. |
| **[Going beyond](#s-going-beyond)** | Pushing a permission past what it normally does, like phasing through a wall. 1 Aura, for a sword or a spell. If you have to make a case for why it's normal, it's going beyond. |
| **[Ground](#the-ground)** | Tags on the place itself. Bring one into your roll for 1 Aura. In a chase, the die for the way ahead. |
| **[Harmony](#oracle)** | An Oracle reading: two of the three dice match. |
| **[Heal die](#s-healing)** | What a heal rolls against: a d6, a size bigger for each Catch on the patient's Hurt track. |
| **[Hold](#s-actions)** | Doing nothing this round apart from your stride, for two actions next round. |
| **[Hook](#s-aftermath)** | Something at the end of a session that pulls the crew into the next one. |
| **[Hurt](#s-blaze)** | Your Hurt track: three bars of three boxes, one track for body and mind. The loose boxes clear at the end of every scene. |
| **[Hurt bar](#s-blaze)** | Three boxes on your Hurt track. Fill one and write a Catch in it, an injury like *cracked ribs*. You start with three bars. |
| **[Intent](#s-intents)** | What a die is for, and who or what it's aimed at. Say it before you roll. |
| **[Item](#s-items)** | A thing you carry. A plain one's just what it is: a line on your roll when it fits, and it does what that thing does. |
| **[Keeps it going](#s-staying)** | The tags that are a threat's health. Every win strips one. Strip the last and it falls, unless a tag like *Forms* says otherwise. |
| **[Keepsake](#s-carrying)** | Something that matters to your character, carried on top of your equipment and utility. It has no rule of its own. |
| **[Layered](#s-layered)** | A tag for special encounters only: the first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls one Challenge die for every tag it has left keeping it going, and its best die counts. |
| **[Lead](#s-teamwork)** | An action whose steps you hand out to friends for this round: an order, a rallying song or a blessing. They don't have to fit. |
| **[Leg](#s-legs)** | A day on a journey, or one stage of it. It runs like a round. |
| **[Let it bite](#s-aura)** | Bringing a Catch, or any real trouble, down on yourself in the story. The GM runs with it, and you take 1 Aura. |
| **[Lines and veils](#s-lines-veils)** | What the table won't have in the story at all (a line), or won't play out in detail (a veil). |
| **[Log](#s-log)** | One sentence a session, kept with the party sheet. |
| **[Material](#s-attunement)** | What an attuned thing needs before it reaches its next stage, found in the story or bought. A summoning attunement's material is souls. |
| **[Mook](#s-staying)** | Challenge 2 or 3, with none or one tag keeping it going. Falls to one good win. |
| **[Move action](#s-movement)** | An action that rolls extra squares past your stride. Its dice add. |
| **[Named](#s-staying)** | Challenge 4 or 5, with two tags keeping it going. |
| **[Odds](#s-oracle-odds)** | Before an Oracle roll, say how likely it is. Likely: two Boon dice, keep the higher. Unlikely: keep the lower. |
| **[Opening](#s-win)** | A way to end a Mook or Named threat in one win: its weak point, helplessness, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two tags. |
| **[Oracle](#oracle)** | Three d20s that answer questions about the world. |
| **[Party slot](#downtime)** | One of the two downtime slots the crew shares. It's an action for the crew. |
| **[Perfected](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's third stage: 3 permissions, and 3 more with a Burden and a Catch. Six at most. |
| **[Permission](#s-permissions)** | Something your character can do, finishing *I can…*: a doing word, and how, what or where, like *climb sheer walls*. It lives on a Title, and it's 1 step on a roll it fits. |
| **[Power](#s-faction-board)** | A faction's muscle, on a die. The crew has one too. |
| **[Pressure](#downtime)** | One of the four things pushing on the crew each downtime: personal, party, faction, world. |
| **[Project](#s-projects)** | Something the crew sets out to build or bring about. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, what you wrote happens. |
| **[Pursuer](#chases)** | In a chase, whatever's after you, on a die. Past d2, it's shaken off. |
| **[Question](#s-question)** | In an investigation, what the crew wants to know, written as a stake. Each tag on it is something that's hiding the answer. |
| **[Quick heal](#s-healing)** | 1 Aura: clear one box on one person, no roll. It still takes your action. |
| **[Reputation](#s-party-faction)** | What the city says about the crew, in one line. A step on any crew roll it fits. One for every Goal done. |
| **[Round](#s-round)** | The GM telegraphs, everyone takes one action, the round ends. |
| **[Round budget](#s-budget)** | What the GM telegraphs with each round: about 5 steps for each player. A Breather's about half, and Hard's about half again. |
| **[Scar](#s-blaze)** | A Hurt box crossed out for good, after a Blaze. It's for the story, and it doesn't do anything else. |
| **[Scene](#s-aura)** | One place and one problem. Aura refills when a new one starts. |
| **[Scene clock](#s-scene-clock)** | A die on the whole scene. It steps down every round. Past d2, the scene turns. |
| **[Set action](#s-set-actions)** | A row on your sheet, like Fight or Sneak, with everything that fits it nearly every time already counted: 1 for the action, and 1 for every line. |
| **[Site](#s-site)** | Somewhere the crew goes into and has to find its way through. One scene, with an alarm for its clock. |
| **[Size](#s-building-die)** | One die size. Stepping down one size is a d8 becoming a d6. |
| **[Slot](#downtime)** | A unit of downtime. Two each, and two for the crew. |
| **[Soul](#s-summoning)** | A fallen creature caught with your Summon dice and kept in your summoning attunement. It comes out as a summon, with its own die. |
| **[Spark](#s-spark)** | One for each Title, all back every downtime, and one for taking trouble. Roll again, take an extra action, make an opportunity, add a permission, or turn down trouble. |
| **[Split](#s-splitting)** | Turning your steps into more, smaller dice. One for every action in the roll, and one more past 6 steps. |
| **[Stake](#s-stakes)** | Something the crew wants from a scene, with tags in the way. |
| **[Standing](#s-standing)** | How a faction sees the crew: Enemy, Wary, Neutral, Friendly or Ally. |
| **[Step](#s-building-die)** | The unit of a roll. Each step is one size of die: 1 is a d2, 6 is a d12. |
| **[Stride](#s-movement)** | Free movement every round. 3 squares to start. |
| **[Study](#s-study)** | An intent that finds a hidden tag, a straight answer, or something here that helps. |
| **[Summon](#s-summoning)** | A soul called out with your action. It acts in the round like anyone, rolls its own die, and one hit sends it back to its slot. |
| **[Supplies](#s-journey)** | On a journey, a clock for the crew's food and water. It steps down every leg nobody feeds the crew. |
| **[Swap](#grow)** | In downtime, trading a permission for another that fits your Title, or two of them for a Boon, and back. |
| **[Tag](#s-challenges)** | Something true about a threat, a stake, a place or a faction. It does what it says. |
| **[Take the hit](#s-take-the-hit)** | Stepping in front of an attack aimed at someone within your stride and taking it yourself. It's free, for anyone, with no defence roll. |
| **[Telegraph](#s-telegraphs)** | The GM saying, face up, what's coming, at whom, and how hard. One aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. |
| **[Tempered](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's second stage: 2 permissions, and 2 more with a Burden. |
| **[Threat budget](#s-threat-budget)** | 10 a character with two Titles, and more with every Title after. It sizes Tough and how long a scene runs. |
| **[Tick](#s-stakes)** | A clock or an event stepping down one, or a faction's agenda climbing one. |
| **[Title](#s-title)** | Who you are: a role or a school of magic, with a trait and up to six permissions. It counts 1 where it fits, and each one's a size of Confidence and a Spark. |
| **[Title idea](#s-title-ideas)** | A place to start a Title: one of 14 roles and 15 schools, with permissions, traits and going-beyond examples. |
| **[Top face](#s-the-roll)** | A die's highest number. 1 Aura back, unless it's a summon's. |
| **[Tough](#s-tough)** | Works like hit points: a number, like *Tough 40*. Every winning die takes off what it rolled, and at 0 it's gone. *(first)* means nothing under it can be stripped till it's gone. |
| **[Trail](#s-trails)** | A way to something hidden: a person who knows, a place that shows it, or a thing that proves it. Leave three. |
| **[Trait](#s-traits)** | Something you are. Each Title brings one, and you can grow more. Passive, triggered, invoked, once a scene or once a downtime. |
| **[Trouble](#s-trouble)** | The GM turns something on your sheet, or your plan, against you and offers a Spark. Take it and it happens. Spend a Spark to turn it down. |
| **[Utility](#s-carrying)** | Tools and things you use up, like rope, a lantern or a smoke bomb. You carry 3, and cross off what you use. |
| **[Weak point](#s-hidden)** | The tag that holds a threat together. Break it and it falls. |
| **[Wealth](#s-wealth)** | What money can do for a faction or the crew, on a die. Spend a step for an extra move, another faction's move, or something bought. In downtime, the crew's can buy a tie. |
| **[Win](#s-the-roll)** | Beating the other die. Ties go to the defender. |
| **[X-card](#s-x-card)** | A card anyone can tap to rewind the scene, no questions asked. |
