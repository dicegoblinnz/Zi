Here are the rule terms in the book, one line each, and each one links to its rule.

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **[Action](#s-actions)** | What you spend to do something. One a round. Hold it and you've got two next round, and Resolve, a trait or a few other things can give you another. |
| **[Advantage](#s-intents)** | Several dice on one intent. Your best die counts. |
| **[Agenda](#s-agendas)** | What a faction is doing right now, on a die that climbs. Past d12, it happens. |
| **[Alarm](#s-alarm)** | A site's scene clock: how much the place knows you're there. Noise steps it down too. Past d2, the place knows. |
| **[Animal tag](#a-shifter)** | A Shifter's: something an animal does, like wings or a breath attack, caught against its die and yours until downtime. It counts as a line when it fits, and you can strip one instead of taking a Hurt. |
| **[Answer](#s-answering)** | Spending your action to stop a telegraph before it lands. Friends can put theirs in too. |
| **[Arc](#s-campaigns)** | Four to six sessions around one Party Goal. |
| **[Archetype](#archetypes)** | What every Title is: one of seventeen, each with permissions and a mechanic only it can do. They're the Bard, Bloodsworn, Elementalist, Face, Guardian, Healer, Hunter, Illusionist, Leader, Maker, Oracle, Scholar, Shadow, Shifter, Summoner, Teleporter and Warrior. On most, 1 Aura puts +2 on one die of the mechanic, and +3 if you've taken it twice, and each has a Resolve spend too. |
| **[Area die](#s-area)** | The Elementalist's: one die that says how far your roll reaches instead of an intent, a burst, a cone or a line twice as long. It takes a split and costs no Aura. Threats have areas too. |
| **[Armour](#s-armour)** | Plate, leathers or a shield. Each piece is a line on your Defend, and one can break instead of you taking a Hurt. |
| **[Aspiration](#s-aspiration)** | What your character wants: one thing, and you'd know the day it's done (see [A good Aspiration](#s-good-aspiration)). Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12 you get what you were after, and a new archetype, with a Title you name for it. |
| **[Asset](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for what it can do, like *Standing Army* (+1 Power). It needs a resource paying for it every downtime, or it stops counting. |
| **[Attunement](#s-attunement)** | One attuned thing to start, on top of what you carry. An attuned thing has permissions of its own, grows in three stages, and can't be broken. |
| **[Aura](#s-aura)** | Your luck and flair, for the small stuff. Up to your maximum, full every scene. It brings things in, keeps a Catch quiet, wins a tie when you're acting, and pays for your archetype's +2 and a quick heal. 1 back on every top face, and every time you let a Catch bite. |
| **[Awakened](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's first stage: 1 permission, and 1 more with a Catch. |
| **[Bag](#s-carrying)** | Holds 10 things on top of what you carry. Getting something out takes your action, and putting it away is free. |
| **[Blaze](#s-blaze)** | Taking a Hurt with your wound die at d12. Something extraordinary happens, no roll. Then you can choose to die, or leave the scene and take a Scar. |
| **[Bond](#s-bonds)** | A person who matters to your character, in the crew or not, or a faction, a place or a cause. Each thread is one truth between you, a bond thread or a hostile one, and you can have as many as the story gives you. A faction has three bonds of its own: [Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty](#s-faction-bonds). |
| **[Bond thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's something good between you, like a rescue, a secret you keep for each other or a shared past. Borrow one of their permissions through it for 1 Aura, or strain it for 1 Resolve. |
| **[Boon](#s-boons)** | A permanent upgrade, like +2 stride, or the first Hurt each scene not moving your wound die. It costs two permission slots on one of your Titles, and you can swap it back in downtime. |
| **[Boss](#s-staying)** | Challenge 6, three or more tags keeping it going, multipliers on the big ones, and a weak point. |
| **[Break](#s-conditions)** | Spending an action to beat a condition's die and end it. |
| **[Bring something in](#s-aura)** | 1 Aura: something that fits but isn't on your set action counts on your roll. One of your own permissions, items or traits, the ground, a tag you know, a Catch, or a friend's permission through a thread. |
| **[Burden](#s-boons)** | A permanent downside, like −2 stride. It only ever comes on an attuned thing, where it pays for two more permissions, or for a Boon for you. |
| **[Catch](#s-trouble)** | Trouble that's ready to go: on an attuned thing, or a Scar. Let it bite for 1 Aura, as often as you like, as long as it's real trouble. |
| **[Challenge](#s-challenges)** | The die something defends with: 2 (d4) to 6 (d12). It never steps down, unless a tag like *Crumbling* says so. |
| **[Climbs](#s-climb)** | How the Goal, an Aspiration, a project and an agenda move: from d2 up, a step on every win, one step a roll. Past d12, it's done. |
| **[Clock](#s-stakes)** | A die on a stake that steps down every round nobody works it. |
| **[Combo](#s-teamwork)** | Two or more actions into the same thing at the same time, yours or your friends'. Add the set actions together and roll once, with no Aura. The same permission from two of you counts for each. |
| **[Condition](#s-conditions)** | A tag carrying a die, for you or against you. One step while it lasts, or for a few, a vulnerability. Spend it for its big effect. One of each on a target, and the same one goes on once a round. |
| **[Confidence](#s-confidence)** | Your nerve, and your defence against fear, lies and pressure. It starts every scene on d6. A size up for every telegraph your defence stops and every action that wins, and a size down every time your wound die steps up, every action where all your dice miss, and when fear gets through. |
| **[Consequence](#s-consequences)** | What a landed blow or a costly miss does. The GM names it, and you can offer another that fits and costs about as much. When fear, lies or pressure get through, you choose instead. |
| **[Crew](#s-party-faction)** | The player characters, together. Also a faction, with Power, resources, assets and Reputations. |
| **[Crew action](#downtime)** | The crew's one action a downtime, like any faction's. A player can put a personal slot into it, and two slots on one thing count everything twice. |
| **[Crew asset](#s-crew-tags)** | An asset on the crew's card, like *Informants* or *A Ship*. It does one thing, and it needs a resource paying for it. |
| **[Defence](#s-defending)** | The die rolled against an intent: a threat's Challenge die, or your Defend or Confidence. A threat rolls it once a round, and that roll stands against everyone who goes at it. Each of your defence dice stops one telegraph. |
| **[Defend](#s-defend)** | A set action on every sheet, for keeping from getting hit, with plain armour and a shield as lines on it. Defending, as your action, counts every line on it twice when the telegraphs land. |
| **[Downtime](#downtime)** | Between sessions. The pressures land, everyone spends their slots, and then the factions' actions are rolled. All your Resolve comes back, and your wound die goes back to d2, or to your Scars. |
| **[Epilogue](#s-ending)** | At the end of a campaign, a few sentences on where each character is a year later. |
| **[Equipment](#s-carrying)** | What you wear or wield: weapons, armour and shields. You carry 3. |
| **[Event](#s-stakes)** | Something running on its own clock. Past d2, it happens. |
| **[Extra action](#s-extra-actions)** | One on top of your one a round: from holding your action, 1 Resolve, coming out of hiding, fired-up Confidence, a trait that says so, or the Boon. Its own roll, or the same one as a combo. |
| **[Extra resource](#s-wealth)** | A resource that isn't paying for an asset. It's spent each downtime, not saved: it hires another faction, pays for a new asset, goes in a trade, or takes the crew a tie. |
| **[Face](#s-faction-board)** | The person a faction sends to deal with the crew, written as a threat. |
| **[Faction](#factions)** | A power bigger than one person: Power, resources, assets, an agenda, three bonds, and threads toward the crew. |
| **[Fear, lies and pressure](#s-confidence)** | Telegraphs at your Confidence. Answer them or let them ride, and nobody can intercept them. Each one that gets through drops your Confidence a size, unless you hold your ground for 1 Resolve. |
| **[Fired up](#s-confidence)** | Confidence at d12. Cash it in for an extra action or to bring your wound die down a size, and it drops back to d6. |
| **[Foresight](#a-oracle)** | The Oracle's: the numbers it rolled, swapped into anyone's dice before the round ends, as long as each fits the die. |
| **[Forms](#s-unique-threats)** | A threat with two or more forms. When the last tag keeping a form going is stripped, it changes: it takes new tags, and its multipliers come back on the ones that matter most. |
| **[Frame](#frames)** | A ready-made kind of campaign: a world, a tone, a board of factions and a first job. |
| **[Gap](#chases)** | In a chase, the distance between you and what's after you. Past d2, caught. Past d12, away. |
| **[GM move](#s-gm-moves)** | Something the world does when everyone looks at the GM. Usually a telegraph outside a round. |
| **[Goal](#s-goal)** | What the whole crew is after this arc: one thing they could get done in it (see [A good Goal](#s-good-goal)). Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, the crew has done it, it grows, and everyone writes a new thread. |
| **[Going beyond](#s-going-beyond)** | Pushing a permission past what it normally does, like phasing through a wall. 1 Resolve, for a sword or a spell. If you have to make a case for why it's normal, it's going beyond. |
| **[Ground](#the-ground)** | Tags on the place itself. Bring one into your roll for 1 Aura. In a chase, the die for the way ahead. |
| **[Harmony](#oracle)** | An Oracle reading: two of the three dice match. |
| **[Heal](#s-healing)** | The Healer's roll: an action against the patient's wound die, and each die that beats it brings it down a size. It reaches one person, or a second you can touch with a split. |
| **[Hold](#s-actions)** | Doing nothing this round apart from your stride, for two actions next round. |
| **[Hold your ground](#s-confidence)** | 1 Resolve, when fear, lies or pressure get through, so your Confidence doesn't drop. |
| **[Hook](#s-aftermath)** | Something at the end of a session that pulls the crew into the next one. |
| **[Hostile thread](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's gone bad, like a debt you can't pay or a grudge. It bites like a Catch: 1 Aura when it brings you real trouble, 1 Resolve for a big bite. |
| **[Hurt](#s-blaze)** | What a blow costs you when it lands: your wound die goes up a size. A Hurt at d12 Blazes. |
| **[Influence](#a-face)** | A Face's dice, built but not rolled, sitting on a threat. Roll them before one of its telegraphs lands, and every one of its dice your best one beats does what you say. |
| **[Intent](#s-intents)** | What a die is for, and who or what it's aimed at. Say it before you roll. |
| **[Intercept](#s-intercept)** | 1 Aura and no action: take the blows aimed at a friend within your stride, and your own Defend handles them. A Guardian or a summon does it free. Fear, lies and pressure can't be intercepted. |
| **[Item](#s-items)** | A thing you carry. A plain one's just what it is: a line on your roll when it fits, and it does what that thing does. |
| **[Keeps it going](#s-staying)** | The tags that are a threat's health. Every win strips one, or ticks one off its multiplier. Strip the last and it falls, unless a tag like *Forms* says otherwise. |
| **[Keepsake](#s-carrying)** | Something that matters to your character, carried on top of your equipment and utility. It has no rule of its own. |
| **[Layered](#s-layered)** | A tag for special encounters only: the first time it's attacked in a round, it rolls one Challenge die for every tag it has left keeping it going, and its best die counts. |
| **[Lead](#a-leader)** | The Leader's action: an order or a rallying song, built from your set action, with its steps handed out to friends who can hear or see you for this round. They don't have to fit. |
| **[Leg](#s-legs)** | A day on a journey, or one stage of it. It runs like a round. |
| **[Let it bite](#s-trouble)** | Bringing a Catch, or any real trouble, down on yourself in the story. The GM runs with it, and you take 1 Aura, or 1 Resolve when a Catch bites in a big way, changing the scene or costing you something you wanted. |
| **[Let it ride](#s-defending)** | Leaving a telegraph unanswered, without Defending, so it lands at the end of the round against your defence, where each die stops one. |
| **[Lines and veils](#s-lines-veils)** | What the table won't have in the story at all (a line), or won't play out in detail (a veil). |
| **[Log](#s-log)** | One sentence a session, kept with the party sheet. |
| **[Marked](#a-hunter)** | The Hunter's special condition on a creature, at the die they won with. It gives no step and nobody spends it: anyone who acts against it rolls the Mark die too. It fades a size a round. |
| **[Material](#s-attunement)** | What an attuned thing needs before it reaches its next stage, found in the story or bought. A summoning attunement's material is souls. |
| **[Mend](#s-bonds)** | Making a strained thread right with them, by playing it out, doing them a favour or paying them, in downtime or in a scene with them. Then it's whole again. |
| **[Mini faction](#s-mini-factions)** | A faction another one made, up to three each. It does two of the six outcomes and nothing else, and its parent hands it one resource and one asset to start. It sits out the downtime it's made, then acts on its own. |
| **[Mook](#s-staying)** | Challenge 2 or 3, with none or one tag keeping it going. Falls to one good win. |
| **[Move action](#s-movement)** | An action that rolls extra squares past your stride. Its dice add. |
| **[Multiplier](#s-tough)** | How many wins a tag takes to strip, like *Armoured ×4*. Every win ticks one off. |
| **[Named](#s-staying)** | Challenge 4 or 5, with two tags keeping it going. |
| **[Odds](#s-oracle-odds)** | Before an Oracle roll, say how likely it is. Likely: two Boon dice, keep the higher. Unlikely: keep the lower. |
| **[Opening](#s-win)** | A way to end a Mook or Named threat in one win: its weak point, helplessness, or getting around everything that keeps it going. A Boss only falls to its weak point. Any other opening strips two whole tags. |
| **[Oracle](#oracle)** | Three d20s that answer questions about the world. |
| **[Outcome](#s-faction-outcomes)** | What a faction's action goes for: Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire or Establish. Each changes one thing on a card. |
| **[Party holdings](#s-party-resources)** | Big things the crew owns together, like a horse and cart or a house. A line on any roll they fit, for 1 Aura. |
| **[Perfected](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's third stage: 3 permissions, and 3 more with a Burden and a Catch. Six at most. |
| **[Permission](#s-permissions)** | Something your character can do, or one word for what they're good at, like *climb sheer walls* or *Deceptive*. It lives on a Title, goes on two set actions at most, and it's 1 step on a roll it fits. |
| **[Power](#s-faction-board)** | A faction's muscle, on a die: 1 step, plus 1 for each asset that adds Power. The crew has one too. |
| **[Pressure](#downtime)** | One of the four things pushing on the crew each downtime: personal, party, faction, world. |
| **[Project](#s-projects)** | Something the crew sets out to build or bring about. Starts at d2 and climbs. Past d12, what you wrote happens. |
| **[Pursuer](#chases)** | In a chase, whatever's after you, on a die. Past d2, it's shaken off. |
| **[Question](#s-question)** | In an investigation, what the crew wants to know, written as a stake. Each tag on it is something that's hiding the answer. |
| **[Quick heal](#s-healing)** | Anyone's: 1 Aura brings one person's wound die down a size, no roll. It still takes your action. |
| **[Reputation](#s-party-faction)** | What the city says about the crew, in one line. A step on any crew roll it fits. One for every Goal done. |
| **[Resolve](#s-resolve)** | Your character pushing themselves, so it pays for the big stuff: an extra action, a reroll, going beyond, holding your ground, acting at what the scene's about when you're shaken, or your archetype's Resolve spend. As much as you've got Titles. 1 back at the end of every scene, and all of it after downtime. |
| **[Resolve spend](#resolve-on-your-mechanic)** | Something big each archetype does for 1 Resolve, like the Guardian shrugging off a hit. |
| **[Resource](#s-wealth)** | A permission on a faction's card for where its money comes from, like *Silver Mine*. Each one pays for one asset every downtime, and an extra one, paying for nothing, hires another faction, buys something or goes in a trade. |
| **[Rest](#s-blaze)** | At the end of every scene: roll the Confidence you finished on against your wound die, and a win brings it down a size. |
| **[Round](#s-round)** | The GM telegraphs, everyone takes one action, the round ends. |
| **[Round budget](#s-budget)** | What the GM telegraphs with each round: about 5 steps for each player. A Breather's about half, and Hard's about half again. |
| **[Scar](#s-blaze)** | Your wound, after a Blaze, written as a Catch. Your wound die never goes below a size for each one, and with five, your next Blaze is your last. |
| **[Scene](#s-aura)** | One place and one problem. Aura refills and Confidence starts on d6 when a new one starts, and you get 1 Resolve back when it ends. |
| **[Scene clock](#s-scene-clock)** | A die on the whole scene. It steps down every round. Past d2, the scene turns. |
| **[Set action](#s-set-actions)** | A row on your sheet, like Defend, Fight or Sneak, with everything that fits it nearly every time already counted: 1 for the action, and 1 for every line. |
| **[Shadow pool](#a-shadow)** | A Shadow's: the dice you rolled to get into hiding, to add to any roll after that, as dice or as steps. Each one's gone once you use it. |
| **[Shaken](#s-confidence)** | Confidence at d2. You can't take an action at what the scene's about unless you spend 1 Resolve each time, and otherwise your action goes on pulling yourself together, back up to d4. The condition *Shaken* is one step less on your Confidence, and spending it drops your Confidence a size ([Conditions](#s-conditions)). |
| **[Site](#s-site)** | Somewhere the crew goes into and has to find its way through. One scene, with an alarm for its clock. |
| **[Size](#s-building-die)** | One die size. Stepping down one size is a d8 becoming a d6. |
| **[Slot](#downtime)** | A unit of downtime. You get two, and the crew takes one action, which a slot of yours can go into. |
| **[Snap](#s-bonds)** | What a thread does when you strain it again before it's mended. You still take the Resolve, but cross the thread out. When the last one goes, so does the bond. |
| **[Soul](#s-summoning)** | A fallen creature a Summoner's caught with their Summon dice and kept in their summoning attunement. It comes out as a summon, with its own die. |
| **[Split](#s-splitting)** | Turning your steps into more, smaller dice. One for every action in the roll, and one more past 6 steps. |
| **[Stake](#s-stakes)** | Something the crew wants from a scene, with tags in the way. |
| **[Step](#s-building-die)** | The unit of a roll. Each step is one size of die: 1 is a d2, 6 is a d12. |
| **[Strain](#s-bonds)** | A thread that's nearly snapped. Leaning on a bond thread in the story, like calling in the favour or using their name, strains it and gives you 1 Resolve, free. Doing right by someone strains a hostile thread. You can't borrow through a strained thread. |
| **[Stride](#s-movement)** | Free movement every round. 3 squares to start. A Teleporter's can be a teleport of up to 10 units. |
| **[Study](#s-study)** | An intent that finds a hidden tag or a straight answer. A Scholar can introduce a truth instead. |
| **[Summon](#s-summoning)** | A soul a Summoner calls out with their action. It acts in the round like anyone, rolls its own die, and one hit sends it back to its slot. |
| **[Supplies](#s-journey)** | On a journey, a clock for the crew's food and water. It steps down every leg nobody feeds the crew. |
| **[Swap](#grow)** | In downtime, trading a permission for another that fits your Title, or two of them for a Boon, and back. |
| **[Tag](#s-challenges)** | Something true about a threat, a stake or a place. It does what it says, and a big one carries a multiplier. A Maker can give an item tags for the scene, and they work like permissions. |
| **[Take a breath](#s-confidence)** | Your action: your Confidence comes up a size, as far as d6. |
| **[Telegraph](#s-telegraphs)** | The GM saying, face up, what's coming, at whom, and how hard. One aimed at you lands wherever you've gone. Fear, lies and pressure come as telegraphs too. |
| **[Tempered](#s-attunement)** | An attuned thing's second stage: 2 permissions, and 2 more with a Burden. |
| **[Thread](#s-bonds)** | One truth you share with a bond. A bond thread's something good: borrow a permission through it for 1 Aura, or strain it for 1 Resolve. A hostile thread's gone bad, and it bites like a Catch. You start with two bond threads with the crew, and three if you write a hostile one too. A faction's threads go one way, and they're how it sees the crew. |
| **[Threat budget](#s-threat-budget)** | 10 a character with two Titles, and more with every Title after. It sizes multipliers and how long a scene runs. |
| **[Tick](#s-stakes)** | A clock or an event stepping down one, a faction's agenda climbing one, one win off a multiplier, or one Hurt. |
| **[Title](#s-title)** | The name you give your archetype: one role or one school of magic, real and specific, with up to six permissions. It counts 1 where it fits, and each one's 1 Resolve on your maximum. |
| **[Title idea](#s-title-ideas)** | A place to start a Title: twenty-nine roles and schools, each with the archetypes it suits, and permissions, traits and going-beyond ideas to pick from. |
| **[Top face](#s-the-roll)** | A die's highest number. 1 Aura back, unless your archetype's +2 took it there, it's a summon's, or an Oracle swapped it in. A Warrior's explodes. |
| **[Trail](#s-trails)** | A way to something hidden: a person who knows, a place that shows it, or a thing that proves it. Leave three. |
| **[Trait](#s-traits)** | Something you are, with one effect. You start with two, on their own, and you can grow more. Passive, triggered, invoked, once a scene or once a downtime. |
| **[Trouble](#s-trouble)** | Something going wrong for you that the GM can run with. You bring it yourself, by letting a Catch bite or with no Catch at all, and take 1 Aura for it. |
| **[Utility](#s-carrying)** | Tools and things you use up, like rope, a lantern or a smoke bomb. You carry 3, and cross off what you use. |
| **[Vulnerability](#s-vulnerable)** | Something that makes a hit hurt more, like *Prone* or *Vulnerable to fire*. Each one that fits a win that does harm adds a tick, and you choose where it goes, so one can strip two plain tags. The same one never counts twice. |
| **[Ward](#s-wards)** | A condition like *Warded* or *Blessed* that you put on a friend. Nothing pushes back, so the die you built is its die. One friend, or a second you can touch with a split. |
| **[Weak point](#s-hidden)** | The tag that holds a threat together. Break it and it falls. |
| **[Win](#s-the-roll)** | Beating the other die. Ties go to the defender. |
| **[Wound die](#s-blaze)** | How hurt you are: a d2 to start, a size up for every Hurt, and what a heal has to beat. A Hurt at d12 Blazes. |
| **[X-card](#s-x-card)** | A card anyone can tap to rewind the scene, no questions asked. |
