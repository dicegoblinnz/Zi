# The first people

**Kind:** cosmos
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

The idea Abnu grew from: the Anunnaki theory, that visitors came to Earth, changed the apes into intelligent people to mine for them, were taken for gods (the flat-topped ziggurats as landing places), and left.

## What's true
- Mo is using the concept of a first people, not the Anunnaki themselves and not a spacefaring civilisation. (Canon)
- He isn't sure yet how much this matters to the world as it stands. (Canon)
- The twins' people were the first civilisation, the way the Sumerians were. There were "people" before them, but nothing that was a civilisation. (Canon)
