# The Garden of Souls

**Kind:** cosmos
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

Where souls go at death. Anyone with their soul eyes open sees it in place of the sky.

## What's true
- Someone who has activated their soul eyes sees souls all around them, and looking up they see a glorious garden of souls, not an ordinary sky. (Canon)
- It's a garden because plants die far faster and in far greater numbers than creatures do, so most of the souls up there are plants'. (Canon)
- When people die they go up into the Garden and live there. It's the heaven or Eden of other stories, made visible. (Canon)
- From the Garden a soul can look back down at the planet. (Canon)
- A soul in the Garden can jump back down and take a living person's body. Nobody had considered it until the Queen did it. See [[the-jumping]]. (Canon)

- [[the-twins]]: the first to jump back.
