# Rebirth

**Kind:** cosmos
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

After the moon fell the Seven made a way for a soul to choose another life. About one soul in ten is one of these, and it's where natural talent comes from. Mo's inspiration is Buddhist reincarnation.

## What's true
- With nine moons, the souls being made were getting weaker. The Seven made a reincarnation system to answer it. See [[the-fall-of-the-moon]]. (Canon)
- It's a choice. People line up to live another life. (Canon)
- It's much slower than the moons, and can't keep up with the number of souls needed. For every ten souls, one comes from reincarnation. (Canon)
- That's natural talent. A reborn soul is good at things because of what it knew in a past life, without knowing why. (Canon)
- People know that once they jump through they'll lose their memory of the past. (Canon)

## Ties
- [[garden-of-souls]]: where they line up from.
- [[the-jumping]]: the other way back, which steals a living body. Rebirth is the lawful one.
- [[the-soul]]: talent.
