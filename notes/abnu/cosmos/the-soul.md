# The soul and what it can do

**Kind:** cosmos
**Status:** Canon, first pass. Mo isn't married to the awakenings or the schools and wants a session to build the magic properly.
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

Soul is power. A person who awakens their soul can bend it, and what it becomes depends on who they are. How much of it they can bend goes up through four awakenings, and what they bend it into falls into four schools.

## What's true
- People slowly discovered they could activate their souls. In the twins' time this was still new. (Canon)
- Activating the soul is a person's supernatural ability. First inspired by elemental bending, but Mo wants it wider than elements: the magic is bent and shaped by the way you are. (Canon)
- A link held from birth, like the twins', strengthens both without their knowing. (Canon)

### The four awakenings
How much soul a person can control. (Canon, first pass. Mo may rename "awakening" for the later ones.)
- **First, the awakening.** It usually starts with the eyes: soul eyes see other souls, their wavelengths, and the [[garden-of-souls]]. The person controls their soul at a point or a small sphere. One dagger moved, the tip of a fist made hard as steel, one person linked to and a thought shared.
- **Second.** A body part's worth of soul, such as a whole limb. The whole hand hardened, five or ten daggers carried, another person's thoughts and perceptions worked on.
- **Third.** A whole body's worth of soul. It's as far as an ordinary soul can go.
- **Fourth.** The soul works outside the body. The body becomes a sort of limb and the soul is unbound by the physical world, so it can travel at incredible speeds, up to feats at the speed of light. Only the [[anunnaki]] can reach it.
- Each step up is a massive jump in power, and the fourth over the third is bigger again. (Canon)

### The four schools
What a soul is bent into. (Canon, first pass, working names.)
- **The brave soul**: the body. Hardening it, explosive movement, mastery down to the muscle.
- **The divine soul**: mending others' wounds, or inflicting them and ailments. It speeds up a person's own healing and can't heal them past what they could heal naturally. Scars are a big deal, and a divine soul can't heal one unless they've mastered it ("but that's another story").
- **The ambitious soul**: working on the objects around you and changing their properties. At the second awakening a blade can be made watery and liquid and worked in that form as it's swung. Its mastery is teleporting from place to place.
- **The empathetic soul**: connection. Linking to others and sharing ideas and senses, then working on their thoughts and ideas and making them see what you want them to see.

### From the first telling, and where it sits now
- Hardening (a fist like rock or steel, a body a blow doesn't hurt) is the brave soul. (Canon)
- Moving things (knives flying, riding something through the air) is the ambitious soul. (Canon; Claude's placing, Mo to confirm)
- Soul links are the empathetic soul. One to many shares information across a group. One to one, deep, changes what the other believes and experiences, a charm on the mind itself and not a picture in the air. (Canon)
- Many linked to one can be drained: the Queen siphoned a hundred followers' power into herself. See [[the-queens-war]]. (Canon)
