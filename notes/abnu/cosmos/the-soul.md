# The soul and what it can do

**Kind:** cosmos
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

Soul is power. A person who activates their soul can bend it, and what it becomes depends on who they are.

## What's true
- People slowly discovered they could activate their souls. In the twins' time this was still new. (Canon)
- Activating the soul is a person's supernatural ability. First inspired by elemental bending, but Mo wants it wider than elements: the magic is bent and shaped by the way you are. (Canon)
- Soul eyes: an activated soul sees other souls, and the [[garden-of-souls]] in the sky. (Canon)
- Hardening: a fist made to hit like rock, iron or steel, or a body hardened so a blow doesn't hurt. (Canon)
- Moving things: making knives and other objects fly, or riding something through the air. (Canon)
- Soul links: connecting to another soul to send thoughts, and in theory to see through their eyes. One person to many shares information across a group. One person to one, deep, can change what the other believes and experiences, a charm on the mind itself and not a picture in the air. (Canon)
- A link held from birth, like the twins', strengthens both without their knowing. (Canon)
