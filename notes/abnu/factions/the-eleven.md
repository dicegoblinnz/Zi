# The eleven: the Seven and the four above them

**Kind:** faction
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Eleven individuals Abnu created to stop the dead jumping back. Seven are the virtues people now worship. Four more stand above them, each with a task.

## What's true
- Abnu created eleven. (Canon)
- Seven are the seven virtues: the traits that make a good person, morally and in idea. People worship the Seven. (Canon)
- The Seven aren't Anunnaki, but the Anunnaki named them, in the old tongue, and the names stuck. (Canon)
- Their names are Sumerian, not the Bible's (23 Nov, replacing "they go by the virtues' names"): **Gina** (truth, standing firm), **Zid** (faithfulness, doing right), **Sisa** (justice, made straight), **Arhush** (compassion), **Kuzu** (wisdom), **Ursag** (courage) and **Ni** (reverence, humility). (Canon)
- Mo would like eleven virtues in all if that can be made to work. (Proposed, his maybe)
- Four stand above the Seven: (Canon)
  - **The Oracle** sees the future, and helps the Seven and the others act on it.
  - **The Prophet** is Alysta. His soul is pure now, and he's the ideal person for people to follow and take guidance from. He spreads the word of how people should live, so the living can live good lives by the Seven's philosophies.
  - **The dagger-maker** was sent to make an Abnu dagger that permanently seals whatever it stabs, to hunt the Queen and her brother. He became so afraid of it being stolen that he refuses to sleep, sure someone will take it the moment his eyes close. It's slowly destroying his soul and has driven him mad.
  - **The Infiltrator** went into the Queen's circle to win her trust and get close enough to end her.
- The Seven can only take a soul at the moment of its death. (Canon)
- They planned the mass eradication of the Queen and the Anunnaki for a long time. Because of that planning, when she died at the end of her war, catching her and the rest and putting them in one of the moons was easy. See [[the-queens-war]]. (Canon)
- Putting them in the moon is all the eleven's seal was. It isn't the cause of memory loss. (Canon)
- The Infiltrator died in that battle and is trapped in the moon with her. They knew what they were signing up for and are at peace with it. (Canon)
- The Seven saw souls weakening and made [[rebirth]]. (Canon)
- The Prophet has his own entry: [[alysta]]. He now lives in an Abnu amulet the Seven made with him, takes its wearers' bodies, and doesn't know he's the Prophet. The Seven let it go on because the good he does outweighs it, which is a question over them. (Canon)
