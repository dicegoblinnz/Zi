# The Soulless

**Kind:** faction
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Alysta's special agents. Each has their soul held in a copy of his amulet, so when they die the amulet goes on a new body and they carry on.

## What's true
- [[alysta]] found a way to replicate his amulet, though never the way his own works. (Canon)
- He has people infused into those amulets. They're called the Soulless, and they're his special agents, extremely loyal to him. (Canon)
- When one dies, all that's needed is to recover the amulet and put it on a new body, and that person continues from where they left off. (Canon)
- Like his, the amulet kicks the body's own soul out. (Canon)
- He only brings in people who are extremely loyal and who consent. He makes sure they know exactly what they're signing up for: that they'll be stuck like this effectively forever, and there's potentially no way out. (Canon)
- He promises to break them free once he finds a way to break himself free. (Canon)
