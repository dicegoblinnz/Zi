# The fall of the moon

**Kind:** event
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

The big event. The Queen, trapped in a moon, shook it down into the sea. Its fall wipes the memory of every soul that jumps through, and with nine moons the souls made since are weaker.

## What's true
- Time went on, generation after generation. The Queen was stubborn and wouldn't accept that end. (Canon)
- She tried to break out by sheer willpower. It wasn't enough. (Canon)
- She linked to every soul inside the moon. That should have been next to impossible: Abnu suspends souls and stops their use, which is what makes it so powerful, and being trapped in it is as good as dead, if not worse. (Canon)
- Through those souls she made bursts of energy every chance she got, and each one shook the moon to its core. (Canon)
- Every shake brought the moon closer (Mo said "to the equator"), until one day it broke through. It fell into the deepest part of the ocean. (Canon)
- The moon passing through "the field" made a massive disruption, which Mo also calls the rift. Since then, every soul that jumps through loses its memory. (Canon)
- The world carried on as normal. At first the only one affected was the Prophet, [[the-eleven|Alysta]] (not yet called that), who was slowly losing parts of himself. (Canon)
- Then it was noticed that souls being made were losing connection to themselves. Fewer people could awaken, let alone reach the second or third. The world's supernatural ability began to fade, until it was rare: not vanishingly, but not everyone has it. (Canon)
- The reason: the ten moons each served a purpose and worked in synchronicity, a perfect balance for making souls. With one missing, the quality of souls has slowly gone down. (Canon)
- The Seven saw this and made [[rebirth]]. (Canon)
- This is where Mo's telling of the world's creation ends. (Canon)
