# The jumping

**Kind:** event
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

The short, chaotic age when the dead learned they could come back.

## What's true
- The Queen jumped from the Garden back into a living body, and her brother joined her. It was "an infinite resurrection glitch". (Canon)
- They told people they could do it too, and for a short while many did. (Canon)
- Abnu answered by creating eleven individuals to seal it and stop the abuse. See [[the-eleven]]. (Canon)
- The soul already in a taken body stays. It witnesses everything and can do nothing, like sleep paralysis. (Canon; Mo first had it leaving for the Garden and changed his mind)
- Jumping back cost nothing until the moon fell. Since the fall, it costs the jumper's memory, and that's purely the fall's disruption. See [[the-fall-of-the-moon]]. (Canon)
- It may also do something to Abnu. (Proposed, Mo's maybe)
- Jumping is a cancer in the world's Body: a cell that refuses to die when it should, then spreads. The Queen is that cell. A taken body is the virus side of it, a host made to serve. (Canon)
- The Queen still expected to jump back at the end of her war, so the way was open till then. What stopped her was being caught at death and put in a moon. See [[the-queens-war]]. (Canon)

## Retired
- "The memory cost is the eleven's seal working." Replaced on 24 Nov: memory loss is purely the moon's fall. The eleven's part was only putting the Queen and her people in the moon, which led to the fall.
