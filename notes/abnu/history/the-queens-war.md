# The Queen's war

**Kind:** event
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Working label. The twins' rule after they came back, the war between the Anunnaki and the ordinary souls, and the meteor shower that ended it and made the Montian archipelago.

## What's true
- Once the Queen and her brother ruled, there was a lot of infighting and conquest. They used their power to force their regime. It was dictatorial: they expected things to go their way, and since might made right, they got it. (Canon)
- They gathered the [[anunnaki]], and it became the Anunnaki against the ordinary souls. (Canon)
- Her brother was destroyed for good at some point in it, taking a soul-destroying blow meant for her, and she became the mad queen. See [[the-twins]]. (Canon)
- The ordinary souls were far more numerous, and through sheer numbers they overpowered the Queen and her people. (Canon)
- She was cornered in a massive battle and grew desperate. She linked herself to a hundred of her followers, Anunnaki who were powerful already, and siphoned their power into herself alone. (Canon)
- With it she pulled the very rocks from between the moons and brought them down as a meteor shower. (Canon)
- The land was far more connected then. The meteors destroyed it and left an archipelago, which is the Kingdom of [[montia]] today. (Canon)
- She couldn't control the shower as it fell, because her power ran out. It wiped out her and her people. (Canon)
- She didn't mind dying, since she believed she'd just jump back in. (Canon)
- The moment she died she was caught and thrown inside one of the moons, unable to escape. The Seven had been planning the mass eradication for a long time, which is why it went easily when the moment came. They can only take a soul at the moment of death. The Queen and the Anunnaki were all trapped in that moon. (Canon)
- The Infiltrator died there too and is trapped with her. They knew what they were signing up for and are at peace with it. (Canon)
