# Abnu: the order of things

**Changed:** 2026-11-24

The order Mo has told it in. No dates or lengths yet.

1. Ten moons of Abnu make souls. People live, die and go up to the Garden. Nobody comes back.
2. People before civilisation, then the first civilisation, the twins' people. Souls begin to be awakened.
3. The twins are taken as children, rise, and she becomes the first queen of the world. They're assassinated.
4. [[the-jumping]]: she jumps back, he follows, they tell others, and many do it.
5. Abnu makes [[the-eleven]].
6. [[the-queens-war]]: the twins rule by force, the Anunnaki against the ordinary souls, the meteor shower, Montia's islands, and the Queen and the Anunnaki trapped in a moon.
7. Generations pass.
8. [[the-fall-of-the-moon]]: she shakes the moon into the sea. Memory is wiped on the way down, and souls weaken.
9. Awakening grows rare. The Seven make [[rebirth]].
10. Now. More than a thousand years after the Queen was trapped. She's still in the moon on the seabed, and the Spymaster is working to free her.
11. To come, in Mo's planned story: the first chronicle is Dimitri's journey, and the second starts with the Spymaster releasing the Queen.
