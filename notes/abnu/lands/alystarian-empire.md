# The Alystarian Empire

**Kind:** land
**Status:** Canon, a stub
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

The empire [[alysta]] built and rules, where worship of the Seven is banned.

## What's true
- It's the Alystarian Empire. Mo spelled it on 24 Nov. (Canon)
- Alysta built it. His amulet is known there as an heirloom of the empire. (Canon)
- While the amulet lay buried for a century or two, it rotted: by the time he came back, in the body of the emperor of the day, that emperor was surrounded by corrupt officials and the city and nation were on the brink of collapse. (Canon)
- He decided to stay and keep constant watch so its people are good and better off. (Canon)
- The whole nation is banned from worshipping the Seven, because of what Alysta came to think of them. (Canon)
- [[the-soulless]] are his agents in it. (Canon)
