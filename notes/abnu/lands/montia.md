# Montia

**Kind:** land
**Status:** Canon, a stub
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

A kingdom on an archipelago. The islands are what the Queen's meteor shower left of a land that was once joined up.

## What's true
- The land was far more connected before. The meteor shower at the end of [[the-queens-war]] destroyed it and left the Montian archipelago. (Canon)
- It's the Kingdom of Montia today. (Canon)

## Open
- Spelling: Mo dictated it and it came through as "Montea" and "Montaian". The older material spells it Montia and Montian, which is held here till he says otherwise.
- Everything else: its people, crown, factions, and what living on the Queen's battlefield has done to them.
