## 23 Nov 2026

Set up the store. Mo's calls: canon lives in this repo, the older Abnu material is reference only, and Claude challenges ideas that don't click.

First telling recorded: Abnu the rock and the ten moons, the Garden of Souls, what the soul can do, the twins (the first Queen and her brother), the jumping, and the eleven (the Seven and the four above). Different from the older Abnu: the Queen is a human born in the tribes, not the greatest of the first people; she has a twin; Abnu made eleven, not seven. Proposed and waiting: the dagger never reached the Infiltrator, and the Prophet is the older Alysta. 

Settled the same day: Abnu never speaks; a taken body's soul stays and watches; jumping as a cancer, with memory loss as the eleven's seal; Body, Mind and Soul as world, Garden and Abnu; the Seven's names; Alysta as the Prophet; a moon falls, and rebirth is only needed after it. Still proposed: the dagger never reached the Infiltrator. Pick up with Mo's telling of the moon's fall.
