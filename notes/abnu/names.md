# Abnu: names

Settled spellings and each culture's sounds. Mo dictates, so every new name is checked once and then held.

## Settled
- **Abnu**: the rock of the moons. Sumerian for rock.
- **Garden of Souls**
- **soul eyes**
- **the Seven**
- **Gina, Zid, Sisa, Arhush, Kuzu, Ursag, Ni**: the Seven.
- **Alysta**: the Prophet. Mo confirmed the spelling on 24 Nov. Dictation writes "Alistair"; always read it as Alysta.
- **the Oracle**, **the Prophet**: two of the four above the Seven.
- **Alystarian Empire**: Alysta's empire. Mo's spelling, 24 Nov.
- **Anunnaki**: the Queen's people, who can reach the fourth awakening.

- **Dimitri**: confirmed 24 Nov. Not Sumerian, on purpose.

- **Ushumgal**: the serpent. Sumerian ušum-gal, great serpent or great dragon. Dictation may write "Ushmugal". Replaces Jormungandr.

## Rule
- Sumerian is the old tongue of the first civilisation, so it belongs to the Anunnaki and to what they named: the Seven, and Abnu. Later people, like Dimitri, aren't named in it. (Mo, 24 Nov)

## To check with Mo
- **Montia**, **Montian**: the archipelago kingdom. Dictated as "Montea" and "Montaian". Held as the older material spells it.
