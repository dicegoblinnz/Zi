# Abnu: names

Settled spellings and each culture's sounds. Mo dictates, so every new name is checked once and then held.

## Settled
- **Abnu**: the rock of the moons. Sumerian for rock.
- **Garden of Souls**
- **soul eyes**
- **the Seven**
- **Alysta**: the Prophet.
- **the Oracle**, **the Prophet**: two of the four above the Seven.

## Working labels, not names
- the Queen, her brother, the twins
- the dagger-maker, the Infiltrator, the Abnu dagger
- the first people
