# Alysta, the Prophet

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

One of the four above the Seven, made to guide the living toward the virtues. He now lives inside an Abnu amulet and takes over whoever wears it, and he doesn't know he's the Prophet. Mo calls him one of his best characters. Dictation writes "Alistair"; it's always Alysta.

## What's true
- He's the Prophet, one of [[the-eleven]]: the ideal person for people to follow, spreading how to live by the Seven's philosophies. (Canon)
- He lived life after life to keep pushing people toward the virtues. Each was a rebirth into a fresh body, with no other soul in it. (Canon)
- After the moon fell he began losing his memory, in the first half of his journey, and was the first to suffer from the fall. (Canon)
- He realised the next jump might cost him everything, so the amulet was made, with the Seven, to hold his soul once he was back down on the world. (Canon)
- He made that jump, and it did take his memory. (Canon)
- His soul, his entire person, is now trapped in the amulet. In it he never goes up and down again, so he keeps everything from then on. (Canon)
- Whoever wears the amulet has their soul kicked out of their body, and he takes over. It doesn't trap them inside, the way the Anunnaki's jumping did. (Canon)
- Nobody in the world knows he's the Prophet. He doesn't know it himself. (Canon)
- It's intended that the Prophet does the very thing the Seven were made to stop. One person doing it, for a particular job, is excusable where many weren't. (Canon)
- The Seven let it go on because, despite everything, his nature is to care for his people and want them to do good. His presence and actions ripple out and make the world better, and that good outweighs the horrific things he does. Which puts a question over the Seven and their motives. (Canon)
- Mo counts him as technically Anunnaki, in that sense. (Canon)
- Wearing Abnu suppresses the soul, so he can never awaken his. He's always dormant. (Canon)
- He developed Abnu-laced gear instead: handcuffs, whips, bolas and nets with Abnu shaved to dust and worked in, which drain a person's ability to use their soul. With those and his expertise he took down one of the Anunnaki, a massive feat. (Canon)
- He found a way to replicate the amulet, though never the way his own works, and made [[the-soulless]]. (Canon)
- He wants to break himself free of the amulet, and has promised the Soulless he'll free them once he can. (Canon)
- In the story to come he's among those who have to stop the Queen once she's released. See [[dimitri]]. (Canon)
