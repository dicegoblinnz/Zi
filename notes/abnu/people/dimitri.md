**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon, a stub. Mo will tell more later.

One of the core characters, and the lead of the main story Mo has in his head for the setting.

## What's true
- The main "storybook" for the whole setting is Dimitri's journey. He learns about himself, which is the way the world gets explained. (Canon)
- His mentor and father figure is [[the-spymaster]]. He finds out later that the Spymaster has been orchestrating everything, to make the chance for Dimitri to free the Queen and her subjects. (Canon)
- The first chronicle ends and the second starts with the Spymaster releasing the Queen. After that, Dimitri and everyone else, Alysta included, have to find ways to stop the world's destruction and her subjugation of everyone. (Canon; what could be coming)
