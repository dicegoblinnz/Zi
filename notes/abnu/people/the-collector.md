# The Collector

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Also called the Genie. One of the surviving Anunnaki, who needs no body, grants wishes in return for souls, and carries enough of them to topple nations. He's on nobody's side but his own.

## What's true
- He's one of the [[anunnaki]]. (Canon)
- He worked out that the Seven were trapping the Anunnaki when they went up, so he found a way to keep himself grounded. He lost his vessel and still stands on the earth, strong enough not to go up. (Canon)
- That leaves him in the fourth-awakening form for good, which makes him formidable. (Canon)
- He trades for souls. He offers someone a wish, and in return their soul is his when they die. That's why people call him the Collector. He began as a genie for the world to deal with. (Canon)
- He grants a wish by doing it himself, by his own means. Riches: he steals them. A death: he kills them. (Canon)
- What's outside his means he says so and won't take on. He can't bring back the dead, because he can't fetch a soul from the Garden. (Canon)
- He carries the souls in a massive gourd on his back. (Canon; Mo wants it Sumerian-themed)
- With them he could raise an army that topples nations if he wished. (Canon)
- A soul can be spent to disintegrate another, and that works with a soul you hold as well as your own. So he's one of the most powerful beings in the world. (Canon)
- He didn't oppose the Queen and he didn't want to help her. He has his own agenda, which Mo hasn't worked out yet. (Canon)

## What people believe
A genie who grants wishes, and the Collector who comes for you after.
