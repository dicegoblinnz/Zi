# The farmer and his orb

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon. Mo has him mainly for connections later.
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Working label. An Anunnaki who refused the Queen, was killed for it, and had his soul trapped in an orb. The orb now lights a deep cavern like a small sun, and a buried people live by it.

## What's true
- He was one of the [[anunnaki]], and a farmer. He kept refusing to join the Queen. (Canon)
- He commanded nature. Everything growing is a living soul, so he could connect to plants at a deep level, grow them at an exceptional rate and control the natural world around him. (Canon)
- Someone fought him and beat him, and only could because he was protecting his farm. (Canon)
- It was a special commander in the Queen's guard. Mo will tell more when he gets to it. (Canon)
- That man killed him and trapped his soul in an orb before it left his body. (Canon)
- The orb isn't Abnu. It's a containment device they built, so his soul can still radiate from it. (Canon)
- The orb was lost. Mo hasn't settled how: dropped or let go somehow. It went into a river, the river carried it to the sea, and currents and waves lodged it in a crevice, where over time it was pushed until it blocked one of the entrances to a deep cavern that water used to fall into now and then. (Canon)
- There it radiates his energy like a mini sun. It lights the place and makes everything around it grow, so the cavern is lush and green. (Canon)
- In that sense he's still alive. (Canon)
- Generations later the [[molefolk]] came to live by it. (Canon)
