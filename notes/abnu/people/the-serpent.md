# The serpent

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Mo calls him Jormungandr for now, after the Norse serpent. A massive sea serpent, one of the surviving Anunnaki, loyal to the Queen and waiting.

## What's true
- He's a massive serpent in the sea, and one of the [[anunnaki]]. (Canon)
- The land's dangers are great, but underwater is far more dangerous, and he sits at the top of that. (Canon)
- His skin is so cold that anyone who touches it gets frostbite. He doesn't freeze the water he swims through. (Canon)
- He can make enough cold around his body that he looks frozen still, and anyone who gets close is frozen still too. Not as ice: he can drop his body to almost the bottom of cold (Mo said "0 Fahrenheit", meaning the point where everything stops), which is like time stopping. (Canon)
- He's loyal to the Queen. He can speak, but he isn't intelligent enough to scheme or plot the way the Spymaster does. He's waiting for the moment, and then he'll rise. (Canon)
