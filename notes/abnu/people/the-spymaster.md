# The Spymaster

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Working label. One of the Anunnaki, still loose, who has spent well over a thousand years working to free the Queen from the fallen moon. He's the hidden hand behind the setting's main story.

## What's true
- He's one of the [[anunnaki]]. Mo calls him the Spy Master. (Canon)
- He was the Queen's spymaster and was part of her war. His job was to go about spying for her, so he wasn't at the final encounter, and the moon never took him. (Canon)
- He has been plotting for centuries, definitely more than a thousand years, to find a way to break the Queen free. (Canon)
- He is [[dimitri]]'s mentor and father figure. Dimitri finds out later that he has been orchestrating everything, and making the chance for Dimitri to free the Queen and her subjects. (Canon)
- The second chronicle starts with him releasing the Queen. (Canon; Mo says this is what could be coming, not where the world sits now)
