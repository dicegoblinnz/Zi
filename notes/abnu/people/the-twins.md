# The twins: the Queen and her brother

**Kind:** person
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-23

A sister and brother born among the early tribes, taken as small children, who rose to rule and then refused to stay dead. She was the first queen of the world.

## What's true
- Twins, born with a soul link they didn't know they had, which kept strengthening them both. (Canon)
- Their village was raided when they were very young and mercenaries took them. She was forced into prostitution and he was made a child soldier. (Canon)
- Both mastered their craft very young. (Canon)
- He learned to link to many people at once and share what each knew, at a time when getting word across a battlefield was nearly impossible. His unit fought as one, and he became a superb commander. (Canon)
- She learned to link to one soul and shape what that person believed and felt. Her patrons got an experience nobody else could give, and became desperate for her. That brought her wealth and protectors, until she had more power than the person who owned her. (Canon)
- Together they took over the mercenaries who had enslaved them, by being impossible to do without and by being loved. (Canon)
- They conquered a number of local tribes, and where the mercenaries used to kill and pillage she made herself the first queen of the world, ruling many lands around one area. (Canon)
- Rivals and enemies followed. One found a way and killed them both in an assassination. (Canon)
- She died furious and wouldn't accept it. From the Garden she looked down and jumped back. Her brother followed. They took other people's bodies and got their power back. (Canon)
- They told people it could be done. See [[the-jumping]]. (Canon)
