# The Anunnaki

**Kind:** people
**Status:** Canon
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

The returned dead: souls that came back from the Garden into the living world. They were the Queen's side in her war, they can reach the fourth awakening, and only a handful are left.

## What's true
- The Anunnaki are the returned dead: every soul that jumped back. (Canon; Claude's suggestion, Mo's yes on 24 Nov. He doesn't want it to read as zombies, and it isn't that.)
- A soul that has already left a body knows how to do it again, which is the fourth awakening. An ordinary soul stops at the third. That's the divide. See [[the-soul]]. (Canon)
- The Queen and her brother amassed a lot of them as their rule went on, and it became close to a racial fight: the Anunnaki against the "new souls", the ordinary or generic souls. (Canon)
- Not every Anunnaki joined her. [[the-farmer]] refused and was killed for it. [[the-collector]] neither opposed nor helped. (Canon)
- One Anunnaki was devastating, but the power isn't infinite: a group of about ten can still take one down. It's hard, and a big deal, but not impossible. (Canon)
- Alysta took one down using no soul at all, only his expertise and anti-soul Abnu items. It was a massive feat. (Canon)
- At the Queen's death the Seven trapped the Anunnaki who died with her in the moon. Not all of them died in that last encounter, and a handful weren't in it. See [[the-queens-war]]. (Canon)
- Sumerian names are reserved for the Anunnaki. (Canon)

### Who's left
Mo wants it a handful, to keep them super rare. These are the ones he has so far. (Canon)
- [[the-spymaster]]: the Queen's spymaster, away spying when the last battle came. Working to free her.
- [[the-collector]], also the Genie: needs no body, trades wishes for souls. His own side.
- [[the-farmer]]: his soul, trapped in an orb, lighting a cavern.
- [[the-serpent]]: a great sea serpent, loyal to the Queen, waiting.
