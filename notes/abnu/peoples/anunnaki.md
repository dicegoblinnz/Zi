# The Anunnaki

**Kind:** people
**Status:** Canon, thin. Mo has no settled reason for what they are yet and wants one worked out.
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

The Queen's side in her war: the people who can reach the fourth awakening. Everyone else is an ordinary soul, and can't.

## What's true
- The Queen and her brother amassed a lot of the Anunnaki as their rule went on. (Canon)
- It became close to a racial fight: the Anunnaki against the "new souls", which Mo is calling souls, normal or generic souls for now. (Canon)
- The Anunnaki can reach the fourth awakening. An ordinary soul stops at the third. That's the divide. See [[the-soul]]. (Canon)
- One Anunnaki was devastating, but the power isn't infinite: a group of about ten can still take one down. It's hard, and a big deal, but not impossible. (Canon)
- Alysta took one down using effectively no soul at all, only his expertise and anti-soul Abnu items. It was a massive feat. (Canon)
- The idea began as stronger beings, with everyone after them as normal beings. (Canon; Mo's starting point, not a reason)
- One of them, [[the-spymaster]], is still loose and working to free the Queen. (Canon)
- When the Queen died, the Anunnaki were all trapped in the moon with her. See [[the-queens-war]]. (Canon)

## Ties
- [[the-twins]]: who they followed.
- [[first-people]]: on 23 Nov Mo said he was using the first-people idea and not the Anunnaki themselves. He's now using the name for these people.

## Open
- Who are they? Born that way, an older people, or made (by jumping back, or something else)?
- Why can they reach the fourth awakening?
- Are any left in the world now, or were all of them in the moon?
- What do ordinary souls call themselves?

## Proposed
- The Anunnaki are the returned dead: every soul that jumped back. A soul that has already left a body knows how to do it again, which is the fourth awakening, and "new souls" is their word for everyone who hasn't died yet.
