# The molefolk

**Kind:** people
**Status:** Canon, first lines. Mo has "a whole thing" built around them still to tell.
**Changed:** 2026-11-24

Working label. A people buried underground with their whole town, who live by the light of the farmer's orb and have changed to suit the dark.

## What's true
- Generations after the orb settled, a people got stuck underground. Their entire town was buried in the rubble and they couldn't break out. (Canon)
- There were pathways from it to the cavern the orb lights. That became their life force: it's where they could grow plants and have water. See [[the-farmer]]. (Canon)
- They evolved longer, harder claws on their hands and can dig through the very earth, with mole-like features that help them find their way in the dark. (Canon)

## Ties
- [[the-farmer]]: his orb is their sun.
