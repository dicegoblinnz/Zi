# The look options (9 Nov 2026)

The pictures Mo picked the look from, for `notes/look-plan.md`. Each sheet's front and back are filled with Vesk as she stands on the Roots branch, with a picture of our own (her, hooded, against the moon over the rooftops), and its ink-saver front is blank.

- **Sheet A, the Reliquary:** the picture in a gilt arch, Soul over it as the keystone, Body and Mind either side, each a beaded seal with its track curving round it and a box under each size for a Scar. Armour on a plate under each seal (Soul's top left).
- **Sheet B, the Astrolabe:** the picture round in the middle of a brass dial, the three seals set into the dial with their tracks round them, steps into dice engraved round the rim, Aura down the left and armour and Scars for each Root down the right.
- **Sheet C, the Wayfarer:** a journal page with a binding and brass corners, the picture an oval miniature with a bow, the three Roots as wax seals with their tracks in straight rows on ribbons.
- **Home 1:** making a character first, with the four ways in, then the Learn deck, the crew and the rules. Signed in: a bar when a table's live, your characters with their pictures, your games.
- **Home 2:** the crew as big cards to swipe, Play one tonight, and Or make your own under them. Signed in: tonight's character, one tap to play.
- **Home 3:** the builder's first step on the home screen. Signed in: pick up where you left off, drafts included.
- **Play:** the picture at the top of the play screen with Body, Mind and Soul round it, and adding one from the camera button.

Every page is drawn in millimetres on one A4 SVG, so its boxes are where the PDF's fields go. To draw them again: `npm i --no-save @fontsource/cinzel-decorative`, then `node notes/look-options/src/sheet.mjs out && node notes/look-options/src/phone.mjs out && PW_CHROMIUM=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome node notes/look-options/src/shoot.mjs out`.
