What’s changed, part 4
Here’s what’s changed since part 3, and it all came out of the playtests. Sparks are now Resolve,
Confidence swings up and down during a scene, bonds are threads you can lean on, and there’s a new
archetype, the Bard. Where this says it replaces something, go with this one. Everything else in parts 1
to 3 still stands.

1 Resolve
   R E P LAC E S   Sparks.

Resolve is your character pushing themselves, so it pays for the big stuff. You have as much Resolve as you have Titles, so
2 to start, and you can’t hold more than that.

    Spend 1 Resolve to:
         take an extra action this round
         reroll a roll you just failed. You choose whether it’s all your dice or just one, and the threat’s dice stay where they
         landed.
         go beyond what a permission normally does. If you have to make a case for why it’s normal, it’s going beyond. That
         used to cost 1 Aura.
         hold your ground when fear, lies or pressure get through (see section 3)
         go at a threat when you’re shaken (see section 3)
    Get it back:
         1 at the end of every scene, and all of it after downtime
         1 when your Catch bites in a big way, one that changes the scene or costs you something you wanted. That’s
         instead of the usual 1 Aura, and it’s your call when.
         1 when the GM rewards great roleplay, like facing your fear or keeping a promise to yourself
         1 when you strain a thread (see section 5)
    Sparks are gone, and so are making an opportunity and adding a permission for the scene.
    So is GM trouble. The GM doesn’t offer you trouble for a Spark any more, and nobody turns trouble down. Letting your
    Catches bite is up to you now.

2 Aura
   R E P LAC E S   Going beyond for 1 Aura.

Aura keeps the small stuff:
    bringing in something that isn’t on your set action
    keeping a Catch quiet, or turning it your way for a moment
    winning a tie when you’re acting
    what 1 Aura gets your archetype
    a quick heal
    borrowing a permission through a thread (see section 5)

You get it back the same ways as before: top faces, letting a Catch bite, and the GM when a great setup doesn’t land.


3 Confidence
   R E P LAC E S   Confidence counting your Titles, bonds and clothes, and coming back in downtime.

Confidence is how you’re holding up right now. It’s still the die you roll against fear, lies and pressure, and now it moves
up and down during a scene. It never goes below d2 or past d12.

    Start every scene on d6. Clothes that fit the scene start you a size up. Titles and bonds don’t make it bigger any more.
    Fear is a telegraph. The GM telegraphs fear, lies and pressure like any attack, say a dragon’s roar as a d10 at everyone’s
    Confidence, and you answer it, Defend or let it ride, the usual way. That’s your fear check, and there isn’t another one.
    Stop all of it and your Confidence goes up a size. Taking a threat down sends it up a size too. If nothing’s aimed at
    you, it doesn’t move.
    For each one that gets through, you choose: let it shake you and drop a size, bury it and take a Hurt, or hold your
    ground and spend 1 Resolve so neither happens. That’s instead of the GM naming the cost.
    Fill a Hurt bar and write a Catch, and your Confidence drops a size as well.
    Nothing takes it below d2. Anything that would is a Hurt instead, or 1 Resolve to hold your ground.
    At d12 you’re fired up. Cash it in for an extra action, or to clear a Hurt box, and it drops back to d6.
    At d2 you’re shaken. You won’t go at a threat, in a fight or face to face, unless you spend 1 Resolve each time you do.
    Take a breath: spend your action and your Confidence comes up a size, as far as d6.
    At the end of the scene it goes back to d6. Anything that should last is a Catch.

  E X AM P LE

  Dorrie walks into the duke’s ball in her best gown, so she starts on d8. She sees straight through the steward’s lie, so
  she’s on d10. Then the duke’s sister cuts her down in front of the room, and it gets through. Dorrie doesn’t want to

4 The wound die
   R E NAM E D The heal die.


Your heal die is now called your wound die, because a bigger wound is worse. It works the same way: a d6, a size bigger
for each Catch on your Hurt track, and a heal has to beat it.

5 Bonds and threads
   R E P LAC E S   Bonds and connections, and the Confidence a bond with three connections gave you.

A bond is a person, someone in the crew or anyone else. Each connection you’ve got with them is a thread, one truth you
two share, like a debt, a rescue, a secret or a shared past. The more threads, the deeper the bond.

    Borrow through a thread: 1 Aura brings in their permission, the same as before, and it doesn’t strain anything. You
    can’t borrow through a strained thread until it’s mended.
    Strain a thread: lean on them in the story, like calling in the favour, using their name, dragging them into it or using
    the secret, and take 1 Resolve. It’s free, it doesn’t take an action, and the thread’s now strained.
    Mend it: make it right with them, by roleplaying it, doing them a favour or paying them, whatever suits them. That
    happens in downtime or in a scene with them, not in the middle of the fight where you strained it. Then it’s whole
    again.
    Strain it again before it’s mended and it snaps. You still take the Resolve, but cross the thread out. When the last
    thread goes, so does the bond.
    New threads come the way connections did: three when you make your character (write one as a debt and you get a
    fourth), from Grow, and when the Party Goal’s done.
    A thread with someone in the crew goes on both your sheets and counts for both of you, so agree on it together. If
    either of you strains it, it’s strained for both.

  E X AM P LE

  Vesk’s on Vane’s roof with no Resolve left. She drops the fence’s name to the guard captain, who’s in the fence’s
  pocket, and takes 1 Resolve. Her thread with the fence, I owe him a debt I’d rather not discuss, is strained. After the job
  she brings him a cut of the haul, and it’s mended.


Playing the strain
This is for when someone in the crew strains a thread with your character, so it stays honest both ways.

The thread gives the reason. The strain is about that one truth, so nobody has to invent a grudge.

 THE THRE A D              S TRA I N E D, I T S OUN DS L I KE                  I T’S ME N DE D W HE N

 A debt                    “We’re square now. Aren’t we?”                      you pay it back or do them a turn

 A rescue                  “I can’t keep pulling you out.”                     you save them, or finally say thank you

 A secret                  “You swore you’d keep that.”                        you trust them with one of yours

 A shared past             “That’s not who we are any more.”                   you go back to it together

Say how it shows, and what would make it right. Play that and nothing bigger, and once they’ve made it right, let it go.

Play it by what’s left. With two or more threads still whole, it’s a grumble and a “you owe me”. With one, it’s a real problem
and you’ll say so. With none, you’re cold until they make it right, and one more pull snaps it.

It never stops you working together in the moment. You still combo and still take the hit for each other.

  E X AM P LE

  Vesk’s thread with Bram is he carried me out of a fire, and I’ve never said thank you. She’s out of Resolve on a wall and
  yells for him to haul her up again, so she strains it and takes 1 Resolve. Bram’s player says he’s starting to feel like her
  ladder, and she can fix it by saying thank you. It was their only thread, so none are whole now, and he’s cold with her
  until she makes it right. At the bar afterwards she finally says it. The thread’s mended, and she rewrites it now that it’s
  said.

Bard                          Lift them: spend your action, and every friend who can hear you goes up a size of Confidence, as far as
                              d10. The last push to d12 has to be their own.
                              Hold them: when fear, lies or pressure come at you or friends who can hear you, answer all of it with

7 Boons and Banes
   R E P LAC E S   Three rows on the list.

    Sparks becomes Resolve: the Boon is +1 to your Resolve maximum, and the Burden is −1.
    Confidence: the Boon starts you a size up every scene, and the Burden starts you a size down.
    Going beyond: the Boon lets you go beyond once a scene without spending Resolve.


8 Your sheet for now
Until the sheet’s updated, here’s how to use the 13 October one:
    Resolve goes in the Sparks stars. Your maximum is as many stars as you’ve got Titles, plus or minus a Resolve Boon or
    Burden.
    Confidence: ring the die you’re on in its row, and move the ring as it swings.
    Threads go on your bonds’ connection lines, one each. Put a tick beside a line when it’s strained, cross it out if it snaps,
    and if you’ve got a fourth thread with the same person, put it in Notes. Ignore the Three live box.
    The heal die ring is your wound die.
    The printed notes for Rolling, Aura, Sparks, Confidence and bonds still give the old rules, so go with this instead.
