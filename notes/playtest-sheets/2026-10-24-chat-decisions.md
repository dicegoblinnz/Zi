# Chat decisions, 23 and 24 October 2026

Mo's decisions from the chat with Claude after the 23 Oct playtests, and the proposals he agreed to on 24 Oct ("I agree with all that you said"). This is the primary source for the 24 Oct rules change. Where the book had to fill a gap, the call is in `notes/to-confirm.md`, not here. Lines marked **agreed** are Claude's proposals that Mo said yes to.

## What the playtests showed

- A big grid combat ran smoothly and took 2.5 to 3 hours.
- The Warrior (explosions), the Leader and the new Oracle felt great, and every archetype should feel that good.
- The Leader handing steps to the Oracle swung the whole fight. Mo doesn't want the Leader nerfed.
- The Bard did a lot of work and was a bit strong: it could stop every fear coming at the crew.
- The Summoner was weak: it needs big dice from the start, and summoning costs the action. It needs a fresh rework (Mo didn't like Claude's), so it's parked.
- The Hunter felt lacklustre against enemies that die in two hits, more a boss killer than an everyday pick.

## Defence

- Each die of defence blocks one attack. If several people attack you and you can't block them, they go through.
- Your defence permissions are rolled passively. Defending as your action doubles them.
- Dodge or defence is a set action, like Fight, Sneak or Talk. "We don't need armour."
- **Agreed:** plate and shields are lines on Defend. Breaking a piece of armour is a cost you can offer instead of a wound. Confidence works the same way against fear: one block, doubled when you Defend.
- **Agreed:** a threat aims one die at each person, unless a tag says otherwise.

## Permissions

- A permission can only live in two set actions, custom ones included. If it's there and it makes sense, there's no argument.
- You can have the same word more than once, but never the same word twice on one set action. Use synonyms.
- Single-word permissions are back, because of the cap.
- **Agreed:** Defend counts as one of the two. Outside its two, a permission comes in for 1 Aura. Titles still count anywhere they fit. "The same word" means the whole permission.
- **Agreed:** the same word never stacks and different words always do. Two players in a combo are two sources, so their words stack.

## Intercept

- Intercepting costs 1 Aura, and anyone can do it: within your stride, step in and take one target's attacks, even several, choosing which dice. You can still use your action to Defend.
- The Guardian intercepts for free. That's his strength, and it means he can choose not to Defend.
- **Agreed:** Intercept replaces taking the hit and Defend's pull. The Guardian's free intercept covers every friend in his stride. Summons intercept for free. The Take the hit Boon goes, and its Burden becomes "you can't intercept".

## Resolve spends

- Every archetype gets a Resolve spend. The Guardian ignores one instance of damage. The Warrior treats his attack as a vulnerability, a type of its own.
- **Agreed:** Claude's ideas for the other fifteen.

## Vulnerability

- New. Each vulnerability on a target adds a tick to a hit: fire, slashing and prone make one hit four ticks. You still have to hit.
- The same type never stacks, so use different words (*fire vulnerable* and *consistent flames*). Two players combining do stack, because they're two actions. Vulnerabilities can be secret.
- **Agreed:** *Prone* and *Exposed* become vulnerabilities. Secret ones are hidden tags that Study finds. Be sparing with them on the crew.

## Archetypes

- **Bard:** it can split its dice to break as many fears as possible. **Agreed:** *Hold them* is a Defend against fear for everyone who can hear, with the song's lines counting twice, and anything it misses still has to get past each friend's own Confidence.
- **Oracle** (changed mid-play): build a pool of dice from your permissions and roll it as the action, then use it as foresight, setting allies' dice high and enemies' low. **Agreed:** foresight dice only come from splits, so steps a Leader hands over make them bigger, not more of them. Foresight fades at the end of the round, and a number has to fit the die it goes on.
- **Agreed, Hunter:** the Mark jumps to another creature when its target falls.

## Threats

- Tough is gone. Tags carry multipliers instead (*Sword ×2*, *Armour ×4*, *Shield ×3*), usually the creature's Challenge and higher on what matters most to it. The multiplier is wins only, never dice. Vulnerability ticks it down.
- **Agreed:** openings, weak points, Piercing and *first* work as they did, and an opening takes a whole tag. Extra ticks stay on the tag that die went for. A spent condition ticks once.

## Wounds

- No more Hurt boxes: the wound die is your Hurt. It starts at d2, and each Hurt steps it up, d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, then Blaze.
- After a Blaze, the wound becomes a Catch, as a Scar.
- At the end of every scene, roll the Confidence you finished on against your wound die. Win and it heals by one. Downtime recovers all of it.
- **Agreed:** each Scar starts your wound die a size bigger and nothing heals you below it, and with five Scars your next Blaze is your last. Traits and Boons that added bars are a hit or two each scene that doesn't move the die. Anything that worked on boxes moves the die a size. Confidence no longer drops for writing a Hurt Catch.

## Factions

- Four to twelve core factions, depending on the campaign. Each can make up to three mini factions, which are less to run but put more options on the board.
- A faction has a name and a Power die. Assets raise it: a standing army takes you from a d2 to a d4, and so on up to a d12. Wealth is gone.
- Resources and assets are permissions, not tags. Resources earn every downtime, and every asset needs one resource of upkeep. Spare resources pay other factions to use their own action.
- Mini factions only get two specific actions. Making one costs one of the parent's own resources and one of its own assets, double a standard build.
- Actions work at any scale, from a small town to the world stage. The prep's front-loaded, and the work after it should be minimal.
- The outcomes are Destroy, Undermine, Obstruct, Influence, Acquire (takes an asset or a resource) and Establish (adds an asset, a resource or a mini faction).
- Every faction has three bonds, Public Sentiment, Morale and Loyalty, each with its threads.
- **Agreed:** a faction acts like a player and defends like a threat. A good thread is a step, a bad one a vulnerability, and a strained one stops counting until it's mended. Keep the telegraph and the agenda die. Only telegraph the factions the crew can reach. Threads are one-way for factions and people outside the crew. A faction's threads toward the crew replace Standing. Personal bonds can be with factions, places and causes.
