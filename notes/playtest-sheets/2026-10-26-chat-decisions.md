# Chat decisions, 26 October 2026

Mo's answers after reading the 24 Oct rules change and its calls, and his picks from Claude's questions that day. This is the primary source for the 26 Oct rules change and the design work after it. Lines marked **picked** are options Mo chose from Claude's questions.

## Threats

- "The multiplier is as big as it needs to be", with guidance to be creative with placement: a dragon might have *Hide ×8*, and a dark paladin big bad *Plate Armour ×5* and *Shield ×3*. All relative to the NPC, and it has to make sense.
- Vulnerability removes tags, not just ticks inside one tag. One vulnerability strips two tags if they're single, or one tag by two if it's got a multiplier, and the player chooses: "I'll take off the shield and his sword rather than hitting the armour ×3". That's the power of vulnerability.

## Factions

- Mini factions (and hired companies, "hirelings") don't get a turn in the downtime they're made. From the next one they act like any other faction, with their own mind: at first influenced by their creator, then doing their own thing, and influenced by others as the downtimes go on.
- During downtime there is only one action per faction, no exception. Personal downtime slots stay at two each for now.
- **Picked:** the crew takes one faction action a downtime, and each player still has two personal slots, one of which can go toward a crew thing.
- Mo didn't know what "a spare" was.
- Crew assets and faction assets only give universal buffs depending on the fiction present, like *Healers*. The book needs a decent list of assets to be inspired by.

## Confidence and defence

- Confidence is fluid. It always starts at d6. Every successful dodge from defence or Confidence puts it up one, and so does successfully rolling against a threat (a lock opened, an attack landed, a door held, the bell stopped). Stepping your wound die up loses one, but not when your gear or armour takes the hit. Missing with all your dice in an action loses one.
- Defence is your dodging, armour included. Confidence is your mind, resisting fear and other mental effects.
- At d2 you can't take an action toward the task at hand unless you spend Resolve. Otherwise you spend the action to go from d2 to d4.
- At d12 you're on fire: you can surge for a new action, or bring your wound die down one.
- **Picked:** Defending doubles your Defend lines only. Confidence rolls as it is, and the Bard's song is what shields the crew from fear.
- **Picked:** when fear, lies or pressure get through, Confidence drops a size, or 1 Resolve holds your ground. Burying it as a Hurt goes.

## Intercept

- Any creature can intercept, a summon included, but only physical things. Confidence rolls are inner, mental attacks, so intercept doesn't work against fear. The Bard splits his dice against the fear coming at his allies instead.

## Threads and bonds

- A single bond can have several threads. Debt goes: each thread is a bond thread or a hostile thread (a debt can be hostile, a bond is something good). You can have both, and no thread is neutral.
- Every thread can be strained, which means it's almost snapped, so each thread reads as one of: bond, bond strained, neutral, hostile strained, hostile. Write a truth under each.
- **Picked:** two kinds of thread, each of which can strain, and a snapped one's gone.
- **Picked:** a hostile thread bites like a Catch: 1 Aura when it brings real trouble, 1 Resolve for a big bite.
- The ten group questions form threads between the players. Each player starts with two bond threads with the party. Adding a hostile thread lets them add another bond thread, to a maximum of three. In a two-player or solo game, the bond threads go to NPCs instead.
- Borrowing a permission through a bond costs Aura, and can't be done when the thread's strained or worse.

## Characters

- One attuned thing per character at creation. More can be bought in personal downtime, when Mo builds that properly later.
- A permission can be placed on two set actions at most, so *Deceptive* can live in two places. The same word can't go on one set action twice. It has to make sense, and if it doesn't, it costs an Aura.

## The website

- Add all of it to the website and mark it as new.
- **Picked:** the rules go live, marked New, once these fixes are in and tested. The new look, the reading and the builder follow a piece at a time.
- The whole site has to be user friendly, fluid and seamless, especially the character sheet: no endless scrolling, and choosing archetypes, Titles and items has to be easy.
- The dice on the character sheet change shape as they grow. Mo likes the kit (https://app-zi.storyforgenz.workers.dev/kit/) and its Struck design. **Picked:** real die shapes (d4 a triangle, d6 a square, d8 a diamond, d10 a kite, d12 a pentagon), drawn solid like Struck.
- The reference sheet doesn't work for him, and the printable character sheet is too plain. Both need to look better.
- The rules need a good rework for phones first (and laptops and PCs): bite-sized pieces, easy to read and understand, remembering where people left off, bookmarks, and the links popping up a short explanation with a Read more, so you don't leave where you were.
- **Picked:** a Learn deck (swipe cards for the core rules, in order, remembering your place) plus short look-up pages, with the pop-ups and bookmarks in both.
- Use every skill that helps, and be the expert: understand the root of what he's after, not just the words.
