# Chat decisions, 6 to 8 November 2026

Mo's asks in chat from 6 to 8 Nov, after four weeks of playtesting four times a week, and his picks from Claude's suggestions. This is the primary source for the Roots change (`notes/roots-plan.md`). Where he changed his mind during the chat, only the last call is under **Settled**, and the earlier ones are under **Superseded in the chat** so nobody reads them as rules.

## Why

> The actions, you know, like fight and defense and stuff like that. They're a little bit too annoying because people's actions can change based on what they want to do or whatever. So I kind of want to do something like a universal thing that gets applied.

> I want the core attributes or whatever it's going to be called to be like central to like doing almost anything.

## Settled

### Roots

- Three **Roots**: **Mind**, **Body** and **Soul**. He picked the name Roots ("I do like Root. Root is great. Mind, body, soul."). Soul is the presence one: inspire, hold your nerve. Force and Grace (Claude's four) felt like the same thing.
- Every roll is **a Root plus a specialty**, pitched to fit how you're doing it: "I'm going to insight presence, because I am trying to perceive my social standing… So I'm rolling presence and my insight."
- **A specialty can be whatever you want**: "It can live inside insight, it can live inside undead, it can live inside summoning."
- **If it needs justifying, pay Aura**: "everything is based on how you want to pitch it. And if it needs justification, you pay the aura cost to do it. Simple as that." His example: Presence to hold yourself still under a public insult, then reaching for sword mastery's discipline, "just pay the aura and then it's all yours."
- **What adds to what:**
  - Each **permission**: +1 to a Root and +1 to a specialty.
  - Each **Title**: +1 to a Root and +3 to specialties (going wide: Claude's suggestion of one step into each of three specialties, which he kept). Titles hold six permissions at most.
  - **Items**: +1 towards a specialty only, never a Root. "Using a dagger, I could justify adding it into my sword specialist… if I don't have a sword specialist… then I just have a single slot in towards using daggers, which is a single step."
- **No caps, and past d12 is the point**: "I don't mind people going above a D12. That is the whole point… 10 points in body and 3 points in mind. And I roll a D6 for my mind, but then I'm rolling a D12 and a D8."
- **The +1 for the action is gone** (8 Nov): "remove the +1 from action it was confusing people."

### The three tracks

- **Body has Stamina, Mind has Confidence, Soul has Resolve.** All three **start at d12** and **drop a size** for each hit that gets through to that Root. Any of them reaching 0 and the character's defeated: that's the Blaze.
- **A Scar locks the lowest size** of that track, for good: "I got scarred once and my body is d8 if I take one hit I am down to d6 then a second hit brings me to d4, and since I am out because d2 is locked the next wound sends me into a blaze." (His example started at d8. He then moved the start back to d12, below.)
- **Back at d12** (8 Nov): "since we will be spending root stuff like stamina and confidence and resolve we need the back at d12 and just make tanks have more armour on those parts etc. And have ways for them to negate impact etc." So tanks come from armour and ways to negate a hit, not bigger tracks.
- **Recovery** (8 Nov): "Recovery is 4+ on the dice… Stamina, confidence and resolve will be rolled at the end of every scene… if they beat the dice of 4+ meaning d2 will be mortal wound that needs time to recover. Downtime spend will recover 6 steps of your choice. And at the beginning of every downtime you recover one from each root."
- **No willing Blaze**: "No you can never blaze willingly." Spending stops at d2.

### Defence

- **The tracks are the defence rolls** (8 Nov): "when they get attacked they roll their defensive roots accordingly for physical it's stamina. For mental it's confidence and for resolve it resolve."
- **Defending as your action** "just works like any other action you choose a root plus specialty then roll."
- **Armour on every Root**: body, mental and soul armour, a buffer before the track drops, and it counts like an item when you Defend.
- **2:1**: "you can spend 2:1 from other sources to protect yourself against an attack otherwise targeted at a specific root. Meaning say I didn't want to take the body wound on stamina, I would pay resolve and confidence one in each to make it happen." Picked as the exchange rate for paying abilities too ("I like the exchange of 2:1 on ability uses too that's cool").

### Aura

- **Aura is the one thing you spend**, and it swings. Maximum **5**.
- **Gain 1 for every die that wins and for every top face**, and every die counts, defence included: "make all dice rolled gains aura." Splitting into lots of dice and winning lots is meant to pay: "encourages action oriented play."
- **Over the maximum on your own turn**: "if you gained more than your maximum you don't lose any aura until you end your turn so temporarily you may have 7/5 aura but if you did nothing you go back down to 5."
- **1 Aura** adds a permission, or pays for anything you have to justify. **3 Aura** buys an extra action.
- **Archetypes cap actions**: the most actions you can have in a round is one for each archetype, everyone's first free and the rest bought for 3 Aura, so two archetypes let you buy one. "So an archetype gives extra action potential." (He first gave it to Titles: "a two title character gets one action and spend 3 actions to do another then that's it. While a 5 title character can chain up to 4 more actions.")

### Archetypes

- **Archetypes are their own pick, apart from Titles**: "I want something to do with titles to not be tied with archetypes."
- **Taking one gives its passive. Its abilities are bought, six at most.**
- **Archetypes are powered by the tracks**: "archetype, powers it's abilities through root resources while attunment is through aura spends and aura storage… So that we are not starved for aura."
- **Every ability costs 1 step, and stacks**: "make abilities be one step each to make them all relatively balanced. Instead of 1-3. But make it stackable meaning it can bring extra people etc. or hit more people or is bigger or what ever it is it can be bigger by spending more."
- **2:1** from the other tracks (above).

### Attunement

- **Its own thing**, neither permissions nor abilities: "I don't want it to be permissions or ability something cool and unique."
- **It charges**: "You pump your aura into it to power it up… attunement gains activations (or something cool like that)." Claude's word, awakenings, is what the chat used from then on.
- **Each awakening is its own effect**, and any spread works: "a 1 step one and a 10 step one will activate together if they pumped 11." Stacking's welcome: "it can be fun to have it stack where 1 can be pumped a ton of times."
- **Six awakenings**, and **each awakening holds 2 Aura**: "6 permissions under titles, 6 abilities under archetype and 6 awakening under attunement."
- **It scales with what's stored**: "if there is a +1 to a dice when using the attunement for example with one aura stored but if you pumped 3 aura into it it can add +3 to dice rolls made with the attunement. Or you can pump it into 3 seperate single cost aura what ever the spread."
- **Passive awakenings need Aura stored in them, active ones spend it**: "Passive abilities will require aura to be stored into them while active ones just use the aura."
- **Its own passive is another way to gain Aura**: "The awakening gives an additional way to gain aura that will be its passive."
- **Only you pump it**, "but an archetype can."
- **One personal object**: "Attunement is specified to a particular object but that object is personal."
- **It empties at the end of every scene**: "Attunement aura resets after every scene."
- **Pets and summons come through attunement.** Summons: "d4 per aura awakened. So 3 aura is d12 if combined and 10 aura is 5d8 etc. Then have each summon deteriate by one step per round."

### Growing

- **When an Aspiration resolves, take a Title, an archetype or an attunement**: "you can either get an attunement, an archetype, or a title."
- Titles gain permissions, archetypes gain abilities and attunements gain awakenings.

## Ideas he raised, not settled

- **A die flip archetype**: "the ability to flip a dice to the opposite end of that dice… if I rolled a 2 on the dice on a d10… you flip the dice over to the other end, and that can help you potentially succeed." Claude suggested the Gambler: once a round, flip any die you can see after it lands (its size + 1 minus the roll), more flips for a cost, and a flipped die never pays Aura.
- **Starting picks**: "Maybe you can have 5 quote unquote points at the beginning of the game… 3 archetypes and 2 titles with no attunement slots." And "I want the game to start with 2 archetypes… Or maybe give it a choice."
- **Ranged attacks** have "been getting a little bit weird and wonky. We will need to figure that out later."

## Superseded in the chat

- Four attributes (Force, Grace, Presence, Wit), then three with Mind as Confidence and Soul as Resolve as buffers, then Stamina, Confidence and Resolve as d2-up wound dice. The settled shape is the three tracks above.
- Roots as the defence roll, and recovery by rolling the Root: replaced by the tracks as the defence roll and recovery on a 4+.
- Aura capped at 2 per Title: rejected for 5, with the overflow on your own turn.
- Aura from one die per roll: rejected for every die.
- Actions capped by Titles: moved to archetypes.
- Tracks starting at d8: back to d12.
- Abilities costing 1 to 3 steps: one step each, stacking.
- Attunements as a vessel that catches overflow and breaks for you, and a 1, 3, 6, 10 ladder: replaced by awakenings that each hold 2.
- Spending a last step to Blaze on purpose: never.
