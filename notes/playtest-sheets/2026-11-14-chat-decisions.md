- **Every Title, archetype and attuned thing comes with its one permission, ability or awakening, new characters too**, so the six crew are rebuilt around it (picked over only the ones gained later).

## Settled

- **Specialties are gone.** "We are killing special actions and actions in general."
- **Roots are built from Titles and permissions**: each Title puts 3 on the Roots (spread how you like) and each permission puts 1 on its Root.
- **Every thing on the sheet that fits adds its step**: Titles, permissions, archetypes, abilities, attuned things, awakenings and items. "We go back to what made this game what it is."
- **Anything you have to explain costs Aura.** "Anything that is explained and not clear it needs an aura spend. Simple as that."
- **Picking what fits has to be quick.** "It is annoying to count each action but we need to find a good way to make it as easy as possible to choose and add."
- **No action limit from archetypes.** Extra actions cost 3 Aura, then 4, then 5, and so on.
- **Every archetype has a Trait**, always on, beside its effect (its mechanic) and its abilities.
- **Each attuned thing adds 2 to your Aura maximum** and no longer gets you Aura its own way. Its power comes from losing Aura to power it, not pumping, so it gives Aura and eats it.
- **Each Title, archetype and attuned thing comes with one permission, ability or awakening**, at the start and every time one's chosen later.
- **New characters start on 35 points**: 5 for a Title, archetype or attuned thing, 1 for a permission, ability, awakening or item, with at least one of each of the first three recommended and the choice the player's, and more points for one-shots or catching up.
- **The dice roller takes more than one action at once**, going at them as well as coming at you.
- **Explanations on the phone are question bubbles**, a tap to read more instead of taking up the screen.

## Not settled

- **Growing by points** (2 a session, banked) and **an Aspiration as five things** instead of a die, from 8 Nov. Part 1 keeps growth as it is (downtime's slots and the Aspiration's die) till Mo says yes.
- **More parts of the rework are coming.** Mo: "This is the first part there will be more."
