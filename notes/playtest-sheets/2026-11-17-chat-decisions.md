# Chat decisions, 17 November 2026

Mo's ask in chat on 17 Nov, part 3 of the rework that comes in parts: six fixes on top of the new core (`notes/core-plan.md`). This is the primary source for `notes/fixes-plan.md`. The calls Claude made filling the gaps are in `notes/to-confirm.md` under 17 Nov.

## What he said

> Make the fixes below across the rules, character creation, the character sheet, examples and the website. Keep them consistent with each other and with the core. Only stop to ask me about decisions that are really mine. Use NZ spelling.

> **Fix 1: Remove the permission-for-Trait trade.** There's no longer any rule that lets a player trade or swap permissions for a Trait (for example, two permissions for one Trait). Take out every trace of it. Traits come only from archetypes, as their always-on passive.

> **Fix 2: Resources.** Every character has three Resources. A Resource is declared in the moment, like "as it happens, I've got rope on me" or "I've got a flask for this." Players don't list it ahead of time. Items, abilities and similar things can give more Resources. Resources are separate from items.

> **Fix 3: Items.**
> - Items are real gear the character has equipped: weapons, shields, armour, and tools like grappling hooks.
> - A character carries up to six items.
> - Items come in six tiers. Tier 6 is a d12's worth. Give the tiers proper, evocative names that fit the game, and offer me a couple of naming sets to pick from.
> - When an item fits the action, it adds its tier in steps on top of the Root. Example: Body 8 plus a tier 4 sword is 12 steps, which is 2d12, or any split the player wants.
> - Armour doesn't add steps. It soaks hits, which works like extra health against incoming harm. Build out how soaking works within the harm rules (1 point a hit, hitting another Root for 2, multiplied hits from vulnerabilities and deadly monsters).
> - Dodging uses Body. Some items can add steps only to avoiding harm, and never to anything else.
> - Make sure items fit with attunement locking Root points and with how Aura is spent.

> **Fix 4: Capacitor items.** Some items work as a battery or capacitor. You can store Aura or points in them, then spend those to power your own abilities. Work out how they fill, how much they hold by tier, and how they're spent, with no exploits like infinite Aura farming. Name the item type well.

> **Fix 5: Archetype abilities.** Write about ten abilities for each archetype to give variety for now. More will come later. Each should be a clear permission, fit the archetype's Trait and the dice-as-intents core, and show where it spends Aura. No pre-built actions or numerical tag stacking. Check with me on whether the Scholar is still in before writing for it.

> **Fix 6: Check the loop under tension.** Run the full play loop (scene, telegraphs and threats, rolls and splits, harm, Aura, healing and Toll) under real pressure, with threats on the board and the party stretched. Show me where it holds, where it drags, and where it breaks, and propose fixes. Remember my targets: rolls quick on paper and phone, a big roll should feel like a fistful of dice, spotting a clever fit must survive, and the big fight of a session should take about 45 minutes.

> **How to deliver.** Split the work into bite-sized parts so several chats can build them at once while it all keeps working together. Put the plan for these fixes in the repo notes before building. When it's done, give me a short summary of what changed and the decisions waiting on me.
