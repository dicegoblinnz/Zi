# Stacking: the plan

Mo's 14 Nov rework, part 1 of a rework that comes in parts. Specialties go, and every roll is a Root plus everything on your sheet that fits, a step each, the way the game worked before it lost itself. His words and his answers are in `notes/playtest-sheets/2026-11-14-chat-decisions.md`, and the calls Claude made filling the gaps are in `notes/to-confirm.md` under 14 Nov.

Several chats build it at once, one prompt each, and every chat reads this plan first. Each one owns its own files, works on its own branch, and lands back into `rules/2026-11-14-stacking`. Nothing merges to `main` till Mo says, because the live companion has to keep working for his four campaigns. The prompts themselves are in Mo's doc "Zi'Dara rework, part 1: prompts for each chat".

## Why

- Rolls got narrow. A roll was one Root and one named specialty, so a character's Titles, archetypes and attuned thing sat on the sheet doing nothing on most rolls. Stacking is the game: competent people putting everything they've got into the moment.
- Rolls took too long to build. Playtests still averaged 1 to 3 minutes a roll as players hunted for what counted, so picking what fits on the phone has to be quick, a tap a thing.
- The dice roller and the roll card could only put your dice up against one thing.
- Explanations took up the phone's screen.
- An extra action for 3 Aura got too cheap once everything stacks, and the cap of one action for each archetype felt like a tax on picking archetypes.

## The rules after part 1

Newest wins, and everything from Roots that Mo hasn't changed stands: the three tracks, Aura's gains (every die that wins and every top face) and its overflow while you're acting, defence, armour on a Root, 2 for 1, Scars, the Blaze, recovery, conditions and threads.

- **Roots:** Body, Mind and Soul. A Title puts 3 on your Roots, spread however you like (all 3 on one, 2 and 1, or 1 on each). A permission puts 1 on its Root. Nothing else adds to a Root, and specialties are gone.
- **A roll:** one Root that fits how you're doing it, and 1 step for every thing on your sheet that fits what you're doing: each Title, permission, archetype, ability, attuned thing, awakening and item you're using. An archetype taken twice is two things. Plainly fits is free, and each one you have to explain is 1 Aura. Going beyond is 1 Aura. Bringing something in (the ground, a threat's tag, a Catch, or one thing off a friend's sheet through a bond thread) is 1 Aura and a step. Boons don't add steps. Every roll's at least 1 step, and steps into dice is the same table.
- **Combos:** one Root, the roller's. Each friend's action brings every thing on their sheet that fits, 1 each, and a split. Your own extra action in the same roll is 1 step and a split.
- **Extra actions:** no cap. Your first action's free, and each one you buy with Aura costs 3, then 4, then 5 and so on, back to 3 each round. Holding, coming out of hiding and the Extra action Boon are on top, and don't put the price up.
- **Archetypes:** each gives its mechanic, its Trait (always on, free, one each) and one ability, the player's pick off its list, Push included. More abilities are bought, six at most, and paid from the tracks as now. There's no action limit.
- **Attunement:** each attuned thing puts 2 on your Aura maximum (5 becomes 7), and gets you no Aura its own way. It comes with one awakening (a second with a Catch, the stages as now). A passive awakening is powered, not pumped: while you're acting, free and no action, take Aura off what you've got and off your maximum till the scene ends, up to 2 in each (4 for the same one twice), and it works for as much as is powering it. Wins and top faces fill you back up to the lower maximum. An active awakening spends Aura when you use it, and so does calling a Summoner's souls. Only you can power it, or a Maker.
- **Making a character:** 35 points, more for a one-shot or catching up. A Title, archetype or attuned thing is 5 and comes with its one permission, ability or awakening. Each extra permission, ability, awakening or item is 1. The book recommends at least one Title, one archetype and one attuned thing, and the rest is the player's choice. Threads, Connect and the Aspiration cost nothing.
- **Growing:** an Aspiration past d12 brings a new Title, archetype or attuned thing, each with its one, and a downtime slot buys what a point does. Growing by points (2 a session, banked) and an Aspiration as five things wait for Mo's yes.
- **Characters already saved** keep everything they've got, even past 35, with their Roots recounted.

## The shared pieces

Every chat codes to these names and shapes, so the pieces fit when they land.

- **The sheet** (`rootsSheet()` in `src/companion/roots.js`, from the crew in `book.json` or from a build):
  - A Title is `{ text, roots: { Body, Mind, Soul }, permissions: [{ text, root }], boons }`, its `roots` adding up to 3.
  - `c.roots` adds up every Title's spread and 1 for each permission on its Root.
  - Nothing has specialties. `c.specialties` stays as an empty list till prompts 6 to 9 have landed, so a screen that hasn't moved yet still draws.
  - An archetype is `{ name, id, home, summary, for, trait: { name, text }, abilities, have }`: `abilities` is its whole list from the book, and `have` the names of the ones this character's got, the one it came with first.
  - An attuned thing is `{ name, stage, awakenings, catch, burden }`, with no `aura`.
  - `c.numbers.aura` is 5, plus 2 for each attuned thing and the Aura Boon's 2.
  - `c.points` is `{ start, spent }` on a build, `start` being 35 unless the player said more.
  - An old build's Title with a `root` reads as 3 on that Root, and its `specialties` and a permission's `specialty` are ignored. An old crew sheet reads the same way.
- **Things:** `thingsOf(c, s)` lists everything on the sheet that can add a step, as `{ key, kind, name, root }`, with kinds `title`, `perm`, `arch`, `ability`, `attuned`, `awk` and `item`. Keys last across a save and a reload: `title|i`, `perm|i|j`, `arch|i`, `ability|i|<name>`, `attuned|i`, `awk|i|<name>`, and an item's `itemsOf()` key. Two of the same thing never share a key (the second gets `|2`).
- **A roll:** `{ root, using: [key], cases: [key], other, borrow, beyond, extra, friends, handed, conds, pool, against: [{ die } or { n }], aim: [the target for each die] }`, counted by `countRoll(c, s, r)`: the Root, 1 for each key in `using`, the rest as now, and Aura for each key in `cases`, going beyond, what's brought in and the extra actions bought.
- **Extra actions:** `extraCost(n)` is 3 + n, n being how many you've bought with Aura already this round. The state's `bought` counts them, and `doneActing()` clears it. The Worker checks the price, not a count of archetypes.
- **Aura's maximum:** `auraMax(c, s)` is `c.numbers.aura` less what's powering awakenings. `s.power` (by `i|name`) replaces `s.store`, and empties at the end of the scene.
- **The state** goes to `v: 3`, and `normalRoots()` moves a v2 state across.
- **Points:** `costOf(build)` in a new, pure `src/companion/points.js` gives `{ spent, lines: [{ what, cost }] }` at Mo's prices. The build checks the crew with it, and the builder counts with it.
- **Several things at once:** `readLanes(lanes)` in `src/companion/roller-rules.js`, pure, reads a throw of several lanes (`{ mine, theirs, def, each }`, each read by `readDice()`) and adds up the Aura across them. The roller and the roll card's several targets both read with it.
- **Explanations:** `tip(id, html, label)` in `src/companion/tip.js` is a ? that opens an explanation over the screen and closes with a tap anywhere else or Escape, its words in the page so it reads offline. Every explanation in the companion is one of these, never a paragraph on the screen. Prompt 0 laid down a first cut (the native popover and `.tip` in `companion.css`), and prompt 4 finishes it.
- **A crew sheet in 03b:** `**First Title:** *The best cat burglar in the city* (Body 3). Permissions: *move unseen* (Body), …`, a spread written `(Body 2, Soul 1)`. `**Archetypes:** [Shadow](#a-shadow) (*Push*) · [Shadow](#a-shadow) (…)`, each archetype with the abilities it's got. `**Points:** 35`, then what they went on. No `**Specialties:**` or `**Its Aura:**` line.
- **A Trait** sits in its archetype's rule box as `**Trait:** *Name*. What it does.`

## How each chat works

- Branch `rules/2026-11-14-stacking-<piece>` off `rules/2026-11-14-stacking` and push it, so it gets its own Preview on the `zidara` Worker.
- Stay in your own files.

- Finish with what's built, the calls you made, anything another prompt needs to know, the Preview link, and which prompts can start now, going by the sections below.
