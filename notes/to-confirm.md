# For Mo: calls to confirm (24 Oct 2026)

Your rules left a few gaps, and the book has to say something in each one, so Claude made a call. Every one of these is in the book now, written as a rule. Tell Claude "yes", "no, do this instead" or "leave it for now", and the book and the change log get updated. Your own open questions are at the bottom of `content/changes.md`, and your 23 and 24 Oct decisions are in `notes/playtest-sheets/2026-10-24-chat-decisions.md`.

## 24 Oct: the ones that matter most

1. **How big a multiplier is.** You said it's usually the creature's Challenge, higher on what matters most, and gave *Sword ×2*, *Armour ×4*, *Shield ×3*. The book reads that per tag: the Challenge on the tag that matters most, more if the whole fight's about it, less on the rest.
   - Old *Tough 40* on one tag is ×6. Plain *Tough* went onto the tag that best says why it lasts, like the Painted Knight's *A Hundred Years of This ×6*.
   - Bosses whose Tough was split 20 and 20 now have ×6 on the main tag and ×3 on the other: the Wandering Giant (*Armoured ×6*, *Mournful ×3*), the Guildmaster (*Beloved ×6*, *Deep Pockets ×3*), Redhand (*Armoured ×6 (first)*, *Feeds ×3*), the Bell Below (*The Song ×6*, *Barnacle Hide ×3*), the Hollow Gentleman (*Borrowed Faces ×6*, *Feeds ×3*) and the Queen's Champion (*Perfect Guard ×6*, *The Crowd's Favourite ×3*). That's about three wins more than they had. Redhand's ten wins the long way, four or five rounds for a crew of four.
   - The Iron Sergeant (Challenge 5) is *Armoured ×5* (it was 20).
   - A threat with Forms gets smaller ones, about ×3 with two forms and ×2 with three, because they fill back up each time.
   - They're written for a starting crew of four. Add 1 to each for every 10 the threat budget's over 40, and take 1 off for every 10 it's under (a tag that gets to ×1 is plain). Playing alone, *Armoured ×6* is *Armoured ×3*.
   - If you meant the Challenge on every big tag, those bosses go up to ×6 and ×6.
2. **Hiring another faction.** From your words ("every faction has one action, use their own faction to do things for you"): the hired faction spends its own action that downtime where the payer says, rolled with its own dice, and the payer keeps its own action. A faction with a bad thread toward the payer won't take the money, and the money buys the action, not the win.
3. **Defend.**
   - Confidence doubles by its steps when you Defend (a d6 is 3 steps, so 6), and each pool gets one split.
   - A defence past 6 steps is several dice, and each one stops one telegraph.
   - With nothing on your Defend it's a d2 either way, so Defending doesn't help against blows, but it still doubles your Confidence.
   - A condition's step on your Defend, like *Warded*, counts once, and Defending doesn't double it.
   - *Poisoned*'s spend is now "your Defend's a d2 until the round ends".
4. **Intercept.**
   - 1 Aura for each friend you step in for, and you can take as many of their dice as you like, from as many enemies.
   - It works against fear, lies and pressure too, and then your Confidence handles them.
   - The GM's people intercept for free, like a bodyguard, and she says so before you roll.
   - A summon intercepts for free, for one person in its stride, and one hit takes it out. Not said yet: whether one summon can cover several friends.
5. **Vulnerabilities.**
   - One only adds to a win that's doing harm, a Hurt or stripping a keeps-it-going tag, and not to a win that places a condition or moves something.
   - Extra ticks stay on the tag the die went for, and they don't spill over, so a vulnerability's wasted on a plain tag. The GM chapters say to save them for threats with multipliers.
   - On the crew, one vulnerability makes a hit two Hurts, so be sparing with them.
6. **Wounds.**
   - The rest roll's an ordinary roll: a tie goes to the wound, and 1 Resolve rolls it again.
   - *Unyielding* and the Hurt Boon: the first Hurt each scene doesn't move your wound die. Playing alone gives you the same, so with *Unyielding* it's the first two. A crew of your own doesn't get it.
   - The Hurt Burden: you Blaze a size sooner, on a Hurt at d10.
   - *Tend the Crew* is once per scene now: when the scene ends, everyone in the crew who was in it rolls two Confidence dice for their rest, and the best counts.
   - Heals, quick heals, *Field Dressing*, *Healer's Hands*, the medical kit and the Bloodsworn's Aura all step the wound die down a size, and nothing heals anyone below their Scars.
   - The Healer's *Pull them back*: someone who Blazes stays in the scene, still takes the Scar, and their wound die goes to d10 (or their Scars). Their extraordinary moment and the choice to die still happen.
7. **Factions.**
   - A big asset takes as many wins as its multiplier to destroy or to take, and the taker gets it whole.
   - A bad thread only adds a tick to a Destroy or an Acquire.
   - With one party slot, the crew can go at one asset or resource out on its own, guarded by 1 step plus whatever Power assets are really there. Obstructing a bigger faction's action needs both slots.
   - Writing an agenda, Establishing with nobody in the way, and coming for the crew don't roll. A faction coming for the crew rolls its d12 on the table when it's telegraphed, and never against the crew.
   - "A die at its agenda" is the crew's *Set back its agenda* move: beat the agenda's die and it steps down one, and the faction's action still lands.
   - *Work for it* pays a resource for a big job, and the GM says so when she offers it.
   - Influence on a faction's face is always open to one slot, and trading costs no slot.
   - None, or as many bad threads as good, means it does business. A faction the crew's already crossed starts with one bad thread.
   - New agendas start at the table's die, without the old extra step.
   - In a war, each side splits its one action: one die at its war agenda, one Destroying an enemy asset.
   - The "don't know what it does?" d6: 1–2 works its agenda, 3 Establishes (or writes a new agenda), 4 Destroys or Undermines its worst rival, 5 comes for the crew, 6 spends a spare to hire someone (or Acquires).
8. **The crew's card.**
   - A new crew starts with one resource, *Odd jobs*, paying for one asset, *Four or five of us and a back room* (+1 Power), so it's Power d4 with nothing spare.
   - Crew assets don't add a step to rolls they fit any more. Only Power assets count, and *Fences*, *Friends at Court*, *Influential*, *Rooted* and *A Ship* were reworded to do one thing each.
   - The crew's Establish can't make a brand-new resource. It builds an asset with a spare, makes a mini faction, or builds back what the crew lost. New resources come from a Goal, a big job, a project, or off a faction. Without this, a spare buys a slot that makes a resource, and it snowballs.
   - The crew can make mini factions too.
   - Holdings don't need a resource paying for them.
   - The crew doesn't have the three faction bonds: its people are the characters, and its Reputations do the job of Public Sentiment.
   - Party resources are called **party holdings** now, so they don't clash with a faction's resources.
9. **Archetypes.**
   - The Bard's song can split as often as it likes. What it misses still has to get past each friend's own Confidence, the Bard's included. The Bard's own Confidence isn't doubled when she sings (she's not Defending).
   - The Oracle's swapped numbers don't give Aura and don't explode, and Isolde's foresight comes off The dead.
   - The Guardian's +2 goes on any Defend or Confidence die, so it works when he lets it ride too.
   - Resolve spends go with their mechanic, so when the mechanic takes your action, the spend does too.
   - The Warrior's *Opened*: the hit that opens the target ticks once more as well.
   - *Masterwork* covers the tags you make with one making roll. The Bloodsworn's Aura never heals below your Scars.
   - Not said yet: whether *Vanish*, *Become it*, *Call the horde* and *Swap* take your action, and who picks where the Hunter's Mark jumps.

## 24 Oct: smaller calls

- Single words are permissions again, but magic stays narrow.
- An attuned thing's permissions go on two set actions at most, like any permission.
- *Breaker* works "whatever its multiplier", to match *Piercing*.
- *Flurry* is a new tag: it can aim two dice at one person in a round. *Two Knives* does the same with its two knives, because aiming at two people in a round is what every threat can do now.
- A broken piece of armour's mended with a downtime slot, and usually a purse of gold.
- Attuned armour's "Armour a size bigger" lines are each a line on your Defend.
- Burdens on attuned things that used removed rows: *Others First* is the Regen Burden, *River Cold* the Heals-on-you Burden, *Spun Thin* the Hurt Burden, and *Never Gives Ground* is "you can't Defend as your action".
- A faction gives you things when its threads toward the crew are good. Buying from one needs a spare resource and no bad thread.
- Session Zero and Building Your Own Frame start with four factions, to meet your four to twelve.
- In Frames, the faction cards are written short: Power assets in brackets after the Power die, other assets on their own line, and threads toward the crew at the bottom. Factions that don't deal in money still have resources, whatever keeps them going (*Every Drowning*, *The Dark Gaps*). Spare resources follow the old Wealth: d12 gave two spare, d8 to d10 one, d6 and under none.
- Old faction strength tags are Power assets now (*Watchful*, *Rooted*, *Secretive*...). *Wealthy* became resources.
- Standing going up one is now "writes a good thread". A Friendly start is a good thread toward the crew, and Wary a bad one.
- Projects that paid crew tags now pay crew assets (*The Ledgers*, *An Ear in the Sickroom*), which need a resource. *Our Map* is a Reputation now, because crew assets don't add steps.
- Confidence: "anything that should last is a Catch" is now "is a Hurt", and *Down a size* names the cost of a miss as well.
- In Tactical Play: cash fired-up Confidence in before the scene ends (a sure step off your wound beats rolling for one), and finish high, because your rest rolls the Confidence you finished on.
- An ally beside the crew: its Challenge die stops one telegraph, it intercepts for free, and the crew intercept for it for 1 Aura.
- Playing alone, only telegraph the factions you can reach. Two or three are plenty.
- One-way threads: "it's yours, and theirs toward you is a different thread."

## Watch at the table

- **How long bosses take** with multipliers per tag, and whether vulnerabilities speed them up enough.
- **How fast the wound die climbs.** A vulnerable hit is two sizes, and a rest only brings it down one a scene.
- **Healers against big wounds.** A d12 wound's hard to beat with a heal.
- **The Oracle's 1** swapped into a boss's defence roll.
- **The Warrior's explosions against bosses.** An exploding 13 used to take 13 off Tough, and now it's one tick. If it feels flat, each explosion could add a tick.
- **A faction buying resources** with a spare, because a bought resource pays every downtime after.

## Smaller calls (16 Oct)

- A big Catch bite (1 Resolve) is only for a written Catch. Trouble with no Catch still pays 1 Aura.
- The Scholar's truth counts free on any roll it fits, because it can't sit on a set action. The GM can ask for a smaller one, and in an investigation a truth can't change what happened.
- Court favours are threads. Calling one in strains it (you take 1 Resolve) and they do the thing. Once it's mended, the favour's paid and you rewrite the thread, so a favour still only works once.
- A faction buying one of the crew's contacts snaps one thread.
- Retiring: instead of handing a thread on, you introduce a friend to one of your bonds, and they write a thread with that person.
- The Long Haul ship's *Hot Reactor* is trouble aboard, not a Catch, so anyone aboard can bring it in for 1 Aura.
- Crew threads go on both sheets, so the ready-made characters now carry everyone's threads. Isolde's thread with Vesk is the part they both know (*She stole the book that started all this*), and her secret stays in her crew answers.
- No Resolve for great roleplay when you play alone, because there's no GM to give it.
- A missing player's threads can still be strained, and you tell them when they're back.
- The Oracle chapter's "You gain a connection" is now a thread.
- An Elementalist can Study a whole room with an area (Exploring).
- The journey action "Lead the way" is now "Find the way", so it doesn't sound like the Leader's lead.
- A journey is one scene, so Confidence can wear down over days of bad weather. Worth watching in playtest.
- The Bard's *Lift them* reaches friends who can hear, not the Bard.
- "Let it ride" is back, as your word for leaving a telegraph to your one defence roll.

## The ones that mattered most (16 Oct)

1. **The crew's archetypes.** Your examples set Bram (Guardian and Leader), Ash (Elementalist), Dorrie (Face), Wren (Hunter), Isolde (Scholar) and Vesk (Shadow). The other Titles are Claude's picks:
   - Vesk's *Raised by the Guild* is a Shadow, so she's a Shadow twice (+3).
   - Dorrie's *The woman with a hundred names* is a Shadow (a spy hiding in plain sight).
   - Isolde's *The last student of a dead order* is an Oracle.
   - Wren's *An outrider from the border war* is a Hunter, so she's a Hunter twice (+3).
   - Ash's *The Court's runaway heir* is a Face.
   - Later on: Vesk's third and fourth Titles in Growing are Shadows too (+4, then +5), and Isolde's third Title in the campaign arc is an Oracle.
2. **Traits for a new character.** You said keep yours until traits are sorted. So a new character picks two traits from the list, not tied to a Title, with a short note that traits are being reworked. A Title gained later brings no trait.
3. **Title ideas stay as ideas.** The twenty-nine roles and schools are still in Title Ideas and the Crew, and each now says which archetypes it suits (Rogue a Shadow, Captain a Leader, Medium a Summoner or an Oracle, the Face idea a Face or a Shadow, and so on). Long Haul's three ideas: Pilot a Warrior or Leader, Spacer a Maker, Systems a Scholar or Face.
4. **When Confidence goes up.** "Stop all of it" is counted over a round. Taking a threat down in a combo lifts everyone who put an action in. Still unsaid, with Claude's suggestion:
   - stopping all of it and taking a threat down at the same moment: one size, not two;
   - "if nothing's aimed at you, it doesn't move" doesn't stop the lift for taking a threat down;
   - it moves at the end of the round, once you know you stopped all of it.
5. **Fear with a condition attached.** A telegraph at Confidence with an intent like *Frightened* or *Dread* still does that if it gets through, unless you trade it for a cost, and for fear the cost is one of your three choices (shake, bury, hold). Without this, Lanternfall's Dread could never land.
6. **Traits that touched Confidence.** *Iron Will* and *Style* now start you a size up. *Cool Under Pressure* and the *Heartened* condition bring you back up, as far as where you started the scene.
7. **Charming.** It adds a temporary thread for the scene. Strain it for 1 Resolve, then strain it again to snap it for another, and that's 2 Resolve a scene. Suggestion: you can borrow through a temporary thread, but not strain it.
8. **The downtime reroll** costs 1 Resolve (it was a Spark). All your Resolve comes back after downtime, so it's free in effect. Fine, or should it come out of next session's Resolve?
9. **Conditions on friends.** You said wards and blessings reach one person, or two with a split. The book does the same for every good condition you put on a friend (*Heartened*, *Rallied*, *Inspired* and the rest), because the area that used to spread them is gone. Two at most?
10. **Healing permissions on attuned things** (Sister Hale's Thimble, the Surgeon's Casebook). For a Healer they're a line on the heal roll. For anyone else they're a free quick heal once a scene. Keep that, or cut the second half?
