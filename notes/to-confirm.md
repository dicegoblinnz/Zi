### 1. The rules

Written into The Core, Building Your Character, How Archetypes Work, Conflict's Your Action and Appendix D on 14 Nov. Where the book says what prompt 2's calls say (a Boon's 2 points, or 1 on a Title with no permissions, and an attuned thing's awakenings past the ones it comes with at a point each), they're under 2 and not here.

1. **An ability's a step on a roll you use it on**, like Wren's roll to mark when she pays for Mark the pack. Push comes once you've rolled, so it's never a step. That's how the crew's rolls in the plan count (none of them counts an ability), and it stops Push being a free step on every roll.
2. **An awakening's a step on any roll it fits, whether you power it or use it or not**, the way the crew's rolls count *Long shot* and *A circle anywhere I can kneel*. Powering a passive one is what gets you its +1s, and an active one costs its Aura only when you use what it does.
3. **Armour's a step on any roll it fits**, not only when you Defend, since it's an item you're using: Dorrie's court clothes when she passes as someone of rank, the way the plan counts them.
4. **Something you use up is a step on the roll you use it in, and then it's crossed off**, so Isolde's salt packet in her ward is gone after it. Otherwise a smoke bomb would be a step on every roll for ever.
5. **Your keepsake and your bag are free**, and everything else you carry is a point, what's in your bag included. That's how the crew's points add up.
6. **An Awakened thing hasn't got room for an awakening you buy.** It holds the one it comes with and one for its Catch, so the book says buying more waits till it's grown.
7. **Take an archetype twice and it's still the one Trait**, since it's on all the time anyway. Its Push is +3, and one Push between the two is enough, the way Vesk's first Shadow came with Push and her second with Vanish.
8. **In a combo, anything of a friend's they have to explain is 1 Aura, and they pay it.**
9. **A ward's die past 6 steps reaches a second friend you can touch**, the way a split's second die does, so Isolde's 10-step ward is *Warded d12* on Bram and *Warded d8* on Vesk.

### 4. Question bubbles

1. **A tap anywhere else only closes the bubble.** Whatever's under that tap isn't pressed too, so a stray tap can't hit Next, End the round or a link while you're reading. A tap on another ? opens that one.
2. **The card comes up under its ?** (over it when there's no room under), as wide as the phone less a margin, with the screen dimmed a touch behind it. On a laptop it's a narrow card centred on its ?, and on a browser that can't place it by its ?, it's in the middle of the screen.
3. **The ? is ink, not gold or violet**, a small token like the kit's pieces, since it's nobody's. It fills in while its card's open.
4. **Every card has a title**, what it's about, which is also what a screen reader hears on its ? (How hiring works, About the scene clock).
5. **In a fold, the ? sits at the top of the fold, on the right**, so it shows once the fold's open. A button can't go in a fold's heading.

### 5. The dice roller

1. **The roller says all the Aura a throw pays**: 1 for every die that wins and 1 for every top face, added up across every action. It only used to count top faces. A die that stops a blow coming at you is a win, so it pays. A tie going at them pays nothing, since it's theirs unless you pay, an area die pays once however many it beats, and their top faces pay nobody.
2. **A new action starts as you going at them**, since going at more than one thing is what more than one action's mostly for. The first one on a phone that's never set it still starts as them coming at you, as before.
3. **A throw holds up to 8 actions**, each up to 12 dice a side as before. Add another action waits till the open one's got dice on.
4. **Opening the roller again opens the first action**, with the rest folded into a line each.

### 6. The play screen

1. **Tapping a permission in brings its Title with it**, an awakening brings its attuned thing and an ability its archetype, since the thing it's on usually fits too. Tap it out if it doesn't. That's what gets Vesk's lock to four taps: her Body, *pick a mechanical lock*, *know how a lock's built* and her picks, 13 steps.
2. **The chips come in three groups:** what built that Root, then what you've rolled with that Root before (the latest first), then the rest of your sheet. Pick the same lock twice and its things are at the top the second time.
3. **Again is the last roll's Root and what went in it**, counted on your sheet as it is now. What it was up against, its extra actions and anything brought in start fresh, since they change every roll, and anything crossed out since stays out.
4. **An ability on a roll's paid when you roll it**: 1 off its home track, or 2 for 1 when that track hasn't got a size to spare. The card says what it'll cost as you tap it in, and Roll waits till it can be paid.
5. **Going at more than one thing**, each die says which it's at. A new one takes the last die that's sharing a target, and you can move any die in Your dice. Aura's 1 for each die that beats what it's at and 1 for each top face, the way the roller reads it.

7. **Defend in When it lands uses the same chips**, with your armour on that Root in already, bar what's broken. A Defend's explaining and abilities are paid when it rolls.
8. **When a roll answers a telegraph on the live table**, only the dice at that telegraph go up as the answer. Anything else you aimed at on the same card is yours to sort out at the table.
9. **The last roll on the play screen is the Again tile**, saying how it went last time. The GM's look still has the Last roll line.
10. **Old traits from before Roots** say they don't come across, since archetypes have a Trait of their own again.
11. **Putting your action into a friend's answer on the live table** brings a step for each thing that built the Root you pick, till the live table lets you tap in what fits a thing at a time (prompt 8).
