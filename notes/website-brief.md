# The website brief

Mo's answers from 16 Oct 2026, given before the redesign. Everything built for zidara.world follows this. When Mo changes his mind, change it here.

## The look

- A modern game screen: dark by default with a light switch, crisp type, cards, glowing highlights, dice as icons.
- Signature colours: arcane violet and radiant gold.
- What was wrong with the first look: it read like a document, the colours were wrong, and it was too plain. It worked fine on phones.
- Mo likes the feel of D&D Beyond (the sheet front and centre), World Anvil (rich lore), Demiplane (dark, glossy cards with big art) and Alchemy (cinematic and atmospheric).
- It has to look good with no art, with spaces that get better as art arrives.
- Fully separate from Dice Goblin: no shop branding and no credit. The home page gets its own intro about the game. The Welcome chapter keeps Mo's words, Dice Goblin line included.
- The home page depends on who's there. Newcomers get what Zi'Dara is and why it's great. Signed-in players go straight to their characters and games.
- Silent unless someone turns sound on. A telegraph landing buzzes the phone.

## Who and where

- For Mo's playtest groups, GMs running it at home, brand-new players and groups playing online.
- Players use the website in their phone's browser. No app store app.
- Also used on the GM's laptop and tablet, and on a TV or a screen shared on a video call.
- Sheets and rules keep working offline. Live play needs internet.
- Sign-in with Google, Discord, Apple or an emailed link. Invite-only at first.

## The character companion

- Four ways in: a guided builder with info bubbles, start from a crew member, a blank sheet, or random in one tap.
- Its number one job is building a roll fast: tap an action and see its steps and dice ready, with the lines that need Aura marked. Playtests found rolls take 1 to 3 minutes while players hunt for permissions.
- Dice: roll in the app, with a proper animation, or roll real dice and tap in the result. The player chooses.
- A sheet is seen by the player and their GM, nobody else.

## The GM companion and live play

- Prep: build threats (Challenge, tags, tough tags, the budget), plan scenes (objectives, stakes, the twist), keep campaign notes and NPCs that players see once revealed, and use generators (names, places, the Oracle).
- Live: set the round's telegraphs and send them to phones, see the party's Aura, Resolve, Confidence and Hurt as they change, track the round's step budget and each threat's tags, and push a picture, a map or the telegraphs to the table screen.
- A telegraph shows on the target player's phone and they answer it there. The threat's die and their answer roll together, in the app or with real dice, and the GM sees the result straight away.

## Worlds

- Everything: linked wiki pages for places, people and factions, maps with pins, timelines and family trees.

## Order

- The new look first, then the character companion. Each piece goes to Mo's playtests as soon as it's ready, and what the groups say shapes the next.

## Already decided, 13 Oct

- The rules are open to everyone. Accounts are invite-only at first.
- The site is the one master copy of the rules, and the book, references and character builder are built from it.
- Worlds hold official content and GM-built worlds.
- Rule changes come four ways: Mo tells Claude, Mo edits in the browser, playtesters leave notes, and trusted co-writers suggest edits for Mo to approve.
- Live telegraphs run on every phone and, when wanted, on a shared table screen.
